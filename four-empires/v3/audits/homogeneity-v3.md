# Homogeneity check, v3 turn 525–550

Four documents, four agents working blind, measured against the four human-written turns of 500–525 as a control.

## Verdict: ~85% probability of one author, against ~97% in v2

Real progress, and the progress is in *format*. Egypt writes numbered council Items; Rome writes continuous narrative essays with a fiscal table; China writes topical memoranda with bolded sub-labels; the Maurya writes a reign chronicle with blockquoted reasoning boxes and an appendix row. Lexical signatures differ properly — the Maurya spells numbers out at 6.0 per thousand words against Egypt's 0.5; China owns *instrument* and *register*, Egypt owns *floor* and *compartment*, Rome owns *per cent* and HS. The em-dash and the aphorism are gone.

**But the format diverged and the mind did not.** All four are written by an institution with identical virtues, identical epistemic hygiene, identical appetite for self-correction, and identical emotional temperature. The homogeneity moved from the sentence to the character of the court.

## The three strongest tells

### 1. Affect is at zero in all four, and the variance collapsed

Emotional or evaluative vocabulary per 1,000 words:

| | Egypt | Rome | China | Maurya |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Human turns** | 1.1 | 0.5 | 1.6 | 2.5 |
| **v3 turns** | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.2 |

Every v3 hit is a false positive on inspection — "the bitter seed", "the standing fear" — doctrinal nouns, not feelings. **Across 50,000 words and four empires there is not one instance of a court feeling anything.**

The human control is not merely warmer, it is *differently* warm per country. Human Maurya: *"a court that was miffed to the point of grief and moving at the speed of injured pride"*. Human Egypt: *"The mood in the chamber is grief over the squandered century-long head start, not embarrassment over the leak."* Human China: *"A court that has just declared its own monopoly gone is not drunk on its own success."* Human Rome: *"Rome does not respect statehood; it respects the capacity to do it harm."* Four temperaments — hot, elegiac, self-auditing, cold. v3 gives four instances of one: level, unhurried, never pleased with itself, never angry, never afraid.

**Four real chanceries do not share a nervous system.**

Related: nobody boasts. Not once, in a set including one empire that just stormed nineteen cities and one that just opened a second ocean. Human Rome *"trumpets this loudly and largely truthfully."*

### 2. Four courts independently built the same epistemic apparatus in the same 25 years

Every one articulates the distinction between *shown* and *found*:

- **Egypt:** "A number a man is shown is not a number he has found... The counts stand as floors and are not to be treated as measurements."
- **China:** "Cold reading: a counted position is a displayed position... a garrison that wishes a number seen can arrange to be counted."
- **Maurya:** "a flush side may be a side whose ports have not been found."
- **Rome:** "a purpose-built observation that returns nothing has returned something."

Same philosophical move — absence of observation is not observation of absence, a displayed quantity is a lower bound — reached independently by four courts with no shared scholarly tradition, in one generation, and stated explicitly rather than practised silently. Four labels for one apparatus: Egypt's *firm/reported/rumor/assumed*, China's *Cold reading:*, the Maurya's fact-then-interpretation, Rome's *the file*.

In the human control only Egypt and China mark confidence at all, they mark it differently, and human Egypt *fails* to mark it in one place — which is the error that turn dramatises. **No v3 document ever fails to mark anything.**

### 3. Nobody is stupid, corrupt, defensive or self-flattering, in four states at once

Every v3 document contains an admitted error stated without excuse, a named internal loser with a note on whether the losing office kept standing, a priced downside on a decision taken anyway, a "what this does not tell us" self-audit, and a standing order created to fix a fault found by use.

Not one contains propaganda, an unexamined assumption, a self-serving account, a suppressed inconvenient number, or a claim the document does not immediately qualify. **Real state archives are mostly composed of exactly those things.** Four courts incapable of fooling themselves are one author incapable of writing a court that fools itself.

## Convergent institutional invention

| Invention | Egypt | Rome | China | Maurya |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standing non-engagement observation order for a distant coast | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Written escalation ladder for foreign contact, before any contact | ✔ | — | ✔ | ✔ |
| Printed standard return justified because a nil return is informative | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Enumerated withholding list published beside a disclosure | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Deliberate deception with true position recorded and cost priced | ✔ | refuses, but announces the refusal | ✔ | ✔ |
| Display of manufactured strength to a counting rival | observes it | — | ✔ | ✔ |
| Quarantine / contact-disease discipline adopted this turn | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Needle-deflection telegraph, blocked on insulation | held already | — | ✔ | ✔ |

Three courts inventing a written contact ladder for an unvisited coast in the same twenty-five years does not happen. Two independently building a compass-needle telegraph and independently naming *insulation* as the blocker is the second.

A shared construction also survived the em-dash purge. Rome: *"states that it is withholding it rather than leaving it to be noticed."* China, same turn: *"as your own court kept its reckoning and said so plainly rather than let it be discovered."*

## Shared decisions

All four halt an advance and all four give a supply-and-distance reason. All four decline to fire first, in writing, three in near-identical words. All four refuse to raise the principal land tax and all four name and reject the alternatives. All four report a squeezed margin and none is in crisis. All four concede technological inferiority to a rival and none disputes it. All four disclose partially and record the withholding internally.

**Four real governments in one generation would produce at least one that overreached, one that raised a tax rather than cut a programme, one that fired on somebody, and one that told itself its position was better than it was.**

## The strongest counter-evidence for independence

Shared-event descriptions do not match numerically. Egypt reports the beach display on eleven occasions; the Maurya applied Second Degree five times. And **Egypt's document contains a hole the Maurya's fills**: the Maurya find a cairn with a squared timber and unreadable characters in 544, and Egypt — which describes its survey squadron exhaustively — never mentions planting it. That is a genuine asymmetry of the kind a single author usually smooths, and it is why the verdict is 85 and not 95.

## Recommendations for the next turn

**Cross-cutting, highest yield first:**

1. **Mandate affect.** Instruct at least two courts to be in a mood — one triumphalist, one aggrieved — and let the mood distort the document. A court that has just conquered nineteen cities should sound like it.
2. **Mandate one unflagged error per document.** A claim the court believes, is wrong about, states with full confidence, and does *not* qualify.
3. **Force asymmetric length.** Budgets of roughly 7,000 / 11,000 / 14,000 / 18,000, allocated by who had the eventful turn.
4. **Force asymmetric coverage.** Give each country two or three domains it is forbidden to discuss, different per country.
5. **Ban the universal self-audit close.** At most two of four may end with open questions.
6. **Vary the four-part scaffold.** It appears in all four v3 documents and none of the four human ones, and is buying a large share of the homogeneity for free. Let one empire fold intelligence into narrative, one give no territory list, one summarise its letters rather than quote them.

**Per country:**

- **Egypt** — retire "Accepted cost:", which is the em-dash's replacement. Mark only half the foreign figures and let the rest stand as assertions the council forgot to check. Give it one decision it makes badly and defends, visibly motivated by the treasury's interest and presented as neutral.
- **Rome** — change least; it is the strongest and least generic, the only one with no confidence apparatus at all. Push further its own way: let it be openly triumphant about the strip, let the Senate flatter itself once, let one fiscal figure be a fiction the *curatores* have stopped questioning.
- **China** — delete "Cold reading:" outright; it is Egypt's apparatus relabelled. Let its caution live in what it does. Give it a genuine pathology: a suppressed censorate report, a shaded memorial, a faction that wins an argument it should have lost.
- **Maurya** — halve the self-audit. It confesses four faults in its own Order, an overstatement in its notice, a twenty-nine-year failure to Rome. Let it defend itself once. Keep the Sixth Degree reasoning, which is the only real moral position in the set, and push it toward self-righteousness.
