# v3 audit record, turn 525–550

Every finding below was produced by a reviewer agent holding only the documents named, blind to the coordinator's prior conclusions. This is a digest of findings and resolutions, not the verbatim reports — those ran to tens of thousands of words across eleven passes and existed only in the coordinator's session. **The digest is the durable record; treat it as a summary written by an interested party, because it is.**

Passes run, in order: four tech verifications, four broad sense-checks, four full-history coherence audits, one encounter adjudication, one letters audit, one homogeneity check.

---

## What the audits caught that the coordinator did not

Listed first because it is the most useful thing here. In every case the coordinator had read the document and missed it.

**Refuted by the world, not by the text.** Egypt's aircraft carried a 40–120 hour engine overhaul life. Egypt's own Atlantic patrol has run 18–22 knot craft over five thousand miles of coast since the 460s — which at that life means an overhaul every five days of steaming. The patrol could not exist. The constraint was invented to satisfy coordinator pushback that implied the numbers were too high; the source's only real ceiling was airframe aluminum, and the previous turn had built the works that lifted it.

**The clue in the court's own file.** China's colour-corrected lens "failed" for want of separating the bending of light from its splitting, four paragraphs after the same document recorded that its mirror telescope shows no fringing while every glass instrument does. Same observatory, both facts maintained.

**A category error in an exhaustiveness claim.** China's electrode search covered "every metal the arsenals stock and every mineral the alchemists keep." Carbon is neither, and charcoal is the material that empire holds in the greatest quantity and best-characterised form, burned by the ton for the powder works in that very turn.

**A number that is 97 per cent of its own ceiling.** China's literacy at 41 million against a demographic maximum of 42.3 million, assuming universal literacy in every cohort schooled in the period. *(This finding was later shown to rest on a false start date — see Corrections below.)*

**A surplus that was the sum of its own suppressions.** The Maurya's 106 million closing surplus equalled, to within one million, the amount by which five budget lines had been held below their historic share of revenue — administration worst, flat at 240 million, the lowest in eight centuries, in the reign that added a continent.

**The signature phrase of the whole exercise**, from the Maurya audit: *the growth is asserted at the level that matters and derived at the level that is easy to check.* Presentation arithmetic closed exactly in all four documents while the causal arithmetic underneath did not.

## Sense-check (the broad "does anything look wrong" pass)

The pass that found the territorial freeze. **All four powers independently chose consolidation in the same twenty-five years** — Rome deferring the Yucatan, Egypt declining the Caribbean, China holding nothing on a coast it had charted, the Maurya converting founding to holding. Each had a locally plausible reason, which is what made it hard to see: the homogeneity showed up as a shared *decision*, not shared phrasing.

Each freeze also had a manufactured licence. Rome fired its own Yucatan trigger and deferred anyway on four reasons that did not bind. China invented a fifty-year supply prerequisite for a crossing its previous turn had already made and resupplied. The Maurya's was a scoring rule leaking into the prose: *"the ruler had two fights and spent them elsewhere."*

Also found: every turn hedging its own books into ranges while hedging foreign estimates correctly; extensive cataloguing of inactions; and pervasive narration of the documents' own drafting — much of which the coordinator had explicitly requested and later had to remove.

## Corrections to the audits themselves

Three findings were forwarded to countries and later withdrawn. Recording them because the failure mode is instructive: **a reviewer's premise can be wrong, and the coordinator forwarded all three without checking.**

1. **China's literacy ceiling** assumed the loop began in 525, so that everyone over twenty-five at 550 was schooled before it started. The loop began in **500**; the 7 per cent figure is the 500 AD rate. The cohort inside the loop is everyone under fifty, roughly three quarters of the population. Corrected figure: 30 million.
2. **China's twenty-year electrical delay** was treated as a defect requiring explanation. Research takes time, and this is the hardest thing that empire is attempting with no theory of the force. The duration needed no justification.
3. **Rome's migration** was measured against six years because the mainland allotments opened in 544–545. People who landed on the islands in the 530s can move to the mainland when ground opens. The correct denominator is the whole period plus internal redistribution.

Related, and the coordinator's error rather than a reviewer's: **a file was modified while its audit was running.** Three of the Maurya's findings were damage from restoring a struck section without revising the text written around its absence. Do not mutate a document under a live audit.

---

## Standing lessons

- Reviewers holding only the source, with the failure mode named and no knowledge of what the coordinator has already said, outperform the coordinator's own reading by a wide margin. Run them **before** pushing back, not after.
- Pushback that implies a direction produces a fabricated justification for that direction. Ask what the constraint *is*, never whether a number is too high.
- A clean report must be an acceptable outcome, stated in the brief, or reviewers will manufacture findings.
- Never tell a country what it observed. Ask what it does and what its standing orders are, then adjudicate contact from both sets of orders.

## Where the loop stands, and what comes next

All four turns have been through three rounds of audit-and-fix. Round three ran
the two tech re-verifications and a from-scratch letters audit against settled
files, and every finding from it has been applied. Commit `c0fcd5a`.

**Round three, what the passes confirmed rather than found.** Rome's fleet
ledger, powder chain, transport ledger, western population rebuild, 288-million
breakdown and both fiscal tables; Egypt's seven revenue streams, opening-revenue
reconstruction from individual stream rates, reserve arithmetic, settlement
ledger, research tables and every source citation, individually checked; China's
spending and revenue tables, literacy age bands, selection fractions, Northern
Road, far-coast logistics and the whole Suppression Margin section against
canon; the Maurya's building ledger at 1,587 with full propagation, standing
charge at 125, revenue at 2,616, research ledger on both axes, and Territory
reconciling to 57 harbours item by item. Rome's compound-steam horsepower move
was ruled **supported** against the source's own gate and four downstream
dependencies, and is closed.

**Three coordinator rulings made this round.**

1. **The Maurya volume of 530 was never lost.** An earlier ruling had it sent and
   taken by banditry on the Mesopotamian road. That was an invented in-world
   event covering a gap in the coordinator's own process: the volume had simply
   not been delivered, because distribution had not happened. Withdrawn. See
   COORDINATOR-NOTES 3c.
2. **Rome's western disclosure to the Han went at about 500, not 515.** Canon was
   split; the reconciliation is that Rome answered on the return of the voyage,
   in the closing years of the source window, before the generation in which its
   one message went to Pataliputra. Neither canonical document is in error, and
   the draft's "filing error" framing is removed.
3. **The Antilles collision is unadjudicated and is deliberately unforwarded.**
   Rome holds Hispaniola, Cuba and "the lesser islands of the chain"; Egypt holds
   nine harbour posts on the arc north from Trinidad; and Rome has entered a
   twenty-five-year null sighting as evidence and drawn a strategic conclusion
   from it. Whether the two lines miss turns entirely on what "the chain" means,
   and the Rome source uses it for the Lesser Antilles. **Do not send this to
   Rome as an audit finding** — it would tell Rome what it saw. Adjudicate it on
   geography and standing orders, as the Australia encounter was, and let the
   result reach Rome as material in INCOMING.

**Structural change, applied to all four.** Every turn now carries an empty
INCOMING section before MESSAGES OUT, with subheadings for documents received,
intelligence returns and technical assessments. Each court was told not to guess
at what will arrive and not to soften a position in expectation of it.

**Still open, in order.**

1. Re-run the passes against `c0fcd5a`. Every pass so far read a file state that
   has since changed, so all of them are stale, including the ones that came back
   clean. A homogeneity re-check matters most: the last one read v2-era text and
   the length spread has since tightened from 8 per cent to 7.
2. Re-run the spy adjudications. They were run against turns that no longer
   exist.
3. **Only then, step 2: distribution.** Into each INCOMING section goes the
   actual text of letters sent to that court, the adjudicated spy returns, and
   the technical verdicts. This includes the Maurya volume of 530, which Rome has
   spent its whole turn reasoning about the absence of, and China's letter of 537

   honoured in full.
4. Countries revise against what they have just learned. Replies open.
5. Hostile review before anything is treated as canon.

The homogeneity recommendations for the *next* generation are unactioned by
