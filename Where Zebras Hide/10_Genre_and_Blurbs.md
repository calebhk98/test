Blurb: 

After witnessing the impossible, Mira has three months to learn what kills most people who try, or become another statistic in a war humanity doesn't know exists.

Jason's abilities make him a target for those who want what he knows. His solution? Teach Mira to fight creatures that shouldn't exist, in places that shouldn't be real, using skills that defy every law she thought governed reality.

But the more she learns, the more visible she becomes. And there are things hunting by instinct that would cross worlds for prey like her.

Earth has stayed safe by staying silent. That silence is about to break.

Option 2 Blurb:

Mira's coffee date ends with blue light and an impossible choice.

Jason can do things that shouldn't exist. When his past catches up to him, he offers her three options: forget everything, lose him forever, or follow him into a world where knowledge itself is lethal. She chooses to stay.

Now she has three months to master skills that take years to develop, or die trying. What starts as survival training becomes something darker when she discovers why Jason's abilities make him a target, and what's really hunting people like them.

Because Earth isn't special. It's safe. And there are things that would change that in an instant if they knew humanity existed.

Some secrets were buried for a reason. And Mira just helped dig one up.
