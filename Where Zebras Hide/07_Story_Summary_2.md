A 2nd summary: The Premise Earth is not a magical wasteland; it is a biological hiding spot. A parallel dimension, rich in "Mana," overlaps our own. This other world is filled with predatory creatures that feed on energy. However, crossing the barrier between worlds costs an exponential amount of energy.

* The Great Filter: For powerful, sentient monsters, Earth isn't worth invading. The energy cost to cross over is higher than the energy they would gain from eating humans, who naturally have zero Mana.  
* The Ecosystem: Humans are safe only because we are "invisible." We are like 7 billion silent zebras hiding in tall grass. The Mechanics (The Rules of Survival)  
1. Information is a Bio-Hazard: The moment a human truly understands how Mana works, their biology shifts. They begin producing Mana.  
2. The Scent: Mana acts as a beacon. The more you have (and the more you use it), the brighter you shine to the predators in the other dimension.  
3. The "Science" Shield: Human history, skepticism, and the dominance of science are actually a form of psychological camouflage. By convincing the population that magic is fake, humanity is kept from "lighting up" and attracting the apex predators.  
4. The 3-Month Rule: Once "infected" with the truth, a human must learn to cycle/control their Mana within 3 months, or the energy build up will cause them to likely be noticed by predators. The Characters  
* Jason: A "Newton-level" genius who stumbled upon Mana by accident. He treats magic like physics/math. He is powerful but stays alive by mastering "OpSec", hiding his scent. He is hunted not by the government, but by jealous, lower-tier human practitioners who want to steal his proprietary techniques.  
* The Protagonist (MC): A grounded, "non-nerd" regular person. They have no interest in fantasy or power. They are dragged into this world solely because of their relationship with Jason.  
  * Motivation: Not curiosity, but Devotion. They refuse to be a "pet" or liability.  
  * Role: They represent the gritty, practical will to survive. The Plot Arc Act I: The Infection Jason is ambushed by human hunters and forced to use a randomized teleport, dragging the MC along. He reveals the truth: He will have to leave, leaving her alone, or if he tells her, she will be stuck with this.  
* The Conflict: The MC must choose between losing Jason or a lethal new life.  
* The Choice: The MC chooses to stay. They begin training not for glory, but to avoid dying from the "3-month" energy toxicity.

They then train, travel to the other world. and even have to protect Earth when the knowledge gets leaked globally.
