**Mira's Clone Ability**

Mira's ability centers on creating physical clones of herself. Unlike constructs or magical projections, these clones are fully living beings that produce their own mana naturally once complete enough to do so. The cost of a clone is in its construction, not its upkeep.

**Progression:**

*Phase 0 ,  Puppet:* Mira's first clones are pure extensions of her body. She sees through their eyes simultaneously with her own, and they move exactly as she moves. When she takes a step, it takes a step. They are limbs, nothing more.

*Stage 1 ,  Standard clones:* This is the level Jason operates at. The clones gain a degree of independent movement ,  not true autonomy, but something like a sub-brain running in the background. She can direct them the way a person presses a key to move a character in a video game. She still sees through their eyes, but it becomes more subconscious, easier to filter.

Most practitioners find the step from Phase 0 to Stage 1 uncomfortable. Operating something that wears your own face like a video game character is genuinely strange, and the initial reaction is almost universal. Most push through it because the utility is worth it and the feeling fades with use. Mira has the same reaction ,  it isn't a unique flaw in her, just a standard one she never quite gets past the same way others do.

*Stage 2 ,  Living clones:* Mira is a film photographer, and that habit of eye ,  noticing when something is fractionally off ,  carries into how she looks at her clones. She starts noticing small details that don't match. Jason tells her not to bother.

*"Fixing those details just burns more mana for no real gain."*

She does it anyway. He tells her there's no meaningful difference. She tells him she can still see the issues, and keeps refining. Eventually Jason notices something that shouldn't be possible ,  their mana is growing.

Around the same time, Mira mentions that she can't easily separate her senses from them anymore. Where before she could filter their perspective to the background, now it's fully combined with her own. Jason thinks on it and reasons that her clones had become complete enough ,  detailed enough in their biology ,  that their bodies crossed the same threshold any human does when they truly understand mana. They started producing it on their own.

They are now fully living things. Outside of combat, this means Mira regenerates mana several times faster with clones active. In combat it doesn't help much yet ,  the clones still share her mana pool at the point of creation, so producing several of them costs most of her available mana, leaving all of them critically low. She mentions this to Delilah ,  that they're basically useless in a real fight, and that it's too bad there's no way to create them already at full mana.

Delilah says: *"Well, cloning isn't really copying, is it? It's creation. There's no reason what you create has to match your current state."*

*Stage 3 ,  Full mana clones:* Working from Delilah's observation, Mira figures out how to create clones initialized with full mana rather than a share of her own. The modification requires significantly more precision, which drops her numbers ,  roughly 3 of these versus the 9 she could previously maintain ,  but they are now genuinely combat viable.

The consciousness dynamic has fully shifted by this point. All clones share a single hive mind with her. They see with all eyes, hear with all ears, and experience everything simultaneously. She is not commanding them ,  they are her, spread across multiple bodies. The more clones she maintains, the more simultaneous sensory and cognitive input she is processing at once. Reabsorbing them collapses a distributed mind back into one body, causing severe pain ,  not from mana overload, but from the cognitive compression of that reintegration.

Later, Mira mentions to Delilah that she wants to be able to create more clones but doesn't have the mana capacity yet to sustain them.

Delilah says: *"Too bad your clones can't just make their own clones."*

Mira agrees immediately. *"Yeah. That would be nice."*

Jason asks: *"Why can't they?"*

Both Mira and Delilah turn to look at him.

*"It's impossible,"* Delilah says. *"That would be completely broken."*

Mira nods. *"And even if it weren't, the mental load alone would break me. I already get headaches managing three of them."*

Jason pushes back. He points out that she already shares consciousness with her clones, that the mental cost is real but a significant part of maintaining them is mana overhead, not just cognition. He says something being broken by the standards of what anyone has done before doesn't make it impossible ,  fusion is broken energy, cooking meat is an overpowered strategy relative to anything else in the animal kingdom, and those things exist. He acknowledges he has no idea how she would do it in practice, and that it would require someone with an unusual amount of patience and attention to detail. But he can't think of a single reason it's actually impossible.

He doesn't push further. He corrected the logic. That's enough for him.

Mira figures out the rest herself.

*Stage 4 ,  Recursive clones:* Because her clones are fully living beings producing their own mana, a clone creating a clone generates a genuinely independent biological entity. The mana cost traces back to the grandchild clone's own biology, not to Mira. The ceiling of the ability becomes almost entirely cognitive. At her best, she can sustain 200 clones each capable of producing 200 of their own, recursively. Reabsorbing at that scale is agonizing enough to collapse her entirely.

**Why Jason can't replicate it:**

Jason attempts to replicate the technique after observing it and fails. The barrier isn't conceptual ,  he understands the principles completely. The barrier is that execution requires a specific kind of granular, detail-oriented focus that isn't how his mind works. He can identify that the door exists. He cannot thread the needle. The ability stays distinctly Mira's because it reflects something true about how she thinks.

**Stage Placement:**

*Phase 0* ,  Chapter 8, early in the montage. Cycling is stable, Jason expands her training. First clones are introduced here as the next skill.

*Stage 1* ,  Chapter 8, same montage. She progresses from puppet to standard clones within the same training block. The discomfort moment of operating something that wears her face is a good small beat within the chapter.

*Stage 2* ,  Begins quietly in Chapter 8's background, Jason notices the mana growth in Chapter 9 or 10 as a secondary scene alongside the creature encounters. The refinement happened off-page; the discovery is the scene.

*Stage 3* ,  Chapter 11 when Delilah arrives. Mira complains about combat uselessness, Delilah makes the creation observation. The recursive conversation happens in Chapter 12 once she's working with the new clones and hitting their limits.

*Stage 4* ,  The conversation spark happens in Chapter 19 during the intelligent creature encounter when the stakes make it feel urgent rather than theoretical. The actual breakthrough and development carries through Chapters 20 and 21\.
