# Planning documents review, part 1 (Ch 1 to 15 and the shared docs)

Scope: STYLE, CHARACTERS, RULES, CREATURES, TIMELINE, outline/README, BOOK_MAP, outline/ch01 to ch15, and reviews/critic/round2_critic.md as evidence. Ch 16 to 30 are the other reviewer's.

Format of each finding: file and exact quote, what is wrong, fix. "Cut" means delete the line. Findings are ranked most serious first inside each section. Section 7 traces the round 2 critic problems back to document lines; it cross-references findings above and is not counted again.

## 1. Contradictions

**1.1 The Box needs a Jason clone, and Jason cannot clone.**
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "A clone of Jason keeps fighting it on a loop"
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "Jason understands the technique completely and can't do it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "A copy of him is fighting it on a loop."
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "That's not how it works for me."
Wrong: the Box, the book's second headline technique, runs on exactly the ability RULES says he lacks; Ch 8 also has him teach clones ("You're building a body that's you, out of you."), and Ch 10's "dying hundreds of times" has him feeling his own clones die.
Fix: RULES: "A figure made from his own mana, not a clone in Mira's sense (it has no senses of its own and shares nothing with him), keeps fighting it on a loop." Ch 10: replace "A copy of him" with "a figure of him the Box makes." Then Jason's "That's not how it works for me" is true: his figure is a tool, her clones are people.

**1.2 Ch 3 offers erasure the rules say he cannot do, and it implants a memory the rules do not allow.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "or all of him, the whole two years"
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "Years is beyond almost anyone."
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "just tonight (the attack, the woods, this conversation), so she'd remember him moving away"
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "It leaves no gap she'd notice and no side effects."
Wrong: three problems. Two years is outside RULES. "Remember him moving away" is an implanted memory, and RULES only has deletion. "No gap she'd notice" is false when she is 300 miles from home with an unexplained evening, and her texts to Jess, Sarah and Mom all say she is somewhere else.
Fix: Ch 3: "He says he can take the last few weeks of him, cleanly, or just tonight. The second he'd have to cover with a story, because she would wake up in the wrong place. He says it plainly and says he has done it before." Drop "all of him". If the full-erasure threat is wanted, add to RULES: "Jason is one of the few who can take years. It costs him days."

**1.3 Ch 11 and Ch 12 both stage the "creation, not copying" discovery, and Jason is jealous of the wrong person.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "She tells Jason in the morning. He admits he never thought of it that way."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Jason stops and asks how. She explains. He's delighted and a little jealous."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Delilah said cloning is creation, not copying. Mira has been chewing on it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "**Funny:** the dessert, and being jealous of Delilah's idea."
Wrong: Ch 11 has Mira work it out in one night and tell Jason the next morning. Ch 12 treats it as unsolved and shows her telling him again. The idea is "hers" in Ch 11 and "Delilah's" in Ch 12.
Fix: end Ch 11 on her working it out alone at night and telling nobody. Ch 12 keeps the Box reveal. Ch 12 "Jason in this chapter": "jealous of Mira's idea, because he can't do the technique at all."

**1.4 Ch 12 requires a half-smile that Ch 1 forbids, on a different argument topic.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Mira remembers Jason's odd half-smile at the students arguing about predictability in the coffee shop, the night it all started. Now she knows what he found funny."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "Jason doesn't react to the content."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "smirk at the students"
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "two students start arguing about free will and physics"
Wrong: Ch 1 says no reaction, no smirk, topic "free will and physics". Ch 12 says half-smile, topic "predictability". A Ch 12 drafter sees only the previous chapter, so this will contradict whatever Ch 1 says.
Fix: cut the sentence from Ch 12. If the callback is wanted, change Ch 1 to allow "Jason glances at them once and says nothing", and make Ch 12 match the Ch 1 topic.

**1.5 When and how Jason tells her about the hunters is stated four ways.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Jason at dinner, too casual: "So. There are some people camped about a mile out.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "Over coffee, the first morning he feels them"
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "Dinner on day two of the hunters' watch"
Quote [revision/TIMELINE.md]: "two days of watching (he tells her the first morning)"
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "She notices he's keeping the promise from Chapter 9"
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "Jason promised in Chapter 5 to tell her when he decides things about her."
Wrong: dinner versus morning coffee, day one versus day two. The promise is from Ch 5 or Ch 9. If he feels them the first morning and tells her at dinner on day two, he sat on it for 36 hours, which breaks "I'm telling you."
Fix: pick one. Ch 12 end: "Jason, at breakfast: "Some people camped about a mile out last night."" Ch 13 header: "Day one, breakfast, then dusk of the same day, then dawn." Promise: "the Ch 5 promise, and the Ch 9 fight about it."

**1.6 Clone counts disagree between TIMELINE and RULES, and RULES lists them out of order.**
Quote [revision/TIMELINE.md]: "Three to six Stage 3, used in the fights"
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "End of Act II | three to four Stage 3"
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "She has three full-strength clones"
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "Ch 21 to 24 | a few dozen held easily day to day" (listed above the Ch 19 and Ch 20 rows)
Wrong: "three to six" against "three to four" against "three". The RULES table also puts Ch 21 to 24 before Ch 19 and 20, so a drafter reading top to bottom sees the count drop and rise.
Fix: TIMELINE: "Three, sometimes four." RULES table in chapter order.

**1.7 Marcus's text is answered and not answered in the same chapter.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "She sends one word back: "fine"."
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "Marcus's text, which she doesn't answer."
Fix: Old-life beat: "Marcus's text, and the one word she sends back." Also fix the TIMELINE line "Marcus texted in February; she sent one word back", which is right.

**1.8 The first lies to Sarah and Mom happen in Ch 2 and again in Ch 4.**
Quote [revision/TIMELINE.md]: "First phone lies to Sarah and Mom."
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "Mira texts Jess ("Staying with a friend tonight"). Mom asks about the milk for Sunday. Mira lies smoothly and is startled by how smoothly."
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "She calls Sarah and invents a spontaneous road trip. She calls Mom and says she'll miss Sunday dinner, sorry about the milk."
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "She sees she has texts she missed during the walk."
Wrong: Ch 2 already has the first lies, so "first" is wrong. The Ch 2 sentence "Sarah: "You ok? You never skip gallery stuff" comes after Mira's own lie about not making it tomorrow" cannot be put in order, because Sarah's text is a reply to a lie Mira has not sent yet. "During the walk" refers to a walk that does not exist in Ch 2.
Fix: Ch 2: "Sarah has texted: "Still on for gallery night tomorrow?" Mira answers that she can't make it. Sarah: "You ok? You never skip gallery stuff."" Ch 4 is the second round: she now has to explain more than one missed night, and the lie gets bigger. TIMELINE: "The lies get bigger".

**1.9 Delilah felt the Box "for weeks", but the Box is introduced in the chapter before she arrives.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "For weeks she's felt "a new warmth" out this way, then something big and strange (the Box) that rattled her teeth."
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "A stronger stray crosses near the creek."
Wrong: Ch 10 is Mira's first sight of the Box and happens "a few days" before Ch 11. Either the Box has been running for weeks or Delilah is wrong.
Fix: Ch 11: "Two nights ago she felt something big and strange (the Box) that rattled her teeth." Keep "weeks" for the new warmth only.

**1.10 The hovering hand is six inches in Ch 1 and "twelve inches" in Ch 8 and STYLE.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "His hand stops six inches from her elbow, then drops."
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "Twelve inches: at most 1, as a callback in the hand scene if you want it."
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: ""Two years". "Twelve inches". The hovering hand."
Wrong: the callback does not match what it calls back to. "Twelve inches" is a leftover from the old draft that no current document explains.
Fix: delete "Twelve inches" from Ch 8 and STYLE.

**1.11 CHARACTERS says Jason has no habits and does not lecture. The outlines give him both.**
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "No other quirks: no fidgeting, no props, no habits added to make him seem real."
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "He paces when she struggles. He keeps starting to help and stopping himself."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "He doesn't volunteer technical explanations."
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "Jason teaches it like physics"
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "It's the one thing he talks about without being asked."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "A lot of what you're calling headache is mana overhead, not thinking, and..."
Fix: CHARACTERS: "He explains clearly when he decides to, and about his own work he is the one person who runs long." Ch 7: drop the pacing, or have him do something useful (fetch food). Ch 12: cut the headache sentence. Delilah's "Don't you start." works without it.

**1.12 The witch-trial history is told in Ch 4, then again as "the only time the book explains it" in Ch 12.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "The witch trials were worse. Afterward, people like him pushed everyone toward thinking magic was nonsense."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Keep this to one told story with Mira's reactions. It's the only time the book explains it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: ""That's why your science teachers were so sure of themselves, honey. We made sure.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "The full story comes later, from someone else."
Wrong: by Ch 12 Mira already knows the conclusion, so Delilah's punchline lands as old news, and "only time" is false.
Fix: Ch 4: Jason says towns got wiped out and stops: "There's a lot of history. Someone else should tell it." Ch 12 keeps the full story, and Mira's reaction can be that this is the first time she's heard the why.

**1.13 The TIMELINE says Ch 6 is a highway trip, but both ends of it are in east Tennessee.**
Quote [revision/TIMELINE.md]: "Sat Feb 10 to Mon Feb 12 | Highway, rest stops, Appalachian foothills"
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "a cabin in the Appalachian foothills (east Tennessee)"
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee."
Wrong: Crossville to the east Tennessee foothills is about two hours. Two and a half days of "highways, rest stops" has no reason to exist, and Delilah's "forty miles over the ridge" sits in the same region.
Fix: give a reason in Ch 6 Scene 1: "Jason takes back roads for two days, doubling back, because he wants to be sure nobody is behind them." Put the cabin in a region far enough that the trip is plausible, or shrink the trip to one afternoon.

## 2. Instructions a drafter will follow too literally

**2.1 Verbatim lines that need a setup the outline does not give.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "I hated Dave. I hated his carrots."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "Most people are boring."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "He remembered everything except to ever ask me on an actual date."
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "How old were you when you first almost died?" / "Ten.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: ""Too bad your clones can't just make their own clones." / "Yeah. That'd be nice." / "Why can't they?""
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: ""Do you think you're selfish?" / "Yeah. I do.""
Wrong: Dave is a coworker she still sits near, so a past-tense "I hated Dave" needs a story she is telling; nobody knows who says "Most people are boring" or about what; "Ten." arrives out of nowhere in the middle of a childhood story. The drafter invents a setup to carry each line, and the invented setup is where the amateur seams show (the therapist and keyboard-habit inventions in the Ch 2 critic report are the same mechanism).
Fix: for each verbatim line give one sentence of context in the outline: who says it, to whom, in answer to what. Or demote the line to "the sense of" and drop the quotation marks. For "Most people are boring", say who says it and what it replies to.

**2.2 "Detail to use" items are symbols with the meaning removed on instruction.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "the billboard for a personal injury lawyer, and the dog with its ears flattened in a minivan window. Keep these from the old draft, and don't spell out what they mean."
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "**Detail to use:** spring peepers loud in the creek."
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Don't use an outside event or object to mirror a feeling"
Wrong: a dog with flattened ears in the middle of a "what is coming for me" conversation is an outside object standing for her fear, which STYLE bans two files later. The outlines also mandate a prop per scene (soap in paper, notepad logo, breath fogging, peepers), so every chapter gets a hand-placed detail the reader is meant to catch.
Fix: replace every "Detail to use" with "Optional texture, pick none or one that fits what you've written." Cut the dog and the billboard.

**2.3 "Plant for later" sections tell the drafter which details matter, then the outline says not to emphasize them.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "**Don't flag this as important**, and don't say it will matter."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Underline that this conversation matters. The reader should almost miss it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "He looks at Mira a beat too long, like he's just heard himself."
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "He watched it too, for one beat too long."
Wrong: a drafter that has been told "this is the key plant" will stage it. The only reliable way to keep a plant light is to not tell the drafter it is one. The outline also stages the plants itself with the exact pointing gesture STYLE bans ("looked at the door a beat too long").
Fix: remove "Plant for later" from the drafter-visible outlines. Keep it in a separate planning file. Write plants as ordinary content: Ch 12 "Jason asks, mouth full, why they can't, and nobody answers him because Delilah is already telling him not to start." No look.

**2.4 Quota beats: "one old-life beat and one romance beat" per chapter.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Every chapter includes one beat from her old life and one romance beat (see the outline). Both can be a single line."
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "She looks at the creature photo on her phone and almost sends it to Nora, as a joke she couldn't explain. She deletes the draft."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "In Spain she realizes her phone plan doesn't work there. For the first time, nobody from home can reach her. She's surprised it feels like relief."
Wrong: the quota produces a bolted-on line in chapters that do not need one, and the Ch 1 round shows what happens when old-life beats are listed in order: Nora, Mom, milk and Dad in one breath. A phone plan not working in a chapter that has just crossed an ocean twice is filler.
Fix: delete the quota. Keep old-life material only where it changes something (Ch 9 Nora, Ch 15 Nora, Ch 8 Mom).

**2.5 Bullet lists that will be recited in order.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "he leaves, and she never sees him again / he erases tonight, or him, and leaves / she follows him without knowing, and is probably found and killed / he tells her everything, and it changes her"
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "warming her hands / making terrible coffee taste decent / how to read whether a stranger's warmth means them harm / how to behave around strong people without starting something"
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "he sets her coffee within reach without asking / he sits on her side of the porch steps / he stops saying goodnight from his doorway and says it from hers"
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "how old are you, how'd you meet him, are you two together"
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "the job and Marcus / the film and the darkroom / Nora and her parents / Jess, once"
Wrong: the Ch 3 draft read the four options out as a list (critic Ch 3 finding 7.1), and every list above will be delivered the same way, in order, one item per beat.
Fix: write each as a purpose, not a list. Ch 3: "He tells her what he can do for her and what each choice costs, and she interrupts him before he's finished." Ch 8: "Show one small change in how he is around her over the next few days. Pick it yourself." Ch 12: "Delilah teaches practical things Jason has never bothered with. Show two."

**2.6 Traits written to be performed.**
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "She cries, sometimes, and hates it, so she gives herself a time limit."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "cries for exactly four minutes on her phone timer"
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "He can't cook either, which is why he eats her cooking without complaint"
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Before each round she says, "For the record, I still think this is wrong." Every time. He says, "Noted," every time, and it becomes their joke and also not a joke."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "She asks Mira personal questions in the first hour."
Wrong: each trait gets a mechanical tell (a timer, a refrain, a catechism). The four-minute timer is also then listed as a plant. STYLE says no habits "invented to make a character seem real"; these are exactly that.
Fix: Ch 15: "cries behind the house until she's done, then washes her face." CHARACTERS: delete "so she gives herself a time limit." Ch 12: say it once at the first round and again when it matters, and let the joke form or not. Delilah: "Asks Mira something personal early, because she's nosy."

**2.7 "Mira notices" instructions put the pointing in the outline.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "She notices he's keeping the promise from Chapter 9, and it matters to her."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "She notices that this time he's asking. Keep it to one line of her noticing"
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "She notices he doesn't say "definitely"."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "Mira notices the vagueness."
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "One line, quickly covered, and Mira notices."
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Don't have Mira note that something is suspicious or file it away"
Wrong: the outlines instruct exactly the behaviour STYLE bans.
Fix: replace each with the observable fact: Ch 13 "He tells her the day he feels them and says that's what he promised." Ch 15 "He asks instead of announcing." Ch 7 "He says "probably nothing" and goes inside."

**2.8 Lines in quotation marks in the outline that are not marked verbatim.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "Five stars. The ice machine works."
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: ""Happy whatever today is." She says, "You remembered the date and not the holiday?" He says, "I'm aware of the holiday.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "this is either love or a medical condition"
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: ""You let me think it was me!" "It was also you.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "That might actually be the healthiest response I've ever seen to this conversation."
Quote [revision/outline/README.md]: "Copy only the lines marked **verbatim**."
Wrong: README says only verbatim lines are copied, but the outlines carry dozens of quoted lines that are not marked. Haiku will copy them, so the book gets the outline author's jokes in every chapter. They are also all polished pairs, the thing STYLE calls amateur.
Fix: either mark them "(sample, don't copy)" or paraphrase them without quote marks.

**2.9 "Make it funny" assigns humour.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "Make this funny: Jason ordering at the bakery badly every morning, and the baker visibly enjoying it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "She laughs so hard at the beach one that he starts laughing too."
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "Keep it, but make it a laugh, not a grade."
Wrong: STYLE says humour is occasional and comes from what she notices. A scene told to be funny, with the joke named, is a gag. "Visibly enjoying it" is also a stage direction.
Fix: describe the situation without the verdict: "Jason has bad Spanish and the baker is patient about it." Cut "make it funny".

**2.10 Delilah's offhand remark gives away the answer, so Mira's "working it out" has nothing to do.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: ""Well, cloning isn't really copying, is it? It's creation. There's no reason what you create has to match your current state.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "Delilah's remark was a nudge; the idea is hers."
Wrong: the verbatim line is the full solution in plain words. Mira's darkroom insight afterwards is a restatement. Also nobody explains how Delilah, who has never seen a clone, knows enough to say this unprompted.
Fix: Delilah says something looser and slightly beside the point: "You're filling them up from your own tank? Why would you do that?" Mira's work in the dark is the insight. Remove the verbatim marking.

## 3. Weird or unnatural writing in the documents

**3.1 Stage directions the documents ban elsewhere.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "he says, too casually, "Happy whatever today is.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Jason at dinner, too casual"
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "He looks at Mira a beat too long, like he's just heard himself."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "After training, casually, as if he's been thinking about it for weeks, which he has"
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "awkwardly, like a man texting with gloves on"
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: ""looked at the door a beat too long""
Wrong: "too casually" and "a beat too long" are a director's notes, and the drafter turns them into narration ("he said too casually"). They are also the vague microgestures STYLE lists as amateur.
Fix: say what he does or says. Ch 7: "He says "Happy whatever today is" while pouring the coffee, and she has to decide whether to take it as a joke." Ch 13: "He sends the message with a lot of concentration and slightly too much force; she has never seen him do it."

**3.2 The author's voice is in the files the drafter reads.**
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "Items marked *(proposed)* are my inventions to fill gaps. Change any of them."
Quote [revision/CREATURES.md]: "These are options to react to, not decisions. Pick, mix or throw out."
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "Note for you, not for the prose: it's the time zone, and she works it out months later. Don't hint at it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Cut the fusion and fire comparison, and the cooking-history claim."
Wrong: "Change any of them" and "throw out" tell a Haiku agent the characters and creatures are optional, so names and designs drift. The Ch 12 "cut" refers to something no drafter has seen. "Note for you" tells the drafter a secret it will leak.
Fix: strip the author notes. Anything marked "(proposed)" is either decided or removed. Delete references to drafts and cuts the drafter cannot see. Put the time-zone note nowhere (see 4.4).

**3.3 CHARACTERS reads as a stat sheet.**
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "**Bad at:** cooking, asking for help, accepting comfort, leaving things unfinished."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "**Careful about people, careless about everything else.** Consent matters to him with humans. The Box doesn't bother him."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "**Lonely, and doesn't admit it.** He thinks being alone is the price of staying alive."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "**Never write him as:** a scared kid, a socially awkward nerd, a rambling explainer, a silent nodder, a man described by what he doesn't do, or someone who is always right."
Wrong: bolded traits followed by their meaning; the drafter will perform each one. "Never write him as" lists six negatives that the outlines then violate in other ways (he is scared in Ch 5, silent in Ch 8's hand scene). "The Box doesn't bother him" contradicts "Careful about people": the Box imprisons a sentient creature.
Fix: rewrite as three or four things a person would notice: what he does when he's worried (checks things), what he does when he's happy (explains too much), what he does when he's wrong (decides again). Drop the bold labels. State the Box issue as his disagreement with Mira, not as a character defect.

**3.4 Over-designed romance beats.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "giving her his jacket without comment, then pretending he isn't cold"
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "He takes his jacket off and puts it around her without asking, and she lets him."
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "he stops saying goodnight from his doorway and says it from hers"
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "It's Valentine's Day. Neither of them mentions it until he says, too casually, "Happy whatever today is.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "He kisses her forehead before they cross, quickly, and they both pretend it was nothing."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "She leans on his shoulder. He lets her."
Wrong: the jacket is used twice; "pretending he isn't cold" and "pretend it was nothing" are the Hallmark version of understatement; "He lets her" and "she lets him" are null-space beats STYLE bans; Valentine's Day on Day 3 of her fever is cute for the author's benefit.
Fix: one jacket (Ch 4 only). Cut the Valentine's date or let it pass without remark. "Goodnight from her doorway" is cut. Forehead kiss: "He kisses her, short, on the forehead, and they cross." No pretending.

**3.5 Dialogue no one would say.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: ""Lives are always at stake. That's not a special condition that overrides ownership, Mira. That's just a more emotionally compelling version of the same argument.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch05.md]: "Ask me again and I'm leaving."
Quote [revision/outline/ch05.md]: ""Nothing guarantees anything. At least this way I'd die understanding why.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: ""You had me. I had a crayon.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "You split it. You did the thing with the three angles. I didn't teach you that."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: ""I've been leaving since I was seventeen. It's the thing I'm best at." It's the saddest thing he's said, and he says it lightly."
Wrong: "emotionally compelling version of the same argument" is a debate-club sentence; "the thing with the three angles" narrates the fight to the person who was in it; the last one tells the reader it is sad and how to feel.
Fix: Ch 14: "Everybody's got somebody who'll die without something. That doesn't make it theirs." Ch 13: "Where did you get that? The three sides. I never showed you that." Ch 13 last: drop the commentary after the quote.

**3.6 Twee community examples.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: ""There's a woman in Tucson who proofs bread with it. Thirty years. Never fought anything in her life.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "A man in Ontario who keeps bees."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "Oh, honey, no. Is he teaching you like that? Lord. Sit down, I brought tea."
Wrong: bread and bees are quirk-list people; the sample voice line is a ready-made Southern-grandmother catchphrase set (honey, Lord, tea) that the drafter will repeat in every Delilah line; "honey" is also in Ch 12 and Ch 15.
Fix: examples without charm: "a dental hygienist in Tucson, a retired surveyor in Ontario." Delilah's voice: "She says what she thinks and asks the next question before you've answered the first." Cap "honey" at once per chapter.

**3.7 Delilah's grandkids line is used twice.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: ""My daughter thinks I'm in a book club.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "Delilah's daughter "thinks I'm in a book club", which is a model of what Mira is becoming."
Wrong: same line, two chapters, and the second one carries the explanation ("a model of what Mira is becoming") in the outline text.

## 4. Story logic

**4.1 The central choice has no sound reason.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch05.md]: ""You said most people who learn this die. But you also said most of them figure it out alone. Kids stumbling into it. Loners teaching themselves. I wouldn't be alone. I'd have someone who already knows what he's doing.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch05.md]: ""Nothing guarantees anything. At least this way I'd die understanding why.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: ""You just happened to be there.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "he leaves, and she never sees him again"
Quote [revision/outline/ch05.md]: "the people from the sidewalk know Columbus."
Wrong: nothing makes her start. She is safe if she does not learn, and he will erase her or leave. Her stated argument assumes she will learn anyway, and "die understanding why" is only true if she starts, which is the thing in question. Ch 3 also says she "just happened to be there" and offers to leave her at home, while Ch 5 says the attackers know Columbus and saw her. Both cannot be true: the people who tried to kill Jason know her face, her city and that she is his friend.
Fix: Ch 3: Jason says the attackers saw her, and that is the first reason he cannot leave her alone. Option 1 becomes "leave her and hope they lose interest," which he does not believe. Ch 5: her argument becomes "If they know my face, then I'm in this whether I learn or not. I'd rather know." Keep the romance as the unspoken second reason. Ch 5 Scene 5: say plainly she can't return because the attackers know the apartment.

**4.2 The hunters' plan contradicts RULES, and Jason stays two days for curiosity.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "He thinks they're waiting for a third person, someone who blocks ordinary teleports. A random one can't be blocked, and he's curious how they'll even try."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "one person to block a normal teleport, one to track it if the block fails."
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "a targeted teleport can be blocked or tracked. A random one can't."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "Curiosity about the blocker beats his caution, and that's a real flaw."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "In a crisis he is fast, focused and practical. He acts before he talks."
Wrong: the hunters have seen the random teleport at least once and still bring a blocker and a tracker that RULES says cannot work; the whole scene hangs on that. The one thing the character sheet says is that Jason is practical in a crisis, and the outline has him sit two days next to the people who tried to kill him, with Mira in the house, out of curiosity. The two mid-tier creatures arriving at dusk on the same day is the convenient timing STYLE bans.
Fix: give the hunters a plan that could work (they have found a way to pin a teleport once it starts, or they are only watching, not attacking). Give Jason a real reason to stay: Mira cannot yet be moved because she is mid-training and a fresh move would expose Delilah, or he needs a day to warn Delilah. The creatures arrive because the cabin has been loud for months and the outline can say so without making it the same evening.

**4.3 The book never says how hunters or Delilah find the cabin, and the phone plot is a hole.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "This is the hunters' first whisper since the sidewalk. Don't confirm it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "A truck slowed on the road in February. Jason said "probably nothing"."
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: "She checks her phone. Mom has left two voicemails."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "Lives off-grid: cash, no paper trail."
Wrong: a man whose whole survival is untraceability lets his partner keep her own phone, number and voicemail at a hideout cabin for four months. The truck is either the hunters in February (then why wait until June?) or nothing (then it is a dead plant). The documents never explain how the hunters finally arrive.
Fix: Ch 6: Jason has her power the phone off and swap the SIM; she gets a prepaid number. Mom's voicemails and Nora's call then need Mira to give out the new number, which is a decision with a cost. Cut the truck. Ch 13: Jason or Mira says how: for example a practitioner Delilah gossiped to, which also gives Delilah a flaw (her gossip in CHARACTERS).

**4.4 The time-zone mystery cannot survive an adult reader.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "Her phone says 4:47 PM. They left the coffee shop at twenty to six, minutes before the attack. An hour has gone, backward."
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "She doesn't solve it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "she suddenly understands the clock in Tennessee: Central Time. She laughs out loud at breakfast, and has to explain to Jason why a four-month-old mystery just solved itself."
Quote [revision/TIMELINE.md]: "She can't explain it. It's the time zone, and she works that out later."
Wrong: the phone updates itself, the GPS has just told her she is in Tennessee, and she lives in the Eastern time zone next to Central. Any adult in Ch 2 gets it in ten seconds. Four months of not solving it, and Spain's clock being what finally solves it, is the author's plant being protected by the character's stupidity. Also, 5:40 plus "several blocks" plus 4:47 Central (5:47 Eastern) leaves about seven minutes for a walk the outline calls "several blocks" and a conversation under streetlights.
Fix: cut the plant. Ch 2: she sees 4:47, thinks "Central", and the time zone becomes the first hard proof of distance. Delete the Ch 14 payoff and the TIMELINE line. If the "hour" beat is wanted for tension, use the other fact: her phone says it is almost five and the attack happened at nearly six.

**4.5 Random teleports: a range that can be shaped, a Ch 3 "I can't aim it", and four land landings in a row.**
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "so no possible landing was open water"
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "he can't aim it, so it's a bus or a rental car tomorrow."
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "I didn't pick Tennessee specifically. That's what randomized means."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: ""Seventy percent of the planet is water. I'm a little surprised it only took three.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "Four random jumps: a sheep pasture in Wales, a beach in Chile where she gets sand everywhere, a field in Quebec in the rain."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "Over the border by road. Don't over-explain how. "There are parts of the border that are mostly trees.""
Wrong: (a) he can shape the range, so he can aim; Ch 3 says he cannot. (b) South of Columbus within 300 miles are the Ohio River, Kentucky Lake, Lake Cumberland and Dale Hollow, so "no possible landing was open water" is false. (c) Ch 13's own joke says 70% water; three consecutive land landings in Ch 15 out of four jumps (and the fourth is never named) contradicts it. (d) With a shapeable range, Spain to North America should take one jump, not four via Chile. (e) A car does not cross a forested border.
Fix: RULES: "so that landing in water was very unlikely." Ch 3: "he can't pick the spot." Ch 15: one jump, narrowed to the north-east coast; he lands them in a Quebec field because that is the land his range had. Cross on foot, with the car bought after. Delete Wales and Chile. Delete the quip in Ch 13, or keep it and let Ch 15 have one ocean jump too.

**4.6 Ch 15's yes is not motivated, and the logistics ignore RULES.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "**Her no:** clear, immediate, not dramatic. Beating something worn out on her own ground is not walking into its home."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "She sleeps on it."
Quote [revision/outline/ch15.md]: "she's tired of being the one who gets protected."
Quote [revision/RULES.md]: "Sleeping there isn't safe until a person can hold shielding without thinking."
Wrong: a clear no, Delilah's no, then no new fact and a night's sleep: the yes comes from the plot. Her reason (tired of being protected) is a mood, and Jason's reason (training ground) is weaker than the safe option. Also he is about to bring a four-and-a-half-month trainee into a place RULES says is lethal for sleepers.
Fix: give them a fact. After Spain, the hunters can find them anywhere on Earth by ordinary means; the other side is the only place nobody with a body can follow. Jason says so, and says it is a worse option than staying. Mira's yes then comes from weighing two bad places. Her "tired of being protected" becomes the second reason, said once.

**4.7 Ch 9 and Ch 3 small plausibilities.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "Jason keeps it busy, a little wall of something it bumps against, and makes it a lesson: "Feel how it's different from the brambles.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "Jason kills it, fast and almost casual, the way someone swats a wasp."
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "after the kill his hands are shaking too, a little."
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: "They're about 370 miles from Columbus."
Quote [revision/outline/ch02.md]: "Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee."
Wrong: a man who has killed four of these in a month and swats this one like a wasp does not stand and give a lesson while it threatens his partner, nor shake afterward. The 370 miles is a driving figure; a teleport covers about 300 straight-line miles.
Fix: Ch 9: he kills it in two lines and teaches afterward, over the body. Hands shaking: cut, or make it hers only. Ch 3: "about three hundred miles".

**4.8 Creature physics that fails a skeptical reader.**
Quote [revision/CREATURES.md]: "surfaces too matte to show their shape / or glassy in the wrong places / shadows that don't match their outline"
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "a shadow that doesn't match its outline"
Quote [revision/CREATURES.md]: "Long, eyeless, translucent body. You can see something like organs as soft gray shapes."
Quote [revision/outline/ch09.md]: "Frost spreads on the leaves where it lies, because it loses heat into the air so fast."
Wrong: a shadow is the shape of the thing that blocks the light, so it cannot "not match" except for translucence; translucent and matte-with-no-highlights cannot both hold. Something that loses heat to the air fast ends up at air temperature, so it cannot freeze leaves on a Tennessee April night.
Fix: keep "no highlight where a living thing should have one" and "her eye keeps fixing the picture"; cut the shadow line; choose translucent or matte. Frost: drop it or set the stray fight in late March.

**4.9 Mira's inference in Ch 13 is a non sequitur.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: "Changing a technique like that while being attacked, with no time, is hard. Her mature clones taught her how hard."
Wrong: mature clones are about clone-building precision, not about reshaping a teleport mid-attack. The "she works it out herself" beat then needs an inference the story has not set up.
Fix: Jason says the cost himself in one flat sentence ("It took everything I had that night to make it do that."). Drop the clone reference.

**4.10 The ownership argument has no teeth.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "Years ago, before Columbus, he got known for a few new techniques. Owen and others came to him, polite."
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "He comes back with the practical problem: you can't unteach anything. Once Owen has it, Owen's friends have it, and so do their friends."
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "Right now he knows exactly who has it."
Wrong: Owen's use is to teleport newcomers away. Jason could do that himself on Owen's call, or teach Owen alone; the "can't unteach" point applies to everything he teaches and he taught Mira. The outline wants the reader unsure whether Jason is wrong, but gives him only the weakest version.
Fix: give Jason a concrete harm: a technique that moves a person anywhere is also a way to take one out of a locked room, and the people Owen travels with include some Jason would not trust with it. Give Owen's side the counter: there are not enough Jasons to be on call.

**4.11 Spain.**
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "Jason has euros: "an account I set up years ago, for if.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: "The rental is cash-friendly and a little damp, above a bakery."
Quote [revision/CHARACTERS.md]: "cash, no paper trail"
Wrong: an "account" contradicts the cash-only description and needs a card or ID he does not have after leaving the cabin at dawn with nothing. Short-term rentals in Spain are required to register guests' ID.
Fix: "Jason had cash in the bag he always keeps ready; the room is rented off the books by the baker's cousin." Cut "account".

## 6. Rules that fight each other

**6.1 Length targets against "never pad".**
Quote [revision/outline/README.md]: "Write only its own chapter, in Mira's first person, past tense, at about the word count given."
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "A shorter chapter is better than a padded one. Never add jokes, quirks, side beats or extra exchanges to hit a word count."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: "Shorter is fine; never pad."
Quote [revision/outline/ch04.md]: "Keep it moving; this is a transition chapter."
Wrong: "Shorter is fine" appears on Ch 1 to 3 only; Ch 4 to 15 give only a target. The Round 2 drafts came in under target (1,597 against 2,500 to 3,000) and the critic called them thin.
Fix: pick one. Suggested: "Hit the beats. If you are 15% short, stop. If you are 20% over, cut." Put it once in README and delete the per-chapter variants.

**6.2 Sentence-length rules against the verbatim and outline dialogue.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Never more than two sentences in a row under six words. At most three fragments per chapter."
Quote [revision/outline/ch01.md]: ""Do you ever get lonely?" "Sometimes." "You're different." "Different how?""
Quote [revision/outline/ch14.md]: ""Because I made it. It's mine." / "That's it?" / "That's it.""
Wrong: both quotes are four consecutive sentences of under six words, and Ch 1 had 50% under six after the drafter followed the outline.
Fix: STYLE: "Verbatim exchanges are exempt from the short-sentence count." Or lengthen the surrounding narration by instruction ("Put a full sentence of action between short lines of dialogue").

**6.3 Banter ban against scripted banter.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Quips in serious scenes. In danger, confession, grief or a revelation, no jokes"
Quote [revision/outline/ch06.md]: "Tell me what I need to know to stay alive in the next three months and I'll have an existential crisis about the rest of it later."
Quote [revision/outline/ch13.md]: ""Seventy percent of the planet is water. I'm a little surprised it only took three.""
Wrong: the first is a joke in the middle of learning she is prey; the second is Jason's joke straight after he put her in the ocean twice. STYLE would reject both.
Fix: Ch 6 line: keep but cut the "existential crisis" half, which is a quip. Ch 13: have her make the remark after the second jump, not him.

**6.4 "No habit or prop more than twice" against props the outlines require.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "No habits invented to make a character seem real."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: ""For the record, I still think this is wrong." Every time."
Quote [revision/outline/ch08.md]: "She counts the days on the calendar by the door"
Wrong: the notepad list (Ch 5), the calendar (Ch 8), the mug (Ch 7), the jacket twice, the timer (Ch 15), the refrain (Ch 12) are all outline-demanded.
Fix: pick the five props that pay off and cut the rest. Remove the sentence from STYLE or say "Outline props are exempt, and nothing else is."

**6.5 "Listened to it twice" is banned in STYLE and requested in Ch 11.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "Don't repeat information ("listened to it twice", "folded it flat, twice")"
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: "A voicemail from Mom she listens to twice"
Fix: "A voicemail from Mom: Dad's back is better, the neighbors got a dog."

**6.6 "Verbatim overrides everything" against the limits.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "**Lines marked verbatim override everything in "Never do these".**"
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: "**A chapter's own Limits override the default counts below.**"
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: ""Two years": at most 3."
Quote [revision/outline/ch03.md]: ""You love me so you've been lying to me for two years?""
Wrong: it is unstated whether the verbatim line counts toward the "Two years" limit; the Round 2 critic found "Two years of Tuesdays" five times in three chapters anyway. The "Never do these" list includes reversals ("Not X. Y.") that the verbatim lines use freely.
Fix: add: "Verbatim lines count toward limits." And cap "two years" book-wide, not per chapter.

**6.7 Priming by quoting the bad examples.**
Quote [revision/STYLE.md]: ""He doesn't answer, which is answer enough." "She doesn't respond." "He notes this without comment.""
Quote [revision/outline/ch07.md]: ""He doesn't answer, which is answer enough": 0. He answers."
Quote [revision/outline/ch11.md]: ""Delilah gives her a look": 0."
Wrong: every banned line is reproduced in the drafter's context, which is where Haiku gets them. The outlines' "Limits" read like a list of the previous draft's faults ("Jason 'I don't know': 0", "Props ... (sticks, phones, notes): 0") for a drafter who has not seen that draft.
Fix: describe the pattern in a sentence without the example. Remove every "X: 0" limit that quotes the phrase.

**6.8 "Do not foreshadow" against "Plant for later".**
Quote [revision/outline/BOOK_MAP.md]: "**Do not foreshadow anything beyond your chapter** except the setups your outline lists."
Quote [revision/outline/ch10.md]: "The real answer comes later in the book."
Quote [revision/outline/ch12.md]: "She remembers it when she erases Marcus in Ch 23."
Wrong: the drafter is told both to say nothing about the future and to include a list of future setups, some of them with the future spelled out.
Fix: delete "later in the book" and "Ch 23" references from outlines. Plants are phrased as present facts only.
