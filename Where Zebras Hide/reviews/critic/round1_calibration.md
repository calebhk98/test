The Sonnet critic reviewed round-1 Ch 1 to 3 blind (a copy of CRITIC.md with the round-1 examples removed). Its report is `round1_critic.md`. It was scored against 60 issues the author and the author's reviewer had found, kept outside the repo so the critic couldn't see them.

| | count |
| --- | --- |
| Found | 53 |
| Partly found | 5 |
| Missed | 1 |
| Not scored (author disagreed with it) | 1 |

- **Partly found:**
  - Jason citing Sarah's recommendation (it flagged the line as banter, but not that Sarah is Mira's friend)
  - every Jason action doing a narrative job
  - Marcus's "in town" ambiguity
  - Jason as the withholding genius
  - explaining emotions the dialogue already showed
- **Missed:** "which one is worse" (an unexplained line)
- **Not scored:** "suspiciously optimized love interest"

It also found about a dozen real problems that neither list had. All were spot-checked against the drafts and are real:
- shoes versus boots
- Henderson "three pages left" versus "not started"
- the scone hater reversed
- "No path" versus "the path behind us"
- the laptop that vanishes
- "My sister" versus "Not even Nora?" read as two people
- Oregon outside the shaped range
- the truck arriving on cue
- a dialogue splice with unclear speakers

**Caveat:** the Ch 1 to 3 outlines were rewritten from the author's critique, so many of the fact findings came from comparing the draft with those outlines' "Do not" lists. On later chapters, whose outlines weren't rewritten that way, expect fewer fact catches. The craft categories (jokes, props, personification, telling, rhythm) were found from the brief, not the outline.

**Change made:** two lines were added to CRITIC.md category 7, for unexplained lines and for characters knowing things they can't know.
