## 1. Contradictions

**1.1 RULES.md, the zebra image against its own population line.**
Quote: `Mira's image is "seven billion zebras hiding in tall grass, and they can't see us because we don't smell like food."` and, higher up, `Earth's population is **eight billion**.`
Problem: One document says eight billion and then puts seven billion in Mira's mouth. Ch23 bans "Seven billion" and the Ch30 fix list changes it, so the Ch6 drafter will write the wrong number.
Fix: `Mira's image is "eight billion zebras hiding in tall grass, and they can't see us because we don't smell like food."` Then drop the Ch23 limit.

**1.2 RULES.md and ch27/ch28: Jason "can't aim", yet Jason "teleports teaching groups".**
Quotes: ch27 `She can't get there; Jason can't aim a teleport.` ch28 `He teleports teaching groups out of danger all day, at random, and it's cheap for him, so he does it until his hands shake.` RULES: `he shaped the range on the fly: south, away from the Great Lakes and the coast`.
Problem: Three claims can't all hold. He can shape the range (RULES), he can't aim (ch27), and he moves groups of untrained producers "at random" all day (ch28). If he can shape a range he could shape one over the Indian subcontinent. "Cheap" and "hands shake" also contradict each other.
Fix: Decide that range shaping works only inside a few hundred miles of him, written into RULES. Ch27: `Jason could shape a jump to land somewhere in the region, not on a street. Mira asked him not to.` Ch28: `He moved groups short distances, all day, to places he'd shaped for them`. Cut "until his hands shake" and use something he does instead.

**1.3 RULES.md, Jason cannot clone, yet the Box has "a clone of Jason".**
Quotes: `A clone of Jason keeps fighting it on a loop`. Ch18: `Jason puts a clone of himself in his place inside it`. RULES: `Jason understands the technique completely and can't do it.`
Problem: The doc tells the drafter in one place that Jason cannot make clones and in another that he does so every time he builds a Box.
Fix: Rename it. `A construct of Jason, a crude puppet he built for exactly this, keeps fighting it on a loop. It can't think, which is why it is not a clone of anyone.` Ch18: `He left a puppet of himself in his place`.

**1.4 ch19 end, a forgotten clone, against "one mind".**
Quote: `a clone she forgot to pull back is making coffee, badly, in the kitchen. She and it look at each other. She laughs.`
Problem: RULES makes Stage 2+ clones one mind with merged senses, and Ch19 is Stage 4. She can't forget one, and two bodies can't "look at each other" as separate parties. It also breaks the "no coffee imagery" habit.
Fix: Cut. Replacement: `She wakes in the kitchen, in a body that had been standing at the counter for an hour. The kettle was on. She had no memory of putting it on, and it did not feel strange.` That shows the no-main-body idea with no fake separateness.

**1.5 RULES.md/ch20/ch30, reabsorbing into "one skull" against "no main body".**
Quotes: RULES: `none of them is the original or the main one`. 05_Chapter_30: `collapsing back into one skull that hadn't been built to hold it`. Ch20: `She wakes up in the grass hours later`; Rules in play: `She notices she woke up in a different body from the one she fell asleep in`.
Problem: Reabsorbing needs a destination. The docs never say what survives when 8,000,000 bodies come down to one, which body that is, or why "the last five" matter.
Fix: Add to RULES: `When she pulls bodies back they fold into whichever body she is closest to, usually the one with the most room. Which body that is changes every time and does not matter to her.` Ch30: replace "one skull" with "one body".

**1.6 RULES.md, "engine for Act IV".**
Quote: `The scout never returning is what makes its masters start to care. That is the engine for Act IV.`
Problem: No Act IV outline from ch22 to ch29 has a master, a scout or a servant. Act IV is driven by human leaks, and the scout's master appears only in ch30. A drafter reading RULES will plant masters where they don't belong.
Fix: `The scout never returning is why one of its masters eventually crosses to look (Ch30). The engine for the rest of Act IV is human: leaks, then evidence, then more producers.`

**1.7 TIMELINE.md against ch22, bottle or mug.**

**1.9 ch17 and ch18 on the battery compromise.**

**1.10 ch16, she doesn't know there is no light, yet a rule about screens.**

## 2. Instructions a drafter will follow too literally

**2.1 Ch26 and ch28 repeat four set pieces.**
Quotes: Ch26: `a clinic waiting room where a nurse is cycling badly between patients`, `a church parking lot where a pastor is preaching that it's demonic`, `a governor's press conference on a TV in a diner`, `a high school gym in Hartford`. Ch28: `a hospital in Boston where nurses cycle between patients`, `a church opened as a shelter, a pastor who preached last week that it was demonic`, `a TV in a diner where a clone of hers is eating`, `a National Guard unit in Hartford`.
Problem: Two chapters, one Haiku each, the same four scenes. The reader meets the pastor twice.
Fix: Ch26 keeps the clinic and the parking lot in Ohio, and the pastor goes; ch28 keeps the National Guard and the cardboard-sitting circle in New Jersey. Diner TV once only, in ch28, and Ch26 uses a radio in a car.

**2.2 Bulleted lists will be recited in order.**
Ch28 scene 1: `About forty of her teach ... About twenty of her fight ... About thirty coordinate ... About twenty are rotating`. Ch28 scene 2: four places in a row. Ch26 scene 4: four places in a row. Ch29 scene 1: three damage examples. Ch17 scene 5's seven-step cascade. Ch16 scene 3's three-things-she-sees.
Problem: A drafter writes one paragraph per bullet. The result is a tour.
Fix: Give each list a number to hit and let the drafter choose. Ch28: `Show two of the four jobs through one clone each. The split is 110 in total and is in RULES; don't recite it.` Ch29: `Pick one of the three damage examples and make it the scene.` Ch17 scene 5: `Write the cascade in her body: the hit lands, she is thrown, she fires. The shield count (two shields facing it, a cushion behind) stays; the step list doesn't.`

**2.3 Verbatim lines that need a setup the drafter can't see.**
- ch17: `"You told me this in the car. That they're out there. I didn't understand it until now."` The drafter hasn't read Ch6's car scene and is also told `Repeating Chapter 6's "not worth the trip" exchange word for word: 0.` They can't tell what is forbidden.

Ch17: `In Ch6 Jason told her, in the car, that things on the other side know about Earth and mostly don't care.` Ch26: `In Spain (Ch14) she told him that he had "cured cancer in his garage" and refused to hand over the cure.` Ch24: `Jason said it to her in the motel in Ch4 or Ch5; she half-remembers it.` Ch26: `Jason sends Owen a message that says only that he is going to teach everything. ## 4. Story logic

**4.1 Ch27 and CREATURES, the death toll and its mechanism.**
Quotes: `About a hundred million dead.` / CREATURES: `Its mana pulls in and consumes every producing person for kilometers around it`; `Ordinary people die in its wake: buildings it walks through, roads jammed with people fleeing, fires, crushes, and the collapse of everything the dead were running.`
Problem: The mechanism kills producers, and by day 8 there are few of them. The other deaths (crushes, fires, collapse of services) cannot reach a hundred million in three days. A skeptical adult adds it up and rejects it, and the whole back half hangs on this number.
Fix: Drop the hundred million, or move it to "within a month". For this chapter: `By day 10 nobody had a number. The first estimate was in the millions; the second was ten times that and nobody believed either.` Rework in CREATURES: `The dead are mostly people caught in the panic and in the failure of the places it walks through; the ones it takes directly are the producers.`

**4.2 Ch27, "densest places on Earth".**
Quotes: `It walks slowly toward Delhi, through some of the densest places on Earth.` / Do not: `Use "rural India". The region is dense and connected: Rajasthan toward Delhi.`
Problem: Most of Rajasthan is among the least dense parts of India. The correction in Do not is wrong, and the line is easy to catch.
Fix: `It walks toward Delhi along the road between Jaipur and Delhi, past towns that run into each other.` Cut the Do not.

**4.3 Ch27, nothing happens to Delhi, the military or the government.**
Gap: The outline never says whether it reaches Delhi, and never mentions India's army, air force or nuclear weapons. `Practitioners threw everything at it and barely marked it.` Ch28 treats the day as ordinary.
Fix: Add one line to ch27 scene 4: `The Indian Air Force hit it for a day and a half. Then someone decided to stop, because each strike drew more people toward it.` Decide where it stops and say so.

**4.4 Scale: the death toll against the book's tone.**
Quotes: ch29: `eleven people died who might not have`; she `hates herself for checking that first`. Ch29: `She's made peace with not saving most people`. Ch28 opens with `The world fights back, badly and bravely` and a Walmart lot.
Problem: Eleven deaths get a beat of self-hatred and a hundred million get three sentences. The world response (National Guard, Boston nurses, a New Jersey Walmart) reads as a different crisis. Either the dead are too many or the chapters too calm.
Fix: Reduce the number (4.1) or add two details to ch28 that show the world has changed: no flights, no power in a named city, hospitals turning people away. Ch29: let the eleven be part of a bigger weight, not the only one.

**4.5 Ch30, 40,000 and 8,000,000 have no ladder.**
Quotes: RULES: `Ch 20: a one-time burst of about a thousand ... then a reabsorption that knocks her out for hours` and `Act IV: about 110 sustained`. Ch30: `She starts with two hundred.` ... `a colony of eight million`.
Problem: Her ceiling in August was a thousand and cost her consciousness; ninety days of practice at 110 hasn't been shown to scale it to 8,000,000 (8,000 times higher). Ch29 says that pulling back ninety was `the worst reabsorption since the field`. Ch30 then asks her to pull back eight million and survive.
Fix: Add a ladder to RULES: `Ch26: a burst of about 3,000 in a gym. Ch28: 20,000 in one night against wolves, pulled back over a day.` And lower the final number: `two million`. Eight million is a gesture, not a number; if the author wants it, give a reason (the creature's attack feeds her; the colony grows because she can't stop it).

**4.6 Ch30, Stage 3 clones start empty.**
Quotes: RULES: `They take nothing from her reserve, start nearly empty, produce at full adult strength from the first second, and fill up within minutes.` Ch30: `the attack broke against layered shields like a wave against a breakwater` and the creature `kills the two hundred almost casually` in seconds.
Problem: Newly made clones are empty for the first minutes, and the creature kills each one in seconds. Layered shields from 8,000,000 new bodies need a minute to fill. The battle timing doesn't fit the rule the book wrote.
Fix: The first 200 are the ones who buy time: `Two hundred was how many I could fill in the seconds I had. They were bait.` Add one clause to ch30: `The second round took four minutes to fill, and the creature didn't use them.` Or change RULES Stage 4 so descendants start with their parent's charge.

**4.7 Ch30, the colony is the meal.**
Quotes: scene 4 `is a colony of eight million the thing that finally tells everything over there that Earth isn't worth it, or the loudest signal this planet has ever sent? She doesn't know.` Scene 5: `Eight million bodies, each producing mana, are the loudest thing on Earth.` RULES: `A massive being has to find a meal the size of a city before crossing is worth what it loses.`
Problem: By the book's own rules, eight million adult-strength producers in one place is the biggest meal ever offered. The outline treats it as a one-line "beacon thought" and then says in the next scene that it is the loudest thing on Earth, as if it knows. The two scenes disagree about what she knows.
Fix: Decide. Scene 4: cut the thought. Scene 5: `Every second the colony stood in that field, it was the biggest meal on Earth. I knew that, and I didn't want to be a thing that things crossed for.` That is the reason she has to hurry, and it is a sequel hook.

**4.8 Ch30, Theo's group half a mile from the base.**
Quotes: `Theo's teaching group, driven up from Hartford this morning by Owen's people, is half a mile away.` and `Theo's group is half a mile away, and fifty untrained producers there are a feast.`
Problem: Owen and Delilah would not put fifty untrained producers next to the two loudest people in the country, when the book has said creatures cross for exactly that. The group is placed there to raise the stakes.
Fix: `Owen's people had put the Hartford groups in a church camp six miles down the road. The sweep of Jason's lockdown reached about half of it.` Or drop the group from ch30 and let Mira's reason be the lockdown failing.

**4.9 Ch30, Jason shouting and running while the lockdown holds.**
Quotes: Scene 2: `he spends several minutes locking the area down ... and quietly starts building a Box around Mira`. Scene 3: `Jason is shouting and running toward her.` Old draft: `I heard Jason shouting.`
Problem: Jason is doing the draining part of the fight, which in ch18 left him unconscious. He can't also be running. What he shouts, and why she ignores it, is not given.
Fix: Scene 3: `Jason stayed where he was, because the lockdown needed him to. He called my name once.` Scene 6: he reaches her at the end, which is the "running" beat, and what he shouts is `Stop` or her name.

**4.10 Ch30, the final Box.**
Quotes: `she feels the Box he started during the fight close around her ... She can't tell what it means, and neither can the reader.` and Do not `Explain the box at the end.`
Problem: RULES defines the Box as a prison for creatures. Boxing Mira "in case" is unmotivated; the outline asks for it as a hook and bans any explanation. A reader will take it as Jason imprisoning her.
Fix: Cut it, or give a reason in scene 2 that she can half-hear: `He said he was building a place to put me if it came to that.` Then the close is a protection, not an attack.

**4.11 Ch28 against ch30, Kael's question has no payoff.**
Quotes: ch28: `"Each of them is so aware. Conscious. Making ether. Are they people?"` ch30: `It died in four seconds. I barely registered the loss`; 40,000 more die.
Problem: The book asks the question and then spends 40,000 of her without a reaction. The answer is "they're me", but she feels each die (ch18: `she feels them die`).
Fix: In ch30, one sentence from the five who can see Jason: `It had killed two hundred of me and I remembered it as the first four seconds.` Or add the cost to ch29's limit: `she can't count how many she's lost today.`

**4.12 Ch21, the coffee-shop slip and her refusal to erase.**
Quotes: `Without thinking, holding it, she warms it, and it steams back up in her hands.` / `She could erase the last minute from him, and she won't, not to Marcus, not over a coffee cup.`
Problem: Seven months in, with shields clamped for the weekend and every creature on camera, she warms a cup in front of a coworker in a public coffee shop. Erasure is the community's `normal cleanup tool`, and a minute is trivial. Ch23 then shows her erasing him anyway, "because it should be someone who loves him". The refusal is for the plot.
Fix: Let the slip be genuinely unconscious (she is tired and sitting with Jason). Let her decide to erase and then see Marcus laugh it off before she can decide whether to. `She could have. By the time she thought of it he had already said "Neat trick" and moved on to the weather. She told herself she could still do it. She did not.`

**4.13 Ch21, returning to the café where they were attacked.**
Quote: `Monday, at the old coffee shop, she runs into Marcus.`
Problem: The shop is where the hunters attacked in Ch1, and Where things stand says the hunters still know Columbus. The dramatic reason for going is that Marcus is there, and the writer then claims "the danger in this chapter is her".
Fix: Move the encounter to the street outside her old apartment, or to the grocery store: `They were buying milk when Marcus came around the end of the aisle.` (It also pays off the milk joke.)

**4.14 Ch21, the extra clone outside her parents' house.**
Quote: `She puts a clone outside on the back step, shielded tight, to keep watch`.
Problem: She says her mana near family is a risk and then adds a second producing body at their door. RULES: `Many clones together are loud.`
Fix: `She sat up on the couch with her shields tight and her eyes on the yard.` Cut the clone. Or keep it and have the point be that she knows it's a risk and does it anyway.

**4.15 Ch21, the realisation is not new.**
Quotes: Where things stand: `She knows the rule: telling her family would put them at risk, and so would her own mana near them.` End: `Driving out of Columbus, she realizes she can't come back.`
Problem: She knew this in February and it was Jason's rule. The ending is billed as the turn from devotion to duty (CHARACTERS: `The outline marks the turn ... around the Ch 21 homecoming`) but nothing in the weekend changes her reason, and the weekend went fine.
Fix: Give the turn a cause. She hears a neighbour's kid in the house say "Mom, that lady's hot" and realises she was leaking through the shield. Or make the cause Marcus: for the first time she is the one who put someone else at risk.

**4.16 Ch22 vs the book: one bottle video summons the strong ones.**
Quote: `A video from São Paulo has been going around for three weeks.` ... `There's going to be a meeting of the strong ones`.
Problem: The community has survived four hundred years of videos. A man who stops a bottle for half a second is a weak trigger for a first-ever meeting. Also a man who has just started cannot do that (RULES: `Onset is small`).
Fix: Add Manchester, Priya (not yet), and one other thing to the reason: `Three videos in a month, one of them from a hospital in Leeds.` Or accept the story: the meeting is not about the video but about Jason's invitation. Change the São Paulo video to something a new producer could do: a phone flashlight that wouldn't switch off, a cup of tea that stayed hot.

**4.17 Ch22, Mira tells Owen how Jason shaped the Ch1 teleport.**
Quote: `he shaped a teleport so she wouldn't drown, and didn't mention it`.
Problem: Owen's whole argument is that Jason's teleport can save newcomers. Telling him it can be shaped is giving him the argument and breaking Jason's confidence. A loyal Mira would not.
Fix: `he remembers things. He remembered my coffee order for two years. He's lonely, and funny, and wrong about a lot of things, and he's trying.` Cut the teleport detail.

**4.18 Ch22, "rich, quietly" and "invited".**
Quotes: `Being good at mana can make you rich, quietly.` Rules: `There is no government, no membership and no list.` Ch22: `for the first time anyone remembers, Jason is invited`.
Problem: The meeting presumes a list and a host. "Rich" is asserted and then banned in the limits.
Fix: `The host owned the house; he'd lent it for the weekend.` Cut the money aside.

**4.19 Ch22, the "burn the world down" warning is never paid.**
Quotes: `He'll burn the world down to keep you safe, and he won't ask you first.` Jason section: `His temper: shows once, at Owen.`
Problem: In Act IV Jason does the opposite. He gives everything away, and ch29 has him ask her first. The warning is written for a story that doesn't happen.
Fix: Change Owen's line to something he could be right about: `"He'll protect you, and he won't tell you what it costs him until it's too late."` (It matches Ch17's battery and Ch29's toll.) Cut the Jason "too fast" beat.

**4.20 Ch19, Jason is complacent in a way his own character forbids.**
Quotes: `Jason is complacent: people have laughed at magic for centuries`; Jason section `Wrong: about the walls. He'll find out.` CHARACTERS: `He reads people well.` RULES: `Partial understanding can finish itself. This is why Jason won't explain mechanics to someone who hasn't started.`
Problem: The man who refuses to describe mechanics for fear of leaks wouldn't wave off Kael on leaks. Ch22 and ch23 repeat the complacency. He is wrong four times to give Kael and Mira a reason to say "told you so".
Fix: Jason agrees with Kael and doesn't know what to do. `Jason said, "I know. I've known for a while. What do you want me to do, take down the internet?"`

**4.21 Ch23, a week passes before anyone checks on Marcus.**
Quotes: scene 1 `Marcus's long text`, scene 2 `Within a week, three named viewers start producing`, scene 3 `They drive overnight.`
Problem: Marcus is on the edge of producing, and Mira (who knows what that costs) drives to Columbus after more than a week.
Fix: Make it the same day: the text arrives that morning, and the London segment is two days after Columbus. Or reorder: Columbus (scenes 1 and 3) first, then London, ending on the car ride.

**4.22 Ch23, Marcus isn't the leak.**
Quote: `Marcus has been digging because of her ... She did this.`
Problem: Marcus's forum posts are not what the anchor read, what Cass found or what the world learned. Mira's guilt is for a different leak from the one that mattered, and the erasure feels like a tangent.
Fix: Let Mira say it: `Marcus wasn't the leak. He was the one I could see.` Or tie him to Cass: he posted in the thread Cass later reads.

**4.23 Ch24, how Delilah finds the sixteen, and what happens to the video.**
Quote: `Sixteen people on campus watched the follow-ups closely enough to be at risk.`
Problem: Nobody can know that by viewing figures. Erasing sixteen viewers does nothing about the video still online. The outline never says it was taken down. Ch23 says forum posts stay up.
Fix: Delilah: `They watched it on the library's computers; someone pulled the logs.` Add: `Owen's people took both videos down that morning.` Or make the number "all we could find" and say so.

**4.24 Ch25, "A few thousand figure it out" against hospitals and millions.**
Quotes: `A million people are shown, and a few thousand figure it out together.` / Day 1: `Hospitals report warmth in the chest`. ch26: `so many people are producing that survival skills save more than they endanger`. ch27: `millions have been learning`.
Problem: The docs never say how many are producing on any day. The jump from "a few thousand" to "millions" in three days, to a creature in India by day 8, is invented in the draft.
Fix: Add a table to TIMELINE: Day 0, tens of thousands; Day 3, about two million (RULES: `Take a class: nearly everyone`); Day 8, about forty million. Ch25: `a few thousand figure it out together, and every one of them was already telling someone else.`

**4.25 Ch25, "Mira's erasures bought a few weeks".**
Quote: `Mira's erasures in October bought a few weeks. They didn't change the outcome.`
Problem: The sixteen students and Marcus did not slow the São Paulo video, the London segment or the Cass streams. Credit is wrong.
Fix: `Nothing we did in October bought us anything.` Or let Jason say it.

**4.26 Ch26, what Jason "gives away" is mostly ordinary.**
Quotes: `He's going to record everything: cycling, shielding, sensing, and then the teleport, the Box and the lockdown.` / `what took him years, explained in minutes`. RULES: cycling, shielding, sensing are `Ordinary techniques`; `Most practitioners can't teleport at all`; the Box can be built only by Jason.
Problem: The sacrifice is that he gives away what he owns. But the first three are things Delilah teaches, and the last three can't be learned by millions. The ownership plot is hollow unless the docs say what's his.
Fix: Add to RULES: `What Jason owns is his way of teaching: the sequence he found, which gets most people to cycle in days, not weeks. The Box, the teleport and the lockdown are his by being impossible for anyone else.` Ch26: `He recorded the sequence. He recorded the Box too, and no one who watched it would be able to make one.`

**4.27 Ch27, "Jason can't aim", the evacuation rule and the mana message.**
Quotes: `With her teaching clones' experience, she builds an evacuation rule: get producing people away from its path first, upwind of its range; get the non-producing behind them.` / RULES: `a technique for sending a short message to someone you know without knowing where they are.`
Problem: Mana isn't wind; "behind them" puts non-producers nearer the creature; and she messages people she doesn't know through contacts of contacts, which the rule doesn't allow.
Fix: `get the people who can feel it out of its path; they pull it toward whatever they're running to. Everyone else moves with them or away.` And `she reached the people Anjali and Owen knew, one at a time, each passing the next name along.`

**4.28 Ch17, the attack from nowhere, and Kael appears at the right moment.**
Quotes: `Something attacks her from nowhere.` / `Someone laughs nearby ... Kael`.
Problem: The attacker is never explained, and Kael is near enough to see exactly what she did. A coincidence that STYLE bans (`Convenient timing`).
Fix: `Kael had been following them for two crossings. He said so. The thing that hit her was one of his, and he'd called it off at the last second.` That also makes Kael's invitation real.

**4.29 Ch18, the message to Delilah.**
Quote: `The lockdown Jason put up is still standing around them, and she can't take it down. Even if it had gone, Delilah is fourteen hours away in Tennessee.`
Problem: A mana message works at any distance, so the second reason is irrelevant. Two excuses for one failure reads like padding.
Fix: Keep the lockdown. Cut the fourteen hours.

**4.30 Ch18/ch30, boxing in minutes.**
Quotes: ch18 `Then he starts building a Box around the whole area from the outside in, which takes minutes`. RULES: `Building one takes a long time and a lot of power.`
Fix: RULES: `A new Box takes a long time. In an emergency he can close an existing Box's walls around a new place in minutes, at great cost.` Ch18/30 use that.

**4.31 Ch29, Theo gets cycling in two hours.**
Quotes: `gets cycling in two hours. It's clean and stable, better than hers was after a week.` RULES: `Most take one to two weeks. Jason took two days.`
Problem: Faster than the book's prodigy. Mira's own first partial cycle came on the second morning (TIMELINE ch7). The "natural" label is a plot device and doesn't return.
Fix: `He had it by the end of the session. It took Mira a day and a half.` Or let it take a day.

**4.32 Ch28, Anjali was placed to die.**
Quote: ch22 `Anjali (proposed name), a quiet practitioner from Jaipur who makes jewelry`; plant `Anjali, who returns by message in Ch 27.`
Problem: A named friendly character from the one place the catastrophe lands. Her earrings ("for when this is over") are a pathos plant in ch26.
Fix: Keep her in Jaipur, but let her survive for now, and make her loss come from the work (she is a teacher who was in the wrong place). Cut the earrings plant from ch26.

## 5. Over-specification and gaps

**5.1 Over-specified clone totals.**
Quotes: ch28 `Clone counts: use exactly 110, split 40 teaching, 20 fighting, 30 coordinating and 20 rotating or in reserve. No other totals.` ch29: `110 total, about 90 pulled back. No other totals.` TIMELINE repeats the split.
Problem: A spreadsheet in prose. Drafters recite it and are barred from "a hundred or so". Where ch30 starts from, the 110 out in the world, isn't addressed.
Fix: `About 110, some teaching, some fighting, some coordinating. Don't give a breakdown.` Ch30 where things stand: `Mira has about a hundred of herself out in the world. They are not in the field.`

**5.2 Ch30 creature dialogue and ending left to the drafter.**
Quotes: `It speaks, its voice from beside it, politely. To Jason first: where is the one I sent? Then, curious, to Mira.` and `Then it's gone.` with Do not `Confirm whether she lives.`
Problem: The creature's speech, Jason's reply and what "gone" means (killed or fled) are invented by Haiku. If it died, it will come back (RULES), and if it fled, it knows where she is. The ch18 scout speech is also required and will be copied.
Fix: Write four lines of dialogue. Jason: `"It isn't here."` Creature: `"Then where is it?"` And state: `The creature was dead, as far as I could tell. It would not stay dead.`

**5.3 Chain units in ch19 and ch20.**
Quotes: ch19 `She gets a chain three deep before it collapses` / `five deep`. Ch20: `chains of eight, then twenty, then sixty`.
Problem: "Deep" is generations in ch19 and apparently count in ch20. A sixty-deep chain would be sixty generations; RULES says `each generation adds time`.

**5.4 Clone range.**
Gap: RULES never says how far clones can be from her and still be her. Ch26 sends clones across the East, ch27 has thirty on messages for India, ch30 has `as far as her sense reaches`. The late book depends on this.

**5.5 Ch19, why she wants more of herself.**
Quote: `Mira wants more of herself.`
Problem: The chapter doesn't say why, and ch18 says `Her decision to get more.` The reason (she couldn't protect Jason alone) is available and unused.
Fix: `Mira wants never to be alone with his body in the dark again.`

**5.6 Ch25 verbatim dialogue lacks speakers.**
Quote: `"Can we stop it?" / "The spread? No. It's too late." / "Then what do we do?" / (Mira) "Teach." / (Jason) "As many people as we can, as fast as we can."`

**5.7 Thin scenes with large word budgets.**

Problem: STYLE says `Never add jokes, quirks, side beats or extra exchanges to hit a word count.`

**5.8 BOOK_MAP gives every drafter the ending.**
Quote: `Mira ends the book as a colony of eight million bodies, saving what she can and unable to hear the man shouting her name.` and `Do not foreshadow anything beyond your chapter`.
Problem: The ch1 drafter reads the last image and is invited to foreshadow it.

**5.9 Ch29, Theo's return.**
Quotes: CHARACTERS `He comes back once more, late in Ch 29 or in Ch 30.` Ch30: Theo appears only as a location.
Fix: Either give him one line in ch30 (`Theo was one of the fifty. I didn't look for him.`) or cut the CHARACTERS promise.

## 6. Rules that fight each other

**6.1 STYLE on fragments, against ch30's countdown.**
Quotes: STYLE `At most three fragments per chapter. ... Never more than two sentences in a row under six words.` Ch30 scene 5: `Four million, then three million, then two million. ... One hundred thousand. Ten thousand.` and the old draft's `One million. Five hundred thousand.`
Fix: STYLE: `The Ch30 countdown is exempt.` Or mark the countdown verbatim in the outline.

**6.2 STYLE on portents, against ch23 and ch27.**
Quotes: STYLE `Jason doesn't deliver portents ("This is the opening act." "The day they start caring is the day everything ends").` Ch27 verbatim: `No. This is the opening act.` Ch23 verbatim: `We'll know when we've crossed it, because things will start coming through that have never come through before.`
Problem: The style sheet quotes the ch27 line as a bad example, and the outline commands it. Ch23's line is a smaller version.
Fix: Cut the ch27 verbatim. Keep ch23's (it states a rule).

**6.3 STYLE on "I know" against 10+ allowances.**
Quotes: STYLE `"I know." as a reply` is stock filler. Allowed in ch18, ch21, ch23, ch24, ch25 and ch29, each with `Allowed, because it's an answer.`
Problem: The rule is exceptions. A drafter will decide "I know" is fine.
Fix: STYLE: `"I know" is fine as a reply when the speaker is agreeing with a fact; it's filler when it stands in for feeling.` Delete the per-chapter "allowed" notes.

**6.4 STYLE: every chapter needs a romance beat and an old-life beat.**
Quote: `Every chapter includes one beat from her old life and one romance beat (see the outline). Both can be a single line.`
Problem: Ch27 to ch30 fill the slot with the toast, the blanket, the latte dream, Mom's head count. STYLE also says `If something is in the scene only to ... seem quirky, to fill length ... cut it.`
Fix: `Include a romance or old-life beat where one fits. Some chapters will have neither.` Cut the old-life beat from ch30, the romance beat from ch27.

**6.5 Analogy limit against the outlines.**
Quotes: STYLE `At most one new analogy per scene.` and `Analogies and similes listed in the outline are the only ones in that scene.` Ch17 scene 3 lists a ship, a pressure front and (in scene 6) cats on a lap; ch30 requires `through a wall` plus a contact sheet.
Fix: Cut to one per scene in the outlines.

**6.6 Triplets limit against the outlines.**
Quotes: every chapter `Triplets: at most 1.` Ch20 ending image: `the towel, the tap still running, the last light on the field`. Ch29 damage list is three items. Ch30 `Four million, then three million, then two million.` plus verbatim triplets.
Fix: Pick one image in ch20; one damage example in ch29.

**6.7 Verbatim override against what the verbatim lines do.**
Quotes: STYLE `Lines marked verbatim override everything in "Never do these".` Verbatim lines include `Not the dimensional walls. The informational ones.` (reversal), `Maybe both` pattern (ch23 "Both."), `I was not one person anymore. I was not forty thousand people.` (triplet), ch27's portent.
Problem: The override makes the banned list optional wherever an outline likes the old line, which was most of the old draft's tics. The verbatim list is how the amateur patterns re-enter.
Fix: Cut the verbatim marks from every line that breaks a STYLE rule, and rewrite them.

**6.8 "Describe nothing" against "light for three seconds".**
Quotes: ch16 Do not `Describe what things look like, except during the three seconds of light.` and Scene 2 `Describe what it is, not what it isn't: a whole-body sense of pressure and texture`; Scene 1 `Nothing for her eyes`.
Fix: Cut "except during the three seconds"; the sentence already allows description in scene 3 only.

**6.9 Length target against "don't pad".**
Quotes: STYLE `A shorter chapter is better than a padded one.` Outline: `Length: about 3,500 words.` for ch22, ch23, ch26, ch27, ch28, each of which has a scene the outline itself calls thin.
Fix: README: `If a scene has nothing left to do at half its target length, stop.`

## The ten changes to make first

1. **Fix the scale of the late catastrophe** (4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4). Cut "a hundred million" or set a mechanism for it, correct the geography, and show the world changing.
