## 2. Decisions only you can make

Each item has my recommendation. If you don't change an item, I'll use the recommendation.

### The four big ones

**D1. The clone ceiling and the 8-million ending.** MR wants a cap of a few thousand, with 8 million as a self-destructive one-off. NR wants 8 million kept and set up properly.

*Recommendation: keep 8 million, and set it up.*
- In Ch 29, establish two numbers: what she can sustain (about 110) and what she can briefly burst to.
- Also in Ch 29, show a reabsorption she barely survives, so the reader knows that reabsorbing at scale can kill her.
- Give the Ch 30 creature a defense that only saturation from every side can beat, so numbers win for a reason.
- Give her a reason she must pull them back fast. Eight million bodies, each producing mana, are the loudest signal on Earth, and every second they stay up invites the next crossing. This uses the book's own rule, and it closes the biggest gap NR found ("why not reabsorb slowly?").

**D2. The Ch 28 death toll.** One creature killing 100 million people breaks the economics in Ch 6. It also makes Ch 29's parking-structure world, still running on generators, hard to believe. NR would keep the number and explain the mechanism. MR would cut it.

*Recommendation: cut it to tens of thousands over several days, plus a region the creature never leaves.* That region becomes a piece of Earth that has stopped being Earth. It stays unstoppable, which is what your own note asks for, and the world can still function enough for Ch 29.

**D3. The timeline.** Currently: February start, about 3 months of training, a few weeks on the other side, and an 11-day Act IV.

*Recommendation: keep Act I-III roughly as it is, and slow Act IV down.*
- Ch 23-25 (videos, community meetings, containment) span about two to three months.
- Breach to ending (Ch 26-30) stays fast, about two weeks, because viral spread really is that fast.
- The book then runs from February to about November.

I'd date every chapter in the timeline file.

**D4. Where the romance ends up before Ch 30.**

*Recommendation: they become a couple in Act III.* Ch 21 already has the look. Make it a kiss, and have Mira say it back. That turns Act IV into a test of a real couple, and the ending loses something they actually had. The alternative is to keep the burn slow all the way and make the five Miras the only payoff, but four reviewers already say it's too thin.

### Smaller rule decisions (I'll use these unless you say otherwise)

- **D5. Why untrained people die within about 3 months.** Their output keeps rising. By about three months an uncycled person is loud enough that mid-tier crossings become worth it. "Corpse to disappearing" (Ch 4) is the difference between being killed here and being dragged across. One mechanism only, with no internal toxicity.
- **D6. The barrier weakening in Ch 30.** Cut it. Crossings get worthwhile because the payoff rises; the wall stays the same. Fix the outline line to match.
- **D7. "Science was founded by mages."** Soften it to "after the witch trials, practitioners sponsored skepticism." Ch 4 already says "sponsoring of rationalism". Your later explanation goes into the rules: towns that discovered magic together got loud and were wiped out.
- **D8. Intelligent creatures "don't care" against the scout sent "for entertainment."** Both can hold. They don't care about Earth as food, but something odd draws a scout, and the odd thing is Jason's repeated crossings. His trips cause Ch 19, which gives his choices a real cost.
- **D9. Coming back after death.** Only intelligent beings with souls come back. Strays and mid-tiers just die. That's why Jason boxes the scout instead of killing it.
- **D10. Mira being "not special".** Her talent is ordinary. Her patience and photographer's eye are unusual, and the clone technique suits that kind of mind. Cut the line "Mira isn't special."
- **D11. Teleporting.** Fully random is the default. He can narrow the range at a mental and mana cost, which he did in Ch 1. This makes Ch 3 and Ch 13 consistent.
- **D12. Memory erasure.** It removes memories and any partial understanding, but only before awakening. After awakening it can't stop production. Delilah teaches Mira erasure in Act II as the community's standard tool, which Mira hates learning ("My mind is mine"). That sets up Ch 25.
- **D13. The photonless world.** There is no natural light, and heat moves only by touch. Practitioners can make light on purpose. Every scene on the other side follows this.
- **D14. Standalone or series.** The ending stays as written and leaves the door open. The personal arc (D4) closes before it.

## 5. The format for a Haiku-ready outline

Haiku will copy the outline's wording into the prose. Every "he doesn't answer" in the outline becomes a "he doesn't answer" in the chapter. So the rewritten outline describes intentions and beats, not stage directions, and quotes only dialogue we want used word for word.

Each chapter entry will have:

- **Date, location, chapter length target** (about 3,000 words)
- **Opening state and closing state:** what is true at the start and what has changed by the end
- **Two to four scenes.** For each:
  - where it happens
  - what Mira wants
  - what's in the way
  - the beats
  - what changes
  - one concrete sensory detail to use
- **Jason's intention in the chapter**, written as a want or fear ("he doesn't want to tell her and he's bad at hiding it"), never as a stage direction
- **The romance beat and the old-life beat**
- **Rules in play**, pointing to the rules sheet
- **Setups to plant and payoffs to land**
- **Verbatim lines:** only the dialogue we want kept exactly, marked as verbatim
- **Do not:** chapter-specific traps

**Companion files every Haiku agent gets:**
- `RULES.md`: one page, the D-decisions
- `CHARACTERS.md`: what each character wants, fears, is bad at, and how they talk
- `TIMELINE.md`: a date for every chapter, clone counts, who knows what
- `STYLE.md`: the tic list from the reviews, as find-and-avoid items: "I know." as a reply, null-space beats, "Not X. Y." reversals, explaining a moment after showing it, "filed that away", "his jaw works", the twelve-inches motif, the coffee images

Each Haiku agent would also get the last 300 words of the previous chapter, so the joins line up.
