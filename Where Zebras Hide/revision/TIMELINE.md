# Timeline

There's no year on purpose. Days of the week are fixed where the text depends on them. Chapter numbers use the new plan (section 4 of REVISION_PLAN.md).

## The three-month clock

- Onset is the morning of **Saturday, February 10** (Ch 5). The quiet window closes around **May 10**.
- Ch 8 ends with the window about two-thirds gone, around April 10.
- Ch 12 is three months in. She has made it through the window. The longer danger, the first few years, is still ahead (see RULES).
