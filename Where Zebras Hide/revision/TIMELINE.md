# Timeline

There's no year on purpose. Days of the week are fixed where the text depends on them. Chapter numbers use the new plan (section 4 of REVISION_PLAN.md).

## The three-month clock

- Onset is the morning of **Saturday, February 10** (Ch 5). The quiet window closes around **May 10**.
- Ch 8 ends with the window about two-thirds gone, around April 10.
- Ch 12 is three months in. She has made it through the window. The longer danger, the first few years, is still ahead (see RULES).

## Who knows what

- **Mira's family:**
  - Thinks she quit her job to travel with Jason, then moved to Vermont with him.
  - Mom stopped asking about promotions after March.
  - Nora is suspicious from Ch 9 on ("reading a script").
- **Jess, the roommate:** Mira pays her share through May, then gives up her half of the lease (Ch 12). Her things are boxed in Jess's spare closet.
- **Her job:** she quit by email on Feb 12, giving a family emergency. Marcus texted in February; she sent one word back.
- **Delilah:**
  - Has known Jason by reputation for years.
  - Learns he crosses in Ch 15.
  - Jason warns her on day one of the hunters' watch in Ch 13.
- **The hunters:**
  - Have known Jason since before Columbus.
  - Saw her on the sidewalk in Ch 1, so they know her face and Columbus. Learn in Ch 13 that she's a practitioner who can fight.
  - Owen meets her at the lake house in Ch 22.
- **Kael:** knows Jason by reputation. Meets Mira in Ch 17.
