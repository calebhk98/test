# Chapter 26: Cascade

**When and where:** Days 1 to 3. The Vermont base living room, and, through Mira's clones, a dozen places across the eastern US.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- A streamer, Cass, awakened on camera, and the internet worked out the rest. Hospitals are seeing symptoms.
- Nora asked Mira if this is why she left. Mira said yes and asked her not to read the explanations.
- Jason admitted the shield failed.
- They decided to teach. Mira has started splitting into teaching clones.
- Jason has never shared his techniques publicly. He taught Mira, and no one else.

## What this chapter does

Jason gives away everything and still believes it was his. Mira says out loud what no one else will. The world reacts, and Mira is in that world: her clones are in clinics, schools and parking lots.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The camera (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason sets up a camera in the living room. He's going to record everything: cycling, shielding, sensing, and then the teleport, the Box and the lockdown.
  2. **Why it's worth it now:** so many people are producing that survival skills save more than they endanger, and the threshold is already crossed. He says this once.
  3. He records for hours. He's clear and methodical, and it's devastating to watch: what took him years, explained in minutes.
  4. Show his struggle through things he does: he stops recording twice and sits with his hands over his face. Once he deletes a take and does it again because he can't make himself say the last step. Then he says it.

### Scene 2: "You should have done this before" (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. "You should have done this before."
  2. He doesn't concede: "It was mine. It still is. I just don't get to keep it anymore."
  3. They fight, hard, about Owen's student and Delilah's boy, all the dead kids. He holds his position, and it costs him to hold it, and she can see that.
  4. **Mira says what nobody else will:** "At this scale nobody's consenting. Every one of these videos does to people what you wouldn't do to me in the woods." He says, "Yeah. I know." Allowed, because it's an answer. Then: "Neither did you, with Marcus."
  5. She throws his own question back from Spain: "You cured cancer in your garage, Jason." He says, "And now I'm giving it away. It's still mine."
  6. They stand in that for a moment. Neither of them is clean.

### Scene 3: Owen (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mana message from Owen: "Took you long enough, asshole."
  2. Jason laughs. It's not a good laugh. Then he writes back, and for the first time in his life he asks anyone for help: "I know. Help."
  3. Owen does.

### Scene 4: Everywhere (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** to reach as many people as she can.
- **Beats:**
  1. Her clones go out, by car, bus and on foot. Practitioners Owen sends drive some of them farther.
  2. By day 2 she is holding about a hundred and ten of herself, rotating one out and a fresh one in every few minutes.
  3. Show the world reacting through specific scenes her clones are actually in:
     - a clinic waiting room where a nurse is cycling badly between patients
     - a high school gym in Hartford where she teaches sensing to a hundred terrified kids
     - a church parking lot where a pastor is preaching that it's demonic, and his congregation is warm in the chest anyway
     - a governor's press conference on a TV in a diner
  4. Jason calls his parents for the first time since spring, and walks them through sensing on speakerphone, badly, while his mother cries. Mira hears it from the kitchen.
  5. Priya, by mana message: she's running a group in Albany, and her notebook is now a curriculum.
  6. Others treat Mira's body nearest Jason as "the real one". She lets them.

### End (about 200 words)
Night of day 3. Mira's body in the living room, the one people call "the real one", watching Jason upload the last video. He sits back. She sits down next to him on the floor. End on the upload bar finishing.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to undo nothing, and to save as many people as he can. Both are true.
- **His position:** unchanged. "It was mine." Forced, not converted.
- **Asks for help** for the first time in his life.

## Romance beat
