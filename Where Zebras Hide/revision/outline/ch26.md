# Chapter 26: Cascade

**When and where:** Days 1 to 3. The Vermont base living room, and, through Mira's clones, a few places across the eastern US.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- A streamer, Cass, awakened on camera, and the internet worked out the rest. Hospitals are seeing symptoms.
- Nora asked Mira if this is why she left. Mira said yes and asked her not to read the explanations.
- Jason admitted that keeping it secret failed.
- They decided to teach. Mira has started splitting into teaching clones.
- Jason has never shared his techniques publicly. He taught Mira, and no one else.
- **What Jason owns:**
  - His teaching sequence, which gets most people cycling in days instead of weeks.
  - The teleport, which only very strong practitioners could learn.
  - The Box and the lockdown, which nobody else can do.
- He has kept them private because a teleport can take a person out of a locked room. Owen's counter is that there aren't enough Jasons to be on call.
- In Spain Mira asked him whether he would keep a cure for cancer he had found alone in his garage.

## What this chapter does

Jason gives away what he owns and still believes it was his. Mira says out loud what no one else will. The world reacts, and Mira is in that world: her clones are in clinics, schools and parking lots.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The camera (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason sets up a camera in the living room. He is going to record his teaching sequence, and then the teleport, the Box and the lockdown.
  2. He says once why it's worth it now: so many people are producing that survival skills save more than they endanger.
  3. He records for hours, careful and exact. It's hard to watch: what took him years, laid out in minutes. Show his struggle through what he does, one or two things: he stops recording and sits with his hands over his face, or he deletes a take and does it again because he can't make himself say the last step.
  4. When he gets to the Box, Mira stops him. She says he is about to teach a million people how to build a prison. He says it's the best training tool there is and people are going to die without it. She says that's true and it's still a prison. He records it, because it's his to give. The Box and the lockdown are his by being impossible for anyone else to do, so the recording teaches people to understand them, not to build them. She doesn't leave the room. The disagreement stays where it has always been.

### Scene 2: "You should have done this before" (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira tells him he should have done this before. He doesn't concede.
  2. They fight, hard, about Owen's student and every kid who died alone in a field. He holds his position, and it costs him to hold it, and she can see that.
  3. Mira says what nobody else will: at this scale nobody is consenting, and every one of these videos does to people what he wouldn't do to her in the woods. He tells her she's right, and that she did the same to Marcus.
  4. She reminds him of her question in Spain: whether he would give away a cure he had found alone in his garage. He says he's giving it away now, and it is still his.
  5. They stand in the kitchen with that between them until the kettle clicks off.
  6. Jason sends Owen a message that says only that he is going to teach everything. Owen's reply comes back at once. Jason laughs once, short. Then he writes back a single word asking for help, for the first time in his life. Owen helps.

### Scene 3: Everywhere (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** to reach as many people as she can.
- **Beats:**
  1. Her clones go out, by car, bus and on foot. Practitioners Owen sends drive some of them farther.
  2. By day 2 she is holding about a hundred and ten of herself.
  3. On day 3 she goes to about three thousand for one hour in a high school gym in Hartford, where several thousand terrified people have gathered, to teach sensing in small groups. She pulls them back afterward, slowly, and it costs her an evening.
  4. Show the world reacting through the gym and one other place a clone is actually standing: a clinic waiting room in Ohio where a nurse is cycling badly between patients, a parking lot in Ohio where neighbors sit on car hoods, or a radio in a car carrying a governor's press conference. Show the gym plus one.
  5. Jason calls his parents on a number they can trace, for the first time in his adult life, and walks them through sensing on speakerphone, badly, while his mother cries. Mira hears it from the kitchen.
  6. Anjali, by mana message, from her sister's town in eastern Rajasthan, where she went to teach her family: she is running sensing for twenty people at a time in a courtyard.
  7. Priya, by phone: she's running a group in Albany, and her notebook is now a curriculum.
  8. Others treat Mira's body nearest Jason as "the real one". She lets them.

### End (about 200 words)
Night of day 3. Mira's body in the living room, the one people call "the real one", watching Jason upload the last video. He sits back. She sits down next to him on the floor. End on the upload bar finishing.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to undo nothing, and to save as many people as he can. Both are true.
- **His position:** unchanged. It was his. Forced, not converted.
- **Asks for help** for the first time in his life.

## Romance beat
The floor, the upload bar, his shoulder.

## Old-life beat
Mom texts that she and Dad are learning the breathing from a video, and asks whether that's right and whether it's safe. One of Mira's bodies already teaching in Ohio drives to her parents' house that evening to teach them properly.

## Rules in play
- Teaching becomes worth it once enough people are producing.
- Footage and explanations spread it.
- Clones: about 110 sustained, rotating, with a burst of about 3,000 once, and no main body (others choose one).
- The first lesson is sensing.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "You should have done this before." Mira says this to Jason in the living room, after the last take of the sequence.
- "It was mine. It still is. I just don't get to keep it anymore." Jason answers her.
- "Took you long enough, asshole." Owen's reply to Jason's message that he is going to teach everything.

## Limits
- Calling Jason a genius or brilliant: 0. Show it.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Verbatim lines count toward every limit.

## Do not
- Show the content of any recording. The reader never hears the techniques.
- Make Jason say he was wrong to keep it.
- Summarize the world's reaction. Every reaction is somewhere a clone is standing.
