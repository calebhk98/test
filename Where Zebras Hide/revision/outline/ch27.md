# Chapter 27: Incursion

**When and where:** Days 8 to 10. Vermont, with Mira's clones teaching across the eastern US. The creature is in Rajasthan, India, walking toward Delhi, seen through footage and messages.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Since day 0 (the Cass stream), millions have been learning. Jason released every technique he has. Mira holds about 110 clones, teaching and fighting.
- Mid-sized creatures have started crossing in cities, drawn by crowds of new producers who can't cycle.
- Owen is helping. Priya runs a group in Albany. Mira's parents learned sensing from her clone.
- Nobody has seen anything big yet. Jason's warning: "things will start coming through that have never come through before."
- At the lake house Mira met **Anjali** *(proposed name)*, a quiet practitioner from Jaipur who makes jewelry and was kind to her.

## What this chapter does

The first truly massive crossing. It's the scale the toll used to make impossible, and now the meal is big enough. It has a human face, and Mira does something about it, even from across the world.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Anjali (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mana message from Anjali: "It's here." Then, ten minutes later: "It's so big."
  2. Then nothing. Mira sends back, and sends back again. No answer.

### Scene 2: The footage (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Phone footage floods out of Rajasthan.
  2. **What it is** (CREATURES.md): a single colossal pale body, hundreds of meters tall, eyeless, built like the small ones but the size of a hill that walks. Light sits on it wrong; it looks flat even from miles away. It shows up on every camera.
  3. **How it kills:** its mana pulls in every producing person for kilometers around it as it walks. They fall where they stand, or are dragged toward it. Ordinary people die in its wake: buildings it walks through, highways jammed with people fleeing, fires, crushes.
  4. It walks slowly toward Delhi, through some of the densest places on Earth. It doesn't hurry, and it doesn't notice resistance.
  5. Mira's line: "It moves like weather. Like a natural disaster with intent."
  6. She thinks of the vast thing she felt pass over them on the other side, and knows that was bigger.

### Scene 3: Doing something from here (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to help people she can't reach.
- **Beats:**
  1. She can't get there; Jason can't aim a teleport.
  2. But she can coordinate. Through mana messages she connects practitioners in India through Anjali's contacts and Owen's. With her teaching clones' experience, she builds an evacuation rule:
     - get producing people away from its path first, upwind of its range
     - get the non-producing behind them
  3. She runs it from Vermont with thirty of her bodies on messages at once. It saves people, and not nearly enough.
  4. The count climbs over three days: tens of millions, then more. By the time it stops, about a hundred million.
  5. Show the number through one place: Anjali's street, in a video someone posts, empty.

### Scene 4: It leaves (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. On day 10 it stops, turns, and goes back across, full.
  2. Nobody drove it off. Practitioners threw everything at it and barely marked it.
  3. It wasn't defeated. It was finished.

### Scene 5: The opening act (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira looks at Jason. "Is this the thing you were always afraid of?" "No. This is the opening act."
  2. Let the line land, and then end on something physical, such as Mira's body by the window pulling a blanket around her while the rest of her keeps teaching.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to go there and fight it. He could jump at random and hope to land in India, and he'd die and change nothing if he did.
- **Frightened**, the way she's rarely seen him. He stays close to "her real one".

## Romance beat
She asks him not to try the random jump, and he stays. It costs him.

## Old-life beat
Nora calls in tears. She's been following Mira's instruction; she hasn't read the explanations, and she isn't producing. "I'm so scared." Mira, from one of her bodies, stays on the phone with her for an hour.
