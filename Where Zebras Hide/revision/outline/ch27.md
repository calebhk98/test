# Chapter 27: Incursion

**When and where:** Days 8 to 10. Vermont, with Mira's clones teaching across the eastern US. The creature is in India: it crosses in eastern Rajasthan near the Haryana border, then walks slowly north-east toward Delhi and on into the Gangetic plain. Mira sees it through footage and messages.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Since day 0 (the Cass stream), millions have been learning. Jason released everything he has. Mira holds about 110 clones, teaching and fighting.
- Mid-sized creatures have started crossing in cities, drawn by crowds of new producers who can't cycle.
- Owen is helping. Priya runs a group in Albany. Mira's parents learned sensing from her clone.
- Nobody has seen anything big yet. Jason's warning was that when the crowd is big enough, things will start coming through that have never come through before.
- At the lake house Mira met Anjali, a quiet practitioner from Jaipur who makes jewelry and was kind to her. She is at her sister's town in eastern Rajasthan, teaching her family.

## What this chapter does

The first truly massive crossing. It is the scale the toll used to make impossible, and now the meal is big enough. It has a human face, and Mira does something about it, even from across the world.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: It's here (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mana message from Anjali: it's here. Ten minutes later: it's so big. Then nothing. Mira sends back, and sends back again.
  2. Phone footage floods out of Rajasthan.
  3. **What it is:** a single colossal pale body, hundreds of meters tall, eyeless, built like the small ones but the size of a hill that walks. Light sits on it wrong, and it looks flat even from miles away. It shows up on every camera.
  4. **How it kills:** its mana pulls in every producing person for kilometers around it as it walks. They fall where they stand, or are dragged toward it. Ordinary people die in its wake: buildings it walks through, highways jammed with people fleeing, fires, crushes. It walks slowly toward Delhi, through some of the densest places on Earth, and on into the plain. It doesn't hurry, and it doesn't notice resistance.
  5. Mira says aloud, to Jason, what it moves like.
  6. She thinks of the vast thing she felt pass over them on the other side, and knows that was bigger.

### Scene 2: Doing something from here (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** to help people she can't reach.
- **Beats:**
  1. She wants to go, and she can't help from there. Jason could aim a jump at the region, and he would arrive alone and drained in the middle of it with nothing that works on something that size. Mira asks him not to, and he stays. It costs him.
  2. She can coordinate. Through mana messages she connects practitioners in India through Anjali's contacts and Owen's. With her teaching clones' experience, she builds an evacuation rule: get producing people away from its path first, upwind of its range, then get the non-producing behind them.
  3. She runs it from Vermont with thirty of her bodies on messages at once. It saves people, and not nearly enough.
  3a. On the night of day 9, wolves cross into several cities at once, and she goes to about 20,000 of herself for the night to hold them. Give it two sentences, seen only as tiredness in the thirty on the messages. No wolf fight on the page. She starts folding them back at dawn.
  4. The count climbs over three days: tens of millions, then more. By the time it stops, about a hundred million.
  5. Anjali never answers again. Show the count through one place: a video someone posts of the street in her sister's town, empty.

### Scene 3: Nora (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Nora calls in tears. She has been following Mira's instruction. She hasn't read the explanations, and she isn't producing. She says she is so scared.
  2. Mira, from one of her bodies, stays on the phone with her for an hour.

### Scene 4: It leaves (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. On day 10 it stops on the plain, turns, and goes back across, full. Nobody drove it off. Practitioners threw everything at it and barely marked it. It wasn't defeated. It was finished.
  2. The count by the time it stops is about a hundred million. Give it once, attached to a person or a place Mira has seen.
  3. Mira asks Jason whether this is it. He says no: that one came for a meal, and he doesn't know what the next one will come for.
  4. End on one thing a body does while the others work: she is eating, or she is holding the phone against her shoulder. Pick one.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to go there and fight it. He could aim a jump at the region, and he'd die and change nothing if he did.
- **Frightened**, the way she's rarely seen him. He stays close to "her real one".

## Romance beat
She asks him not to jump, and he stays. It costs him. Later, while thirty of her work the messages, he sits beside her real one and keeps a hand on her back.

## Old-life beat
Nora's call. It comes because Nora has seen the footage from India and Mira is the only person she can reach who isn't on the news.

## Set up in this chapter
- Anjali is in her sister's town in eastern Rajasthan with her family when it crosses. Her last message is two lines. Nothing after.
- The evacuation rule is on the page as a rule Mira states plainly: producers away first, the rest behind them.
- On the night of day 9 she holds about 20,000 of herself against wolves. Two sentences, as tiredness, and no fight.
- Jason answers that that one came for a meal, plainly, and says he doesn't know what the next one will come for.
- The vast thing she felt on the other side comes to mind for one sentence and she doesn't say it aloud.

## Rules in play
- The toll: the payoff finally matches the cost for a massive being.
- Producing people are drawn in. Non-producers die of the aftermath.
- Every creature shows up on camera.
- The mana message.
- Jason can aim a jump. Going there would not help.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "It moves like weather. Like a natural disaster with intent." Mira says this to Jason in scene 1, watching the footage.
- "Is this it?" Mira asks Jason in scene 4, after it has left.
- "No. That one came for a meal. I don't know what the next one will come for." Jason's answer.

## Limits
- The word "toll" means only the crossing cost. Use "dead" or "count" for casualties.
- Casualty numbers without a place or person: at most 1.
- Don't explain why more people producing means bigger things cross. The reader knows.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Verbatim lines count toward every limit.

## Do not
- Have anyone defeat it.
- Make Mira only a phone viewer. She coordinates.
