# Chapter 7: Day One

**When and where:** Tuesday, February 13, to Wednesday, February 14 (Valentine's Day). The cabin.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has been producing for three days. She has headaches, odd hunger and fatigue.
- She can sense her own mana, like a heartbeat that isn't her heart.
- Her phone is off and stays off at the cabin.
- She knows:
  - the other side exists
  - things cross for meals, and the bigger they are, the bigger the meal they need
  - she'll get louder as she gets better
- She knows Jason's real history, and that his parents are fine.
- She has about three months to get quiet.

## What this chapter does

Magic as a physical skill that she's bad at. Frustration, anger, and a tiny breakthrough she earns herself, with her own photographer's way in.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Cycling (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** to learn the thing that keeps her alive, fast.
- **In the way:** Jason teaches it the way it worked for him, and that way doesn't work for her.
- **Beats:**
  1. Cycling: move it through you in a loop instead of letting it leak out. He compares it to holding your breath underwater. This is the scene's one analogy. Being able to cycle means being quiet.
  2. Hours cross-legged on the cabin floor. His instructions were obvious to him at nine and mean nothing in her ribs.
  3. One specific physical failure. Every time she almost has it, it slips out on the left side, like a cramp letting go. Her knees ache. She's starving again.
  4. When she struggles, he goes to the kitchen and brings her food.

### Scene 2: The fight (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to know her odds.
- **Beats:**
  1. Quiet, frustrated anger. She asks how long it took him, in the verbatim exchange below. She asks how long it takes most people. A week, he says. Two.
  2. She asks what happens if she can't do it at all.
  3. **Jason answers badly.** If she can't, she is loud, so they move, and keep moving, and he deals with whatever comes, until she can. He answers at once, with a plan he has clearly made already. She says so.
  4. She snaps that she is a liability with a deadline. He says she's a student, not a liability. They're both wrong.
  5. She goes outside to cool off.

### Scene 3: Night (about 450 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Separate rooms, with a wall between them.
  2. Lying in bed, she stops trying.
  3. She thinks about the darkroom: you can't grab an image coming up in developer, you have to wait and keep the tray rocking. This is the scene's one analogy. She stops pushing and just rocks it.
  4. A flicker. The loop holds for half a second. It's gone when she tries to grab it, but it was there.
  5. She lies awake grinning at the ceiling.

### Scene 4: Morning (about 550 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Wednesday, February 14. She does it again on the cabin floor before breakfast: a partial cycle, leaky but real.
  2. Jason stops on his way past with the coffee pot.
  3. **He reacts specifically.** He tells her where she lost it, on the left side, about two seconds in, and asks if she felt that. She did. He tells her it's good. He is visibly pleased.
  4. He says the verbatim line below.
  5. Before she starts again, he puts a mug next to her knee and says one plain thing about the date: it's Valentine's Day. No joke, no follow-up. Then he goes back to the stove.
- **End:** her trying again, and the loop holding a little longer.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her to get this, so badly that he's a worse teacher for it.
- **Fears:** that she won't.
- **Bad at:** explaining something that was obvious to him at nine.
- **He shows love by:** the food, the mug, the plan he made for her failing.

## Romance beat
The mug and the one plain remark about the date.

## Old-life beat
None in this chapter.

## Rules in play
- Cycling: most people take one to two weeks. She gets a partial cycle on day two, through her own image. That's fast but not unheard of, and Jason is impressed, not stunned.
- Output rises.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "How long did it take you?" / "Two days." Mira asks and Jason answers, in Scene 2, in the middle of her frustration.
- "Again." Jason to Mira, once, at the end of Scene 4, after he has told her the attempt was good.

## Limits for this chapter
- Jason answers every direct question she asks directly.
- When she does something right, he says what was right about it.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- One body reaction per scene.
- Analogies: one per scene.

## Do not
- Make her secretly gifted. It's stubbornness and her own image.
- Write the training as a summary. Stay in specific moments.
- Have Jason pace, or hover while she struggles.
