# Chapter 7: Day One

**When and where:** Tuesday, February 13, to Wednesday, February 14 (Valentine's Day, which neither of them mentions until the end). The cabin.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has been producing for three days. She has headaches, odd hunger and fatigue.
- She can sense her own mana, the "heartbeat that isn't your heart".
- She knows:
  - the other side exists
  - things cross for meals, and the bigger they are, the bigger the meal they need
  - she'll get louder as she gets better
- She knows Jason's real history, and that his parents are fine.
- She has about three months to get quiet.

## What this chapter does

Magic as a physical skill that she's bad at. Frustration, anger, and a tiny breakthrough she earns herself, with her own photographer's way in. A first hint that the danger from people hasn't gone away.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Cycling (about 1,000 words)
- **Mira wants** to learn the thing that keeps her alive, fast.
- **In the way:** Jason teaches it like physics, and she isn't a physicist.
- **Beats:**
  1. Cycling: move it through you in a loop instead of letting it leak out. "Like holding your breath underwater. If you can cycle, you're quiet. If you can't, you're a porch light."
  2. Hours cross-legged on the cabin floor. He gives instructions ("you're pushing it, don't push it, guide it, it's a gradient") that sound like sense and mean nothing in her ribs.
  3. One specific physical failure. Every time she almost has it, it slips out on the left side, like a cramp letting go. Her knees ache. She's starving again.
  4. He paces when she struggles. He keeps starting to help and stopping himself.

### Scene 2: The fight (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to know her odds.
- **Beats:**
  1. Quiet, frustrated anger. "How long did it take you?" "Two days." "How long does it take most people?" "A week. Two."
  2. "What happens if I can't do it at all?"
  3. **Jason answers badly:** "Then you're loud, and we move, and we keep moving, and I deal with whatever comes, until you can." He says it fast, like he's had the plan ready for days. That scares her more than silence would have.
  4. She snaps something unfair: "Great. So I'm a liability with a deadline." He snaps back: "You're not a liability, you're a student." They're both wrong, and both know it.
  5. She goes outside to cool off.

### Scene 3: The car on the road (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Outside, she watches a pickup slow on the gravel road below the cabin, sit for a moment, then drive on.
  2. Jason is on the porch behind her. He watched it too, for one beat too long. "Probably nothing."
  3. She doesn't believe him. She notices he doesn't say "definitely".
- This is the hunters' first whisper since the sidewalk. Don't confirm it.

### Scene 4: Night (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Separate rooms. She notices the wall between them in a way she didn't notice the space between the motel beds.
  2. Lying in bed, she stops trying.
  3. She thinks about the darkroom: you can't grab an image coming up in developer, you have to wait and keep the tray rocking. She stops pushing and just rocks it.
  4. A flicker. The loop holds for half a second. It's gone when she tries to grab it, but it was there.
  5. She lies awake grinning at the ceiling like an idiot.

### Scene 5: Morning (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She does it again on the cabin floor before breakfast: a partial cycle, leaky but real.
  2. Jason stops mid-step on his way past with the coffee pot.
  3. **He reacts, specifically:** "You lost it on the left side, about two seconds in. Did you feel that?" She did. "Okay. That's... okay. That's really good, actually." He's trying not to be too pleased in case she stops. He fails.
  4. Then: "Again."
  5. Before she starts again, he puts a mug next to her knee and goes back to the stove, humming. It's Valentine's Day. Neither of them mentions it until he says, too casually, "Happy whatever today is." She says, "You remembered the date and not the holiday?" He says, "I'm aware of the holiday."
- **End:** her trying again, and the loop holding a little longer.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her to get this, so badly that he's a worse teacher for it.
- **Fears:** that she won't, and that the truck on the road wasn't nothing.
- **Bad at:** explaining something that was obvious to him at nine.
- **He shows love by:** the plan he already made for her failing, the mug, the date.

## Romance beat
The Valentine's exchange. Small and awkward.

## Old-life beat
She checks her phone. Mom has left two voicemails. She listens to one, and doesn't call back yet.

## Rules in play
- Cycling: most people take one to two weeks. She gets a partial cycle on day two, through her own image. That's fast but not unheard of, and Jason is impressed, not stunned.
- Output rises.
