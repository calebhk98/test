# Chapter 29: The Hundred

**When and where:** Day 10, night, to day 11, morning. A parking structure in Hartford (one clone), and the Vermont base.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft. This is one of the strongest chapters in the old plan, so keep its heart.

## Where things stand

- Mira has held about 110 of herself for nine days, rotating one every four or five minutes:
  - 40 teaching
  - 20 fighting
  - 30 coordinating
  - 20 rotating or in reserve
- She hasn't slept more than four hours at a stretch in nine days.
- A colossal creature killed about a hundred million in India on days 8 to 10, then left. Nothing has crossed that big since.
- Kael asked if her clones are people. She said, "They're me."
- Delilah is driving up.
- Jason stays near "her real one" and teleports groups to safety all day.

## What this chapter does

What it costs her, told from inside. A scared boy who's a natural. Delilah tells her the truth. Mira chooses to rest, and the rest has a price.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Inside (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Open inside her: the one thinking this has probably only existed for a few hours. It doesn't matter; they're all her.
  2. The headache isn't a headache anymore. It's the weather inside her skull.
  3. **Show the damage concretely:**
     - one of her repeats the same sentence twice to a teaching group and doesn't notice until a man points it out
     - one of her looks at a list she wrote an hour ago and doesn't recognize the handwriting as hers
     - she flinches at her own reflection in a car window, unsure for a second which of her it is
  4. She's made peace with not saving most people, the way you make peace with something you haven't accepted: she keeps moving and doesn't look at the number.

### Scene 2: Theo (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A teaching clone runs a session in a parking structure in Hartford: fifty people, a generator's work lights, concrete pillars.
  2. A kid in the back corner, Theo *(proposed name)*, seventeen, in a high school hoodie, gets cycling in two hours. It's clean and stable, better than hers was after a week.
  3. "You're a natural," she says.
  4. He looks up, terrified. "I don't want to be a natural. I want to go home."
  5. Through a hundred other pairs of eyes she's teaching, fighting, coordinating. This one sits in a parking structure watching a seventeen-year-old be afraid of his own talent.
  6. "I know. Me too." This is the exception to the "I know" rule. It's the best line in the plan.
  7. She keeps teaching. He keeps learning. Three blocks away something crosses, and two of her go to handle it while the session doesn't pause.
  8. After the session she gets his name, and tells him to find Priya's group in Albany. He says he will.

### Scene 3: Delilah (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah arrives at the Vermont base and checks Mira's mana before saying hello. Her face does something Mira can't read.
  2. "How long have you been running this many?" "Nine days." "And the rotation?" Mira explains. Delilah does the math and doesn't like it.
  3. "You're not accumulating rest, honey. You're just distributing the exhaustion."
  4. "What else would you have me do?" "If you burn out, who keeps all this going?"
  5. Delilah has already organized relief: Owen's people, Priya's best teachers, practitioners who can cover what she covers for ten hours. Mostly. Not everything.
  6. **Mira makes the call herself.** She thinks it through and says yes. Delilah doesn't force it; she made it possible.

### Scene 4: Jason (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason hasn't said anything about the numbers all week. Now he does, and not as logistics: "I'm not asking about the coverage. I'm asking about you."
  2. That's what makes her cry, not Delilah's math.
  3. He says it's her call, and means it.

### Scene 5: Ten hours (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She pulls about ninety of herself back at once. It's the worst reabsorption since the field in August. She keeps twenty out on things that can't be covered.
  2. She sleeps for ten hours. **This is the first silence inside her head in nine days.**
  3. She wakes, and finds out:
     - a teaching session in New Jersey stopped mid-sentence when its relief didn't arrive
     - a fighting line in Hartford dropped for forty minutes
     - eleven people died who might not have
  4. Theo's group is fine. She checks that first and hates herself for checking that first.

### End (about 200 words)
"Her real one" on the floor of the base, back against the wall, about a hundred and ten of her out in the world again. Jason sits down right next to her. She leans into him because this one of her can afford to. End on the lean and one concrete detail, such as his hand on her knee or the smell of burnt toast. Don't end on "neither of them says anything".

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to make her stop, and doesn't, because they agreed. Then he asks the question that matters.
- **His love:** he's been within half a mile of "her real one" for days. Show it in one line, not explained.

## Romance beat

## Old-life beat
