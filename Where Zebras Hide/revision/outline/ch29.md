# Chapter 29: The Hundred

**When and where:** Day 10, night, to day 11, morning. A parking structure in Hartford (one clone), and the Vermont base.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has held about 110 of herself for eight days, teaching, fighting and coordinating, rotating one every four or five minutes.
- Last night she went to about 20,000 for the night against a surge of wolves, and folded them back slowly over the day.
- She hasn't slept more than four hours at a stretch in eight days.
- A colossal creature crossed in Rajasthan on day 8, walked for three days and left, killing about a hundred million. Nothing has crossed that big since.
- Kael asked if her clones are people. She told him they are her.
- Delilah arrived at the base this evening.
- Jason stays near "her real one" and moves groups to safety all day.

## What this chapter does

What it costs her, told from inside. A scared boy who's a natural. Delilah tells her the truth. Mira chooses to rest, and the rest has a price.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Inside (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Open inside her: the one thinking this has probably only existed for a few hours. It doesn't matter; they're all her.
  2. The headache has become a place she lives in.
  3. **Show the damage concretely.** Pick one of these and make it the scene: one of her repeats the same sentence twice to a teaching group and doesn't notice until a man points it out; one of her looks at a list she wrote an hour ago and doesn't recognize the handwriting as hers; she flinches at her own reflection in a car window, unsure for a second which of her it is.
  4. She doesn't look at the numbers from India. The work leaves no room to.

### Scene 2: Theo (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A teaching clone runs a session in a parking structure in Hartford: fifty people, a generator's work lights, concrete pillars.
  2. A kid in the back corner, Theo, seventeen, in a high school hoodie, has cycling by the end of the session. It took Mira a day and a half. She tells him he's a natural.
  3. He looks up, terrified, and says what he wants.
  4. Through all her other eyes she's teaching, fighting, coordinating. This one sits in a parking structure watching a seventeen-year-old be afraid of his own talent.
  5. She keeps teaching. He keeps learning. Three blocks away something crosses, and two of her leave the session to handle it. Theo watches them go.
  6. After the session she gets his name and tells him to stay with his group. Owen's people will move them.

### Scene 3: Delilah (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah checks Mira's mana before saying hello. She puts two fingers on Mira's wrist, which she doesn't need to do.
  2. She asks how long Mira has been running this many. Eight days. She asks about the rotation and Mira explains. Delilah does the math and doesn't like it.
  3. Delilah tells her she isn't resting, she's spreading the tired around.
  4. Mira asks what else she would have her do. Delilah asks who keeps all this going if she burns out.
  5. Delilah has already organized relief: Owen's people, Priya's best teachers, practitioners who can cover what Mira covers for ten hours. Mostly. Not everything.
  6. Mira makes the call herself. She thinks it through and says yes.

### Scene 4: Jason (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason hasn't said anything about the numbers all week. Now he does, and not as logistics.
  2. She cries.
  3. He tells her it's her call.

### Scene 5: Ten hours (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She pulls about ninety of herself back at once. It's the worst reabsorption since the field in August. The twenty that stay out lie down where they are, in rooms Delilah's people guard, and sleep when she sleeps.
  2. She sleeps for ten hours, and dreams she is in her old apartment looking for her keys. This is the first silence inside her head in eight days. She wakes not knowing which body she is in.
  3. She wakes, and finds out: a teaching session in New Jersey stopped mid-sentence when its relief didn't arrive; a fighting line in Hartford dropped for forty minutes; eleven people died who might not have.
  4. She checks Theo's group first. They're fine. Then she reads the list of eleven, and it sits on top of the numbers from India, which she still hasn't looked at.
  5. Owen's people are moving the Hartford groups to a church camp six miles from the base, because Jason told Owen he can stretch his lockdown to cover it and Hartford isn't safe. It is the nearest place with enough beds inside that reach. Theo's group is one of them.

### End (about 200 words)
"Her real one" on the floor of the base, back against the wall, about a hundred and ten of her out in the world again. Jason sits down right next to her. She leans into him because this one of her can afford to. End on the lean and his hand on her knee.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to make her stop, and doesn't, because they agreed. Then he asks the question that matters.
- **His love:** he's been within half a mile of "her real one" for days. Show it in one line, not explained.

## Romance beat
Jason asking about her. The lean.

## Old-life beat
The dream of her old apartment and the keys.

## Rules in play
- Clones are all her, rotating, with no main body. People call one "her real one".
- Reabsorbing at scale nearly floors her.
- Exhaustion spreads across bodies instead of going away.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "The headache wasn't a headache any more. It was a room I lived in." Narration, opening scene 1.
- "I don't want to be a natural. I want to go home." Theo says this in the parking structure, answering that he's a natural.
- "Me too. Stay with your group." Mira's answer to Theo.
- "You're not resting, honey. You're spreading the tired around." Delilah says this to Mira in the base kitchen, after the math. It is her one "honey" of the chapter.
- "I'm not asking about the coverage. I'm asking about you." Jason says this to Mira, after she has gone through Delilah's plan for the relief crews.

## Limits
- Clone counts: about 110, and about 90 pulled back at once. The 20,000 only as last night.
- "Honey": once, in the verbatim line.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Verbatim lines count toward every limit.

## Do not
- Have Delilah force the rest.
- Make the rest free.
- Write any crossing bigger than wolves.
- Explain why more people producing means bigger things cross.
