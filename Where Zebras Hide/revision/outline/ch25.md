# Chapter 25: Breach

**When and where:** Day 0, about November 10, through day 1. The Vermont base, mostly through screens.
**Length:** about 3,200 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Incidents keep spreading: the São Paulo mug, the London anchor's leak, a few new producers.
- Mira erased sixteen students and her friend Marcus, and told Priya the truth.
- The community still has no plan. Jason still half believes the science shield will hold.
- Mira and Jason are a couple. They agreed neither decides about the other alone.

## What this chapter does

This is the point of no return for the world. It isn't one explanation. A famous person awakens on camera, and millions of people work out the rest together. Mira's erasures turn out to have bought a few weeks. The family comes back into it.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The stream (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A huge streamer, Cass Rivera *(proposed name)*, about twenty million followers, has spent weeks doing "Is It Real?" streams about the magic videos: the São Paulo mug, the London segment, Hannah the Leeds nurse's hospital interview. Debunking them, and getting closer each time.
  2. Live, mid-sentence, Cass stops. Puts a hand on their chest. "Wait. Wait, that's... oh." Then a headache, then laughter, then panic.
  3. Cass understands it and can't put it into words. The stream glitches at the key moment, so viewers hear only fragments: "the thing about the...", a laugh, a sharp breath.
  4. Mira watches it on Jason's laptop and feels sick.

### Scene 2: The internet works it out (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Within hours:
     - clips in forty languages
     - frame-by-frame breakdowns
     - threads connecting Cass to São Paulo and London
     - someone's tidy explainer
     - a teenager's whiteboard video
  2. Nobody has to be told. A million people are shown, and a few thousand figure it out together.
  3. Practitioners take things down, and they come back up faster.
  4. Show this through specific posts, view counts and one explainer's thumbnail, not a summary.

### Scene 3: It's in hospitals (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Day 1. Hospitals report warmth in the chest, headaches, a fever that isn't a fever.
  2. Mira's erasures in October bought a few weeks. They didn't change the outcome. She gets up and starts messaging: Delilah, Owen, Priya. If it's coming, she wants a list of who can teach.
  3. Jason calculates out loud, and admits he doesn't really know: "Days. Maybe a week or two."

### Scene 4: Nora (about 500 words)
- **Mira wants** to protect her sister without lying again.
- **Beats:**
  1. Nora calls. A coworker went to the ER with chest warmth. Nora has seen the Cass stream. "Is this what's wrong with you? Is this why you left?"
  2. Mira has a choice. She tells Nora one true thing and one instruction: "Yes. Don't read the explanations. Don't watch the breakdowns. Please. I'm asking you."
  3. Nora is quiet. "Okay." And: "You're scaring me." "I know." This is an answer, so it's allowed.
- **Changes:** for the first time, Mira's family knows something.

### Scene 5: Teach (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. "Can we stop it?" "The spread? No. It's too late." "Then what do we do?"
  2. Mira answers before he can, because she has been thinking it since Priya: "Teach." Jason: "As many people as we can, as fast as we can."
- **End:** on her starting to split, the first dozen of her standing up in the living room.
