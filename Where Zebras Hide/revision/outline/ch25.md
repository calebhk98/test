# Chapter 25: Breach

**When and where:** Day 0, about November 10, through day 1. The Vermont base, mostly through screens.
**Length:** about 3,200 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Incidents keep spreading: the São Paulo bottle, the London anchor's leak, a few new producers.
- Mira erased sixteen students and her friend Marcus, and told Priya the truth.
- The community still has no plan. Jason still half believes the science shield will hold.
- Mira and Jason are a couple. They agreed neither decides about the other alone.

## What this chapter does

This is the point of no return for the world. It isn't one explanation. A famous person awakens on camera, and millions of people work out the rest together. Mira's erasures turn out to have bought a few weeks and no more. The family comes back into it, and Mira decides what to do.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The stream (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A huge streamer, Cass Rivera, about twenty million followers, has spent weeks doing streams about whether the videos are real: the São Paulo bottle, the London segment, Hannah the Leeds nurse's hospital interview. Debunking them, and getting closer each time.
  2. Live, mid-sentence, Cass stops and puts a hand on their chest. They say the word wait twice, and then that something is oh. Then a headache, then laughter, then panic.
  3. Cass reaches the idea while saying something else, and the stream drops the moment before the sentence ends. Viewers hear only fragments.
  4. Mira watches it on Jason's laptop and feels sick.

### Scene 2: The internet works it out (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Within hours, show the spread through specific posts, view counts and one explainer's thumbnail, not a summary. Use two or three of: clips in forty languages, frame-by-frame breakdowns, threads connecting Cass to São Paulo and London, a teenager's whiteboard video.
  2. Nobody has to be told. A million people are shown, a few thousand figure it out together within hours, and by midnight tens of thousands are producing, and every one of them was already telling someone else.
  3. Practitioners take things down, and they come back up faster.

### Scene 3: It's in hospitals (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Day 1. Hospitals report warmth in the chest, headaches, a fever that isn't a fever.
  2. Mira's erasures in October bought a few weeks. They didn't change the outcome. Jason calculates out loud and admits he doesn't really know: days, maybe a week or two.
  3. Mira asks him whether they can stop it. He says the spread can't be stopped, it's too late. She asks what they do, and answers herself: teach.
  4. Jason agrees: as many people as we can, as fast as we can. He starts listing what they'll need. Mira starts messaging Delilah, Owen, Priya and Anjali. If it's coming, she wants a list of who can teach.
  5. Jason tells her to her face that Kael was right and she was right, about the shield. He is frightened and says so. She takes his hand across the laptop.

### Scene 4: Nora (about 500 words)
- **Mira wants** to protect her sister without lying again.
- **Beats:**
  1. Nora calls. A coworker went to the ER with chest warmth. Nora has seen the Cass stream and asks whether this is what's wrong with Mira, and whether it's why she left.
  2. Mira tells Nora one true thing and gives one instruction: yes, and don't read the explanations or watch the breakdowns. She asks her to.
  3. Nora agrees. She says Mira is scaring her. Mira says she knows.
- **Changes:** for the first time, Mira's family knows something.

### End (about 200 words)
Mira starts to split. The first dozen of her stand up in the living room.
