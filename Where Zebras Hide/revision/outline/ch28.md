# Chapter 28: Mobilization

**When and where:** Day 10. Everywhere Mira's clones are: Hartford, Albany, rural Vermont, a Walmart parking lot in New Jersey, her parents' street in Columbus. "Her real one" is at the Vermont base with Jason.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- On day 8 a colossal creature crossed in Rajasthan and walked toward Delhi for three days. About a hundred million people died. It left on its own, full, on day 10. Nobody could stop it.
- Anjali, a practitioner Mira met at the lake house, is gone.
- Mira coordinated evacuations from Vermont by mana message. It saved people, and not nearly enough.
- Jason released everything he has. Owen is helping. Priya runs a group in Albany.
- Mira has held about 110 clones since day 2, rotating one every four to five minutes. Last night she went to about 20,000 for the night, to hold a surge of wolves crossing into several cities at once. She has been folding them back slowly all day and is nearly down to 110.
- Mid-sized creatures cross into cities constantly now, drawn by crowds of people producing with no shielding.
- In July Jason boxed a talking scout from the other side. As the Box closed Mira said once that it was afraid. It was boxed anyway.

## What this chapter does

The world fights back, badly and bravely, and has visibly changed. The panoramic chapter, told through the places Mira is standing. Kael finds one of her and asks the question nobody else has.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: What she's doing (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Show her work concretely through two of its four jobs, one clone each: teaching the teachers (competent practitioners who run their own groups of fifty), fighting wolves (the pale, many-jointed pack hunters that cross into populated areas), coordinating routes and evacuations, or rotating in and out. She never fights the big things; she knows her ceiling. About 110 in total. Don't give a breakdown.
  2. **The rotation.** Every four or five minutes, one of her that has been fighting for hours folds back in and a fresh one takes its place. The fresh one knows everything the tired one learned, because they're the same person.
  3. When a crossing hits a teaching session, she keeps explaining cycling with one voice while her shield drives something back with another body, and picks up the sentence where she left off.

### Scene 2: The world (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Show the world through two places where a clone stands: a National Guard unit in Hartford trying to integrate the first soldiers who can shield, with Owen yelling at a colonel; and a Walmart parking lot in New Jersey where a teaching group of fifty sits on flattened cardboard.
  2. Show one government response through a TV in a diner where a clone of hers is eating, because they all have to eat. The same broadcast shows that the world has changed: flights are grounded, Delhi has no power, and hospitals are turning people away. Use two of the three.
  3. Her clone on her parents' street in Columbus has been rotating through for a few minutes between sessions. Mom and Dad come out to the porch, because Mom has been texting that morning and this is the nearest body. Both of them sense now. Dad is surprisingly good at it, and annoyingly proud.

### Scene 3: Kael (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Kael finds one of her clones in Albany. He looks at her, then at the other Miras visible across the lot.
  2. He asks how many of her there are right now. She tells him. Kael, for once, has to think before he talks.
  3. He says it's insane. She says it's working. He agrees it is working.
  4. Then the real question: each of them is so aware, conscious, making ether, so are they people? Mira thinks once of the scout in the Box, one sentence, and then answers.
  5. He asks if she is sure, then asks again in different words. Leave the doubt open.
  6. Kael's belief that the deep can be lived with has taken a beating since Rajasthan. He says he thought they could talk to them, maybe some of them, but not that one.

### Scene 4: Priya (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira's Albany body visits Priya's group: two hundred people in a high school cafeteria, handouts photocopied from Priya's notebook.
  2. Priya is exhausted and scared, and now she is the one teaching sensing. She tells Mira the work is eating her whole life.

### End (about 400 words)
Back at the Vermont base, "her real one" is on the kitchen floor. Jason makes toast, because it's the only thing he can cook, and brings it to her. Delilah's message comes in: she's been driving since yesterday and she's an hour out. End there.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to do more than he can. He teleports teaching groups out of danger all day, to places he picks, and it's cheap for him, so he does it until his hands shake. He checks on her real one between jumps.
- **Stays close** to her real one. He doesn't say why.

## Romance beat
Jason brings her toast, the only thing he can cook, and sits beside her on the kitchen floor while she eats it in four places at once.

## Old-life beat
Her clone on her parents' street (scene 2, beat 3). It comes up because Mom has been texting and this clone is the nearest one. Mom and Dad both sense now. Dad is surprisingly good at it, and annoyingly proud.

## Set up in this chapter
- Mira answers Kael's question about whether her clones are people with a short answer that she says plainly. Kael doesn't accept it, and the chapter leaves him not sure.
- In answering, Mira thinks once, in one sentence, of the scout in the Box in July. She doesn't tell Kael.
- Kael says his belief that they can talk to some of the things from the other side has taken a beating since Rajasthan.
- Last night Mira held about 20,000 of herself to cover a surge of wolves, and is folding them back slowly over the day. Show it as tiredness in the bodies nearest the camera, not as a number.
- Delilah's message arrives in the last scene: she has been driving since yesterday and she is an hour out.

## Rules in play

- Mid-sized things cross for crowds.
- Teleport is cheap for Jason, and he can aim it.
- The mana message.
- Kael says "ether".
