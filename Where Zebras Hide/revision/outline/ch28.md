# Chapter 28: Mobilization

**When and where:** Day 10. Everywhere Mira's clones are: Boston, Hartford, Albany, rural Vermont, a Walmart parking lot in New Jersey, her parents' street in Columbus. "Her real one" is at the Vermont base with Jason.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- On day 8 a colossal creature crossed in Rajasthan and walked toward Delhi for three days. About a hundred million people died. It left on its own, full. Nobody could stop it.
- Anjali, a practitioner Mira met at the lake house, is gone.
- Mira coordinated evacuations from Vermont by mana message. It saved people, and not nearly enough.
- Jason released every technique. Owen is helping. Priya runs a group in Albany.
- Mira has held about 110 clones since day 2, rotating one every four to five minutes.
- Mid-sized creatures cross into cities constantly now, drawn by crowds of people producing with no shielding.

## What this chapter does

The world fights back, badly and bravely. The panoramic chapter, told through the places Mira is standing. Kael finds one of her and asks the question nobody else has.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: What she's doing (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Lay out her work concretely:
     - About forty of her teach. Mostly she teaches the teachers, competent practitioners who can then run their own groups of fifty. Sometimes beginners, when there's no one else.
     - About twenty of her fight. Mostly the "wolves", the pale, many-jointed pack hunters that cross into populated areas now. Never the big things; she knows her ceiling.
     - About thirty coordinate: routes, messages, evacuations, finding survivors and sending them to teaching groups.
     - About twenty are rotating out or in reserve.
  2. **The rotation.** Every four or five minutes, one of her that has been fighting for hours folds back in and a fresh one takes its place. The fresh one knows everything the tired one learned, because they're the same person.
  3. **When a crossing hits a teaching session:** she keeps explaining cycling with one voice while her shield drives something back with another body, and picks up the sentence where she left off.

### Scene 2: The world (about 1,000 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Specific scenes, each one where a clone stands:
     - a National Guard unit in Hartford trying to integrate the first soldiers who can shield, with Owen yelling at a colonel
     - a hospital in Boston where nurses cycle between patients
     - a Walmart parking lot in New Jersey where a teaching group of fifty sits on flattened cardboard
     - a church opened as a shelter, the pastor from Chapter 26 now learning to sense, crying
  2. Governments are trying to control the story and failing. Some countries close borders, as if that does anything.
  3. Show one government response through a TV in a diner where a clone of hers is eating, because they all have to eat.

### Scene 3: Kael (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. On day 10, Kael finds one of her clones in Albany. He looks at her, then at the three other Miras visible across the lot.
  2. "How many of you are there right now?" She tells him. He goes quiet, and Kael never goes quiet.
  3. "That's insane. You know that's insane." "It's working." "I'm not saying it isn't working. I'm saying it's insane. Those are not the same statement."
  4. Then the real question: "Each of them is so aware. Conscious. Making ether. Are they people?"
  5. **Mira answers on the page:** "They're not people I make. They're me." He looks at her for a long time, and she can see he isn't sure he believes her. Neither is the reader, and that's fine.
  6. Kael's belief that the deep can be lived with has taken a beating since Rajasthan. He says so: "I thought we could talk to them. Maybe some of them. Not that one."

### Scene 4: Priya (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira's Albany body visits Priya's group: two hundred people in a high school cafeteria, Priya's notebook photocopied and stapled into handouts.
  2. Priya is exhausted and brilliant. She's scared, and now she's the one teaching sensing.
  3. Priya: "You were right. It ate my whole life." Then: "Somebody had to."

### End (about 400 words)
Back at the Vermont base, "her real one" is on the kitchen floor, and Jason is making toast because it's the only thing he can cook. She eats it in four places at once. Delilah's message comes in: she's driving up. End there.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to do more than he can. He teleports teaching groups out of danger all day, at random, and it's cheap for him, so he does it until his hands shake.
- **Stays close** to her real one. He doesn't say why.
