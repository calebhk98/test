# Chapter 20: Evolution

**When and where:** Mid-August. A field near the Vermont base, then the other side over the following two weeks.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira can make clones that make clones. Her best so far is a chain five generations deep, and reabsorbing it knocked her flat.
- Before that she held three clones, sometimes four. Kael thinks she is in the top hundred of a few thousand practitioners.
- Jason understands how chains work and cannot do them.
- They have crossed many times but have never slept on the other side; her shields have to hold while she sleeps.
- The hunters can find them anywhere on Earth by ordinary means. They found the cabin through Delilah's gossip, and Delilah has owned it.
- She has fought alone before, on Earth and on the other side.
- She and Jason are clearly in love. They said it once, in an argument in February, and not since. Kael laughed at them for it.
- The battery argument is quiet, but it isn't gone.
- She has decided she needs far more of herself.

## What this chapter does

She finds out how big she can get, and what it costs. She makes a decision alone, the way Jason does, and they have it out. Then they finally say it.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: A thousand (about 1,000 words)
- **Mira wants** to know her limit before anything forces her to find it.
- **Beats:**
  1. Ten days of practice since Kael's, in two or three sentences built from specific days: chains of eight generations with two or three clones at each step, then twenty bodies in all, then sixty. Each one hurts a little less to pull back, because she is learning to pull back in pieces.
  2. Then an afternoon in a field near the base while Jason is in town for supplies. She didn't tell him.
  3. Clones make clones that make clones, and she keeps going: dozens, hundreds, about a thousand of her standing in the grass.
  4. **What it is like:** she is everywhere in the field at once and feels the wind on a thousand faces. It isn't crowded. It is huge. One of her is crying and the rest find that interesting.
  5. Then she pulls them back. It is like swallowing the field. The headache becomes a sound.
  6. She wakes in the grass hours later, in the dark, with no memory of falling. She is in a different body from the one that was standing when she started, and that doesn't matter.
- **By the end:** she knows her limit and what it costs, and she is alone in the dark.

### Scene 2: The fight (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** him to understand she had to know.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason found her unconscious in the field when he got back. He is past angry. He is scared in a way she has never seen.
  2. He tells her she decided alone and didn't tell him. She starts to defend it, hears the sentence coming out of her mouth, and says it is his line.
  3. He sits down in the grass next to her and takes her hand.
  4. She apologizes properly.
  5. **They agree on something real:** neither of them decides about the other alone again. She isn't sure either of them can keep it.
- **By the end:** they have an agreement, and both of them know it will be tested.

### Scene 3: Sleeping over there (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Over the next two weeks she practices holding her shields while she sleeps, until they hold on their own without her. As a backup, one of her stays awake anyway. She is her own night watch.
  2. Their first night on the other side: total dark, moss, his breathing, and her clone sitting up a few feet away, keeping watch with her own mind.
  3. She tells him it is the safest she has felt in months. He tells her to sleep.
- **By the end:** she has slept on the other side.

### Scene 4: Her fight (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mid-tier on the other side, rested and at home. She fights it alone, with Jason ready but not in it.
  2. **She wins with something only she has:** a small chain of clones, each placed by feel. Some of them make more, behind it, where it isn't looking. It can't track that many of her at once. It isn't elegant. It is hers.
  3. Afterward she throws up from adrenaline.
- **By the end:** she has won with the technique he can't do, and he watched her do it.

### Scene 5: Saying it (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Back in Vermont, she is washing off the other side at the outdoor tap. He brings her a towel and stays.
  2. Delilah told her to make him say it again to his face when he isn't shouting. Mira decides she will go first.
  3. She tells him she loves him, plainly. He kisses her.
  4. Afterward he tells her something he has never said. In February, when she said no to the erasure, he told himself he didn't do it because she had said no.
  5. Then he tells her the real reason.
- **End:** one image: the tap still running.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her alive above everything, and finally to stop pretending he can manage how he feels.
- **Sees himself in her** when she decides alone. It is the turn of his arc: he starts asking.
- **Confesses** why he didn't erase her.

## Romance beat
The kiss, and the confession. They are a couple from here on.

## Old-life beat
None this chapter.

## Rules in play
- Stage 4. A burst of about a thousand, in mid-August. Reabsorbing at scale knocks her out for hours.
- She can only make new bodies within about a mile of an existing one.
- Reabsorbed bodies fold into whichever body she is closest to. Which body that is changes and doesn't matter to her.
- Shields held in sleep, with her own clone on watch.
- No main body.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "It was because I couldn't stand you forgetting me." Jason says it to Mira at the tap, after she tells him she loves him, in answer to why he didn't erase her in February.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Analogies: one per scene.
- Photography images: none this chapter.
- Body reactions: one per scene.

## Do not
- Make the thousand easy, or the reabsorbing painless.
- Write the kiss scene as a big speech.
- Let her narration explain his confession.
