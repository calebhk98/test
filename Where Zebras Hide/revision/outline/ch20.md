# Chapter 20: Evolution

**When and where:** Mid-August. A field near the Vermont base on a Tuesday afternoon, then the other side over the following two weeks.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira can make clones that make clones. Her best so far is a chain five deep, and reabsorbing it knocked her flat.
- Kael thinks she's in the top hundred of a few thousand practitioners.
- They have crossed many times but have never slept on the other side; her shields have to hold while she sleeps.
- She and Jason are clearly in love and have never said it again since Chapter 3. Kael laughed at them for it.
- The battery argument is quiet, but it isn't gone.
- She has decided she needs far more of herself.

## What this chapter does

She finds out how big she can get, and what it costs. She makes a decision alone, the way Jason does, and they have it out. Then they finally say it.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: A thousand (about 1,000 words)
- **Mira wants** to know her limit before anything forces her to find it.
- **Beats:**
  1. First, ten days of practice since Kael's: chains of eight, then twenty, then sixty, each one hurting a little less to pull back, because she's learning to pull back in pieces instead of all at once. Two or three sentences, as specific days.
  2. Then a Tuesday afternoon. She's in a field near the base while Jason is in town for supplies. She didn't tell him.
  3. Clones make clones that make clones, and she keeps going: dozens, hundreds, about a thousand of her standing in the grass.
  4. **What it's like:**
     - She is everywhere in the field at once. She feels the wind on a thousand faces.
     - It's not crowded. It's huge.
     - One of her is crying and the rest find that interesting.
     - A photographer's thought from one of them: the field looks like an overexposed print of one woman, repeated.
  5. Then she pulls them back. It's like swallowing the field. The headache becomes a sound.
  6. She wakes up in the grass hours later in the dark, with no memory of falling.

### Scene 2: The fight (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** him to understand she had to know.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason found her unconscious in the field when he got back. He's past angry: he's scared in a way she's never seen.
  2. "You decided that alone. You didn't tell me." She hears herself about to defend it, and realizes it's his line.
  3. **They both see it at the same moment.** She's done to him exactly what he keeps doing to her.
  4. She apologizes properly. He laughs at the irony, then stops laughing and sits down in the grass next to her and doesn't let go of her hand.
  5. **They agree on something real:** neither of them decides about the other alone again. She's not sure either of them can keep it.

### Scene 3: Sleeping over there (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Over the next two weeks she practices holding her shields while she sleeps, until they hold on their own without her. As a backup, one of her stays awake anyway. She is her own night watch.
  2. Their first night on the other side: total dark, moss, his breathing, and her clone sitting up a few feet away, keeping watch with her own mind.
  3. She tells him it's the safest she's felt in months. He says that's the most worrying thing she's ever said.

### Scene 4: Her fight (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mid-tier on the other side, rested and at home. She fights it alone, with Jason ready but not in it.
  2. **She wins with something only she has:**
     - A small chain of clones, each placed by feel. Some of them make more, behind it, where it isn't looking.
     - It can't track that many of her at once.
     - It isn't elegant. It's hers.
  3. Afterward she throws up from adrenaline. Keep this beat.
  4. Don't call this her first fight alone, because it isn't.

### Scene 5: Saying it (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Back in Vermont, she's washing off the other side at the outdoor tap. He brings her a towel and doesn't leave.
  2. Delilah told her to make him say it again to his face when he isn't shouting. She decides she'll go first.
  3. She tells him she loves him, plainly. He kisses her, or she kisses him. Let it be a little clumsy and real.
  4. Afterward, the thing he's never said: "In the woods. I could have erased you. I told myself it was because you said no." Beat. "It was because I couldn't stand you forgetting me."
  5. She takes that in. Don't let her narration explain it.
- **End:** an image: the towel, the tap still running, the last light on the field. Don't end on a sentence about what they've become.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her alive above everything, and finally to stop pretending he can manage how he feels.
- **Sees himself in her** when she decides alone. It's the turn of his arc: he starts asking.
- **Confesses** why he didn't erase her.

## Romance beat
The kiss, and "I couldn't stand you forgetting me." They are a couple from here on.

## Old-life beat
Mom texts a photo from a family barbecue she missed, captioned "Everyone asked about you!" Mira saves it.

## Rules in play

- Shields held in sleep, with her own clone on watch.
- No main body. She notices she woke up in a different body from the one she fell asleep in, and that it doesn't matter.

## Limits for this chapter

- "Neither of them looks away": 0.
- "Barely, messily, with none of Jason's elegance": 0. Describe this fight's specific shape.

## Do not
- Make the thousand easy, or the reabsorbing painless.
- Have Jason watch her collapse without going to her.
- Write the kiss scene as a big speech.
