# Chapter 9: First Contact

**When and where:** About April 12, at night. The woods below the cabin, near a creek.
**Length:** about 2,800 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Two months in. Mira can cycle without thinking and shield a little. She can make a handful of clones (puppets with a little independence), and fixes their small flaws because they bug her.
- Since the hand scene, she and Jason are close but not together. They touch easily and haven't named anything.
- She knows things cross for meals. She has never seen one.
- She told Mom she quit and is traveling with Jason.
- Jason promised in Chapter 5 to tell her when he decides things about her.

## What this chapter does

The danger becomes real and specific. It's small, it fells a tree, and Jason kills it like it's nothing. Then she learns he has been doing this without telling her.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The slip (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Nora calls. Nora is upset that Mira skipped her birthday dinner in Chicago, and keeps asking where she really is. Mira lies. Nora says, "You sound like someone reading a script." They hang up badly.
  2. Mira goes out on the porch and cries, and for a minute she stops cycling without noticing. Her shield loosens.
- **Detail to use:** spring peepers loud in the creek.

### Scene 2: It comes (about 1,000 words)
- **Mira wants** to see it, then to be anywhere else.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason comes out fast. "Don't move. Feel left, down by the creek." She feels it before she sees it: something small and bright to her mana sense, coming up the slope.
  2. It isn't coming for her. There's a patch of brambles by the creek that's been faintly producing mana on its own, and it's eating that. Then it notices her.
  3. **Show it through her photographer's eye:**
     - Cat-to-fox-sized, long, eyeless, translucent; she can see soft gray shapes inside it.
     - A fan of fine whiskers around a lipless mouth, turning toward her like a satellite dish.
     - Light from the porch lands on it wrong: no highlight where a living thing should shine, and a shadow that doesn't match its outline.
     - Her eye keeps trying to fix the picture and can't.
  4. Jason keeps it busy, a little wall of something it bumps against, and makes it a lesson: "Feel how it's different from the brambles. Different texture, right? That's what alive feels like."
  5. She is furious he's teaching right now, and she is also learning, and she hates both.
  6. It panics and swipes at the air, and a young tree splits and comes down across the path. It's the size of a cat.
  7. She takes a photo on her phone without thinking. It shows up clearly: pale, eyeless, wrong.

### Scene 3: The kill (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason kills it, fast and almost casual, the way someone swats a wasp.
  2. It drops. Frost spreads on the leaves where it lies, because it loses heat into the air so fast.
  3. She has to sit down, right on the porch steps. Her hands won't work.
  4. She asks a practical question because her brain grabs for one: "Do we bury it?" They do. It weighs almost nothing, like a paper lantern.

### Scene 4: The other thing he didn't say (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if that's what's coming for her.
- **Beats:**
  1. "Is that what would come for me?"
  2. "That? That came for a weed. It noticed you because you went loud for a minute. The ones that come for people are bigger, and later, when you're louder."
  3. She asks how he knew where to look so fast. Long pause. It's the fourth one this month. He's been feeling them cross and killing them before they got near the cabin, while she slept.
  4. She explodes: "You said you'd tell me when you were deciding things about me." He says he didn't want her scared while she was still learning to cycle. She says it wasn't his call.
  5. He apologizes badly, then well.
  6. She doesn't forgive him yet, and says so, and stays sitting next to him anyway.
- **End:** the two of them on the steps, the brambles down by the creek, and her phone with the photo in her lap. Don't explain the feeling.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her safe, and her not scared, in that order. That order is the problem.
- **Flaw, on display:** deciding alone and telling her later. This is the first time she names it.
- **Not cold:** after the kill his hands are shaking too, a little. She notices.

## Romance beat
She stays sitting next to him while angry. He takes his jacket off and puts it around her without asking, and she lets him.

## Old-life beat
Nora's call, and "reading a script".

## Rules in play
- Any plant or animal can produce mana naturally, so the brambles draw strays.
- Things on camera show up clearly.
- They lose heat fast on Earth.
- Small things notice by scent.

## Plant for later
- The photo on her phone.
- Jason's pattern, named: "You said you'd tell me."

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "Is that what would come for me?"

## Limits for this chapter
- One body reaction per scene.
- "She has to sit down afterward" as the whole reaction: 0. Give her the practical question and the shaking hands.
- Jason "almost casually": once.
- Similes for the creature: at most 2. Use specific visual details instead.
- Triplets: at most 1.

## Do not
- Make the creature evil. It was eating, then it panicked.
- Have Jason lecture after the kill.
- Name the creature type in dialogue. If Mira names it in her head, it's her own nickname.
