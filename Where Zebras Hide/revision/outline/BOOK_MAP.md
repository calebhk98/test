# Where Zebras Hide: book map

Every drafter gets this file, plus RULES.md, CHARACTERS.md, CREATURES.md, STYLE.md and the outline for their own chapter. You won't see the other chapters, so this map tells you where yours sits.

**Do not foreshadow anything beyond your chapter** except the setups your outline lists. Write as if Mira doesn't know what comes next, because she doesn't.

## The book in one paragraph

Mira, a 26-year-old office analyst in Columbus, has been in love with her friend Jason for two years of Tuesday coffees. Then people attack him on the street, and he teleports them both to Tennessee. Jason can do things that shouldn't be possible, and the ability spreads through understanding: once your brain truly understands how mana works, your body starts producing it. Producing it makes you food for creatures on the other side, an overlapping dimension with no light, where everything runs on mana. Most people who start die within months. Mira chooses to learn anyway, to stay with him. She trains, fights, crosses to the other side, and becomes extraordinary at one thing: making clones of herself that are all her. Then the secret breaks. The world starts producing mana, Earth becomes worth the trip for things that never bothered before, and Mira ends the book as a colony of eight million bodies, saving what she can and unable to hear the man shouting her name.

Point of view: first person, past tense, Mira, throughout.

## Chapters

**Act I: The Infection (February)**
1. **Normal.** A Friday at the office and an extra coffee with Jason. Leaving the coffee shop, they're attacked, and he teleports them.
2. **Displaced.** Woods in Tennessee, a clock an hour off, a phone full of lies to send. Jason asks for twenty minutes.
3. **The Ultimatum.** What he is, why he has to leave, an accidental "I love you", the offer to erase her memory, four bad options. She asks for time.
4. **The Walk.** Hours of walking to a town while he tells her the safe parts: history, the community. A motel with two beds. Lying to her family.
5. **The Choice.** She chooses in the morning. He explains. It starts: a warmth like a fever. They can't go home.
6. **Orientation.** A cash car, headaches, the first sensing lesson, the zebra conversation, and the real story of Jason's childhood. They arrive at the cabin.
7. **Day One.** Learning to cycle, and failing at it, then a flicker, then a partial cycle.

**Act II: The Hunt (February to June)**
8. **Routine.** Weeks at the cabin. Her first clones. The hand scene.
9. **First Contact.** The first creature: small, and it fells a tree.
10. **The Box.** Jason's prison-and-training trick. Their first real moral fight. Her clones become one mind.
11. **Visitors.** Delilah arrives and offers to teach. "Cloning isn't copying."
12. **Progress.** Three months in. Delilah teaches erasure. Clones at full strength. "Why can't they?"
13. **The Hunters.** Hunters watch the cabin. Two creatures attack at dusk. At dawn, a three-jump escape that ends in Spain.
14. **Why He Won't Share.** In Spain he tells the hunters' story and refuses anyway. The ownership argument.
15. **Departure.** Vermont. Jason asks her to cross to the other side. She says no, then yes. A phone call to her sister. They cross.

**Act III: The Other Side (late June to September)**
16. **Arrival.** No light anywhere. She makes some. Her first fight there.
17. **The Ecosystem.** Learning the other side. Hiding from something vast. What really powers their crossings. Kael watches her shields.
18. **The Scout.** A talking creature crosses into Vermont. Jason traps it and collapses. Mira guards him alone.
19. **Contact.** Kael properly. Her clones make clones for the first time, and it floors her.
20. **Evolution.** A thousand of her in a field on a Tuesday. Sleeping on the other side. They become a couple.
21. **Return.** Labor Day in Columbus: the apartment, the undeveloped film, dinner with her parents, Marcus. She realizes she can't come back.

**Act IV: The Collapse (September to November)**
22. **Cracks.** A meeting of the powerful in a mansion. Factions. Owen, the hunter, in person.
23. **Escalation.** Marcus has been asking questions because of her. A London anchor reads a leak aloud. Mira erases her friend.
24. **Containment.** A campus, sixteen erasures, and Priya, who is too late to erase.
25. **Breach.** A celebrity livestream. The internet works out the rest. Day 0 of the end.
26. **Cascade.** Jason gives away everything, still believing it was his. Mira's clones start teaching the world.
27. **Incursion.** Something the size of a hill walks out of the other side in Rajasthan.
28. **Mobilization.** The world fights back badly. Mira is a hundred and ten people at once.
29. **The Hundred.** Nine days without real rest. Theo. Delilah. The ten hours, and what they cost.
30. **Threshold.** The scout's master comes looking for its scout. Two hundred, forty thousand, eight million. Then dark.
