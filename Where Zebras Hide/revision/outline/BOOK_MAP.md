# Where Zebras Hide: book map

Every drafter gets the acts up to and including their own, plus the "What follows" line of their own act. You won't see the other chapters, so this map tells you where yours sits. Write as if Mira doesn't know what comes next, because she doesn't.

## The book in one paragraph

Mira, a 26-year-old office analyst in Columbus, has been in love with her friend Jason for two years of Tuesday coffees. Then people attack him on the street, and he teleports them both to Tennessee. Jason can do things that shouldn't be possible, and the ability spreads through understanding: once your brain truly understands how mana works, your body starts producing it. Producing it makes you food for creatures on the other side, an overlapping dimension with no light, where everything runs on mana. Most people who start die within months. Mira chooses to learn anyway. She trains, fights, crosses to the other side, and becomes extraordinary at one thing: making clones of herself that are all her. Then the secret breaks, and Mira is pulled into something far bigger than the two of them.

Point of view: first person, past tense, Mira, throughout.

## Act I: The Infection (February)

1. **Normal.** A Friday at the office and an extra coffee with Jason. Leaving the coffee shop, they're attacked, and he teleports them.
2. **Displaced.** Woods in Tennessee, a phone full of lies to send. Jason asks for twenty minutes.
3. **The Ultimatum.** What he is, why he has to leave, an accidental "I love you", the offer to erase her memory, the options. She asks for time.
4. **The Walk.** Hours of walking to a town while he tells her the safe parts: history, the community. A motel with two beds. Lying to her family.
5. **The Choice.** She chooses in the morning. He explains. It starts: a warmth like a fever. They can't go home.
6. **Orientation.** Two days on back roads, headaches, the first sensing lesson, the zebra conversation, and the real story of Jason's childhood. They arrive at the cabin.
7. **Day One.** Learning to cycle, and failing at it, then a flicker, then a partial cycle.

**What follows:** Act II is the months of training at the cabin, and the people who come looking.

## Act II: The Hunt (February to June)

8. **Routine.** Weeks at the cabin. Her first clones. The hand scene.
9. **First Contact.** The first creature: small, and it fells a tree.
10. **The Box.** Jason's prison-and-training trick. Their first real moral fight. Her clones become one mind.
11. **Visitors.** Delilah arrives and offers to teach. One loose remark sets Mira thinking about her clones.
12. **Progress.** Three months in. Mira's clones at full strength, and Jason jealous. Delilah teaches erasure. The history of the witch trials. People are camped a mile out.
13. **The Hunters.** Hunters watch the cabin. Two creatures attack on the second evening. At dawn, a three-jump escape that ends in Spain.
14. **Why He Won't Share.** In Spain he tells the hunters' story and refuses anyway. The ownership argument.
15. **Departure.** A jump to a Quebec field, the border on foot, then Vermont. Jason asks her to cross to the other side. She says no, then yes. A phone call to her sister. They cross.

**What follows:** Act III is the other side itself, and what it teaches her.

## Act III: The Other Side (late June to September)

16. **Arrival.** No light anywhere. She makes some. Her first fight there.
17. **The Ecosystem.** Learning the other side. Hiding from something vast. What really powers their crossings. Kael watches her shields.
18. **The Scout.** A talking creature crosses into Vermont. Jason traps it and collapses. Mira guards him alone.
19. **Contact.** Kael properly. Her clones make clones for the first time, and it floors her.
20. **Evolution.** A thousand of her in a field in mid-August. Sleeping on the other side. They become a couple.
21. **Return.** Labor Day in Columbus: the apartment, the undeveloped film, dinner with her parents, Marcus. She realizes she put someone at risk and can't come back.

**What follows:** Act IV is the secret getting out.

## Act IV: The Collapse (September to November)

22. **Cracks.** A meeting of the powerful at a lake house. Factions. Owen, the hunter, in person.
23. **Escalation.** Marcus has been asking questions because of her. A London anchor reads a leak aloud. Mira erases her friend.
24. **Containment.** A campus, sixteen erasures, and Priya, who is too late to erase.
25. **Breach.** A celebrity livestream. The internet works out the rest. Day 0 of the end.
26. **Cascade.** Jason gives away everything, still believing it was his. Mira's clones start teaching the world.
27. **Incursion.** Something the size of a hill walks out of the other side in Rajasthan.
28. **Mobilization.** The world fights back badly. Mira is about a hundred and ten people at once.
29. **The Hundred.** Nine days without real rest. Theo. Delilah. The ten hours, and what they cost.
30. **Threshold.** The scout's master comes looking for its scout. Two hundred bait bodies, then the chains run. Mira ends as a colony of about eight million, saving what she can and unable to hear the man shouting her name. (For Ch 28 to 30 drafters only.)

**What follows:** this is the end of the book.
