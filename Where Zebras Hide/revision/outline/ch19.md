# Chapter 19: Contact

**When and where:** Early August. The other side, at Kael's place, over two or three visits.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- A talking creature crossed into Vermont, sent by something above it to see what kept crossing. Jason locked it down and boxed it. Mira kept its attention and warned him before it sent a message home. Then he collapsed, and she guarded him for thirty hours.
- It said they will want to know where it went. Mira knows the intelligent ones have masters and souls.
- She holds three clones, sometimes four, and has decided that isn't enough. She never wants to be alone with his body in the dark again.
- They know Kael: delighted, rude, carries small creatures on him, calls mana ether and the other side the deep. He followed them for two crossings, and he invited them.
- The hunters can find them on Earth by ordinary means, given time, so the other side is where they are safest. They found the cabin through Delilah's gossip.

## What this chapter does

- Kael's way of living.
- An outsider's view of Jason, and of how close the secret is to breaking.
- Mira's first attempt at clones making clones, which floors her.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Kael's place (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Kael lives in a hollow ringed by plants that don't press on shields there. He has worked something out with them.
  2. Creatures come and go from him like birds to a feeder.
  3. He seems happy here, which disturbs her most, because everyone else she knows is surviving.
  4. He believes humans could live alongside the deep, maybe even bargain with what is in it. Jason thinks that is how you get eaten. They argue like old friends who have never met.
- **Detail to use:** a small creature settles on Mira's shoulder. It is cool and weightless and she freezes. Kael tells her it likes her and not to make a thing of it.
- **By the end:** she has seen a way of living on this side that she hadn't imagined.

### Scene 2: What Kael sees (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. **A new angle on Jason.** Kael respects his work more than anyone's alive, and pities him. He says it to Jason's face. Jason has no answer.
  2. Kael looks at Mira's mana and guesses she is already in the top hundred, out of the few thousand of them, after six months. Mira is shocked. Jason is not.
  3. She asks how he can know. He says it is the same way you'd know someone was strong if they lifted a truck, and that there is no list because there aren't enough of them to need one.
  4. Kael asks what she did before. She says she made pivot tables, and has to explain what those are to a man who lives in the dark.
  5. Kael asks how long the two of them have been ridiculous about each other. Each answers separately and badly.
- **By the end:** Jason has been seen clearly by someone else, in front of her.

### Scene 3: The walls (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Kael has news from Earth-side friends.
  2. **One concrete near-miss.** This spring a woman in Manchester worked it out from a forum thread and posted a video. People caught it fast and erased forty viewers, but one had already started, and nobody reached him in time.
  3. Kael tells Jason the walls are thinning, and asks how many videos it will take.
  4. **Jason agrees with Kael and doesn't know what to do about it.** He has known for a while. His whole protection has been that people don't believe, and he can see that running out. He asks what Kael wants him to do.
  5. Mira expected him to say people would laugh it off, and he doesn't. Mira connects it to what Delilah told her about the witch trials in one line. Nobody in the hollow has a plan, Kael included.
- **By the end:** all three of them know the secret is closer to breaking than any of them wanted, and nobody has a move.

### Scene 4: Clones making clones (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** never to be alone with his body in the dark again.
- **Beats:**
  1. At Kael's, where it is safe, she tries it for the first time. One of her makes another one of her.
  2. The new body powers itself and is her. The load spreads into its brain too.
  3. She gets a chain three generations deep before it collapses back into her. The pain of pulling it in drops her to the ground.
  4. **Jason goes to her at once.** Kael watches, fascinated.
  5. She tries again on the next visit and gets five generations, and collapses again.
  6. Jason says he understands exactly how it works and couldn't do it. His mind doesn't hold that much fine detail at once. He is proud and a little frightened.
- **By the end:** she can do something Jason can't, and she knows it will cost her every time.

### End (about 200 words)
Back in Vermont, she sleeps for fourteen hours. She wakes in the kitchen, in a body that had been standing at the counter for an hour. The kettle was on. She had no memory of putting it on, and it did not feel strange.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to take Kael's warning seriously, and he does. He has no answer.
- **Caring:** goes to her at once when she collapses.

## Romance beat

## Old-life beat
Kael asks what she did before, and she has to explain pivot tables to a man who lives in the dark.

## Rules in play
- Top hundred is an estimate from visible strength, in a community of a few thousand.
- Footage can teach some viewers.
- Once someone has started, erasure can take memories but can't undo the understanding.
- Stage 4: clones make clones, self-powered and still her. Reabsorbing hurts. There is no main body.
- Jason can't do it.
- Jason won't explain mechanics to someone who hasn't started, because partial understanding can finish itself.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "You built the best house in the world and you live in it alone." Kael says it to Jason, about the Box and the lockdown and everything else he has built, after he tells Jason he respects his work.
- "The walls are thinning, Jason. Not the ones between us and the deep. The ones between people. How many videos do you think it takes?" Kael says it to Jason after telling the Manchester story.
- "I know. I've known for a while. What do you want me to do, take down the internet?" Jason answers Kael with it.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Analogies: one per scene.
- Witch-trial history: one line from Mira, no exposition.
- No labels for Jason's genius.

## Do not
- Take her past five generations of clones.
- Have Kael explain the deep's history.
- Give anyone a plan for the leaks.
