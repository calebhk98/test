# Chapter 19: Contact

**When and where:** Early August. The other side, at Kael's place, over two or three visits.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- A talking creature crossed into Vermont, sent by something above it to see what kept crossing. Jason locked it down and boxed it. Mira kept its attention and warned him before it sent a message home. Then he collapsed, and she guarded him for thirty hours.
- It said "They will want to know where I went." Mira knows the intelligent ones have masters and souls.
- She has decided three or four of her isn't enough.
- They know Kael: delighted, rude, carries small creatures on him, calls mana "ether" and the other side "the deep". He invited them.

## What this chapter does

- Kael's way of living.
- An outsider's view of Jason, and of how close the secret is to breaking.
- Mira's first attempt at clones making clones, which floors her.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Kael's place (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Kael lives in a hollow ringed by plants that don't press on shields there. He has worked something out with them.
  2. Creatures come and go from him like birds to a feeder.
  3. He's happy here, which is what disturbs her most. Everyone else she knows is surviving. Kael is living.
  4. He believes humans could live alongside the deep, maybe even bargain with what's in it. Jason thinks that's how you get eaten. They argue like old friends who've never met.
- **Detail to use:** a small creature settles on Mira's shoulder. It's warm and weightless and she freezes. Kael says, "She likes you. Don't be weird about it."

### Scene 2: What Kael sees (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. **A new angle on Jason.** Kael respects his work more than anyone's alive, and pities him: "You built the best house in the world and you live in it alone." He says it to Jason's face. Jason doesn't have an answer.
  2. Kael looks at Mira's mana, the way someone sizes up a person who lifts heavy weights, and guesses: "Top hundred, I'd say. Out of the few thousand of us. In six months." Mira is shocked. Jason is not.
  3. She asks how he can know. "Same way you'd know someone was strong if they lifted a truck. There's no list. There aren't enough of us to need one."

### Scene 3: The walls (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Kael has news from Earth-side friends.
  2. **One concrete near-miss.** Last spring a woman in Manchester worked it out from a forum thread and posted a video. People caught it fast and erased forty viewers, but one had already started, and nobody reached him in time.
  3. "The walls are thinning. Not the dimensional walls. The informational ones. How many viral videos are you from catastrophe?"
  4. **Jason is complacent:** the science shield has held for centuries, and people laugh at magic videos. Kael: "People laughed at a lot of things right up until."
  5. Mira takes Kael's side, which surprises Jason.
  6. Don't re-explain the witch trials; Delilah told her. Mira can connect it herself in one line.

### Scene 4: Clones making clones (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** more of herself.
- **Beats:**
  1. At Kael's, where it's safe, she tries it for the first time. One of her makes another one of her.
  2. The new body powers itself and is her. The load spreads into its brain too.
  3. She gets a chain three deep before it collapses back into her. The pain of pulling it all in drops her to the ground.
  4. **Jason goes to her at once.** He doesn't watch or wait. Kael watches, fascinated.
  5. She tries again the next visit and gets five deep, and collapses again.
  6. Jason says he understands exactly how it works and couldn't do it: "It's like you're threading a needle with a thousand threads at once. My hands aren't built for that." He's proud and a little frightened.

### End (about 200 words)
Back in Vermont, she sleeps for fourteen hours. When she wakes, a clone she forgot to pull back is making coffee, badly, in the kitchen. She and it look at each other. She laughs.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to take Kael's warning seriously, and can't quite. The science shield is the only thing that has ever kept him safe.
- **Wrong:** about the walls. He'll find out.
- **Caring:** goes to her at once when she collapses.

## Romance beat
Kael: "So how long have you two been ridiculous about each other?" Both of them start to deny it at the same time and stop.

## Old-life beat
Kael asks what she did before. She says "pivot tables", and has to explain what those are to a man who lives in the dark. It's funny, and it's a little sad.

## Rules in play
- "Top 100" is an estimate from visible strength, in a community of a few thousand.
- Footage can teach some viewers.
- Erasure fails once someone has started.
- Stage 4: clones make clones, self-powered and still her. Reabsorbing hurts.
- Jason can't do it.
