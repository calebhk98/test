# Chapter 22: Cracks

**When and where:** Late September. A lake house in the Adirondacks owned by a wealthy practitioner. One evening and the next morning.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira and Jason are a couple. They agreed that neither decides about the other alone.
- She can burst to about a thousand clones, and that knocks her out. Day to day she holds a few dozen.
- She went home for Labor Day and realized she can't come back, because she's a danger to the people she loves. Her reason for all of this has shifted from Jason to them.
- In Columbus, Marcus saw her warm a cold coffee in her hands. She called it an insulated cup.
- Kael warned that the informational walls are thinning. Jason thinks the science shield will hold.
- She has met Owen only as a name: the hunter who talks, who lost a student, who wants Jason's teleport to save newcomers.
- There's no organization and no list. She is guessed to be top hundred of a few thousand.
- Delilah teaches erasure as a normal tool.

## What this chapter does

The community tries to act together for the first time and fails. Mira sees Jason among his own kind: still an outsider. She meets Owen in person. Someone warns her about Jason, and she has to say why she's with him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Why there's a meeting (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mana message from Delilah. A video from São Paulo has been going around for three weeks.
  2. **The video:** a man at a bus stop knocks a glass bottle off the bench, and it stops a few inches above the pavement for half a second before it falls and breaks. He looks as shocked as anyone. Nothing flashy, nothing to point at, just wrong. Debunkers keep failing to explain it.
  3. People are scared. There's going to be a meeting of the strong ones, and for the first time anyone remembers, Jason is invited.
  4. They learn this by mana message, and Jason has to reply to six of them. He hates it.

### Scene 2: The house (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A huge lake house. Being good at mana can make you rich, quietly.
  2. About two dozen people, every one of them loud to her sense, all holding it in. The room hums.
  3. **Social texture, through Mira's eyes:**
     - People stare at her; the top-hundred gossip got here first.
     - People don't meet Jason's eyes.
     - The host is effusive with Jason in a way that's really fear.
     - Mira realizes everyone here has a word for mana and nobody's matches.
  4. Delilah is there. She hugs Mira and doesn't let go for a while.
  5. Mira meets **Anjali** *(proposed name)*, a quiet practitioner from Jaipur who makes jewelry. She's the one person who talks to Mira about something other than mana, which is earrings, and gives her a mana-message contact "in case".

### Scene 3: Factions (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Named speakers with real proposals:
     - **Owen (go public on our terms):** teach the basics, cycling and shielding, quietly to anyone at risk, before it's forced on us.
     - **Delilah (suppress harder):** erase faster and wider, take down every video. "We've done it for four hundred years."
     - **The host, Gerald Whitcombe** *(proposed name)* **(hide):** wait it out. It's one video. It passes.
  2. They argue. Nobody can give orders, so nothing gets decided.
  3. Mira mostly agrees with Owen, and says so out loud, once. Delilah looks hurt.
  4. Jason is out of place and doesn't hide it: polite, guarded, standing near the wall. When someone finally asks him something, he answers in two sentences and says no more. When asked, he says people will laugh it off like they always have, and Owen laughs at him.

### Scene 4: Owen (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if he's the man who tried to kill them.
- **Beats:**
  1. On the dock at night, Owen finds her. He's tired and decent and nervous.
  2. He saw her fight at the cabin. He respects it.
  3. He tells her about his student himself: a girl of fifteen, two months in, a crossing he could have outrun with Jason's teleport.
  4. He doesn't apologize for the attacks. He says he'd do it again for that girl. Mira hates that she understands.
  5. **The warning, from Owen:** "He'll burn the world down to keep you safe, and he won't ask you first."
  6. **Mira answers why she's with Jason**, in her own words and specifically. For example: he remembered her coffee order for two years and never once asked her for anything; he shaped a teleport so she wouldn't drown, and didn't mention it; he's lonely and funny and wrong about things, and he's trying.
  7. She also says: "He's asking first now." Owen says, "For you, maybe."

### Scene 5: Jason and Owen (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason sees them on the dock and comes down too fast. Plant his temper once: something cold in his voice to Owen.
  2. Mira puts a hand on his arm, and he stops.
  3. He's embarrassed afterward. She's a little scared of how fast he moved.

### End (about 200 words)
Driving home, no consensus. Delilah texts Mira: "I love you, honey, but you're wrong." Mira texts back: "Maybe." End there.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to be anywhere else. He doesn't belong among these people and never has.
- **His temper:** shows once, at Owen. This is what the "burn the world down" warning rests on, together with the cold flash at the cabin in Ch 13.
- **Complacent:** people will laugh it off.
