# Chapter 22: Cracks

**When and where:** Late September. A lake house in the Adirondacks, lent for the weekend by its owner. One evening and the next morning.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira and Jason are a couple. They agreed that neither decides about the other alone.
- She can burst to about a thousand clones, and that knocks her out. Day to day she holds a few dozen.
- She went home for Labor Day and realized she can't come back, because she is a danger to the people she loves. Her reason for all of this has shifted from Jason to them.
- In a Columbus grocery store, Marcus saw her warm a cold coffee in her hands. She called it an insulated cup.
- Kael warned that the walls between people are thinning. Jason agrees with Kael and doesn't know what to do about it.
- The hunters found the cabin because Delilah mentioned Jason's girl to someone she trusted and it travelled. Delilah has said so.
- She has met Owen only as a name: the hunter who talks, who lost a student, who wants Jason's teleport to save newcomers.
- There is no organization and no list. She is guessed to be top hundred of a few thousand.
- Delilah teaches erasure as a normal tool.

## What this chapter does

The community tries to act together for the first time and fails. Mira sees Jason among his own kind: still an outsider. She meets Owen in person. Someone warns her about Jason, and she has to say why she is with him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Why there's a meeting (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A mana message from Delilah. Three videos have gone around in a month. The best known is from São Paulo.
  2. **The bottle:** a man at a bus stop knocks a glass bottle off the bench, and it stops a few inches above the pavement for half a second before it falls and breaks. He looks as shocked as anyone. Nothing flashy, nothing to point at, just wrong. Debunkers keep failing to explain it.
  3. People are scared. There is going to be a meeting of the strong ones, and for the first time anyone remembers, Jason is invited. Delilah has the host promise, in front of everyone invited, that nobody will be touched in his house, and Jason agrees to go only because of it.
  4. Jason has to reply to six mana messages. He hates it.
  5. When she switches her phone on in a town, a text from Marcus is waiting. He asks where she got that cup, because he wants one. She reads it, and the chapter doesn't mention it again.
- **By the end:** they are going, and neither of them wants to.

### Scene 2: The house (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A huge lake house. The host owns it and has lent it for the weekend.
  2. About two dozen people, every one of them loud to her sense, all holding it in. The room hums.
  3. **Social texture, through Mira's eyes.** Show two: people stare at her because the top-hundred talk got here first; people don't meet Jason's eyes; the host is effusive with Jason in a way that is really fear.
  4. Everyone here has a word for mana and nobody's matches. Let it show in dialogue.
  5. Delilah is there. She hugs Mira and doesn't let go for a while.
  6. Mira meets **Anjali**, a quiet practitioner from Jaipur. She is the one person who talks to Mira about something other than mana: where she is from, and how she found her way. She gives Mira a mana-message contact in case.
- **By the end:** Mira knows who is in the room and that she is being watched.

### Scene 3: Factions (about 900 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Named speakers with real proposals:
     - **Owen (go public on our terms):** teach the basics, cycling and shielding, quietly to anyone at risk, before it is forced on them.
     - **Delilah (suppress harder):** erase faster and wider, take down every video. They have done it for four hundred years.
     - **The host, Gerald Whitcombe (hide):** wait it out. It's one video. It passes.
  2. They argue. Nobody can give orders, so nothing gets decided.
  3. Mira mostly agrees with Owen, and says so out loud, once. Delilah puts her cup down and looks at the table.
  4. Jason is out of place and doesn't hide it: polite, guarded, standing near the wall. When someone finally asks him something, he answers in two sentences. He says he agrees the walls are thinning and he doesn't have an answer. Owen tells him that isn't one, and Jason agrees it isn't.
- **By the end:** three plans, no decision, and Mira and Delilah on opposite sides.

### Scene 4: The dock (about 1,100 words)
- **Mira wants** to know whether Owen would do it again.
- **Beats:**
  1. On the dock at night, Owen finds her. He is tired and decent and nervous.
  2. He was at the cabin and watched her fight. He respects it.
  3. He tells her about his student himself: a girl of fifteen, two months in, a crossing he could have outrun with Jason's teleport.
  4. He doesn't apologize for the attacks. He says he would do it again for that girl. Mira hates that she understands. He also tells her the other two hunters stopped looking after Spain and he has not heard from them since.
  5. **The warning.** Owen tells her what he thinks Jason will do.
  6. **Mira answers why she is with Jason**, in her own words and specifically. Show two specifics, and don't mention the teleport. Mira says he asks first now. Owen says that is true for her.
  7. Jason sees them on the dock and comes down too fast, with something cold in his voice for Owen. Mira puts a hand on his arm, and he stops.
  8. He is embarrassed afterward. She is a little scared of how fast he moved.
- **By the end:** she has defended him and seen him prove Owen's point, and she knows both.

### End (about 200 words)
Driving home, no consensus. Delilah sends a mana message. Mira sends one back. End there.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to be anywhere else. He doesn't belong among these people and never has.
- **His temper:** shows once, at Owen.
- **Agrees with Kael** about the walls, and has nothing to offer the room.

## Romance beat
Her answer to Owen about why she is with Jason. Her hand on his arm, and him stopping.

## Old-life beat
Marcus's text, once, early.

## Rules in play
- No organization. Nobody can give orders.
- Strength is visible, so people stare.
- Footage can teach.
- The mana message.
- Words vary.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "He'll burn the world down to keep you safe, and he won't ask you first." Owen says it to Mira on the dock, after she asks whether he would do it again, as his reason to warn her.
- "I love you, honey, but you're wrong." Delilah sends it as a mana message to Mira as they drive home, in answer to Mira siding with Owen.
- "Maybe." Mira sends it back.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Analogies: one per scene.
- "Two years": at most 1.

## Do not
- Have the meeting decide anything.
- Make any faction stupid. All three have a case.
- Resolve Marcus.
