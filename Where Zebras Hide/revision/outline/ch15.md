# Chapter 15: Departure

**When and where:** Late June. Random jumps home from Spain (a pasture in Wales, a beach in Chile, a field in Quebec), then a property in rural Vermont that becomes their base. The crossing happens about June 28.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four and a half months in. She can cycle, shield, sense crossings and erase memories (practiced on her own clone), and holds three full-strength clones, sometimes four, all her.
- She won a fight against a mid-tier using clones from three angles.
- She knows Jason's position on sharing, and Owen's reason, and has said she thinks Jason is wrong about Owen.
- She knows the other side exists. Jason has told her it will be completely dark, and nothing more.
- Delilah is safe at home. She knows nothing about the hunters.
- Mira and Jason hold hands sometimes. Nothing is named.
- Her phone works the way anyone's does once they are back in the United States.

## What this chapter does

Jason asks her to go to the other side so she can practice. She says no for good reasons, then yes for her own. She calls her sister and says goodbye without saying it. They cross.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Getting home (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. "Same way we got here." Four random jumps: a sheep pasture in Wales, a beach in Chile where she gets sand everywhere, a field in Quebec in the rain. The jumps aren't draining for Jason, so four is fine.
  2. She laughs so hard at the beach one that he starts laughing too.
  3. They walk into a town and Jason buys a car from a man with a lot of questions.
  4. Over the border by road. Don't over-explain how. There are parts of the border that are mostly trees.

### Scene 2: Vermont (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A property in rural Vermont, more isolated, with long sight lines, held under a name that isn't his. Jason had it "for if" too. She's starting to wonder how many "for ifs" he has.
  2. Training resumes. She pushes harder than before. The dusk fight did something to her confidence. He's glad and a little alarmed.

### Scene 3: The ask, and the no (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to say no and be right.
- **Jason wants** her ready for what's coming in a year or two.
- **Beats:**
  1. After training, casually, as if he's been thinking about it for weeks, which he has: he asks her to cross with him. He asks instead of announcing.
  2. **His reason is practice:**
     - On Earth she fights things that are worn out from crossing. That's the easy version.
     - As she gets louder over the next year or two, the things that come will be bigger and come more often.
     - The only place she can learn those, rested and at home, with him right there, is over there.
     - The Box can't fake what it's like: everything pressing on you, everything alive.
  3. He says plainly that it is a bad option, and that if there were another way to learn it he wouldn't ask.
  4. **Her no:** clear and immediate, not dramatic. Beating something worn out on her own ground is not walking into its home.
  5. She asks to hear from someone who isn't him.

### Scene 4: Delilah (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira calls her from the porch. Delilah is quiet for a long time, then asks her to say it again.
  2. She didn't know Jason crossed. She thought nobody strong enough to matter could afford the toll. Jason takes the phone and explains vaguely: he has a way to make himself lighter. He doesn't say the Box is involved. Mira notices the vagueness.
  3. Delilah's no: in thirty years, the worst things she fought were the ones that had just crossed and were worn out. They still nearly killed her. She has no interest in visiting their homes, and she'd like Mira not to either. She says "honey" once, here.
  4. Before hanging up, Delilah says quietly that the boy she told Mira about wasn't much more than a kid. Then she's gone.

### Scene 5: The yes (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason doesn't argue. He says Delilah's right to be scared, and that he can't make her go. He's asking, badly, tripping over it.
  2. Mira notices that this time he's asking. One line of her noticing; no speech.
  3. She sleeps on it.
  4. **Her own reason**, in the morning: she's tired of being the one who gets protected. She wants to be good at this, and she is, a little, for the first time in her life at anything. And she wants to see it. She says yes.

### Scene 6: Nora (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Once she has said yes, she realizes she may be gone a long time, and calls Nora. Nora graduated last week, and Mira missed it. Nora's hurt, then softens. Mira tells her she and Jason have moved to Vermont, the first time she has said it to anyone in the family. Nora asks who moves to Vermont. Then Nora talks for twenty minutes about her new job and her apartment and a coworker feud that's been going on for a month.
  2. Mira asks follow-up questions and laughs at the right places.
  3. At the end Nora asks whether Mira is in trouble and whether it's him. Mira says no, and means the second part. They say the verbatim lines below.
  4. Mira hangs up, walks around the corner of the house where Jason is doing his checks, and cries until she's done. She washes her face and goes back.
  5. Write the call without sentiment in the narration.

### Scene 7: Crossing (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. He walks her through it: the pressure, the noise, the dark, and to clamp your shields tight and not loosen them until he says.
  2. He looks at her with the version of worry he lets himself show, kisses her forehead, short, and takes her hand. They cross.
  3. The Vermont woods smell like pine and cold. She tries to hold onto that, because she doesn't know what the other side smells like yet.
- **End:** that moment, just before. Don't show the other side.

## Set up in this chapter
- Jason says he has a way to make himself "lighter" when he crosses. He gives no detail, and Mira notices the vagueness once.
- Delilah mentions the boy again: he wasn't much more than a kid.
- The woods smell of pine and cold in the moment before the crossing.
- Mira cries behind the house after the call to Nora, and stops when she's done. She washes her face and goes back.
- Jason keeps properties "for if", and the Vermont house is held under a name that isn't his.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her ready. He's afraid of taking her, and more afraid of not.
- **Hides:** how crossing really works for him. He says only that he has a way to be lighter.
- **Funny:** the beach jump.

## Romance beat
The yes and the hand. He kisses her forehead before they cross, quickly, and they both pretend it was nothing.

## Old-life beat
Nora's call. It leads from the yes: she's about to go somewhere she can't call from.

## Rules in play
- The toll works like a tax. Delilah assumed it was too steep for strong people.
- Jason makes himself "lighter" (the Box, unexplained here).
- Random teleport isn't draining for him, so four jumps is fine. An aimed one can be blocked or tracked, which is why these are random.
- Phones, names and rentals don't give Jason away. Hunters look for him by mana.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I love you." / "Love you too, weirdo." Mira says the first to Nora at the end of the call, and Nora answers.

## Limits for this chapter
- "Which Jason accepts without comment": 0.
- "He has never once made a decision for her": 0. Use her one line of noticing.
- The explanatory narration in the sister call: 0.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Lines of Mira remarking on what Jason is doing: at most 1 in the chapter.

## Do not
- Describe the other side.
- Explain how Jason crosses.
- Add a timer, a ritual or a counted length to Mira's crying.
- Say that any set-up item will matter later.
