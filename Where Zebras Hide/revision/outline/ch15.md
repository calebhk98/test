# Chapter 15: Departure

**When and where:** Late June. One shaped jump from Spain to a field in Quebec, the border on foot, then a property in rural Vermont that becomes their base. The crossing happens about June 28.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four and a half months in. She can cycle, shield, sense crossings and erase memories (practiced on her own clone), and holds three full-strength clones, sometimes four, all her.
- She won a fight against a mid-tier using clones from three angles.
- She knows Jason's position on sharing, and Owen's reason, and has said she thinks Jason is wrong about Owen.
- She knows the other side exists: no detail, no light mentioned yet.
- Delilah is safe at home. She told Mira and Jason in Spain that her gossip is how the hunters found the cabin.
- Mira and Jason hold hands sometimes. Nothing is named.
- Her phone has been off since February, except in town. Her bag with wallet and keys is with her. Jason has his backpack, with his laptop and the cash left from Spain.
- The hunters know both their faces and what Mira can do.

## What this chapter does

Jason asks her to go to the other side. She says no, then hears the facts that make both choices bad, and says yes. She calls her sister and says goodbye without saying it. They cross.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Home and Vermont (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. One shaped jump from Spain toward eastern Canada lands them in a Quebec field, in the rain. They cross the border on foot where it runs through woods, and buy a car after, from a man with a lot of questions.
  2. A property in rural Vermont, more isolated, with long sight lines. Jason had it set aside for emergencies, like the cash. She's starting to wonder how many of these he has.
  3. Training resumes. She pushes harder than before. The dusk fight did something to her confidence. He's glad and a little alarmed.

### Scene 2: The ask, and the facts (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to say no and be right.
- **Jason wants** her ready for what's coming in a year or two, and out of the hunters' reach.
- **Beats:**
  1. After training he asks her to cross. He asks instead of announcing.
  2. **His reasons.** First, the hunters. After Spain, they can find the two of them anywhere on Earth by ordinary means. The other side is the one place no one with a body follows. Second, training: on Earth she fights things worn out from crossing. As she gets louder over the next year or two, bigger things will come more often. The only place she can learn those, rested and at home, with him right there, is over there.
  3. He says plainly that it is a bad option, and that if staying on Earth were safe he wouldn't ask.
  4. **Her no:** clear and immediate, not dramatic. Beating something worn out on her own ground is not walking into its home.
  5. She asks to hear from someone who isn't him.

### Scene 3: Delilah (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira turns her phone on in the town forty minutes from the base, and calls Delilah. Delilah is quiet for a long time.
  2. She didn't know Jason crossed. She thought nobody strong enough to matter could afford the toll. Jason takes the phone and explains vaguely: he has a way to make himself lighter. He doesn't say the Box is involved.
  3. Delilah's no: in thirty years, the worst things she fought were the ones that had just crossed and were worn out. They still nearly killed her. She has no interest in visiting their homes, and she'd like Mira not to either. She says "honey" once, here.
  4. Before hanging up, Delilah says quietly that the boy she told Mira about wasn't much more than a kid. Then she's gone.

### Scene 4: The yes (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason doesn't argue. He says Delilah's right to be scared, and that he can't make her go. He's asking, badly, tripping over it.
  2. Mira weighs two bad places: Earth, where people who know her face can find her anywhere, or the one place no one with a body follows.
  3. **Her second reason, said once:** she's tired of being the one who gets protected. She says yes.

### Scene 5: Nora (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The phone is still on in town. She calls Nora. Nora graduated last week, and Mira missed it. Nora's hurt, then softens. Mira tells her she and Jason have moved to Vermont, the first time she's said it to anyone in the family. Nora asks who moves to Vermont. Then Nora talks for twenty minutes about her new job and her apartment and a coworker feud that's been going on for a month.
  2. Mira asks follow-up questions and laughs at the right places.
  3. At the end Nora asks whether Mira is in trouble and whether it's him. Mira says no, and means the second part. They say the verbatim lines below.
  4. Mira hangs up, goes behind the house where Jason is doing his checks, and cries until she's done. She washes her face and goes back inside. She turns the phone off.
  5. Write the call without sentiment in the narration.

### Scene 6: Crossing (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. He walks her through it: the pressure, the noise, clamp your shields tight and don't loosen them until I say.
  2. He kisses her forehead, short, and takes her hand. They cross.
  3. The Vermont woods smell like pine and cold. She tries to hold onto that, because she doesn't know what the other side smells like yet.
- **End:** that moment, just before. Don't show the other side.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her ready, and out of reach. He's afraid of taking her, and more afraid of not.
- **Hides:** how crossing works for him. He says only that he has a way to be lighter.

## Romance beat
The yes, and the kiss on the forehead before they cross.

## Old-life beat
Nora's call.

## Rules in play
- The toll works like a tax. Delilah assumed it was too steep for strong people.
- Jason makes himself "lighter" (the Box, unexplained here).
- A shaped teleport can't be blocked or tracked. One jump is enough from Spain to Quebec.
- Her phone is on only in town.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I love you." / "Love you too, weirdo." Mira says the first to Nora at the end of the call, and Nora answers.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Lines of Mira remarking on what Jason is doing: at most 1 in the chapter.

## Do not
- Describe the other side.
- Explain how Jason crosses.
- Add a timer, a ritual or a counted length to Mira's crying.
