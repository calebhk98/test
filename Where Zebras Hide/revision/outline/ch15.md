# Chapter 15: Departure

**When and where:** Late June. Random jumps home (a field in Quebec), then a property in rural Vermont that becomes their base. The crossing happens about June 28.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four and a half months in. She can cycle, shield, sense crossings and erase memories (practiced on her own clone), and holds three full-strength clones, sometimes four, all her.
- She won a fight against a mid-tier using clones from three angles.
- She knows Jason's position on sharing, and Owen's reason, and has said she thinks Jason's wrong about Owen.
- She knows the other side exists: no detail, no light mentioned yet.
- Delilah is safe at home, warned off by a mana message.
- Mira and Jason hold hands sometimes. Nothing is named.

## What this chapter does

Jason asks her to go to the other side. She says no for good reasons, then yes for her own. She calls her sister and says goodbye without saying it. They cross.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Getting home (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. "Same way we got here." Four random jumps: a sheep pasture in Wales, a beach in Chile where she gets sand everywhere, a field in Quebec in the rain.
  2. They walk into a town and Jason buys a car from a man with a lot of questions.
  3. She laughs so hard at the beach one that he starts laughing too.
  4. Over the border by road. Don't over-explain how. "There are parts of the border that are mostly trees."

### Scene 2: Vermont (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A property in rural Vermont, more isolated, with long sight lines. Jason had it "for if" too. She's starting to wonder how many "for ifs" he has.
  2. Training resumes. She's pushing harder than before. The dusk fight did something to her confidence. He's glad and a little alarmed.

### Scene 3: The ask, and the no (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to say no and be right.
- **Jason wants** her ready for what's coming in a year or two.
- **Beats:**
  1. After training, casually, as if he's been thinking about it for weeks, which he has: he wants to take her across.
  2. **His reason, which is a story reason:**
     - On Earth she fights things that are worn out from crossing. That's the easy version.
     - As she gets louder over the next year or two, the things that come will be bigger and come more often.
     - The only place she can learn those, rested and at home, with him right there, is over there.
     - The Box can't fake what it's like: everything pressing on you, everything alive.
  3. **Her no:** clear, immediate, not dramatic. Beating something worn out on her own ground is not walking into its home.
  4. She asks to hear from someone who isn't him.

### Scene 4: Delilah (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira calls her. Delilah is quiet for a long time.
  2. She didn't know Jason crossed. She thought nobody strong enough to matter could afford the toll. Jason gets on the phone and explains vaguely: he has a way to make himself "lighter". He doesn't say the Box is involved. Mira notices the vagueness.
  3. Delilah's no: in thirty years, the worst things she fought were the ones that had just crossed and were worn out. They still nearly killed her. She has no interest in visiting their homes. "And honey, I'd like you to not either."
  4. Before hanging up, Delilah adds quietly, "That boy I told you about. He wasn't much more than a kid." Then she's gone.

### Scene 5: The yes (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason doesn't argue. He says Delilah's right to be scared, and that he can't make her go. He's asking, badly, tripping over it.
  2. She notices that this time he's asking. Keep it to one line of her noticing; don't make it a speech.
  3. She sleeps on it.
  4. **Her own reason**, in the morning: she's tired of being the one who gets protected. She wants to be good at this, and she is, a little, for the first time in her life at anything. And she wants to see it. She says yes.

### Scene 6: Nora (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She calls Nora. Nora graduated last week, and Mira missed it. Nora's hurt, then softens. Mira tells her she and Jason have moved to Vermont, the first time she's said it to anyone in the family, and Nora says "Vermont? Who moves to Vermont?" Then Nora talks for twenty minutes about her new job and her apartment and a coworker feud that's been going on for a month.
  2. Mira asks follow-up questions and laughs at the right places.
  3. Nora, at the end: "Are you in trouble? Is it him?" Mira says no, and means the second part. "I love you." "Love you too, weirdo."
  4. Mira hangs up, walks around the corner of the house where Jason is doing his checks, and cries for exactly four minutes on her phone timer. She washes her face and goes back.
  5. Write the call without sentiment in the narration. No "She didn't tell her how scared she was."

### Scene 7: Crossing (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. He walks her through it: the pressure, the noise, clamp your shields tight and don't loosen them until I say.
  2. He looks at her with the version of worry he lets himself show. He holds her hand steady.
  3. The Vermont woods smell like pine and cold. She tries to hold onto that, because she doesn't know what the other side smells like yet.
- **End:** that moment, just before. Don't show the other side.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her ready. He's terrified of taking her, and more terrified of not.
- **Hides:** how crossing really works for him. Plant it; Mira notices the vagueness, and it counts toward the pattern she names in Ch 17.
- **Funny:** the beach jump.

## Romance beat
The yes and the hand. He kisses her forehead before they cross, quickly, and they both pretend it was nothing.

## Old-life beat
Nora's call.

## Rules in play
- The toll works like a tax. Delilah assumed it was too steep for strong people.
- Jason makes himself "lighter" (the Box, unexplained here).
- Teleport isn't draining for him, so four jumps is fine.
