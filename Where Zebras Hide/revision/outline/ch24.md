# Chapter 24: Containment

**When and where:** Late October. A college campus in upstate New York, then a gas station two miles away, then the car home to Vermont.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- A London anchor read a leaked half-explanation on air, and three viewers started producing. Incidents are multiplying.
- Earlier this month Mira erased her friend Marcus, gently, because he was close to understanding because of her.
- The community still has no plan. Delilah's suppress-harder side has quietly become what most people are doing.
- Mira and Delilah disagreed at the lake house and haven't really talked since.
- Mira can hold a few dozen clones easily, and burst far higher at a cost.

## What this chapter does

The erasures as a real violation, done efficiently by someone very good at it. Then Priya, who is too late to erase, and whom Mira has to tell the truth.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Delilah asks (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah calls. A student named Priya posted a video a week ago, a curious-kid experiment about feeling energy. About three days ago she figured it out, and posted two follow-ups explaining her process. Delilah says the first video is only a video, and the follow-ups are a map.
  2. Sixteen people on campus watched the follow-ups closely enough to be at risk. They watched on the library's computers, and someone pulled the logs. Delilah's people took both videos down that morning.
  3. Delilah asks Mira because Mira can be in sixteen places at once. They half make up on the phone. Delilah says she still thinks Mira is wrong about Owen. Mira says she knows she does.

### Scene 2: Sixteen (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to get it done, and not to be good at it.
- **Beats:**
  1. She splits into sixteen across campus: a dorm, a library carrel, a lab, a bus stop.
  2. Each one sits near a stranger and takes an afternoon of internet rabbit hole out of them. Each feels a moment of confusion and blames being tired. It takes eleven minutes in total.
  3. The horror is the efficiency. Show two of the sixteen. One of them goes too smoothly: a woman says she zoned out and goes back to her sandwich.

### Scene 3: Priya (about 1,400 words)
- **Mira wants** to give her a chance, and not to become Jason in the woods.
- **Beats:**
  1. Priya is twenty, has been producing for about seventy-two hours, and is scared. She keeps asking questions because it's the only thing she can do. She has a physical notebook because she is a student.
  2. Erasure can't undo what she understands now. Taking her memories would only leave her producing and confused. Mira sits across from her in an empty seminar room and tells her the truth in order, the way Jason told her: the production, the scent, the three months, the things that cross.
  3. Priya puts the pen down. After a minute she picks it up and asks the next question.
  4. Priya says she feels like a liability. Mira repeats what Jason told her at the cabin in February, when she said the same thing.
  5. Mira teaches her the first lesson, which is sensing. It's the same lesson Jason gave her first. Priya gets it in ten minutes and says a small, surprised syllable.
  6. Before Priya asks, Mira gives her a contact: a practitioner in the city that Delilah vouches for. She tells her not to post anything else.
  7. Priya asks about the people who watched her video. Mira says she took care of it. Priya works out what that means. She puts her pen down and doesn't pick it up. She asks nothing else. She closes the notebook.

### Scene 4: The gas station and the car (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A gas station two miles away. She locks the bathroom door, sits on the closed lid and stares at the wall for twenty minutes. State the time and what she does. Leave the feeling unstated.
  2. In the car, with the gas station behind them, she tells Jason everything. He says the erasures were clean and Priya is in good hands.
  3. She tells him she doesn't want to do it again. He says she might have to. She says she knows she might, and that she is telling him she doesn't want to.
  4. He tells her it doesn't get easier, and that he'll drive.

### End (about 200 words)
Passenger seat, dark road. She thinks about her own prints from the darkroom in Columbus, the frame of Jason laughing, and then about Priya's careful handwriting. End on the handwriting.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to make this not hers, and can't.
- **Honest:** he tells her it doesn't get easier, because it doesn't.

## Romance beat
The car: she tells him everything, he is honest with her, and he drives so she doesn't have to. His hand finds hers on the seat between them.

## Old-life beat
The darkroom prints, in her mind. They come up in the last scene because she is sitting in the dark car, thinking about handwriting, and her own prints from Columbus are the nearest thing she has to it.

## Set up in this chapter
- Priya has a physical notebook, in careful handwriting, because she is a student. Show it on the table in the seminar room and show her closing it at the end.
- Mira gives Priya a contact before Priya asks for one, and tells her not to post anything else.
- Mira says "I took care of it" in answer to Priya's question about the people who watched her video. Priya works out what it means.
- Mira teaches Priya the first lesson for nothing, without being asked and without making her earn it. State it in one plain sentence.

## Rules in play
- Erasure can't undo understanding once production starts. Priya's 72 hours make her too late; the sixteen haven't started.
- The first lesson is sensing.
- Clones are all her: sixteen places, one mind.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "You're not a liability. You're a student." Mira says this to Priya in the seminar room, answering Priya's saying she feels like a liability. It is what Jason said to her at the cabin in February.
- "The people who watched my video. What about them?" Priya asks this in the seminar room, after Mira has finished teaching her sensing.
- "I took care of it." Mira answers Priya's question.
- "I don't want to do it again." Mira says this to Jason in the car.
- "You might have to." Jason's answer.
- "I know I might have to. I'm telling you I don't want to." Mira's reply to Jason.

## Limits
- Narration explaining the moral weight of the erasures: 0.
- Narration saying how Priya reacts to the answer: 0. Show it in what she does.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Verbatim lines count toward every limit.

## Do not
- Make the erasures look heroic.
- Say Mira taught Priya cycling. It's sensing.
- Explain the mechanism.
