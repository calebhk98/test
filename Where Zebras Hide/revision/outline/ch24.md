# Chapter 24: Containment

**When and where:** Late October. A college campus in upstate New York, then a gas station two miles away, then the car home to Vermont.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. There is no old draft. This is one of the best-planned chapters in the old outline, so keep its emotional structure.

## Where things stand

- A London anchor read a leaked half-explanation on air, and three viewers started producing. Incidents are multiplying.
- Mira erased her friend Marcus two weeks ago, gently, because he was close to understanding because of her.
- The community still has no plan. Delilah's suppress-harder side has quietly become what most people are doing.
- Mira and Delilah disagreed at the lake house and haven't really talked since.
- Mira can hold a few dozen clones easily, and burst far higher at a cost.

## What this chapter does

The erasures as a real violation, done efficiently by someone very good at it. Then Priya, who is too late to erase, and whom Mira has to tell the truth.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Delilah asks (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah calls. A student named Priya posted a video a week ago, a curious-kid experiment about "feeling energy". Then she figured it out, about three days ago, and posted two follow-ups explaining her process. "The video's just a video. The explanation is a map."
  2. Sixteen people on campus watched the follow-ups closely enough to be at risk.
  3. Delilah asks Mira because Mira can be in sixteen places at once. They half make up on the phone. "I still think you're wrong about Owen." "I know you do." Delilah's line, so it's allowed.

### Scene 2: Sixteen (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to get it done, and not to be good at it.
- **Beats:**
  1. She splits into sixteen across campus: a dorm, a library carrel, a lab, a bus stop.
  2. Each one sits near a stranger and takes an afternoon of internet rabbit hole out of them. Each feels a moment of confusion and blames being tired.
  3. It takes eleven minutes in total.
  4. **The horror is the efficiency.** She's very good at this. She notices she's good at it, and wishes she hadn't noticed.
  5. Show two of them specifically: a boy who laughs at a meme mid-erasure, and a woman who says "sorry, I zoned out" to nobody.

### Scene 3: Priya (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** to give her a chance, and not to become Jason in the woods.
- **Beats:**
  1. Priya is twenty, has been producing for about seventy-two hours, and is the most excited person Mira has met since February. She has a physical notebook full of experiments in careful handwriting.
  2. Erasure won't work on her now. Mira sits across from her in an empty seminar room and tells her the truth in order, without softening, the way Jason told her: the production, the scent, the three months, the things that cross.
  3. Priya asks better questions than Mira ever did, faster than Mira can answer.
  4. At one point Priya puts the pen down and breathes through her nose for thirty seconds. Then she picks the pen back up.
  5. Priya says she feels like a liability. Mira hears herself say Jason's line from February: "You're not a liability. You're a student."
  6. **Mira teaches her the first lesson: sensing.** It's the same lesson Jason gave her at a rest stop in Tennessee. Priya gets it in ten minutes and says "oh" in a small voice.
  7. Mira gives her a contact: a practitioner in the city that Delilah vouches for. Don't post anything else.
  8. "The people who watched my video. What about them?" "I took care of it." **Priya works out what that means**, visibly; her face changes. She asks nothing else, because she's afraid of the answer, and Mira sees the fear.
  9. **Mira notices one more thing:** she just taught someone freely, for nothing, the thing Jason won't share. One sentence.

### Scene 4: The bathroom (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A gas station two miles away. She locks the bathroom door, sits on the closed lid and stares at the wall for twenty minutes.
  2. Don't narrate the feeling. The twenty minutes is the feeling.

### Scene 5: Jason (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. In the car she tells him everything. He listens. He says she did it right, the erasures were clean, Priya's in good hands.
  2. "I don't want to do it again." "You might have to." "I know I might have to. I'm telling you I don't want to."
  3. He doesn't tell her it gets easier.

### End (about 200 words)
Passenger seat, dark road. She thinks about Priya's careful handwriting, and her own prints from the darkroom in Columbus, the frame of Jason laughing. She wonders whether Priya shoots film. Probably not. **End on the film line.** Cut any "she doesn't say anything else."

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to make this not hers, and can't.
- **Honest:** he doesn't say it gets easier, because it doesn't.

## Romance beat
The car, and the honesty.

## Old-life beat
The darkroom prints, in her mind.

## Rules in play
- Erasure can't undo understanding once production starts. Priya's 72 hours make her too late; the sixteen haven't started.
- The first lesson is sensing.
- Clones are all her: sixteen places, one mind.

## Plant for later

- "I took care of it."
- Mira teaching freely.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)

## Limits for this chapter
- Narration explaining the moral weight: 0. Cut "Sixteen people had something taken from them..." as a stated moral.
- "Priya nods slowly. Doesn't ask anything else": 0. Show the fear.
- Triplets: at most 1.

## Do not
- Make the erasures look heroic.
- Say Mira taught Priya cycling. It's sensing.
- Explain the mechanism.
