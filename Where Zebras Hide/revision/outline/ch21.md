# Chapter 21: Return

**When and where:** Labor Day weekend, early September. Columbus: the old apartment, a community darkroom, the coffee shop, and her parents' house in a suburb. Jason comes with her.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira left Columbus on February 9 and hasn't been back.
- **What her family thinks:** she quit her job to travel with Jason, then moved to Vermont with him.
- **The rest:** Jess has her boxed things. Marcus texted twice in February and got short replies. Nora is suspicious.
- **Mira now:**
  - She can make about a thousand of herself, once, and it knocks her out.
  - She can hold shields while she sleeps.
  - She has fought on the other side and guarded Jason alone for thirty hours.
- She and Jason are together, as of the past week or so. They agreed that neither decides about the other alone.
- **She knows the rule:** telling her family would put them at risk, and so would her own mana near them.

## What this chapter does

The homecoming. Everything is the same and she isn't. She sees her family as people who produce nothing: invisible, and safe because of it. She sees herself as the danger in the room. She lets one small thing slip. By the end she knows she can't come back, and her devotion becomes duty.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The apartment (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jess hugs her too hard, then is weird, then is normal.
  2. In the pocket of the coat she left behind, the folded motel-notepad list from February: what's back there, and "What I need to know to stay alive", underlined twice. She reads it standing in Jess's hallway.
  3. Mira's boxes are in the spare closet. The Pentax is in a box with her scarves, and the roll is still in it.
  3. Small things hit her: her mug, her handwriting on a grocery list from February.
  4. Jess asks if she's happy. She says yes, and it's true, and it isn't the whole answer.

### Scene 2: The roll (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She rents an hour at the community darkroom she used to dream about. The film is from early February:
     - the office window
     - frost on her car
     - a frame of Jason at the coffee shop, laughing at something off-camera, that she doesn't remember taking
  2. The print comes up in the tray the way her mana first did, slowly, and you can't grab it.
  3. She looks at the woman in these photos for a long time, then hangs the prints to dry in a careful row.

### Scene 3: Sunday dinner (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** one normal evening, and to keep them safe from her.
- **Beats:**
  1. She brings milk. Mom laughs: "Seven months late."
  2. Mom adores Jason inside ten minutes. Dad asks Jason about grad school for Mira, and Jason, to Mira's horror, takes the question seriously.
  3. Nora on FaceTime, suspicious and sharp, then mollified by seeing Jason, then suspicious again.
  4. **Her mana sense at the table.** Her family produces nothing. They are completely quiet, invisible, the zebras in the grass. She is the loudest thing in the house, and she keeps her shields clamped all evening and gets a headache from it.
  5. She lies about Vermont, about money and about the future. The lies come easily. She notices that, and keeps going.
  6. **Earth, after the other side:** everything in the house is thin, like a print pulled from the developer too early, all mid-grey with no depth. That's her image.

### Scene 4: The watch (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. That night they sleep at her parents' house: Mira in her childhood room, Jason on the couch, at Mom's insistence.
  2. She puts a clone outside on the back step, shielded tight, to keep watch, in case anything comes for her and finds them instead. Through its eyes she watches the dark yard all night.
  3. Mom comes down at 2 am for water. She almost sees the clone through the kitchen window, and doesn't.

### Scene 5: Marcus (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Monday, at the old coffee shop, she runs into Marcus. He's delighted and nosy, and Jason is right there.
  2. Her coffee has gone cold while they talk. Without thinking, holding it, she warms it, and it steams back up in her hands.
  3. Marcus sees. "Neat trick." She laughs it off badly: "Insulated cup." He's not stupid. He looks at the cup a second too long.
  4. Jason sees him see it. Mira says, "It's fine, it's nothing." She could erase the last minute from him, and she won't, not to Marcus, not over a coffee cup. Jason keeps their new agreement and leaves it as her call. Don't hint that this will matter later.

### End (about 300 words)
Driving out of Columbus, she realizes she can't come back. It isn't Jason's rule. She's a risk to everyone she loves just by being near them, and the only thing that keeps them safe is her staying good at this and staying away. End on the skyline in the mirror, then gone. The reader should feel her reason change from him to them; never state it.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her family to like him, desperately, and he's terrible at hiding it.
- **Awkward and sweet:** helping Mom with dishes, answering Dad seriously.
- **Keeps the agreement** about Marcus, and that turns out to matter.

## Romance beat
Jason at her parents' table, being himself. Mom whispers, "He's lovely," and Mira says, "I know," which is allowed here because it's an answer to Mom, not a filler beat.

## Old-life beats
All of it.

## Rules in play
- Ordinary humans produce zero, so they're invisible.
- Her mana near them is a risk.
- Lying protects them.
- Small useful magic, which she uses without thinking.
- The clone as watch.
- Every creature shows up on camera. She's careful about that now.

## Do not

- Have Marcus understand anything yet. He saw a trick, that's all.
- Have anything attack. The danger in this chapter is her.
