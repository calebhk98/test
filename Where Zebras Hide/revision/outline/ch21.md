# Chapter 21: Return

**When and where:** Labor Day weekend, early September. Columbus: Jess's apartment, a community darkroom, a grocery store, and her parents' house in a suburb. Jason comes with her.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira left Columbus on February 9 and hasn't been back. In February Jason said they couldn't go back: the attackers saw her with him and know Columbus, and her new mana was a risk to her family. The hunters can now find them on Earth by ordinary means, given time, and Columbus is the first place they would look. Delilah has heard nothing of the hunters since June. Jason lets her go for one weekend, with her shields clamped the whole time. He hates it. She insisted. That is the risk the reader should feel all chapter.
- **What her family thinks:** she quit her job to travel with Jason, then moved to Vermont with him.
- **The rest:** Jess has her boxed things. Marcus texted in February and got one word back. Nora is suspicious.
- **Mira now:**
  - She can make about a thousand of herself, once, and it knocks her out. Day to day she holds a few dozen.
  - She can hold shields while she sleeps.
  - She has fought on the other side and guarded Jason alone for thirty hours.
- She and Jason are together, as of the past week or so. They agreed that neither decides about the other alone.
- She knows the rule: telling her family would put them at risk, and so would her own mana near them.

## What this chapter does

The homecoming. Everything is the same and she isn't. She sees her family as people who produce nothing: invisible, and safe because of it. She sees herself as the danger in the room. By the end she has put someone at risk for the first time, and her devotion becomes duty.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The apartment (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jess hugs her too hard, then is weird, then is normal.
  2. Mira's boxes are in the spare closet. The Pentax is in a box with her scarves, and the February roll is still in it. She winds it out for the darkroom and loads the roll Jason gave her on the last Friday, which she has carried since.
  3. Show one small thing that hits her: her mug, or her handwriting on a grocery list from February.
  4. Jess asks if she is happy. Mira says yes, and means it.
- **By the end:** she is home and doesn't belong in it.

### Scene 2: The roll (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She rents an hour at the community darkroom she used to dream about. The film is from early February. Show two frames: one ordinary, and a frame of Jason at the coffee shop, laughing at something off-camera, that she doesn't remember taking.
  2. She holds each print up to the light before she hangs it to dry in a careful row.
- **By the end:** she has met the person she was in February.

### Scene 3: Milk (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** one normal evening.
- **Beats:**
  1. Saturday evening, at a grocery store near Jess's. She and Jason are buying milk for tomorrow's dinner, and she is carrying a coffee from the store's counter that has gone cold. She is tired.
  2. Marcus comes around the end of the aisle. He is delighted and nosy, and Jason is right there.
  3. While they talk, without thinking, she warms the cup in her hands and it steams.
  4. Marcus sees it and says it is a neat trick. She says it is an insulated cup.
  5. She could erase the last minute. By the time she thinks of it, Marcus has moved on to the weather. She tells herself she can still do it. She doesn't.
  6. Jason saw him see it. He leaves it as her call, as they agreed.
- **By the end:** she knows she has put Marcus at risk, and that it wasn't Jason or the hunters but her own mana that did it.

### Scene 4: Sunday dinner, and the night (about 1,500 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep them safe from her.
- **Beats:**
  1. She brings the milk. Mom laughs.
  2. Mom adores Jason inside ten minutes. Dad asks Jason about grad school for Mira, and Jason, to Mira's horror, takes the question seriously.
  3. Nora on FaceTime, suspicious and sharp, then mollified by seeing Jason, then suspicious again.
  4. **Her mana sense at the table.** Her family produces nothing. They are completely quiet to her sense. She is the loudest thing in the house, and she keeps her shields clamped all evening and gets a headache from it.
  5. She lies about Vermont, about money and about the future. The lies come easily, and she keeps going.
  6. **Earth, after the other side:** everything in the house is thin, like a print pulled from the developer too early, mid-grey with no depth. That is her image.
  7. In the kitchen, Mom tells her Jason is lovely, and Mira agrees. She takes her first frame of him on the porch, because he is standing there.
  8. That night they sleep at her parents' house: Mira in her childhood room, Jason on the couch, at Mom's insistence. She doesn't sleep. She sits up with her shields tight and her eyes on the yard.
  9. Mom comes down at 2 am for water. Mira says she couldn't sleep.
- **By the end:** she has had her evening, and she is thinking about Marcus.

### End (about 300 words)
Monday, driving out of Columbus. She realizes she can't come back. It isn't Jason's rule. She is a risk to everyone she loves just by being near them, and this weekend she was the one who exposed someone. The only thing that keeps them safe is her staying good at this and staying away. End on the skyline in the mirror, then gone. The reader should feel her reason change from him to them; never state it.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her family to like him, desperately, and he is terrible at hiding it.
- **Warm and a little formal:** helping Mom with dishes, answering Dad seriously.
- **Keeps the agreement** about Marcus and leaves it as her call.

## Romance beat
Jason at her parents' table, being himself. Mom tells her he is lovely.

## Old-life beats
All of it.

## Rules in play
- Ordinary humans produce zero, so they are invisible.
- Her mana near them is a risk.
- Lying protects them.
- Small useful magic, which she uses without thinking when she is tired.
- Every creature shows up on camera.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "Seven months late." Mom says it to Mira at the door on Sunday, when Mira hands her the milk.
- "Neat trick." / "Insulated cup." Marcus says the first in the grocery store after he sees Mira warm the cup; Mira answers with the second.

## Limits for this chapter
- "Two years": at most 1.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Analogies: one per scene.
- Photography images: one.

## Do not
- Have her tell anyone the truth.
- Have Marcus understand anything. He saw a trick, that's all.
- Have anything attack. The danger in this chapter is her.
- Put a clone anywhere near her parents' house.
