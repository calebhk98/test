# Chapter 8: Routine

**When and where:** Mid-February to about April 10. The cabin in the Appalachian foothills, and the supply-run town thirty minutes away. Open on one specific morning in early March.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has been producing since February 10. She can sense, and cycle partially. Her three-month window to get quiet closes around May 10.
- She knows:
  - Jason's real history
  - the other side exists
  - things cross for meals, bigger things for bigger meals
  - getting better makes her louder
- They're in separate rooms. He held her hand once, the morning it started. Neither has mentioned the confession since.
- Her phone is off. She turns it on only in town on the supply run, every ten days.
- She has told her family nothing beyond the road-trip lie. That changes when she calls Mom in this chapter.
- Jason works remotely for a few clients through a prepaid hotspot. He has his laptop in the cabin.

## What this chapter does

Weeks pass, built from a few specific days. A domestic life they didn't plan. Her first clones. The hand scene, which changes how they are with each other for good.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: A morning in March (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Open on one morning. She burns the rice for the third time that week. Jason eats it without complaint. She comments on it.
  2. She can now cycle for hours without thinking about it. Mana is a constant background she no longer attends to.
  3. He starts teaching basic shielding: just keeping it in. Hand positions matter, he insists.
- **Show time passing** with two other specific days, a sentence each. Pick them yourself.

### Scene 2: Evenings, and a call to Mom (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** more of him.
- **Beats:**
  1. New material about his childhood: what it was like at twelve, when strangers showed up who knew what he was doing before he had a word for it, and being the youngest person in every room, then not wanting to be in rooms at all.
  2. She asks if he misses his parents. He answers in the verbatim line below.
  3. She talks him into calling them. He goes outside to do it, and she watches him through the window.
  4. On a supply-run day in town, she turns on her phone and finally calls Mom. She says she's taking a few months to travel with Jason, and that she quit her job. Mom is hurt, thrilled about Jason, and worried, all at once. She asks if he's good to Mira. Mira says yes and means it, and hates every other word she says. Then the phone goes off again.
- **Optional texture:** pick none or one that fits what you've written.

### Scene 3: The first clone (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to master the next thing.
- **Beats:**
  1. Once her cycling is steady, he teaches clones, mechanics first. A clone is a body she builds that is her, out of her.
  2. The first one is a puppet. It moves only when she moves, like an extra limb. That's less strange than she expected.
  3. Within days, the next step: it can move on its own a little, like steering a character in a game. This feels wrong: her own face, moving because she pushed it. Most people get over it in a week. She doesn't quite, and she keeps the feeling to herself.
  4. **Her eye.** She sees small wrong things about the clone, a slight asymmetry in the jaw or hair that catches light wrong, and fixes them because they bother her, like a crooked frame. Jason says it's wasted mana. She says it's bugging her, and keeps doing it.

### Scene 4: The hand (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** him to stop managing the distance.
- **Jason wants** to keep it, and is losing.
- **Beats:**
  1. A shielding exercise. Her shield keeps forming lopsided, forty minutes of it.
  2. He crosses the room and corrects her hands. He starts to pull back. She holds on to his hand and says the verbatim line below.
  3. His struggle shows for a few seconds. Then he stays, and shows her the pressure with his other hand. He stays close for the rest of the session.
  4. **Afterward**, show one small change in how he is around her over the next few days. Pick it yourself. Don't write a paragraph explaining what changed.

### Scene 5: Two-thirds (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Around April 10. She works out that two-thirds of her three months are gone.
  2. She feels the clock for the first time as a real thing: she's quieter than she was, but is she quiet enough? She asks Jason. He says she's close and starts talking about dinner.
  3. She can make several clones now, a handful, and holds them for an hour at a time.
- **End:** that night she forgets to dismiss one. It sits in the corner of her room, looking back at her with her own face.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** this life, and knows it can't last.
- **Small texture:** he eats whatever she cooks.
- **Not stoic:** he talks and teases. At the hand, his composure slips for a moment.
