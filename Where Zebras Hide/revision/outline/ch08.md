# Chapter 8: Routine

**When and where:** Mid-February to about April 10. The cabin in the Appalachian foothills. Open on one specific morning in early March.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has been producing since February 10. She can sense, and cycle partially. Her three-month window to get quiet closes around May 10.
- She knows:
  - Jason's real history
  - the other side exists
  - things cross for meals, bigger things for bigger meals
  - getting better makes her louder
- A truck slowed on the road once. Jason said "probably nothing".
- They're in separate rooms. He held her hand once, the morning it started. Neither has mentioned the confession since.
- She has told her family nothing beyond the road-trip lie. That changes when she calls Mom in this chapter.

## What this chapter does

Weeks pass, built from a few specific days. A domestic life they didn't plan. Her first clones. The hand scene, which changes how they are with each other for good.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: A morning in March (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Open on one morning. She burns the rice for the third time that week. Jason eats it without complaint. She tells him, out loud, that this is either love or a medical condition.
  2. She can now cycle for hours without thinking about it. Mana is a constant background noise, like tinnitus she has stopped hearing.
  3. He starts teaching basic shielding: just keeping it in. Hand positions matter, he insists.
- **Show time passing** with two other specific days, a sentence each. For example: the morning frost stopped coming, and the night she cycled through a whole movie on his laptop.

### Scene 2: Evenings (about 600 words)
- **Mira wants** more of him.
- **Beats:**
  1. Build on what he told her in the car; don't retell it. New pieces only:
     - what it was like at twelve, when strangers showed up who knew what he was doing before he had a word for it
     - being the youngest person in every room, then not wanting to be in rooms at all
  2. She asks if he misses his parents. "I miss the version of me that could've had a normal relationship with them."
  3. She talks him into calling them. He goes outside to do it, and she watches him through the window, pacing.
- **Details to use:** the woodstove, and the radio he keeps on low.

### Scene 3: The first clone (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to master the next thing.
- **Beats:**
  1. Once her cycling is steady, he teaches clones, mechanics first: "You're building a body that's you, out of you."
  2. The first one is a puppet. It moves only when she moves, like an extra limb. That's less strange than she expected.
  3. Within days, the next step: it can move on its own a little, like steering a character in a game. This feels wrong: her own face, moving because she pushed it. Most people get over it in a week. She doesn't quite, and she keeps the feeling to herself.
  4. **Her eye.** She notices small wrong things about the clone, a slight asymmetry in the jaw or hair that catches light wrong, and fixes them because they bother her, like a crooked frame. Jason says it's wasted mana. She says, "It's bugging me," and keeps doing it. That's all. **Don't flag this as important**, and don't say it will matter.

### Scene 4: The hand (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** him to stop managing the distance.
- **Jason wants** to keep it, and is losing.
- **Beats:**
  1. A shielding exercise. Her shield keeps forming lopsided, forty minutes of it.
  2. He crosses the room and moves her hands into position. Both of them go still.
  3. He starts to pull back. She closes her fingers around his hand.
  4. "You told me you loved me," she says. "You don't get to flinch when I hold your hand."
  5. **His struggle is visible:**
     - His hand is tense in hers for a few seconds. He starts to say something defensive and stops.
     - Then he exhales, leaves his hand there, and shows her the pressure with his other hand.
     - He stays close for the rest of the session.
  6. **Afterward, show the change through behavior only**, at most three small things over the next days:
     - he sets her coffee within reach without asking
     - he sits on her side of the porch steps
     - he stops saying goodnight from his doorway and says it from hers
  7. Don't write a paragraph explaining what changed.

### Scene 5: Two-thirds (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Around April 10. She counts the days on the calendar by the door: two-thirds of her window gone.
  2. She feels the clock for the first time as a real thing: she's quieter than she was, but is she quiet enough?
  3. She asks Jason. He says she's "close" and changes the subject to dinner, and she lets him.
- She can make several clones now, a handful, and holds them for an hour at a time.
- **End:** that night she forgets to dismiss one. It sits in the corner of her room, looking back at her with her own face. She isn't sure she's supposed to get used to that.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** this life, and knows it can't last.
- **Small texture:**
  - He secretly can't cook either. She doesn't find out yet.
- **Not stoic:** he talks and teases. At the hand, his composure slips for a moment, and he lets it.

## Romance beat
The hand, and three small changes after it.

## Old-life beat
She finally calls Mom back. She says she's taking a few months to travel with Jason, and that she quit her job. Mom is hurt, thrilled about Jason, and worried, all at once: "Is he good to you?" Mira says yes and means it, and hates every other word she says.
