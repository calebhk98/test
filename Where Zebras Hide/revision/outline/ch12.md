# Chapter 12: Progress

**When and where:** Around May 10 (three months in) to late May. The cabin. Delilah drives over most afternoons.
**Length:** about 3,800 words. This is a full chapter; keep the scenes tight. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira can cycle without thinking, shield her scent and sense creatures. Her clones produce their own mana and are all her. Her clones are still useless in fights: they start nearly empty.
- Delilah, a warm, nosy practitioner from over the ridge, now teaches her in the afternoons. She calls mana "the warmth".
- Delilah said cloning is creation, not copying. Mira has been chewing on it.
- Jason has a Box: a captured creature fighting his clone on a loop. Mira thinks it's a prison. That's unresolved.
- Mira and Jason are close and not together.

## What this chapter does

She survives the window, and learns it was only the first one. Delilah teaches her erasure. Her clones become useful. At dinner, an argument raises an idea nobody quite notices.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Three months (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She wakes, realizes it's been three months, and goes to find Jason, almost giddy.
  2. He's glad, really glad. He also tells her the truth: three months gets you quiet. The first few years get you safe. "You're past the first deadline. Not the last one."
  3. She's deflated, then decides she'll take it.

### Scene 2: Delilah's afternoons, and the witch trials (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason teaches survival in the mornings. Delilah teaches everything else in the afternoons:
     - warming her hands
     - making terrible coffee taste decent
     - how to read whether a stranger's warmth means them harm
     - how to behave around strong people without starting something
  2. Neither teacher covers the other's material.
  3. **The history, told once, through a person.** Delilah tells the story her own teacher told her, of a village in the 1600s:
     - people started looking for witches
     - the looking taught some of them
     - the ones who learned got eaten
     - the bodies looked like proof of witches, and the looking got worse
     
     Afterward, the people who knew pushed everyone hard toward believing magic was nonsense. "That's why your science teachers were so sure of themselves, honey. We made sure."
  4. Keep this to one told story with Mira's reactions. It's the only time the book explains it.
  5. Mira remembers Jason's odd half-smile at the students arguing about predictability in the coffee shop, the night it all started. Now she knows what he found funny.

### Scene 3: Erasure (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** never to learn this, and learns it anyway.
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah teaches erasure as normal cleanup: "You see someone getting too close to it, a neighbor, a kid, you take the last hour, or with care the last few weeks, and they go on with their lives. That's how we've kept the lid on since the trials."
  2. Mira says no. Delilah says she'll need it someday. Mira says, "My mind is mine," and Delilah says, "And that's why you'll be careful with other people's."
  3. **Practice.** Delilah has her practice on one of her own clones: take the last five minutes from the clone before she reabsorbs it.
  4. She does it. When she reabsorbs the clone there's a gap, five minutes of herself she can feel the edges of and can't get into.
  5. She goes outside and is sick in the bushes.
- **Changes:** she can now do the thing she refused to let Jason do to her.

### Scene 4: Full clones (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Box training. Jason partitions his Box so her clone can go in and fight the captured creature while his clone steps back. She can't build or change the Box; she's a tenant in it.
  2. She doesn't last. She reacts to movement instead of mana, always half a second behind. Attempt after attempt, until by the twelfth she lands something that counts as a counterattack before her clone dies.
  3. Her old objection doesn't vanish. Before each round she says sorry to the creature, quietly, every time. Jason notices and doesn't tease her.
  4. **The breakthrough.** Working from Delilah's remark, she has been building clones mature instead of copying her tired, drained self. They take nothing from her, start nearly empty, produce at full strength immediately and fill within minutes, where her old clones trickled.
  5. She shows up to training and her clone fights twice as long. Jason stops and asks how. She explains. He's delighted and a little jealous.
  6. The precision costs her: she could hold about nine before; she can hold three of these.

### Scene 5: "Why can't they?" (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Dinner, all three of them. Delilah has stayed late.
  2. Mira complains: three isn't enough, and she can't hold more.
  3. Delilah, half joking: "Too bad your clones can't just make their own clones."
  4. Mira: "Yeah. That'd be nice."
  5. Jason, mouth full: "Why can't they?"
  6. Make it a real three-way argument with interruptions:
     - Delilah: "Because it's impossible."
     - Mira: "Because I already get headaches with three."
     - Jason: "A lot of what you're calling headache is mana overhead, not thinking, and..."
     - Delilah cuts in: "Don't you start."
  7. He can't name one actual reason it's impossible. He says so, and he says he has no idea how she'd do it.
  8. Then he stops. He looks at Mira a beat too long, like he's just heard himself. He changes the subject to dessert.
  9. Cut the fusion and fire comparison, and the cooking-history claim.
- **Comic beat at the end:** Delilah leaves. Jason tries to make the dessert he promised and burns it. Mira learns he can't cook either. She laughs until she cries. "You let me think it was me!" "It was also you."

### End (about 200 words)
In bed, three clones spread through the cabin, their senses in hers. She does the math on doubling it. Dismisses it. Does it again. End on the math, not on a door image.

## Rules in play
- Three months to get quiet, years to get safe.
- Once someone has started, erasure can take memories but can't undo the understanding or stop production. That's why it's a cleanup tool for people who haven't started. Practicing on her own producing clone works because she's only taking five minutes of memory.
- The Box: only Jason builds it.
- Stage 3: mature clones start empty and fill fast. Nine drops to three.
