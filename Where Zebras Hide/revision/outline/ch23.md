# Chapter 23: Escalation

**When and where:** Early October. The Vermont base, a day's drive to Columbus on the same day Marcus's text arrives, then the Vermont base again two days later. A London TV segment plays on Jason's laptop in the last scene.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- The community met at the lake house and couldn't agree:
  - Owen wants to go public on the community's own terms.
  - Delilah wants to suppress harder.
  - The host wants to hide.
- Mira sided with Owen. Delilah sent her a message saying she loved her and thought she was wrong. Mira sent back one word: maybe.
- A video from São Paulo of a bottle that stopped in mid-air has been circulating for about a month.
- In Columbus, Marcus saw Mira warm a cold coffee in her hands. She called it an insulated cup. He texted asking where she got "that cup" and she hasn't answered.
- Mira learned erasure from Delilah and has only ever used it on her own clones.
- Jason agrees with Kael that the walls between people and the other side are thinning, through leaks. He doesn't know what to do about it.

## What this chapter does

The leak gets personal first, then public. Marcus has been digging because of what he saw Mira do, and she has to erase her own friend. Two days later a London anchor reads a leaked explanation on air to mock it, and that teaches people.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Marcus's text (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The text arrives in the morning. It is long. Since Labor Day he hasn't been able to stop thinking about her cup. He found the São Paulo video. He has been on forums, and he posted asking whether anyone had seen something like what his friend did. He isn't mocking her. He is excited.
  2. Mira feels sick. She thinks this is her doing.
  3. She asks Jason whether Marcus might be producing. Jason can't tell from here. They have to see him. They leave within the hour.

### Scene 2: Columbus (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** Marcus to be fine, and not to have to do this.
- **Beats:**
  1. They reach Columbus in the evening. She meets Marcus at a diner near his apartment, not the coffee shop. He is thrilled to see her. Jason waits outside.
  2. Jason senses him from the lot and tells her: Marcus hasn't started. He's close, and he has been chewing on it.
  3. Mira knows what this is. It is what Jason offered her in February, and she called it a violation. She knows what a gap feels like from the inside.
  4. Jason offers to do it. Under their agreement it is her call. She tells him it is hers to do: Marcus is her friend, and if someone is going to take something from him, it should be someone who loves him.
  5. She does it gently and precisely. She takes the cup, the forums and the weeks of wondering. His posts stay up, because erasing him doesn't touch them. Owen's people will deal with the posts.
  6. Marcus blinks and asks what they were talking about. She makes something up. He tells her about something ordinary at the office. She says goodbye and walks to the car.

### Scene 3: The car (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She cries in the car for most of the first hour. Jason drives with one hand on her knee.
  2. He says the one useful thing he has.
  3. She tells him Marcus wasn't the leak, and says why that is no comfort.
  4. She says she understands now why he offered it to her in February. She would still have said no. He says he knows.

### Scene 4: London (about 1,100 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Two days later, at the base. A London news anchor runs a segment mocking the magic videos, the São Paulo bottle and the rest. For laughs she reads aloud, line by line, an anonymous post that claims to explain how it's done. Mira watches on Jason's laptop.
  2. The post is a half-explanation, much better than it should be. It was leaked by someone in Owen's go-public group, Lise, acting alone. Owen calls Jason, horrified.
  3. Jason works the math out loud, flat, and doesn't like any of it. Every new producer leaves evidence: a body, a video, a symptom. Evidence sends people looking, and looking finds the explanation. He says Kael was right and that he has no plan to stop it. Mira asks how many is enough, and he answers.
  4. Over the next week, show the spread as news fragments and forum posts, not a summary. Three named viewers start producing: Hannah, a nurse in Leeds; Tom, a cab driver; and Mrs. Okafor, a retired teacher.
  5. The anchor didn't believe a word. She read it clearly, to millions.
  6. End on Mira reading Hannah's post from the hospital and closing the laptop.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to take Columbus from her, and doesn't, because they agreed.
- **On the leaks:** he agrees with Kael and doesn't know what to do. He works the numbers out loud in the car and at the base, flatly, and says so when he has nothing to propose.
- **Gentle:** the hand on her knee.

## Romance beat
Him offering and then not taking the choice from her, then driving her home.

## Old-life beat
Marcus himself, and the ordinary thing he talks about before she leaves.

## Rules in play
- Erasure can't undo understanding once production starts. Marcus hasn't started, so it still works on him.
- The train rule: the anchor's reading is a class for millions, so a few figure it out.
- Footage teaches some.
- The evidence loop.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "We'll know when we've crossed it, because things will start coming through that have never come through before." Jason says this to Mira in scene 4, answering her question about how many is enough.
- "Sorry, what were we talking about?" Marcus says this in the diner right after the erasure, to Mira, as if he'd lost his place.
- "You did it right. It's still awful." Jason says this to Mira in the car, as the one useful thing he has.
- "Marcus wasn't the leak. He was the one I could see." Mira says this to Jason in the car, when he starts to say the leak was not her doing.

## Limits
- Triplets: at most 1 (the three new producers).
- One body reaction per scene.
- Verbatim lines count toward every limit.

## Do not
- Make the anchor understand or believe.
- Have Jason do the erasure.
- Explain what was in the leaked post. The reader never learns the mechanism.
- Schedule jokes in the Columbus scene or the car.
