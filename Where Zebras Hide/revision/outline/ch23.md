# Chapter 23: Escalation

**When and where:** October. The Vermont base, then a drive to Columbus. A London TV segment plays on Mira's phone partway through.
**Length:** about 3,500 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- The community met and couldn't agree:
  - Owen (go public on our terms)
  - Delilah (suppress harder)
  - the host (hide)
- Mira sided with Owen. Delilah said, "I love you, but you're wrong."
- A São Paulo video of a coffee mug warming wrong has been circulating for a month.
- In Columbus, Marcus saw Mira warm a cold coffee in her hands. He texted asking where she got "that cup". She hasn't answered.
- Mira learned erasure from Delilah and has only ever used it on her own clones.

## What this chapter does

The leak gets personal. Marcus has been digging because of her. Separately, a London anchor reads a leaked explanation on air to mock it, and that teaches people. Mira has to erase her own friend.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Marcus (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Marcus's long text: since Labor Day he can't stop thinking about her cup. He found the São Paulo video. He's been on forums. He posted asking whether anyone had seen something like what his friend did. He's not mocking her; he's excited.
  2. Mira feels sick. **She did this.**
  3. She checks with Jason: is Marcus producing? Jason can't tell from here. They have to see him.

### Scene 2: London (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A London news anchor runs a segment mocking the "magic videos", the São Paulo mug and the rest. For laughs she reads aloud, line by line, an anonymous post that claims to explain how it's done.
  2. The post is a half-explanation, much better than it should be. It was leaked by someone in the go-public faction. Owen calls Jason, horrified: it was one of his people, Lise *(proposed name)*, acting alone.
  3. Jason, quietly: "Manchester was the warning. This is the thing it warned about."
  4. Within a week, three named viewers start producing:
     - Hannah, a nurse in Leeds
     - Tom, a cab driver
     - Mrs. Okafor, a retired teacher
  5. Show this as news fragments and forum posts, not a summary.
  6. The anchor didn't believe a word. She just read it clearly, to millions.
  7. **Jason's math.** Every new producer leaves evidence: a body, a video, a symptom. Evidence sends people looking, and looking finds the explanation. "How many is enough?" "I don't know. We'll know when we've crossed it, because things will start coming through that have never come through before."

### Scene 3: Columbus (about 1,100 words)
- **Mira wants** Marcus to be fine, and not to have to do this.
- **Beats:**
  1. They drive overnight. She meets Marcus at the coffee shop. He's thrilled to see her.
  2. Jason senses him: Marcus hasn't started. He's close, and he's been chewing on it.
  3. Mira knows exactly what this is. It's what Jason offered her in February, and what she called a violation. She knows what the gap will feel like from inside, because she gave herself one in May.
  4. Jason offers to do it. Under their agreement, it's her call. She says it's hers to do: "He's my friend. If someone's going to take something from him, it should be someone who loves him."
  5. She does it gently and precisely. She takes the cup, the forums and six weeks of wondering. His forum posts stay up; erasing him doesn't touch them. Owen's people will deal with the posts.
  6. Marcus blinks. "Sorry, what were we talking about?" She makes something up. He tells her about Dave's new carrot habit, and she laughs until it turns into something else and she has to go to the bathroom.

### Scene 4: The car (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She cries in the car, properly, for longer than four minutes.
  2. Jason drives and keeps one hand on her knee, and says the one useful thing: "You did it right. It's still awful. Both."
  3. She says she understands now why he offered it to her in the woods. "I still would've said no." "I know." Allowed: this is an answer, not filler.
- **End:** on the radio. Another news story about "the London magic hoax", someone laughing, and Mira switching it off.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to take this from her, and doesn't, because they agreed.
- **Wrong, and he knows it:** the shield isn't holding. He doesn't say "you were right" to anyone. He goes quiet and calculates.
- **Gentle:** the hand on her knee.

## Romance beat
Him not taking the choice from her, then driving her home.

## Old-life beat
Marcus himself, and Dave's carrots.

## Rules in play
- The train rule: the anchor's reading is a class for millions, so a few figure it out.
- Erasure can't undo understanding once production starts. Marcus hasn't started, so it still works on him.
- Footage teaches some.
- The evidence loop.
