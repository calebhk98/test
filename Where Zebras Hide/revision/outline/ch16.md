# Chapter 16: Arrival

**When and where:** About June 28. The other side, for about an hour.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is about four and a half months in. She can cycle, shield, sense crossings and erase memories, and holds three clones, sometimes four, all of them her.
- She has beaten a worn-out mid-tier on Earth, using clones from three angles.
- After Spain, Jason made one shaped jump toward eastern Canada (he can bias a random jump toward a region a few hundred miles across, never pick the spot). They landed in a Quebec field, crossed the border on foot through woods, bought a car, and drove to the Vermont property that is their base.
- The hunters can now find them on Earth by ordinary means, given time. The Vermont house is held under a name nobody has, which buys months and not years. They found the cabin because Delilah mentioned Jason's girl to someone she trusted and it travelled; Delilah has owned that. The other side is the one place nobody with a body follows. Jason told Mira this and called it a bad option.
- Mira said yes after weighing two bad places. She is also tired of being the one who gets protected, and she said so once.
- Jason held her hand while they crossed.
- Jason told her it would be dark. She nodded the way you nod at a number. She knows the other side runs on mana and has creatures.
- Her phone is in Vermont, switched off.

## What this chapter does

Take away her most basic sense in the first line. Make the place alien without making it evil. Then give her a photographer's act: she makes light where there has never been any.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Nothing for her eyes (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Her eyes are open and there is nothing for them to do. No sky glow, no edges, no gray at the corners.
  2. She grabs Jason's arm and he leaves it where it is.
  3. **The mana.** Everything produces it: the ground, the air, whatever moves nearby. It is loud everywhere, and not like a sound.
  4. Jason tells her to clamp her shields tighter, everything she has. It becomes bearable.
  5. Existing here costs mana constantly, the way staying warm costs calories.
  6. It smells of nothing she can name. She holds onto the memory of the Vermont woods from just before they crossed.
- **By the end:** she can stand, and she has learned that the dark is not going to let up.

### Scene 2: Feeling the shape of things (about 550 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Through mana sense she starts to read the place: the density of the ground cover, the different textures of different plants, things moving at different distances. Describe what it is: a whole-body sense of pressure and texture.
  2. **The plants:** ankle-deep moss that presses on her shield the way water presses on a hull. Taller stalks that draw mana out of the air.
  3. Jason is unsettled, and she can feel it. His suppression makes sense to her now: even quiet, he is the loudest thing for a long way.
- **By the end:** she can move through the place by feel, and she understands why he is afraid here.

### Scene 3: Light (about 650 words)
- **Mira wants** to see, just once.
- **Beats:**
  1. She asks whether she can make light. He says practitioners can, but it is expensive here, and nothing here knows what light is, so it won't help her see anything that matters.
  2. She does it anyway: a small light in her cupped palm.
  3. For about three seconds she sees Jason's face, closer than she thought, and the moss under her feet, colorless, the grey of something that never needed to be any color. Show these two.
  4. Her photographer's brain fires on exposure: no shadow detail, nothing with a highlight. She thinks this may be the first light this patch of ground has ever had, and doesn't know if that is true.
  5. It costs far more than she expected. She lets it go.
  6. Jason says it was worth it.
- **By the end:** she has seen something and paid for it, and she would do it again.

### Scene 4: Something that lives here (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to survive without seeing.
- **Beats:**
  1. Ten minutes in, she feels it: about the size of a large dog, much denser than anything on Earth, with a ridge along its back that thrums before it strikes. It is coming straight at them.
  2. Jason tells her not to drop her shield and to match whatever it does.
  3. It hits hard, a committed strike. She staggers and rebuilds the side that took it.
  4. **Her idea, new to her:** she cannot see and cannot feel the edges of anything, so she puts a clone a few steps to her left. Through two bodies she can tell where it is, the way two ears place a sound. It works.
  5. She finds the gap between its strikes and pushes everything through it at once.
  6. It does not die. It decides she is not worth it and withdraws.
- **By the end:** she has fought here, and the fight was hers.

### Scene 5: After (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason asks if she is good. She answers that she is blind and wants to be clear about it. He laughs out loud, which she had not heard from him since they crossed. He says she is not blind, she is using the wrong sense.
  2. She points out that she has had this sense four months and eyes for twenty-six years. He says she will adjust.
  3. He tells her the shield held. She says it held expensively. He says that is the standard here.
  4. She reaches for her coat pocket to check the time, and remembers the phone is in Vermont.
- **End:** following him through the dark by the shape of his mana. Don't compare it to sight.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her to come out of this alive and wanting to come back.
- **Fear:** he is afraid here, as he has been every time, and she can feel it in his mana.
- **Laughs** once, after the fight. She hasn't heard him do that on this side before.

## Romance beat
She grabs his arm and he leaves it where it is. She sees his face in her three seconds of light.

## Old-life beat
She reaches for her pocket for the phone she left in Vermont, to see what time it is.

## Rules in play
- No natural light. Practitioners can make it, at a cost.
- Heat moves only by touch.
- Existing here costs mana constantly.
- Plants press on shields.
- Everything is sensed through mana.
- Two bodies place a sound the way two ears do.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I'm blind. I want to be clear that I'm blind." / "You're not blind. You're using the wrong sense." Mira says the first to Jason right after the creature withdraws, when he asks if she is good. He answers with the second.

## Limits for this chapter
- Reversal constructions ("not X, but Y"): 0.
- Light similes in narration: 0.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Analogies: one per scene.
- Body reactions: one per scene.

## Do not
- Show any creature with eyes.
- Describe how anything looks outside Scene 3.
- Make the creature evil.
- Add jokes to the fight.
