# Chapter 10: The Box

**When and where:** Late April. The cabin and the woods around it.
**Length:** about 3,200 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has seen one creature: small, eyeless and translucent. It felled a tree, and Jason killed it.
- She learned he'd killed several more without telling her, and named his pattern: deciding about her and telling her later. He apologized. They're close again, but she's watching him now.
- She can make a handful of clones that move a little on their own. She fixes their small flaws because they bother her.
- Her phone stays off except in town on the supply run.
- Her quiet window closes in about two weeks.

## What this chapter does

- Jason's best invention, and the first moral fight between them that isn't about her safety.
- Her clones change: they become her, all of them.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The capture (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A stronger stray crosses near the creek. It's dog-sized and low, with a broad faceless head like a dish and a grey, matte hide that gives back no highlight.
  2. Jason doesn't kill it. He fights it for a minute, then does something: she sees a boxed wall of pressure close around it and squeeze smaller and smaller until it's gone from sight.
  3. Inside, he explains, it is still fighting. It thinks it is still in the woods. A figure of him that the Box makes fights it on a loop. The figure is a crude shape of Jason built from his mana, with no mind and no senses of its own. It is not a clone, and Jason can't clone.

### Scene 2: Watching the figure lose (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to understand what she's watching. Then she wants him to stop.
- **Beats:**
  1. He lets her sense into it, as if pressing her ear to a wall. She feels the figure fight the creature, break, re-form and fight again. Dozens of times.
  2. Each round it comes back a little different and lasts a little longer, because the Box rebuilds it from what went wrong.
  3. She realizes this is how he got so good: he has watched it lose hundreds of times and fixed what lost.
  4. She asks whether he feels it when the figure breaks. He says no. It has no senses and nothing of him in it. The only one in there who feels any of it is the creature.
  5. He is excited explaining the design, how the loop works and how it adjusts. It's the first time she sees how much he loves what he built. That makes the next scene harder.

### Scene 3: The fight (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** him to see it the way she does.
- **Beats:**
  1. She calls it a prison. He calls it a training tool. She says it's a prison the creature doesn't know it's in.
  2. He also calls it a battery. She asks what it powers. He says it helps with some things and goes back to talking about the loop.
  3. **A real fracture.** He thinks she's being sentimental about an animal that would have eaten her. She thinks he's comfortable with something he shouldn't be. Both of them have a point.
  4. They don't resolve it. She sleeps badly. He comes to her door at midnight and tells her he isn't going to let it go, but that he heard her. She says that isn't the same thing. He agrees it isn't.
  5. She asks to try the Box herself. He says no, not yet, and gives real reasons: she needs to be able to survive a fight on her own for at least a few seconds before it is worth sending a clone in.

### Scene 4: More of her (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Over the next days, Jason sees that her clones' mana is growing. That shouldn't be possible. He is thrilled and unsettled, and tests it.
  2. His theory: she's been fixing the small flaws for weeks. The bodies got complete enough that they started producing mana on their own, the way a person does.
  3. At the same time, she tells him she can't keep their senses apart anymore. When she's in the kitchen and a clone is on the porch, she's on the porch too. Fully.
  4. **One mind.** She tries to explain what it is like, and he asks her to say it plainly. He tells her that isn't how it works for him: the figure in his Box is a tool, and what she describes is people. She says it's how it works for her.
  5. A small new thing: for a second, she can't tell him which body she "started" in this morning, and finds she doesn't care.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her to see why he's proud of the Box.
- **Is wrong about:** how much the prison matters to her.
- **Proud:** explaining the loop's design, clearly and with real pleasure.
- **Doesn't say:** what the battery powers.

## Rules in play
- The Box is a prison, a training tool and a battery, and only Jason can build one. A construct made of his mana fights inside it. It is not a clone.
- Clones: they produce mana, and they are one mind. Don't use the word "Stage" in narration.
- She doesn't experience a main body. That's just beginning here.
