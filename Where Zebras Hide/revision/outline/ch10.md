# Chapter 10: The Box

**When and where:** Late April. The cabin and the woods around it.
**Length:** about 3,200 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has seen one creature: small, eyeless and translucent. It felled a tree, and Jason killed it.
- She learned he'd killed several more without telling her, and named his pattern: deciding about her and telling her later. He apologized. They're close again, but she's watching him now.
- She can make a handful of clones that move a little on their own. She fixes their small flaws because they bother her.
- Her quiet window closes in about two weeks.

## What this chapter does

- Jason's best invention, and the first moral fight between them that isn't about her safety.
- Her clones change: they become her, all of them.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The capture (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A stronger stray crosses near the creek. It's dog-sized, low, with a broad faceless head like a dish and a grey hide that seems to drink light.
  2. Jason doesn't kill it. He fights it for a minute, then does something: she sees a boxed wall of pressure close around it and squeeze smaller and smaller until it's gone from sight.
  3. Inside, he explains, it's still fighting. It thinks it's still in the woods. A copy of him is fighting it on a loop.

### Scene 2: Watching him die (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to understand what she's watching. Then she wants him to stop.
- **Beats:**
  1. He lets her sense into it, as if pressing her ear to a wall. She feels his clone fight the creature, die, reset, and fight again. Dozens of times.
  2. Each time the clone does something a little differently and lasts a little longer.
  3. She realizes this is how he got so good: by dying hundreds of times on purpose.
  4. "Do you feel them? When they die?" He hesitates, then tells the truth: "A flinch. Like a dream where you fall. You get used to it." Don't make that a throwaway answer. It's the most frightening thing he's told her.
  5. He's excited explaining the design, how the loop works and how it learns. It's the first time she sees how much he loves what he built. That makes the next scene harder.

### Scene 3: The fight (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** him to see it the way she does.
- **Beats:**
  1. "It's a prison." "It's a training tool." "It's a prison it doesn't know it's in."
  2. He also calls it a battery. She asks what it powers. He says it helps with "some things" and changes the subject. She notices, and lets it go for now. The real answer comes later in the book.
  3. **A real fracture.** He thinks she's being sentimental about an animal that would have eaten her. She thinks he's comfortable with something he shouldn't be. Both of them have a point.
  4. They don't resolve it. She sleeps badly. He comes to her door at midnight and says, "I'm not going to let it go. But I heard you." She says, "That's not the same thing." He says, "I know it's not."
  5. She asks to try it herself. He says no, not yet, and gives real reasons: she needs to survive a fight on her own for at least a few seconds before a clone can learn anything.

### Scene 4: More of her (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Over the next days, Jason notices her clones' mana is growing. That shouldn't be possible. He's thrilled and unsettled, and tests it three ways.
  2. His theory: she's been fixing the small flaws for weeks. The bodies got complete enough that they started producing mana on their own, the way a person does when they understand.
  3. At the same time, she tells him she can't keep their senses apart anymore. When she's in the kitchen and a clone is on the porch, she's on the porch too. Fully.
  4. **One mind.** She tries to explain: "It's not that they're like me. They're me. There's just more of me." He looks at her for a long moment and says, "That's not how it works for me." She says, "Well, it's how it works for me."
  5. A small new thing: for a second, she can't tell him which body she "started" in this morning, and finds she doesn't care.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her to see why he's proud of the Box.
- **Is wrong about:** how much the prison matters to her.
- **Lights up:** explaining the loop's design, too fast, with his hands.
- **Hides:** what the battery powers. That's his pattern again, and the reader should notice it even though Mira lets it slide.

## Rules in play
- The Box is a prison, a training tool and a battery, and only Jason can build one.
- Clones: Stage 2. They produce mana, and they are one mind. Don't use the word "Stage" in narration.
- She doesn't experience a main body. That's just beginning here.
