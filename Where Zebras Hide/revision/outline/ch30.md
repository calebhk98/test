# Chapter 30: Threshold

**When and where:** Day 11, late afternoon. A field and tree line near the Vermont base. Theo's group is at a church camp six miles down the road, inside the sweep of Jason's lockdown.
**Length:** about 3,000 words.
**You also get:** the old draft of this chapter. Use it for what happens and in what order. Do not copy its sentences except the verbatim ones, and apply the fix list below to anything you do keep.

## Where things stand

- Day 11. The thing in Rajasthan left yesterday. The estimate of the dead is about a hundred million, counting the collapse of water, power and food across northern India.
- Mira has held about 110 of herself for nine days, and about a hundred of herself are out in the world now. They are not in the field. Last night she rested ten hours, and it cost eleven lives.
- She can make clones that make clones, and the chain keeps running if she lets it. In mid-August she reached about a thousand and reabsorbing them knocked her out for hours. On day 3 she held about 3,000 for an hour. Two nights ago she held about 20,000 for a night. Last night, pulling back ninety at once was the worst since August.
- In July, a talking scout crossed into Vermont. Jason boxed it. It said that its masters would want to know where it went. It never went home.
- She and Jason are a couple. She told him she couldn't stand him forgetting her.
- Theo, the seventeen-year-old natural, is in a group Owen's people drove up from Hartford this morning, after the Hartford line fell during her rest. They are at the church camp. If Jason's lockdown drops, fifty untrained producers there are a meal.
- When she pulls bodies back they fold into whichever body she is closest to. Which body that is doesn't matter to her. She can only make new bodies within about a mile of an existing one.
- Jason can close an existing Box's walls around a new place in minutes, at great cost.

## What this chapter does

The scout's master comes looking for its scout and finds them. Mira knows what she has to do, starts with a few, and then stops steering. Chains of her make chains, and she becomes a colony of about eight million. She wins. To pull herself back fast enough, she breaks. The last five of her can see Jason. Then dark.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: It arrives (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. It arrives wrong, and heavy, like the scout and unlike a stray.
  2. It came through here, in rural nothing, because it's looking for something specific. It knows Jason's signature, because that's what its scout came to investigate in July.
  3. **What it is:** nearly human-sized, the same kind of being as the scout, but taller, with more joints, and a smooth head with a ridge where a face should be. It shifts its density, so it blurs at the edges of her sight and doesn't want to be seen clearly.
  4. It speaks, its voice from beside it. It is polite in a way that's worse than a threat. It speaks to Jason first, then, curious, to Mira. See the verbatim lines.

### Scene 2: The math (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She does the math, fast. Jason could maybe contain it, and it would wreck him. More things are coming; she can feel pressure at the edge of her range.
  2. The church camp is inside the lockdown's sweep. If the lockdown drops, Theo's group is a meal.
  3. Jason's job: while the creature talks, he spends several minutes locking the area down (no teleporting, no crossing, no messages home). He also starts closing an existing Box's walls around the place where Mira stands, at great cost, in case. He says so to her quietly, and she catches only half of it.
  4. She knows the colony would be the biggest meal on Earth, so she has to finish fast. She starts with two hundred, as bait.

### Scene 3: Two hundred, then forty thousand (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Two hundred of her in the field, the tree line and the road. One walks forward and dies in four seconds, and in those four seconds the creature has to look at her instead of at Mira.
  2. **Its tactics:** it kills the two hundred almost casually. It recognizes the danger when the chains reach about forty thousand. It tries to send a message home, and Jason's lockdown stops it, and Jason pays for that. It pulls back, considering, then commits.
  3. Jason stays where he is, because the lockdown needs him to. He calls her name once. Five of her look at him; the rest watch the creature. One sentence from those five about the two hundred dead bodies being her.
  4. The creature is far stronger than any one of her. It is one body with one set of reactions. Forty thousand from every side is still not enough.

### Scene 4: Eight million (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Each of the forty thousand makes more of her, and each of those makes more. She stops steering it. The growth is exponential, and it is how she reaches about eight million and loses herself.
  2. Where they are: bodies spread for miles across fields, woods and roads, standing shoulder to shoulder on the county highway.
  3. The creature attacks and breaks against layered shields, like a wave against a breakwater. The colony answers: eight million of everything she knows, from every direction at once.
  4. One reversal. For a moment it finds the weak point and starts tearing through bodies faster than they can close the gap. It is winning. Then the colony shifts all at once, and the gap closes.
  5. The creature is dead, as far as she can tell, and she knows it will not stay dead.

### Scene 5: Pulling back (about 500 words)
- **Mira wants** to pull them back slowly. She can't.
- **Beats:**
  1. Why she has to hurry: Jason's lockdown is failing; he's spent, and it is coming down in patches. Every second the colony stands in the field it is the biggest meal on Earth. If the lockdown drops, the next crossing comes for the colony and for the camp.
  2. She knows what pulling back ninety did last night, and what a thousand did in August. She does it anyway.
  3. Four million, then three million, then two million. The headache becomes a sound. One hundred thousand. Ten thousand.

### Scene 6: The last five (about 200 words)
- The last five of her are the ones who can see Jason. He has reached her at last, and he is shouting. What he shouts is her name and then to stop. She sees his mouth and doesn't hear it. The sentence breaks off.
- She feels the Box he started close around her, its edges familiar, his architecture. Then she loses it.
- She pulls the last of them back.

## Mira's voice in this chapter
- She is still Mira inside the colony.
- No jokes, no photographer's observation, no old-life beat.

## Jason in this chapter
- **His role:** the lockdown, then reaching her.
- He's frightened in a way she's only seen a few times.
- The Box closing around her at the end is his last act on the page. He closes it without asking her, though he told her he might.

## Romance beat
The five of her who can see him, still Mira and still his.

## Rules in play
- The intelligent ones come back eventually with their memories, so "dead" is for now.
- Lockdown blocks messages.
- Clones are all her, with no main body.
- Reabsorbing at scale can kill her.
- Many clones are loud, and a colony of this size is a meal.
