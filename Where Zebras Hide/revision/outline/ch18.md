# Chapter 18: The Scout

**When and where:** Late July, near dawn, then about thirty hours. The woods around the Vermont base, on Earth.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has crossed many times for a few hours each, to practice.
- She learned Jason pays for their crossings partly by drawing on the captured creatures in his Box. They compromised: fewer trips, and he tells her each time.
- The Box holds each captive with a clone of Jason fighting it on a loop.
- She can layer and aim shields almost by reflex. Kael, who had been following them, saw it and called it expert work.
- She holds three or four full-strength clones, all of them her.
- She and Jason are close, and angry and unresolved about the battery.
- She has never met anything that can talk.
- She has her phone with her. There is no signal at the house or in the woods around it.

## What this chapter does

- Something intelligent comes to Earth, drawn by Jason's repeated crossings.
- Mira is essential in the fight.
- Then Jason goes down, and she has to keep them both alive alone.
- This is Act III's climax, and it is hers.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: It arrives (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira wakes first. Jason is exhausted from the crossings and still asleep. Something has crossed nearby, and it feels organized, like a person standing in a room, not an animal.
  2. She wakes him. Every minute he sleeps, it stands in the trees and listens.
  3. They go out. It is waiting at the tree line.
  4. **What it looks like** (CREATURES.md): about human height and built wrong, with too many joints in the arms and a smooth head with a ridge instead of a face. Its surface is matte, a dull color that doesn't commit to anything.
  5. **It speaks.** It shapes the air with mana, so the voice comes from slightly behind and to the left of where it stands. It learned their language by listening. It says, politely and with curiosity, that it was sent to find out what keeps going back and forth, and that it is curious about Jason.
- **By the end:** they know it can talk and that it knows who Jason is.

### Scene 2: Holding it (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep it from leaving with what it knows.
- **Jason wants** the same, and he needs time.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason says, "Don't let it leave."
  2. While it talks, he spends a few slow minutes locking down everything for five miles: no teleporting, no crossing, no messages. That takes most of what he has.
  3. Then he starts building a Box around the whole area from the outside in, which takes minutes, while he also fights it. He can't do all of it at once.
  4. **Mira's essential action:** she and her clones hold its attention on one side, so it can't focus on Jason. She also feels it gather itself to send something home before the lockdown is complete, and shouts it. Jason blocks the message in time because she warned him.
  5. It tries to teleport, and can't. It tries to cross back, and can't. It tries to send a message, and can't. Each time it realizes what is happening, it gets less polite.
  6. It is afraid, and that surprises her. It says they will want to know where it went. This is her first sense that these beings have masters.
  7. It tells them that even if they kill it, it will come back. Jason says he knows, and that is why he isn't killing it.
  8. Before the Box closes, Mira says out loud that it talks and it is afraid. Jason says it is this or it goes home and tells them. She doesn't stop him, and she doesn't take it back.
  9. The Box closes. Jason puts a clone of himself in his place inside it, steps out, and drops.
- **By the end:** the scout is held, and Jason is down.

### Scene 3: Alone (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** him to wake up, and nothing to come.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason is out cold, breathing, unreachable. She can't move him far, and she can't cross.
  2. The fight was the loudest thing these woods have ever felt. Things start to come. They cross beyond the five-mile line, where the lockdown ends, and walk in.
  3. **Strays first**, in the afternoon: two, then four. Her clones handle them.
  4. **A mid-tier at night.** She can't hold it off and protect his body at the same time, so she has to choose positions and accept a clone dying.
  5. She tries the mana message to Delilah, badly, the way she saw Jason do it. It doesn't go. The lockdown Jason put up is still standing around them, and she can't take it down. Even if it had gone, Delilah is fourteen hours away in Tennessee, and her phone shows no signal.
  6. She doesn't sleep. Around 3 am she scrolls the old Columbus photos on her phone to stay awake: Jess's cat, the office window, Nora's graduation photos that Nora sent. The screen light is the only light on the porch. She talks to him while she watches the dark: about Columbus, about her parents, about being angry at him. She tells him she doesn't know what she would do if he didn't wake up.
- **Details to use:** the dew on his face, which she wipes off. Her clones sitting with their backs to his on all sides, a ring of the same woman.
- **By the end:** it is dawn, he is still unconscious, and she is still holding.

### Scene 4: He wakes (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. About thirty hours later, he wakes. She is there. He sees the ring of clones and understands what happened.
  2. He reaches up and touches her face. He tells her that waking him first is why it never got inside the house.
  3. He says, "You held." She says, "I held." He doesn't make a speech.
  4. He tells her what the scout was: a weak servant, sent to look. He says that if that was a weak one, and doesn't finish.
- **By the end:** she knows what is coming is bigger than what she just fought.

### End (about 300 words)
On the porch at dawn, she counts her clones: three, four. She decides she never wants to be alone with his body in the dark again. She tries to make a fifth body the way she made the first four, out of herself, and it fails, and she tries again. End on her trying.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to keep the scout from reporting back. He spends himself completely to do it.
- **Shows:** fear when it speaks. Gratitude that he can't put into words when he wakes.
- **Says** that waking him first is why it never got inside.

## Romance beat
Thirty hours guarding him. He touches her face when he wakes.

## Old-life beat
At 3 am, guarding him, she scrolls old photos of Columbus on her phone to stay awake: Jess's cat, the office window, Nora's graduation photos that Nora sent her. Lead: she cannot sleep, and the phone is in her pocket (Scene 3).

## Rules in play
- The intelligent ones have souls and come back, so Jason boxes the scout instead of killing it.
- Lockdown blocks teleporting, crossing and messages, out to about five miles. It is draining and slow to set up.
- Building a Box around an area takes minutes and most of what he has.
- The scout was drawn by the repeated crossings.
- The mana message.
- Clones are all her; she feels them die.
- A clone of Jason fights the Box's captive on a loop.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "Don't let it leave." Jason says it to Mira while the scout is still talking.
- "They will want to know where I went." The scout says it when its message fails and it is afraid.
- "Even if you kill me, I'll come back. My soul will remember." / "I know. That's why I'm not killing you." The scout says the first, and Jason answers with the second, before the Box closes.
- "If that was a weak one..." Jason says it to Mira when he wakes and tells her what the scout was. He doesn't finish.

## Set up in this chapter
- The scout says "They will want to know where I went" when its message fails. Give it in a flat, frightened voice. Mira does not follow it up and Jason does not answer it.
- After the fight Mira counts her clones and decides three or four is too few. Show it as a thing she does, not a thing she says: she sits with them ringed around his body and doesn't like how few they are.
- Her mana message to Delilah fails. Give it half a sentence.
- Mira woke him first. Jason says so when he wakes, in one sentence.

## Limits for this chapter
- The scout's voice: describe the beside-it effect once, then just give its lines.
- Show that the scout is far beyond anything she has met. Jason's account in Scene 4 is where it turns out to be weak.
- Use the word servant once. Don't use the word slave.
- Triplets: at most 1.

## Do not
- Have Mira cross alone. She can't.
- Give the scout a name, or have it say who sent it.
- Resolve the battery argument. It is quieter now, and still there.
- Add jokes. This is danger and grief.
