# Chapter 18: The Scout

**When and where:** Late July, near dawn, then about thirty hours. The woods around the Vermont base, on Earth.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has crossed many times for a few hours each.
- She learned Jason pays for their crossings partly with the captured creatures in his Box. They compromised: fewer trips, and he tells her each time.
- She can layer and aim shields almost by reflex. A practitioner named Kael saw it and called it expert work.
- She holds three or four full-strength clones, which are all her.
- She and Jason are close, angry and unresolved about the battery.
- She has never met anything that can talk.

## What this chapter does

- Something intelligent comes to Earth, and it's the result of Jason's repeated crossings.
- Mira is essential in the fight.
- Then Jason goes down, and she has to keep them both alive alone.
- This is Act III's climax, and it's hers.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: It arrives (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Mira wakes first. Jason is exhausted from the crossings and still asleep. Something has crossed nearby, and it feels **organized**, like a person standing in a room, not an animal.
  2. She wakes him. Her waking him first matters, and he says so later.
  3. They go out. It's waiting at the tree line.
  4. **What it looks like** (CREATURES.md):
     - about human height, built wrong
     - too many joints in the arms
     - a smooth head with a ridge instead of a face
     - its body seems heavier than it should be, then barely there
  5. In daylight it looks unfinished, as if nobody chose its color.
  6. **It speaks.** It shapes the air with mana, so the voice comes from slightly behind and to the left of where it stands. It learned their language by listening. It says something curious and polite about Jason: it was sent to find out what keeps going back and forth.

### Scene 2: Holding it (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep it from leaving with what it knows.
- **Jason wants** the same, and he needs time.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason's plan, in four fast words: "Don't let it leave."
  2. He locks down everything for five miles: no teleporting, no crossing, no messages. That takes him most of what he has.
  3. Then he starts building a Box around the whole area from the outside in, which takes minutes, while he also fights it. He can't do all of it at once.
  4. **Mira's essential action:**
     - She and her clones hold its attention on one side, so it can't focus on Jason.
     - She also feels it gather itself to send something home before Jason does, and shouts it.
     - Jason blocks the message in time because she warned him.
  5. It tries to teleport, and can't. It tries to cross back, and can't. It tries to send a message, and can't. Each time it realizes what's happening, it gets less polite.
  6. It's afraid, and that surprises her. "They will want to know where I went." Her first sense that these beings have masters.
  7. "Even if you kill me, I'll come back. My soul will remember." Jason: "I know. That's why I'm not killing you."
  8. The Box closes. Jason puts a clone of himself in his place inside it, steps out, and drops.

### Scene 3: Alone (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** him to wake up, and nothing to come.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason is out cold, breathing, unreachable. She can't move him far, and she can't cross.
  2. The fight was the loudest thing these woods have ever felt. Things start to come.
  3. **Strays first**, in the afternoon: two, then four. Her clones handle them.
  4. **A mid-tier at night.** She can't hold it off and protect his body at the same time, so she has to choose positions and accept a clone dying.
  5. She tries the mana message to Delilah, badly, the way she saw Jason do it. It doesn't go. The lockdown Jason put up is still standing around them, and she can't take it down. Even if it had gone, Delilah is fourteen hours away in Tennessee.
  6. She doesn't sleep. She talks to him while she watches the dark: about Columbus, about her parents, about being angry at him. One true thing she says while he can't hear: "I don't know what I'd do."
- **Details to use:** the dew on his face, which she wipes off. Her clones sitting with their backs to his on all sides, like a ring of the same woman.

### Scene 4: He wakes (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. About thirty hours later, he wakes. She's there. He sees the ring of clones and understands what happened.
  2. "You held." "I held." He doesn't make a speech. He reaches up and touches her face.
  3. He tells her what the scout was: a weak servant, sent to look. "If that was a weak one..." She doesn't finish.

### End (about 300 words)
Mira on the porch at dawn. Three or four of her isn't enough. Not for that. She doesn't know how she'll get more, but she decides she has to. End on her deciding.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to keep the scout from reporting back. He spends himself completely to do it.
- **Shows:** fear when it speaks. Gratitude that he can't put into words when he wakes.
- **Acknowledges:** that her waking him first saved them.

## Romance beat
Thirty hours guarding him. He touches her face when he wakes.

## Old-life beat
At 3 am, guarding him, she scrolls old photos of Columbus on her phone to stay awake: Jess's cat, the office window, Nora's graduation photos that Nora sent her.

## Rules in play
- The intelligent ones have souls and come back, so Jason boxes it instead of killing it.
- Lockdown blocks teleporting, crossing and messages.
- The scout was drawn by the repeated crossings.
- The mana message.
- Clones are all her; she feels them die.

## Plant for later

- Her decision to get more.
- Her attempted message to Delilah.

## Limits for this chapter
- The scout's voice: describe the beside-it effect once, then just give its lines.
- "Extremely powerful" and "weak servant" together: show that it's far beyond anything she's met, then reveal that it's weak to them. Don't say both in one sentence.
- Triplets: at most 1.
- The word "slave": 0. Say "servant", once.

## Do not
- Have Mira cross alone. She can't.
- Give the scout a name, or have it say who sent it.
- Resolve the battery argument. It's quieter now, and still there.
