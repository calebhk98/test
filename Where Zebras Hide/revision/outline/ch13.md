# Chapter 13: The Hunters

**When and where:** Early June. Day one, from breakfast to dusk to dawn of the next morning. The cabin, then ocean twice, then a hillside in Galicia, Spain.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. This is a big chapter. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four months in, quiet, and can fight a little. She has three full-strength clones, which are all her.
- She knows how to erase memories and hates that she does.
- She knows the people from the Columbus sidewalk wanted what Jason knows. They saw her with him, which is why she can't go home. She doesn't know who they are or why they want it.
- She has named Jason's pattern once: deciding about her and telling her later. He promised to stop.
- Delilah visits most afternoons.
- Her phone is off and stays in her coat. Her bag, with her wallet and keys, is with her. Jason has his backpack, with his laptop and cash.

## What this chapter does

The hunters come back. Jason tells her right away. Mira fights a real creature and wins her own way. Then she learns what Jason did for her in Chapter 1, and it changes how she sees him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Day one (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to know what they're going to do about it.
- **Beats:**
  1. At breakfast, Jason has told her that people are camped about a mile out. Two of them, maybe three. He tells her the day he feels them, and says that's what he promised.
  2. They argue about leaving. She wants to go now. He wants to stay one day, and gives his reason: the hunters are watching, not attacking, and if he and Mira move now the hunters will follow and search the hills, and the nearest practitioner is Delilah, forty miles away. He wants Delilah warned first. Mira agrees, reluctantly.
  3. He recognizes them: two were on the sidewalk in Columbus. He names one: "Owen. He's the one who talks."
  4. Why are they only watching? He thinks they're waiting for a third person who they believe can pin a teleport at its start. They're wrong, and Jason knows it. Mira asks how they found the cabin. He doesn't know. Someone talked.
  5. **The ownership argument starts here** and doesn't finish. She asks what they want. He says something he made. She says give it to them. He says no. He says something cold about what he'll do if they come near her, and she doesn't recognize his face for a second.
  6. He sends Delilah a mana message telling her to stay away and to go somewhere else for a few days. He sends it with a lot of concentration and slightly too much force. Mira has never seen it done.

### Scene 2: Dusk (about 1,100 words)
- **Mira wants** to survive, and to not need him.
- **Beats:**
  1. Months of mana at the cabin have been building up: two practitioners, the Box, Delilah's visits. It finally draws something. This is the second evening the hunters have watched.
  2. Two mid-tiers cross at dusk. They're big-dog to small-bear sized, long-limbed and pale, with heads like folded fans that open toward prey. They move together, as if they share one sense.
  3. Jason tells her to feel for the crossing itself, the pressure of something coming through. That's the most useful sense she can grow.
  4. He takes the larger one. She takes the smaller.
  5. **Her own idea:** she sends her three clones in from three sides, so it can't face all of her at once. It splits its attention, and she gets the gap.
  6. It's messy. It hits her shield and the cost of holding it empties her. One clone dies, and she feels it like a dream of falling. She wins.
  7. Jason was ready to step in the whole time and didn't need to.
  8. The hunters watched from the tree line, and Jason knew it.

### Scene 3: The porch (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She sits on the porch steps, shaking and empty.
  2. Jason sits beside her and says what he thinks, straight out. He asks where she got the three sides, and says he never showed her that. He says it was really good, and his voice catches.
  3. She leans on his shoulder.

### Scene 4: Dawn (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The third hunter arrived in the night. At dawn they close in, and the third does whatever they believe pins a teleport.
  2. It doesn't matter. Jason doesn't fight. He takes her arm and goes.
  3. **Full range this time.** Ocean. Dark blue and cold, no land. Her shields hold the water off without her asking them to. A tug on her reserves.
  4. Again: ocean. Again: a grass hillside, grey sky, wind, the distant hum of traffic.
  5. Mira says they were just in the ocean. Twice. Jason says yes.

### Scene 5: What he did in February (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to understand why it was different the first time.
- **Beats:**
  1. Walking toward the traffic, she asks why they didn't land in the ocean back in February.
  2. He explains, almost embarrassed. That night she had no shields, and open water would have killed her in minutes. So in the middle of the attack he shaped the range on the fly: south, away from the Great Lakes and the coast, to make landing in water very unlikely. Tennessee instead of Lake Erie or the Atlantic. He states the cost himself, in one flat sentence.
  3. She doesn't say anything. She takes his hand while they walk. He keeps walking.
  4. She asks why he doesn't just fight them. He answers.
  5. She says he leaves everything. He answers that too.

### End (about 200 words)
A small hotel somewhere in Galicia, Spain. Gas-station food, because that's what was open. Jason is already scrolling property listings on his laptop, and Mira looks at the paper map he picked up at the gas station. End on something specific, such as the gas-station sandwich in its plastic triangle.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to warn Delilah and not be found. He tells her right away this time.
- **His temper:** a flash of cold anger at the hunters.
- **His love, in action:** the range he shaped in February. The pride on the porch.

## Romance beat
Her hand in his on the road. He keeps walking.

## Old-life beat
None this chapter.

## Rules in play
- Small things notice by scent, and mana built up over months draws bigger things.
- Randomized teleport: not draining for Jason, open-surface landings, and a range he can shape or leave full. He can never pick the spot. It can't be blocked or tracked, and the hunters don't know that yet.
- Mana message.
- Clones are one mind; she feels a clone die.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "Owen. He's the one who talks." Jason says it to Mira when she asks which of the people he recognizes is in charge.
- "Where did you get that? The three sides. I never showed you that." Jason says it to Mira on the porch steps after the fight, in answer to what she just did.
- "It took everything I had that night to make it do that." Jason says it to Mira on the road in Spain, in answer to her asking why they didn't land in the ocean in February.
- "Because I don't have anything to prove to them. And I can leave." Jason says it to Mira, in answer to her asking why he doesn't just fight the hunters.
- "I've been leaving since I was seventeen. It's the thing I'm best at." Jason says it to Mira, in answer to her saying he leaves everything. Write nothing after it that tells the reader how to take it.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Describe the fight with the three angles in fresh terms, not as a rerun of any earlier fight.

## Do not
- Have Jason fight the hunters.
- Finish the ownership argument. That's the next chapter.
- Leave Delilah exposed. The mana message covers her.
- Have the phone come out.
