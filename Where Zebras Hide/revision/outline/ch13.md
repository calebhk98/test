# Chapter 13: The Hunters

**When and where:** Early June. Dinner on day two of the hunters' watch, then dusk, then dawn. The cabin, then ocean twice, then a hillside in Galicia, Spain.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. This is a big chapter. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four months in, quiet, and can fight a little. She has three full-strength clones, which are all her.
- She knows how to erase memories and hates that she does.
- She knows the people from the Columbus sidewalk wanted what Jason knows. She doesn't know who they are or why.
- She has named Jason's pattern once: deciding about her and telling her later. He promised to stop.
- Delilah visits most afternoons.
- A truck slowed on the road in February. Jason said "probably nothing".

## What this chapter does

The hunters come back. Jason has kept it from her again. Mira fights a real creature and wins her own way. Then she learns what Jason did for her in Chapter 1, and it changes how she sees him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Two days (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to know why she's hearing this now.
- **Beats:**
  1. At dinner, too casual: there are people camped about a mile out. Two of them, maybe three.
  2. "How long have you known?" "Two days." "And you're telling me now because?" "They haven't done anything." "That's not an answer." She says it's the second time. He doesn't argue.
  3. He recognizes them: two were on the sidewalk in Columbus. He names one: "Owen. He's the one who talks."
  4. Why are they waiting? He thinks they're waiting for a third person, someone who blocks teleports. He's curious whether it'll work. She's appalled he's curious.
  5. **The ownership argument starts here** and doesn't finish. "What do they want?" "Something I made." "Then give it to them." "No." This is the first hint of his flash of temper about them. He says something cold, "If they come near you I'll make them regret it," and she doesn't recognize his face for a second.
  6. He sends Delilah a mana message telling her to stay away for a few days. Mira watches him do it, awkwardly, like a man texting with gloves on. This is the first time she's seen it done.

### Scene 2: Dusk (about 1,200 words)
- **Mira wants** to survive, and to not need him.
- **Beats:**
  1. Months of mana at the cabin have been building up: two practitioners, the Box, Delilah's visits, and now the hunters a mile out. It finally draws something.
  2. Two mid-tiers cross at dusk. They're big-dog to small-bear sized, long-limbed and pale, with heads like folded fans that open toward prey. They move together, as if they share one sense.
  3. Jason tells her to feel for the crossing itself, the pressure of something coming through. That's the most useful sense she can grow.
  4. He takes the larger one. She takes the smaller.
  5. **Her own idea:** she sends her three clones in from three sides, so it can't face all of her at once. It splits its attention, and she gets the gap.
  6. It's messy. It hits her shield and the cost of holding it empties her. One clone dies, and she feels it like a dream of falling. She wins.
  7. Jason was ready to step in the whole time and didn't need to. She sees that he was ready, and that he didn't.
  8. She knows the hunters watched from the tree line, and Jason knew it too.

### Scene 3: The porch (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She sits on the porch steps, shaking and empty.
  2. Jason sits beside her. He talks, too fast: "You split it. You did the thing with the three angles. I didn't teach you that." "No." "That was really good." His voice catches.
  3. She leans on his shoulder. He lets her.

### Scene 4: Dawn (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The third hunter arrived in the night. They come at dawn: one person to block a normal teleport, one to track it if the block fails.
  2. It doesn't matter. Jason doesn't fight. He takes her arm and goes.
  3. **Full range this time.** Ocean. Dark blue and cold, no land. Her shields hold the water off without her asking them to. A tug on her reserves.
  4. Again: ocean. Again: a grass hillside, grey sky, wind, the distant hum of traffic.
  5. "We were just in the ocean. Twice." "Seventy percent of the planet is water. I'm a little surprised it only took three."

### Scene 5: What he did in February (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to understand why it was different the first time.
- **Beats:**
  1. Walking toward the traffic, she asks why they didn't land in the ocean back in February.
  2. He explains, almost embarrassed. That night she had no shields, and open water would have killed her in minutes. So in the middle of the attack he shaped the range on the fly: south, away from the Great Lakes and the coast, so no landing could be open water. Tennessee instead of Lake Erie or the Atlantic.
  3. **She works out the rest herself.** Changing a technique like that while being attacked, with no time, is hard. Her mature clones taught her how hard. He did that math for her before he'd ever told her he loved her.
  4. She doesn't say any of this. She takes his hand while they walk. He looks down at it, then keeps walking.
  5. "Why don't you just fight them?" "Because I don't have anything to prove to them. And I can leave."
  6. She pushes: "You leave everything." He's quiet, then honest: "I've been leaving since I was seventeen. It's the thing I'm best at." It's the saddest thing he's said, and he says it lightly.

### End (about 200 words)
A small hotel somewhere in Galicia, Spain. Gas-station food, because that's what was open. Mira looks at a map on her phone. Jason already scrolling property listings. End on something specific, such as the gas-station sandwich in its plastic triangle. Don't end on "neither of them saying it out loud".

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to finish one more week of training, and to not be found. Curiosity about the blocker beats his caution, and that's a real flaw.
- **His temper:** a flash of cold anger at the hunters. Plant it once.
- **His love, in action:** the range he shrank in February. The pride on the porch.

## Romance beat
Her hand in his on the road. He looks at it and keeps walking.

## Old-life beat
In Spain she realizes her phone plan doesn't work there. For the first time, nobody from home can reach her. She's surprised it feels like relief.

## Rules in play
- Small things notice by scent, and mana built up over months draws bigger things.
- Randomized teleport: not draining for Jason, open-surface landings, a range he can shrink.
- Mana message.
- Clones are one mind; she feels a clone die.
