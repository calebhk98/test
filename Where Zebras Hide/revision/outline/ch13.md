# Chapter 13: The Hunters

**When and where:** Early June. Day one, from breakfast to dusk to dawn of the next morning. The cabin, then ocean twice, then a hillside in Galicia, Spain.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. This is a big chapter. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is four months in, quiet, and can fight a little. She has three full-strength clones, which are all her.
- She knows how to erase memories and hates that she does.
- She knows the people from the Columbus sidewalk wanted what Jason knows. She doesn't know who they are or why.
- She has named Jason's pattern once: deciding about her and telling her later. He promised to stop.
- Delilah visits most afternoons.
- Her phone is in her pocket, as always. Jason keeps a backpack packed with his laptop.

## What this chapter does

The hunters come back. Jason tells her right away. Mira fights a real creature and wins her own way. Then she learns what Jason did for her in Chapter 1, and it changes how she sees him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Day one (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** to know what they're going to do about it.
- **Beats:**
  1. At breakfast, Jason tells her that people are camped about a mile out. Two of them, maybe three. He says he is telling her the day he feels them, which is what he promised, and she notices he is keeping it.
  2. She asks if he knows them. Two were on the sidewalk in Columbus. He names one: "Owen. He's the one who talks."
  3. She asks why they are only watching. He thinks they are waiting for a third person, someone who can block an ordinary teleport, and someone to track it if the block fails. An aimed jump can be blocked or followed. A random one can't, and he is curious how they will even try. She is appalled he is curious.
  4. They argue about leaving. She wants to go now. He wants to stay and finish the week of training. His reason: the hunters are watching, not attacking. If he ran every time someone camped nearby he would be teleporting all the time, and it is easier to wait until it becomes a real threat, just as he didn't run when Delilah showed up. They stay, his way. It is a choice they make together, badly.
  5. She asks how they found the cabin. He says the place has been building mana for months, with two practitioners and the Box, and the brambles only hide so much. They found it the way he would find them.
  6. **The ownership argument starts here** and doesn't finish. She asks what they want. He says something he made. She says give it to them. He says no. He says something cold about what he will do if they come near her, and she doesn't recognize his face for a second.
  7. He sends Delilah a short mana message that the lessons are off for a few days. Mira watches him do it, awkwardly, like a man texting with gloves on. It is the first time she has seen it done. He says nothing to Delilah about the hunters.

### Scene 2: Dusk (about 1,100 words)
- **Mira wants** to survive, and to not need him.
- **Beats:**
  1. Months of mana at the cabin have been building up: two practitioners, the Box, Delilah's visits. It finally draws something. The hunters have been watching since last night.
  2. Two mid-tiers cross at dusk. They're big-dog to small-bear sized, long-limbed and pale, with heads like folded fans that open toward prey. They move together, as if they share one sense.
  3. Jason tells her to feel for the crossing itself, the pressure of something coming through. That's the most useful sense she can grow.
  4. He takes the larger one. She takes the smaller.
  5. **Her own idea:** she sends her three clones in from three sides, so it can't face all of her at once. It splits its attention, and she gets the gap.
  6. It's messy. It hits her shield and the cost of holding it empties her. One clone dies, and she feels it like a dream of falling. She wins.
  7. Jason was ready to step in the whole time and didn't need to. She sees that he was ready, and that he didn't.
  8. The hunters watched from the tree line, and Jason knew it.

### Scene 3: The porch (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She sits on the porch steps, shaking and empty.
  2. Jason sits beside her and says what he thinks, straight out. He asks where she got the three sides, and says he never showed her that. He says it was really good, and his voice catches.
  3. She leans on his shoulder. He lets her.

### Scene 4: Dawn (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The third hunter arrived in the night. At dawn they close in: one to block a normal teleport, one to track it if the block fails.
  2. It doesn't matter. Jason doesn't fight. He takes her arm and jumps at random.
  3. **Full range this time.** Ocean. Dark blue and cold, no land. Her shields hold the water off her, and his hold it off the backpack and her phone. A tug on her reserves.
  4. Again: ocean. Again: a grass hillside, grey sky, wind, the distant hum of traffic.
  5. She says they were just in the ocean. Twice. Jason says seventy percent of the planet is water, and he's a little surprised it only took three.

### Scene 5: What he did in February (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to understand why it was different the first time.
- **Beats:**
  1. Walking toward the traffic, she asks why they didn't land in the ocean back in February.
  2. He explains, almost embarrassed. That night she had no shields, and open water would have killed her in minutes. So in the middle of the attack he changed the random jump on the fly: south, away from the Great Lakes and the coast, to make landing in water very unlikely. Tennessee instead of Lake Erie or the Atlantic. He states the cost himself, in one flat sentence.
  3. **She works out the rest herself.** Changing a technique like that while being attacked, with no time, is hard. Her mature clones taught her how hard. He did that for her before he had ever told her he loved her.
  4. She doesn't say any of this. She takes his hand while they walk. He looks down at it, then keeps walking.
  5. She asks why he doesn't just fight them. He answers.
  6. She says he leaves everything. He answers that too.

### End (about 200 words)
A small hotel somewhere in Galicia, Spain, and gas-station food, because that was what was open. Mira's phone says the plan doesn't work here, and for the first time nobody from home can reach her. She is surprised it feels like relief. She looks at a map on it while Jason scrolls property listings on his laptop. End on something specific, such as the gas-station sandwich in its plastic triangle.

## Set up in this chapter
- Owen's name, said once by Jason, with the line "He's the one who talks."
- The hunters watch from the tree line while Mira fights, and they see her fight with three clones. Jason knows they are there.
- The hunters plan to block and track an aimed teleport. They don't know a random one can't be blocked. Jason says so plainly and does not dwell on it.
- Jason's cold anger about the hunters, once. He says it, and his face changes for a second.
- The range he shaped in February to make landing in water very unlikely. He tells it plainly, as an ordinary fact about that night.
- "I've been leaving since I was seventeen. It's the thing I'm best at." He says it lightly.
- The hunters found the cabin by its mana. Jason says it as a plain fact and doesn't blame anyone.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to finish one more week of training, and to not be found. Curiosity about the blocker beats his caution, and that's a real flaw. He does tell her right away this time.
- **His temper:** a flash of cold anger at the hunters.
- **His love, in action:** the range he shaped in February. The pride on the porch.

## Romance beat
Her hand in his on the road. He looks at it and keeps walking.

## Old-life beat
In Spain her phone plan doesn't work. For the first time, nobody from home can reach her. It follows the long walk and the hotel, when she checks the phone for the map.

## Rules in play
- Small things notice by scent, and mana built up over months draws bigger things.
- Randomized teleport: not draining for Jason, open-surface landings, and a range he can shape or leave full. An aimed teleport can be blocked or tracked. A random one can't. The hunters don't know that yet.
- Hunters find places by their mana, not by records. Phones, names and hotels don't give Jason away.
- Mana message.
- Clones are one mind; she feels a clone die.

- "Where did you get that? The three sides. I never showed you that." Jason says it to Mira on the porch steps after the fight, in answer to what she just did.
- "Seventy percent of the planet is water. I'm a little surprised it only took three." Jason says it to Mira, in answer to her saying they were just in the ocean twice.
- "It took everything I had that night to make it do that." Jason says it to Mira on the road in Spain, in answer to her asking why they didn't land in the ocean in February.
- "Because I don't have anything to prove to them. And I can leave." Jason says it to Mira, in answer to her asking why he doesn't just fight the hunters.
- "I've been leaving since I was seventeen. It's the thing I'm best at." Jason says it to Mira, in answer to her saying he leaves everything. Write nothing after it that tells the reader how to take it.

## Limits for this chapter
- Triplets: at most 1.
- Describe the fight from her place among the three bodies. At most two similes.
- "He looks at her like the question is slightly strange": 0.
- "She absorbs this quietly": 0.

## Do not
- Have Jason fight the hunters.
- Finish the ownership argument. It stays open at the end of this chapter.
- Have Jason warn Delilah about the hunters. They are not after her.
- Say that any set-up item will matter later.
