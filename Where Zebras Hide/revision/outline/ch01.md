# Chapter 1: Normal

**When and where:** Friday, February 9. The office around 4:30 pm, the coffee shop from 5:00 to 5:40, then a walk of several blocks toward a Thai restaurant, about ten minutes if you went the whole way. All of this is Eastern time.
**Length:** about 2,700 words. A chapter under 2,300 has probably skipped texture the scenes need; add observation, not jokes or side beats.
**You also get:** the old draft of this chapter, as raw material. Keep what works, cut the padding, and fix what this outline changes.

## Where things stand

- Nothing supernatural has happened yet. Mira doesn't know magic exists, and the reader shouldn't be told.
- Mira is 26, an analyst, and lives in Columbus with her roommate Jess. The Henderson report is due Monday, and two sections are left.
- She has had coffee with Jason every Tuesday for two years and is quietly in love with him.
- Jason does not know anyone is coming for him tonight, and he has no plan to leave Columbus. This is an ordinary evening for him too.
- He asked to meet on a Friday because he found something for her and didn't want to wait until Tuesday. She doesn't know that. She only knows it's not their day.
- What each of them carries: Mira has a crossbody bag with her wallet and keys, and her phone is in her coat pocket. Jason has a backpack he never goes without, with his laptop, some cash and a few things he always keeps in it.

## What this chapter does

It shows the life she's about to lose and makes the reader like her in it. Jason's distance is visible without being underlined. Then something breaks the evening open, and neither she nor the reader understands what.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The office (about 600 words)
- **Mira wants** to finish the Henderson report, and to stop wondering why Jason asked for a Friday.
- **Beats:**
  1. Flickering lights, a chart in the report that shifts three pixels every time she pastes it back in, and Dave eating baby carrots too loudly at the next desk. The time is about 4:30; Marcus's visit in beat 2 makes that explicit (he says it is half past four, or that she should leave soon if she is meeting Jason at five).
  2. Marcus leans on her cubicle and teases her about Jason. He reminds her she skipped girls' night last month to have dinner with him.
  3. Jason has texted to confirm coffee at five. She answers yes.
  4. She leaves the report unfinished. She puts the phone in her coat pocket and the bag across her body.

### Scene 2: The coffee shop (about 1,300 words)
- **Mira wants** a normal evening with him, and to know why tonight.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason is at the counter collecting two cups when she walks in. By the time she sits, he has put her latte in front of her, and she doesn't remark on it. Show this once. His backpack is on the chair beside him, her bag on hers.
  2. **The film and the darkroom.** Jason asks how the Pentax is going. That leads Mira into the expired film with its green cast, the undeveloped roll still in the camera at home, and the darkroom space she can't afford. She runs on for most of the scene's first third (at least a full page of her talking, with Jason asking one follow-up question and adding one observation of his own). She then apologises, and Jason says the verbatim line below. Once in this scene Jason volunteers something Mira told him weeks ago (a detail, not a compliment), without being asked.
  3. **Her family, spread across the scene rather than delivered at once.** Each item is no more than a clause and comes up because something in the conversation leads to it, at a different point in the scene, never together:
     - Her phone buzzes face-up on the table. It is Nora in Chicago, sending another apartment listing. Mira turns the phone over. She has no plans to move.
     - Jason asks if she's going to look at the listings. That leads to one passing mention of her parents' opinions about what she does next.
     - Mira says she has to get home early on Sunday, since she is bringing the milk to Sunday dinner at her parents'. That leads her to ask about his parents (beat 4).
     - Jess comes up as one clause, when the students at the next table get loud (beat 6): it sounds like her and Jess arguing about the dishwasher.
  4. She asks about his parents, because she has just talked about hers. He says he and they didn't really get along, and moves the conversation back to her darkroom. It slides by; she barely notices.
  5. **The gift.** With the talk back on her darkroom, he reaches into the backpack and gives her a small paper bag: a roll of the discontinued film stock she mentioned wanting to try once, months ago, back when she was only thinking about buying a camera. He found it at a camera shop across town. He says he saw it and thought of her. She is ridiculously happy. Show it in something she does or says too much of, and let her catch herself. The narration does not say she is hiding it. It goes in her coat pocket. She thinks, in narration, that he remembered everything except to ever ask her on an actual date.
  6. At the next table, two students start arguing loudly about something with no bearing on anything (a group project, who owes whom for a sublet), and get louder, while she and Jason are still in the shop. It is noise and nothing more. Jason doesn't react to what they say. It's the reason they leave.
  7. Mira suggests the Thai place her friend Sarah keeps recommending, and says it is about a ten-minute walk. He says yes. They leave at 5:40.
- **Optional texture:** pick none or one that fits what you've written. A photographer's description of her green-tinted bridge photo is available if the darkroom talk needs an image.

### Scene 3: The walk, and the attack (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** an easy walk with him, and she is working up to asking him something personal.
- **Beats:**
  1. They walk several blocks through the February evening, past the park. The walk would take ten minutes, and the attack comes about seven minutes in. It is dusk and the streetlights have come on along the park. The restaurant isn't in sight yet. At an intersection a car or a couple passes, so the street isn't empty. Along the path the park runs down to a river, a dark line behind a railing, and Mira notices it. Once during the walk Mira checks the time on her phone (5:45), so the time is on the page.
  2. Under the orange streetlights, Jason says her expired green film would look strange in this light, and asks whether she'd shoot it anyway. One or two lines, about her and her photos.
  3. Wind makes her stumble. His hand stops a few inches from her elbow, then drops. This is the only hovering-hand moment in the chapter.
  4. She asks if he ever gets lonely. Do not explain why. The reader may connect it to Nora, or to him. (In this scene what is on Mira's mind is that Nora finishes her master's in June and talks about Mira joining her; Mira doesn't know what she wants.) He says sometimes. Then the verbatim exchange below. She asks what he means, and the attack comes before he answers.
  5. **The attack. Nobody is visible.** The air cracks. There is a flash of blue light right in front of them, and something invisible slams into it with a force she feels in her chest. It is about 5:47.
  6. **Jason reacts faster than he could speak.** He says nothing. His hand is on her arm before she understands that anything has happened.
  7. Pressure, ringing, the world lurching sideways, and a sensation across her whole skin that she can't compare to anything she has felt. Partway through, for about a heartbeat, the lurch bends, like a car swerving under her, and then it straightens out. She notices it only as a feeling and doesn't think about it. (Drafter: find your own physical detail; do not use the word "peeled".)
  8. Then ground, dead leaves, trees.
- **End line:** short and sensory. Bare branches against a grey sky. Don't end on a single word like "Trees."

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** an ordinary evening, and to see her face when she opens the film.
- **What he's like here:** warm, easy, attentive. He asks about her and turns questions about himself aside smoothly.
- **At the attack:** instant and silent.

## Romance beat
The film he found for her. The hovering hand. The exchange about being different.

## Old-life beats
- the job and Marcus (Marcus's visit leads into Jason's text)
- the film and the darkroom (Jason's question about the Pentax leads into it)
- the family: Nora (her listing buzzes in), the parents' opinions (Jason asks about the listings), the milk (leads Mira to ask about his parents) and Jess (the students' argument)

Scene 1 carries one old-life thread (the job, with Marcus). Scene 2 carries two: the film and darkroom, and the family. Each family item takes no more than a clause at a different point in the scene, never together. Scene 3 carries only a callback to Nora.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I hated Dave. I hated his carrots." Mira's narration in Scene 1, about Dave and his baby carrots.
- "Most people are boring." Jason says it in Scene 2, answering Mira's apology after she has run on about film and the darkroom (see Scene 2 beat 2). He means she isn't.
- "He remembered everything except to ever ask me on an actual date." Mira's narration in Scene 2, right after he gives her the film.
- "You're different." / "Different how?" Jason says the first line to Mira on the walk, after saying he sometimes gets lonely. She asks the second. The attack interrupts before he answers.

## Limits for this chapter
- "Two years": at most 2, including any verbatim line.
- The hovering hand: once.
- Body-language clichés (face warming, chest fluttering, face lighting up): at most 1. The cap covers emotional reactions only. The impact in Scene 3 is a physical event and doesn't count.
- Jokes or banter lines: at most 4, only in Scenes 1 and 2.
- Jason looking at doors, windows or the street: 0. He doesn't know anything is coming.
- Information given to the reader twice: 0.
- Short-line volleys: put a full sentence of action or thought between short lines of dialogue.

## Do not
- Explain the attack, mention mana, or let Mira guess at magic.
- Show the attackers, or have the narration remark that she didn't see them. She just doesn't see anyone.
- Have Jason say anything during the attack.
- Have Jason do experiments, give technical explanations, or react to the students.
- Give Mira anything to carry through the chapter other than the bag, the phone and the film. Don't make any of them symbols.

## Set up in this chapter
- Jason says he and his parents didn't really get along, then turns the talk back to her darkroom. Mira doesn't question it.
- The undeveloped roll is still in the Pentax at her apartment. She mentions it once.
- The gift roll goes into her coat pocket. She doesn't open it or use it.
- The blue flash, and the invisible thing that hits it. Nobody is seen, and nothing is explained.
- A river runs beside the park path they walk along. Mira notices the dark water once.
- During the jump the lurch bends for about a heartbeat and then straightens. She feels it and says nothing about it.
