# Chapter 11: Visitors

**When and where:** Late April, a few days after Chapter 10. The cabin.
**Length:** about 3,000 words. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira is about ten weeks in. She can cycle and shield. Her clones produce their own mana and are all her, one mind in several bodies.
- She and Jason fought about the Box (a prison it doesn't know it's in) and didn't resolve it. He told her he heard her.
- He calls the Box a battery and won't say what it powers.
- Last week Mom left her a voicemail: Dad's back is better, and the neighbors got a dog.
- She doesn't know any other practitioner exists in person. She knows a few thousand exist worldwide.
- Jason picked this cabin because nobody is supposed to be able to find it.

## What this chapter does

A third person arrives and the world gets wider. Delilah is warm, nosy and funny. Mira then works out something about her clones, alone, at night.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The car (about 600 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. A car stops on the gravel. An older woman gets out with a thermos. Jason goes very still and then very fast, and steps between her and the car. He is genuinely alarmed: nobody is supposed to find this place.
  2. **How she found them:** she lives forty miles over the ridge and has known these hills for thirty years. For weeks she has felt a new warmth out this way. Two nights ago she felt something big and strange (the Box) that rattled her teeth. She came to check on whoever was new, the way you do.
  3. The people hunting Jason don't know these hills. She does.
  4. Jason checks her somehow, satisfies himself that she is a practitioner and no danger, and relaxes, slightly.

### Scene 2: Tea (about 900 words)
- **Mira wants** another person to tell her how this works. Then she wants a friend.
- **Delilah wants** to make sure this girl isn't going to die.
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah calls it "the warmth". She brought actual tea, in a thermos, like a normal person.
  2. She asks Mira something personal within the first ten minutes, because she's nosy: how old she is, how she met him, whether the two of them are together. Mira chokes on her tea.
  3. **The community, through people, not numbers.** Show two or three plain people: a dental hygienist in Tucson, a retired surveyor in Ontario. Most people are like that, small and careful, living ordinary lives. The strong ones are rare, and mostly alone. Delilah knows who is who across half the country and a few places beyond it, and talks about all of them freely, fondly.
  4. She knows Jason by reputation: the boy who won't share. She says it to his face, cheerfully.
  5. She tells Jason he is brilliant and a terrible teacher. He points out that Mira is ahead of schedule. Delilah says she's ahead because she's stubborn, and he is still terrible.
  6. Delilah mentions, in a single line, a boy she once found too late, and moves on to the tea. Mira notices.
  7. Mira asks whether Delilah's family knows about any of this. Delilah says her own daughter thinks she is in a book club. She says it as a fact about her week.

### Scene 3: The question about Jason (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason goes outside. Mira asks Delilah why he won't share. Delilah says he made it, and an artist doesn't give paintings away for free.
  2. Mira says what he has could save lives. Delilah says she doesn't disagree, and that so could a lot of things people choose to keep.
  3. **Romance advice.** Mira asks, sideways, what Delilah would do if someone shouted that he loved her in the woods as a reason to leave. Delilah answers bluntly: be mad for a month, then decide whether the reason was any good, and make him say it again to your face when he isn't shouting.
  4. Mira laughs, and thinks about it for the rest of the day.

### Scene 4: Empty clones (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Delilah offers to come teach the boring stuff that keeps you alive and fed. Jason starts to refuse. Mira says yes before he can.
  2. Mira complains about her clones: when she makes them she fills them from her own reserve, so they start at her own tired level, refill slowly and leave her drained. They're useless in a real fight.
  3. Delilah asks why on earth she'd fill them from her own tank. Then she moves on to asking whether the cabin's woodstove draws properly.
  4. After Delilah drives off, Mira looks across the porch at Jason, thinking about "say it again to your face". That night she lies awake and plays Mom's voicemail once, and thinks about Delilah's daughter and the book club. Later, still awake, she thinks of her old darkroom and works it out alone. A print isn't a copy of the negative as it is. You choose the exposure, you dodge and burn, you decide how it comes out. She has been printing her clones at her own tired exposure, and she doesn't have to.
  5. She doesn't tell anyone. End on her in the dark with the idea, before she tries it.

## Set up in this chapter
- Delilah's question about filling clones from her own tank. She asks it once and moves on to the woodstove. She does not explain how.
- A boy Delilah once found too late. One line, quickly covered.
- Delilah's daughter thinks she is in a book club.
- Delilah talks freely and fondly about everyone she knows across two states.
- Delilah says "so could a lot of things people choose to keep", in answer to Mira's point that what Jason has could save lives.
- Mira says yes to Delilah's lessons before Jason can refuse.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** Delilah gone, then grudgingly to keep her.
- **Fears:** that being found once means being found again.
- **He's funny about it:** territorial, sulky, then caught agreeing with her. Delilah teases him and he takes it.

## Romance beat
Delilah's advice, and Mira looking at Jason across the porch afterward, thinking about "say it again to your face".

## Old-life beat
Mom's voicemail, played once that night (it leads from Delilah's book club), and Delilah's daughter.

## Rules in play
- No vocabulary is standard: Delilah says "the warmth".
- A local practitioner checks on newcomers.
- How she found them: local knowledge, and the Box is loud.
- Small useful magic: Delilah's territory.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "You're brilliant, Jason, but you're a terrible teacher." Delilah says it to Jason across the table, in answer to his sulking about her arriving unannounced.

## Limits for this chapter
- Delilah says "honey" at most once.
- She doesn't call Mira "child".
- "Delilah gives her a look": 0.
- Community examples: through people only, at most three.
- Mentions of how long Mira and Jason have known each other: at most 1.

## Do not
- Have Delilah explain how to build the clones. She asks one question and moves on.
- Have Mira tell Jason what she worked out.
- Have Jason be calm about being found.
- Explain the Box's battery.
- Say any of the set-up items will matter later.
