# Chapter 2: Displaced

**When and where:** Friday, February 9, at about 4:47 pm by Mira's phone. Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee. Overcast, flat grey daylight.
**Length:** about 2,600 words. The scene budgets add up to that.

## Where things stand

- Seconds ago they were on a Columbus sidewalk. Her phone had read 5:42 on the walk, a minute or two before the attack, so the attack came at about 5:44. The air cracked, there was a blue flash right in front of them, something invisible hit it, and a pressure hit her chest so she couldn't breathe. The world lurched. Mira saw no one.
- Jason gripped her upper arm on the sidewalk, and it is still sore.
- She knows nothing about magic and believes in none of it.
- Where things are. Her crossbody bag is across her body, with her wallet and keys. The roll of film Jason gave her and her phone are in her coat pocket. Her scarf is at the coffee shop. Jason has his backpack on his shoulders, with his laptop, cash, a water bottle and a few other things in it. The paper bag the film came in is at the coffee shop.
- Jason has done this before. He is checking whether anything or anyone nearby felt them land, and whether Mira is hurt.
- The Henderson report still has two sections left, due Monday. The roll in her Pentax at home is undeveloped.

## What this chapter does

Disorientation, written like the minutes after a car accident. Mira copes by documenting and checking. Jason is calm and fast, and he tells her nothing yet. The chapter turns on one decision: she considers calling 911 and decides against it. It ends with her calling time on him.

Beats are events and purposes. The drafter finds the details, images and actions.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Waking up wrong, and the clock (about 1,050 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if she's hurt, where she is, and what happened.
- **Her one dominant physical reaction** is nausea. The ringing in her ears and the scraped palms are stated as facts and nothing more.
- **Beats:**
  1. She is on her hands and knees on dead leaves, with ringing ears and scraped palms. She slows her breathing (verbatim line below), and then she is sick behind a tree.
  2. Jason is beside her within seconds. While she is sick he holds her bag strap out of the way. He takes a water bottle from his backpack, gives it to her and tells her to take small sips. He puts the backpack back on.
  3. She sits on a fallen log at the foot of a slope. The log is wet through her coat. She checks her pockets and her bag: the phone and the film box are in her coat pocket, and the bag has her wallet and keys. She has no scarf, and the cold reaches her neck.
  4. **The clock.** Within a few minutes of landing, before Jason's full check if necessary, she looks at her phone. It has signal, and the map puts her near Crossville, Tennessee. The time reads 4:47 PM. The prose names 4:47 once and gives no other clock time. She remembers it was later than that when they left, and she can't explain why it is earlier now. She tries a phone glitch, and a glitch would have broken the map too, and the map works. She doesn't solve it. Note for you: it is the time zone, and she works it out in Spain (Ch 14). Don't hint at it, and don't have anyone mention time zones.
  5. Jason checks her. Show two checks: he takes her wrist and moves it, and he asks about her chest. She says it hurts to breathe in all the way. He presses along her ribs, asking before each press, names the spot she flinched at and says nothing feels broken. She says her upper arm is sore, and he says that was his hand. Then he stands and turns to look at the trees.
  6. She tries a concussion. It explains the ringing and the nausea. She tries to fit the blue flash and the blow to her chest into it, and she can't. The theory also doesn't put her in a forest. She works through it alone, and talks to Jason only about her ribs and arm.
  7. She photographs the nearest trunk and two other different things nearby, a photographer's reflex, and screenshots the time and the map.
- **Observation:** one observation of light or of her hands, in the drafter's own terms. The outline supplies none.
- **Romance beat:** the way Jason handles the water and her ribs. He asks before he touches her and doesn't mention being kind.

### Scene 2: Twenty minutes (about 500 words)
- **Mira wants** answers now.
- **Jason wants** to find out whether anything or anyone nearby felt them arrive. He does not explain this.
- Mira is already sitting on the log when this scene starts.
- **Beats:**
  1. She demands to know what happened and says she wants all of it. Jason is standing, looking at the trees. He is calm. Show it through what he does with his hands and how he answers.
  2. He says he will explain what happened. He says he first needs to know whether anyone near here noticed or felt them arrive. Use "noticed" or "felt", not "saw". He says this in his own words, once. He asks for twenty minutes and gives no reason for the number. His reply to "all of it" is to go on to the time: he says "twenty", and he leaves her demand for everything unanswered.
  3. She says fifteen. He says twenty. She says "Fine" and tells him she is holding him to it.
  4. She sets a timer on her phone and sets the phone to vibrate.
  5. He walks up a rise where the trees thin out. He goes over the crest, or stands with his back to her. From the log she can see only part of him.
- **Observation for this scene:** one observation of Jason's face or hands, in the drafter's own terms. It is not about the colour of his hands or skin, and not about his knuckles.
- Not every exchange ends on a clean reply. She interrupts him once and repeats a question.

### Scene 3: The texts, and the call she makes and abandons (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep her life from noticing she's gone, and to know whether she should call for help.
- **What frightens her:** she was in Ohio ten minutes ago and is now in Tennessee, and she can't say that to anyone in a way that sounds sane. Show her fear in one body detail or one mistake, in the drafter's own terms. She does not make a plan.
- **Beats:**
  1. With the timer running and the phone in her hand, she sees that Sarah has texted asking whether she is still coming to gallery night tomorrow. Mira answers that she can't make it. Sarah writes back asking if she's okay, because Mira never skips gallery night. Mira says work has swamped her.
  2. The messages do not get answered in order with equal weight. Marcus's message arrives while she is answering Sarah, and she answers Marcus first. Marcus sends a photo from Murphy's of the group from work, teasing her about her friend and asking if she is meeting that friend tonight. She says she'll see them Monday and leaves the friend out. Mom texts asking if she is still bringing the milk Sunday and whether she has heard anything about the promotion. Mira answers the milk and leaves the promotion question unanswered. Each reply is one or two lines. Any description of a text message or photo is 40 words at most.
  3. Show the ease of the lying: each reply is sent before she has decided what to say.
  4. The dream test: she opens a new note to herself and types the verbatim sentence below. She looks away and back, and the text hasn't changed. She has no other test.
  5. Thinking of her apartment, with the dishes in the sink, she texts Jess that she is staying with a friend tonight. She hesitates before sending because Sarah might ask Jess about it, and sends it anyway.
  6. **The 911 beat, kept short.** She has a reason to call: something invisible hit her hard in the chest, she was sick, her ribs hurt, and she doesn't know what Jason is. She types 911 and holds her thumb over call. She would have to tell a dispatcher she was in Ohio and is now in Tennessee. She looks up at Jason on the rise and deletes the numbers one at a time.
- No jokes.

### Scene 4: Time (about 350 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The cold comes through the seat of her coat. She does one thing to warm herself, in the drafter's own terms.
  2. One thought about the Henderson file: it is open on her office computer, two sections are unfinished, and she can't get to it from here.
  3. One thought about the Pentax: the undeveloped roll is in it on her desk at home, and the new roll in her pocket has no camera to go in.
  4. Partway through the twenty minutes she checks the timer once.
  5. When it ends the phone vibrates and she stops it. Nothing else happens in the world. She stands, walks to the bottom of the rise and calls up to him that time is up.
- **End:** on her standing at the bottom of the rise, having called up. Do not write him coming down. The next chapter opens with that.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to know whether anything or anyone nearby felt them land, and for Mira to be unhurt.
- **What he does:** he checks her quickly and asks before he touches her. He speaks in full sentences and doesn't explain himself. He has done this before, so he acts without hesitation.
- **Fear:** for her only. He checks her ribs and gives her water first, before he looks at the trees for long. He is not afraid of the teleport.
- His hands do work or they are still. He carries no props, and nothing is hooked under a strap.

## Romance beat
Jason holds her bag strap and hands her water while she is sick, and asks before he presses her ribs. She types 911, looks at him on the rise, and deletes the numbers.

## Old-life beat
The texts: Sarah, Marcus, Mom with the milk and the promotion, and Jess. The Henderson file and the Pentax roll in the wait.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
These are the only sentences the outline supplies.
- "I breathed in for four counts and out for four, and it helped a little." Mira's narration in Scene 1, as she slows her breathing after waking.
- "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." The sentence Mira types in Scene 3 to check she isn't dreaming. Give it no backstory. It is the first test that comes to mind.

## Limits for this chapter
- Jokes or quips: 0.
- Descriptions of him scanning the woods: at most 2.
- Personifying objects (laces, phones, trees): 0.
- One body reaction per scene.
- "Two years": at most 1.
- Describing what Jason is doing up on the rise: at most 2 times in the chapter, each in different terms.
- Her bag is across her body the whole chapter. His backpack is on his shoulders except when he takes out the water bottle. The laptop is not taken out or mentioned. The paper bag and scarf are at the coffee shop and are not mentioned in the woods.
- The repeat-word limit is in STYLE. Describe the light and colour of the place in specific terms that are physically right for flat overcast daylight.
- One simile per scene.
- Birds: 0.
- Between short lines of dialogue, put something Mira thinks or sees that she has not mentioned before. A repeated detail does not count.
- Text messages and photos: 40 words at most for any one description.
- Optional texture: two observations of the physical place or her body that fit the scene. Invent no places or objects.

## Do not
- Explain anything about magic.
- Make Jason scared, fumbling or unable to finish a sentence.
- Have anything happen in the world when the timer ends.
- Give Mira an invented habit or backstory to explain the fox sentence or the breathing.
- Have Jason answer a question about 911, or promise "everything".
- Copy any sentence of this outline into the prose.
