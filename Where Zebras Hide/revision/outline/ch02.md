# Chapter 2: Displaced

**When and where:** Friday, February 9, at about 4:47 pm by Mira's phone. Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee.
**Length:** about 2,600 words. The scene budgets add up to that.

## Where things stand

- Seconds ago they were on a Columbus sidewalk. Her phone had read 5:45 a few minutes earlier. The air cracked, there was a blue flash right in front of them, something invisible hit it, and a pressure hit her chest so she couldn't breathe. The world lurched. Mira saw no one.
- Jason gripped her upper arm on the sidewalk, and it is still sore.
- She knows nothing about magic and believes in none of it.
- The roll of film Jason gave her and her phone are in her coat pocket. Her crossbody bag is on her, with her wallet and keys. She has no scarf (she took it off in the café). Jason has his backpack on.
- Jason has done this before. He is checking whether anything or anyone nearby felt them land, and whether Mira is hurt.
- The Henderson report still has two sections left, due Monday. The roll in her Pentax at home is undeveloped.

## What this chapter does

Disorientation, written like the minutes after a car accident. Mira copes by documenting and checking. Jason is calm and fast, and he tells her nothing yet. The chapter turns on one decision: she doesn't call 911. It ends with her calling time on him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Waking up wrong, and the clock (about 1,050 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if she's hurt, where she is, and what happened.
- **Her one dominant physical reaction** is nausea. The ringing in her ears and the scraped palms are stated as facts and nothing more.
- **Beats:**
  1. She is on her hands and knees on dead leaves. Her ears ring and her palms are scraped. She counts her breathing, four in and four out, and it helps a little. Then she is sick behind a tree.
  2. Jason is beside her within seconds. While she is sick he holds her bag strap clear of her face. He slides his backpack off one shoulder, takes out a water bottle, hands it to her and says to take small sips. He puts the backpack back on. He says nothing about it.
  3. He checks her. Show two checks: he takes her wrist and moves it, and he asks about her chest. She says it hurts to breathe in all the way. He presses along her ribs, asking before each press, and says nothing feels broken. She says her upper arm is sore, and he says that was his hand. Then he stands and turns slowly, looking at the trees.
  4. She tries a concussion. It explains the ringing and the nausea. She tries to fit the blue flash and the blow to her chest into it, and she can't. The theory also does not put her in a forest. She does not ask Jason about the flash.
  5. She checks her pockets and her bag. The phone and the film box are in her coat pocket, and the film box presses against her hip. The bag is on her, with her wallet and keys. She has no scarf, and the cold gets in at her neck.
  6. Her phone works, with signal. The map puts her near Crossville, Tennessee. She sits down on a fallen log at the foot of a slope to look at it. The log is wet through the seat of her coat.
  7. She looks at the time: 4:47 PM. The prose may say the clock is an hour early. It gives neither time and says nothing about when she last looked. She tries a phone glitch. A glitch would have broken the map as well, and the map works. She doesn't solve it. Note for you: it is the time zone, and she works it out in Spain (Ch 14). Don't hint at it, and don't have anyone mention time zones.
  8. She screenshots the time, then the map, then the trees.
- **Observation:** one observation of light or hands. The screen lighting her thumb from underneath is a good model; do not reuse it word for word.
- **Romance beat:** the way Jason handles the water and her ribs. He asks before he touches her and says nothing about being kind.

### Scene 2: Twenty minutes (about 500 words)
- **Mira wants** answers now.
- **Jason wants** to find out whether anything or anyone nearby felt them arrive. He does not explain this.
- Mira is already sitting on the log when this scene starts.
- **Beats:**
  1. She demands to know what happened. Jason is standing, looking at the trees and not at her. He is calm. Show it only through what he does with his hands and how he answers.
  2. He says he will explain what happened. He does not promise everything. He says he first needs to know whether anyone near here noticed them arrive. He says this in his own words, once. He asks for twenty minutes. He gives no reason for the number.
  3. She says fifteen. He says twenty. She says "Fine" and tells him she is holding him to it.
  4. She sets a timer on her phone and sets the phone to vibrate.
  5. He walks up a rise where the trees thin out. He goes over the crest, or stands with his back to her. She sees only that he is standing still.
- **Observation for this scene:** of Jason: his face in the flat light, his hands, or where he puts his weight. Not the trees.
- Not every exchange ends on a clean reply. She interrupts him once and repeats a question.

### Scene 3: The texts, and the call she doesn't make (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep her life from noticing she's gone, and to know whether she should call for help.
- **Beats:**
  1. With the timer running and the phone in her hand, she sees that Sarah has texted: "Still coming to gallery night tomorrow?" Every text message in this chapter is shown in quotation marks. Mira answers that she can't make it. Sarah writes back asking if she's okay, because Mira never skips gallery night. Mira says work has swamped her. She hasn't planned the words and they come out smooth. She notices this once, and it is not mentioned again.
  2. The dream test: she opens a new note to herself and types "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." She looks away, looks back, and the text hasn't changed. She has no other test.
  3. Later in the wait, Marcus texts a photo from Murphy's of the group from work, with a line teasing her about her friend, and asking if she is meeting that friend tonight. She reads it twice before she answers. She says she'll see them Monday, and she doesn't mention the friend.
  4. A few minutes later, Mom texts: "Still bringing the milk Sunday?" and, in the same message, asks whether she has heard anything about the promotion. Mira answers yes about the milk and says nothing new about the promotion. Each answer is one or two lines.
  5. Thinking of her apartment, with the dishes in the sink, she texts Jess that she is staying with a friend tonight. She stops with her thumb over the send button, because Sarah might ask Jess about it, and sends it anyway.
  6. **The 911 beat, kept short.** She types 911 and holds her thumb over call. She would have to tell a dispatcher she was in Ohio and is now in Tennessee. She looks up at Jason on the rise, standing still. She never asks him whether she should call. She deletes the numbers one at a time.
- She is frightened in this scene. No jokes.

### Scene 4: Time (about 350 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The cold comes through the seat of her coat. She pulls her hands up into her sleeves.
  2. One thought about the Henderson file: it is open on her office computer, two sections are blank, and Monday is gone. She has no way to reach it.
  3. One thought about the Pentax: the undeveloped roll is in it on her desk at home, and the new roll in her pocket has no camera to go in.
  4. At ten minutes she checks the timer once. It is half run down.
  5. It reads zero. The phone vibrates once in her hand and she stops it with her thumb. Nothing else happens. She stands, walks to the bottom of the rise and calls up to him that time is up.
  6. He comes down.
- **End:** on her standing her ground at the bottom of the rise, or him walking down toward her.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** to know whether anything or anyone nearby felt them land, and for Mira to be unhurt.
- **What he does:** he checks her quickly and asks before he touches her. He speaks in full sentences and doesn't explain himself. He has done this before and uses no hesitation or false starts.
- **Fear:** for her only. He checks her ribs and gives her water first, before he looks at the trees for long. He is not afraid of the teleport.
- His hands do work or they are still. He has no tics or props, and nothing hooked under a strap.

## Romance beat
Jason holds her bag strap and hands her water while she is sick, and asks before he presses her ribs. She types 911, looks at him on the rise, and deletes the numbers.

## Old-life beat
The texts: Sarah, Marcus, Mom with the milk and the promotion, and Jess. The Henderson file and the Pentax roll in the wait.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I breathed in for four counts and out for four, and it helped a little." Mira's narration in Scene 1, as she slows her breathing after waking.
- "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." The sentence Mira types in Scene 3 to check she isn't dreaming. Give it no backstory. It is the first test that comes to mind.

## Limits for this chapter
- Jokes or quips: 0.
- Descriptions of him scanning the woods: at most 2.
- Personifying objects (laces, phones, trees): 0.
- One body reaction per scene.
- "Two years": at most 1.
- Describing what Jason is doing up on the rise: at most 2 times in the chapter, each in different terms.
- Her bag and his backpack: each is placed once and mentioned at most twice. The bag is not moved around the scene.
- No word except a name appears more than three times. Describe the light and colour of the place in specific terms.
- One simile per scene.
- Birds: 0.
- Between short lines of dialogue, put something Mira thinks or sees that she has not mentioned before. A repeated detail does not count.
- Optional texture: pick two from the cold coming through her coat, the screen's light, and the roll in the Pentax. Add no split trunks, no cheese tables, no invented places.

## Do not
- Explain anything about magic.
- Make Jason scared, fumbling or unable to finish a sentence.
- Have anything happen in the world when the timer reads zero. She calls up to him and he comes down.
- Give Mira an invented habit or backstory to explain the fox sentence or the breathing.
- Have Jason answer a question about 911, or promise "everything".
- Copy any sentence of this outline into the prose.
