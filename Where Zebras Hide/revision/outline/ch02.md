# Chapter 2: Displaced

**When and where:** Friday, February 9, at about 4:47 pm by Mira's phone. Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee.
**Length:** about 2,800 words.
**You also get:** the old draft of this chapter, as raw material.

## Where things stand

- Seconds ago they were on a Columbus sidewalk. It was about 5:47 pm. The air cracked, there was a blue flash, something invisible hit it, and the world lurched. Mira saw no one.
- She knows nothing about magic and believes in none of it.
- She has the roll of film Jason gave her in her coat pocket and her phone in the same coat. Her crossbody bag is still on her, with her wallet and keys. Jason has his backpack on.
- Jason has done this before. He isn't frightened of what he did. He's focused on whether anyone followed them, and on whether Mira is hurt.
- The Henderson report still has two sections left.

## What this chapter does

Disorientation, written like the minutes after a car accident. Mira copes by documenting and checking. Jason is calm, fast and clearly hiding a lot. The chapter turns on one decision: she doesn't call 911. It ends with her calling time on him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Waking up wrong, and the clock (about 1,000 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if she's hurt, where she is, and what happened.
- **Beats:**
  1. Ringing ears, scraped palms, nausea. She counts her breathing, four in and four out, and it helps a little.
  2. Jason is beside her within seconds. He checks her quickly and competently. Show two of the checks. Gentle, practiced. Then he stands and turns slowly, watching the trees.
  3. She tries out an explanation: a concussion. It is the only theory she has, and she can see it doesn't cover the trees.
  4. She checks her pockets and her bag. The bag is on her, with her wallet and keys. Jason's backpack is still on his shoulder.
  5. Her phone works. The map puts her near Crossville, Tennessee.
  6. She looks at the time. 4:47 PM. They left the coffee shop at 5:40, and she checked the time at 5:45 on the walk. The phone is an hour behind where it should be, and she can't explain it. She tries a phone glitch and a wrong clock, and neither fits the map. She doesn't solve it. Note for you, not for the prose: it is the time zone, and she works it out months later. Don't hint at it, and don't have anyone mention time zones.
  7. She screenshots the time, then the map, then the trees.
- **Optional texture:** pick none or one that fits what you've written.

### Scene 2: Twenty minutes (about 600 words)
- **Mira wants** answers now.
- **Jason wants** to make sure nobody followed them before he says anything.
- **Beats:**
  1. She demands to know what happened. He is calm, which frightens her more than panic would have.
  2. He tells her plainly that he will explain, but not until he knows whether anyone followed them. He asks for twenty minutes.
  3. They bargain briefly over the number and settle on twenty.
  4. She sets a timer on her phone.
  5. He walks up a rise where the trees thin out and goes still there, doing something she can't see.

### Scene 3: The texts, and the call she doesn't make (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep her life from noticing she's gone, and to know whether she should call for help.
- **Beats:**
  1. With the timer running and the phone still in her hand, she sees the texts that arrived during the walk. Sarah has written to ask whether she's still coming to gallery night tomorrow. Mira answers that she can't make it. Sarah writes back asking if she's okay, because Mira never skips gallery nights. Mira says work has swamped her.
  2. She texts Jess that she is staying with a friend tonight. Her mother asks whether she's bringing the milk on Sunday, and Mira answers with a cheerful yes. The lies come out smooth and easy, and that startles her. They are small ones.
  3. The dream test: she types the fox sentence, looks away, looks back, and the text hasn't changed. She has no other test.
  4. **The 911 beat, kept short.** She types 911 and holds her thumb over call. She imagines saying she was in Ohio and now she's in Tennessee. She looks up at Jason on the rise, perfectly still and clearly doing something. Whatever this is, police can't help with it, and he knows what it is. She deletes the numbers.
- She is frightened in this scene. No jokes.

### Scene 4: Time (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. She checks the timer. A few minutes are left. She waits, and nobody comes to her.
  2. It reads zero. She stands, walks to the bottom of the rise and calls up to him that time is up.
  3. He comes down.
- **End:** on her standing her ground at the bottom of the rise, or him walking down toward her.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** certainty that they weren't followed, and Mira unhurt.
- **What he's like:** steady, quick, careful with her. He speaks in full sentences and doesn't explain himself. He has done this kind of thing before, and it shows.
- **Fear:** only for her, shown in how fast and careful he is checking her for injuries. He's not scared of the teleport.

## Romance beat
How gently he checks her for injuries. She trusts him enough not to call.

## Old-life beat
The texts: Sarah, Jess, and Mom with the milk.

## Verbatim lines (keep exactly)
- "I breathed in for four counts and out for four, and it helped a little." Mira's narration in Scene 1, as she slows her breathing after waking.
- "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." The sentence Mira types in Scene 3 to check she isn't dreaming. Give it no backstory. It is the first test that comes to mind.

## Limits for this chapter
- Jokes or quips: 0.
- Descriptions of him scanning the woods: at most 2.
- Personifying objects (laces, phones, trees): 0.
- One body reaction per scene.
- "Two years": at most 1.
- Describing what Jason is doing up on the rise: at most 2 times in the chapter, each in different terms.

## Do not
- Explain anything about magic.
- Make Jason scared, fumbling or unable to finish a sentence.
- Have anything happen when the timer reads zero. She calls up to him and he comes down.
- Give Mira an invented habit or backstory to explain the fox sentence or the breathing.

## Set up in this chapter
- The phone reads 4:47. Mira has no explanation for it. She screenshots it and moves on.
- Jason says he needs to know whether anyone followed them. Mira hears it and doesn't dwell on it.
- Mira lies to Sarah, Jess and her mother with no effort. She notices how easy it is, once.
