# Chapter 2: Displaced

**When and where:** Friday, February 9, at about 4:47 pm local time. Woods on the Cumberland Plateau near Crossville, Tennessee.
**Length:** about 2,300 to 2,800 words. Shorter is fine; never pad.
**You also get:** the old draft of this chapter, as raw material.

## Where things stand

- Seconds ago they were on a Columbus sidewalk. The air cracked, there was a blue flash, something invisible hit it, and the world lurched. Mira saw no one.
- She knows nothing about magic and believes in none of it.
- She has the roll of film Jason gave her in her coat pocket.
- Jason has done this before. He isn't frightened of what he did. He's focused on whether anyone followed them, and on whether Mira is hurt.

## What this chapter does

Disorientation, written like the minutes after a car accident. Mira copes by documenting and checking. Jason is calm, fast and clearly hiding a lot. The chapter turns on one decision: she doesn't call 911. It ends with her calling time on him.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Waking up wrong (about 600 words)
- **Mira wants** to know if she's hurt, and where she is.
- **Beats:**
  1. Ringing ears, scraped palms, nausea.
  2. Jason is beside her within seconds. He checks her quickly and competently: asks her name and the day, has her follow his finger, looks at her pupils, feels her scalp for blood. Gentle, practiced. Then he stands and turns in a slow circle, watching the trees.
  3. She tries out explanations: a bomb, a gas line, a concussion.
  4. Her phone works. The GPS says Tennessee, near Crossville.
- **Detail to use:** dead leaves stuck to her coat.

### Scene 2: The clock (about 400 words)
- **Mira wants** facts.
- **Beats:**
  1. Her phone says 4:47 PM. They left the coffee shop at twenty to six, minutes before the attack. An hour has gone, backward.
  2. She screenshots it, then the map, then the trees.
  3. She doesn't solve it. Note for you, not for the prose: it's the time zone, and she works it out months later. Don't hint at it.

### Scene 3: Twenty minutes (about 700 words)
- **Mira wants** answers now.
- **Jason wants** to make sure nobody followed them before he says anything.
- **Beats:**
  1. She demands to know what happened. He's calm, which frightens her more than panic would have.
  2. He doesn't stonewall with "I don't know". He tells her plainly that he'll explain, but not until he's sure they're alone: "Give me twenty minutes. I need to know whether anyone followed us." That last part lands on her; don't have her narration point it out.
  3. They bargain briefly over the time: thirty, fifteen, twenty.
  4. She sets a timer on her phone.
  5. He walks up a rise where the trees thin out and goes still there, doing something she can't see.

### Scene 4: The texts, and the call she doesn't make (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** to keep her life from noticing she's gone, and to know whether she should call for help.
- **Beats:**
  1. She sees she has texts she missed during the walk. Sarah: "You ok? You never skip gallery stuff" comes after Mira's own lie about not making it tomorrow. Mira texts Jess ("Staying with a friend tonight"). Mom asks about the milk for Sunday. Mira lies smoothly and is startled by how smoothly.
  2. The dream test: she types "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog", looks away, looks back, and the text hasn't changed.
  3. **The 911 beat, kept short.** She types 911 and holds her thumb over call. She imagines saying "I was in Ohio and now I'm in Tennessee." She looks up at Jason on the rise, perfectly still and clearly doing something. Whatever this is, police can't help with it, and he knows what it is. She deletes the numbers.
- **She is frightened in this scene. No jokes**, except possibly one small dry thought, unpolished.

### Scene 5: Time (about 300 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. The timer runs out while he's still on the rise. Nobody comes to her.
  2. She stands up, walks to the bottom of the rise and calls up to him: time's up.
  3. He comes down.
- **End:** on her standing her ground at the bottom of the rise, or him walking down toward her.

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** certainty that they weren't followed, and Mira unhurt.
- **What he's like:** steady, quick, careful with her. He speaks in full sentences and doesn't explain himself. He's done this kind of thing before, and it shows.
- **Fear:** only for her, shown as how fast and careful he is checking her injuries. He's not scared of the teleport.

## Romance beat
How gently he checks her for injuries. She trusts him enough not to call.

## Old-life beat
The texts: Sarah, Jess, Mom and the milk.

## Limits for this chapter
- Jokes or quips: at most 1 in the whole chapter.
- Jason's "I don't know": 0.
- Descriptions of him scanning the woods: at most 2.
- Personifying objects (laces, phones, trees): 0.
- One body reaction per scene.
- "Two years": at most 1.

## Do not
- Explain anything about magic.
- Solve the clock.
- Make Jason scared, fumbling or unable to finish a sentence.
- Have anything happen at the exact moment the timer ends.
