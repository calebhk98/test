# Chapter 17: The Ecosystem

**When and where:** July. Back and forth between the other side (a few hours at a time) and the Vermont base.
**Length:** about 4,000 words. This chapter combines two of the old outline's chapters. There is no old draft.

## Where things stand

- Mira has crossed once. There is no light there, and everything produces mana. She made a light for three seconds and saw colorless moss. She fought a dense, dog-sized native creature by placing a clone to her left to locate it.
- Jason said he makes himself "lighter" to cross. He didn't say how.
- She knows the Box is a prison, a training tool and a battery. He has never said what the battery powers.
- They hold hands. He kissed her forehead before they crossed. Nothing is named.

## What this chapter does

Learning the other side as an ecosystem. Hiding from something vast. Learning what has been paying for her trips. Then a surprise attack shows how good she's gotten, and a stranger names it.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Back and forth (about 500 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. They cross for a few hours and come back. Again and again.
  2. She's surprised they come back so often. He says she's not ready to sleep there: her shields have to hold while she's asleep, without her.
  3. On Earth she stares at the sun through the window like someone who just got out of prison.

### Scene 2: How it lives (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** it to make sense.
- **Beats:**
  1. Jason teaches her to read energy flows, territories and safe paths. It's ecology, not magic.
  2. **One relationship she witnesses:** a creature leaking mana from an old wound wanders into a patch of vine. The vine wraps it and drinks from the leak, slowly, without killing it. The creature lets it happen, even seems to rest. She can't decide whether it's horrible or kind.
  3. She thinks of the Box and doesn't say so.
  4. She misreads a territorial marker and walks them toward something's den. Jason turns her by the shoulders. She is mortified, and he's kind about it.

### Scene 3: Something passes (about 700 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Far off, something vast moves. She feels it as pressure, like a ship passing far out at sea. Then it comes closer.
  2. Jason pulls her down. They go completely still, shields locked tight, for minutes.
  3. She feels its attention pass over them like a pressure front, and move on without stopping.
  4. They see nothing. Nobody ever sees one of these on its own side.
  5. Afterward, shaking, she says: "You told me this in the car. That they're out there. I didn't understand it until now." He says some of them know about Earth and don't care, because it isn't worth what crossing would cost them. He doesn't add anything ominous.

### Scene 4: What pays for it (about 800 words)
- **Mira wants** the truth about the cost.
- **Beats:**
  1. Back in Vermont, Jason is grey with exhaustion. Every trip is worse. She pushes until he admits it.
  2. The toll takes a bigger share the stronger you are. To make himself lighter, he pushes part of himself into the Box.
  3. **The battery:** he's been drawing on the captured creatures to carry her across. Every trip is paid for partly by the things in the prison.
  4. She's angry twice: for them, and because he left it out. That's the second time he's hidden something about her since he promised not to.
  5. The Chapter 10 argument comes back, sharper. She tells him plainly she thinks it's wrong. He tells her plainly it's his ability, he built it, and he isn't going to stop using the one thing that makes crossing possible. Neither of them is wrong about the facts.
  6. **Where they land:** she keeps crossing, because she wants this training and it's his call how he pays for it. She doesn't pretend to agree, and he doesn't ask her to. He'll tell her every time he draws on them. It's a disagreement they'll carry, not a wound.

### Scene 5: The attack (about 800 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Next crossing. They're tense with each other. Jason is tired.
  2. Something attacks her from nowhere. Write this fast, in her POV, as a cascade, with her body moving before her thinking:
     - She feels the mana before the hit.
     - She throws a thin shield around herself.
     - She finds the direction and builds a second shield facing it.
     - She feels how strong the hit is. It needs three shields; there's time for two.
     - She swings the third behind her as a cushion.
     - The hit lands and she's thrown. She twists in the air, so she lands ready.
     - She fires before she's finished landing.
  3. It turns and leaves.
  4. She thinks that was just the basics Jason drilled into her.

### Scene 6: Kael (about 400 words)
- **Beats:**
  1. Someone laughs nearby: a man, with things riding on him that she can feel, small and cool against his mana, like cats on a lap.
  2. **Kael**, delighted: "Two facing shields before it landed, and a cushion. Who told you that was normal?" Mira says, "Him?" Kael turns toward Jason: "You let her think that was the beginner course?" He says it's either cruel or the best teaching he's ever seen.
  3. Let the scene carry it: she's not a beginner anymore, and Jason has been quietly very good at teaching her. Don't state either in narration.
  4. Kael calls it "ether" and the other side "the deep". He knows Jason by reputation. He invites them to visit and leaves before they can answer.
- **End:** Mira, still on the ground, turned toward Jason. "Expert level?" He says, "I may have rounded down."

## Jason in this chapter
- **Wants:** her strong enough to survive here without him. He has been hiding what it costs him.
- **His flaw, again:** leaving out the battery. He's sorry he hid it and not sorry he uses it.
- **Funny:** "I may have rounded down."
