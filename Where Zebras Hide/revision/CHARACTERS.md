# Characters

What each person wants, fears and is bad at, and how they talk. Items marked *(proposed)* are my inventions to fill gaps. Change any of them.

A note for whoever drafts from this: show these traits through what people do and say. Never have the narration name the trait.

## Mira

- **Facts:**
  - 26 *(proposed)*. Lives in Columbus, Ohio, in her own apartment with a roommate.
  - Office job: analyst work, pivot tables, the Henderson report due Monday.
  - Shoots film on a Pentax she bought last month, using expired stock that gives a green cast. There is an undeveloped roll in the camera. She has been looking at darkroom space.
  - Her sister lives in Chicago, keeps sending apartment listings, and finishes a part-time master's degree in June. Her parents are pushy about timelines: Mom asks about promotions, Dad mails grad-school applications.
  - Has met Jason for coffee every Tuesday for two years.
- **Wants:**
  - Act I: Jason, and for him to stop managing her.
  - After that: to stay alive without becoming someone she doesn't recognize.
  - In Act IV: to save as many people as she can.
- **Fears:**
  - Being edited, managed or decided for ("My mind is mine").
  - Being a liability or a pet.
  - Later, what she is turning into.
- **Bad at:**
  - Cooking.
  - Letting things go: she refines what others call finished.
  - Asking for comfort. She gives herself four minutes to cry, then washes her face.
- **How she thinks:**
  - Methodical under stress. She documents, tests and builds her own model of what she's told; the zebra image is hers.
  - She translates new things into photography: exposure, grain, latent images, developer.
  - She is funny in a dry, petty way ("I hated Dave. I hated his carrots.").
- **How she talks:** Short and direct when it matters. Wry when she's coping. She asks precise follow-up questions. She pushes back and takes positions.
- **Arc:** Devotion pulls her in. Competence and duty keep her going. By the end she does to strangers what she refused to let Jason do to her, and she knows it. She never stops loving Jason; at the end she can't hear him over the scale of what she's done. The outline should mark where devotion becomes duty, around the Ch 22 homecoming.
- **The lying:** She lies to family to protect them (see RULES). It gets easier, and that scares her.

## Jason

- **Facts:**
  - 28 *(proposed)*.
  - Remote freelance programmer ("contract work, programming stuff").
  - Started producing at 8 or 9 while compulsively working on a diagram he'd invented. Spent two months sure he was dying. Hid it.
  - The community found him at 12, by which point he had three techniques they'd never seen. First nearly died at 10.
  - Left home at 17 by not coming back after a summer. His parents are good people. He calls them sometimes from numbers they can't trace, and tells everyone else he doesn't talk to them. In Ch 1 he tells Mira "we didn't really get along", and she catches the lie in Ch 6.
  - Lives off-grid: he moves money without a paper trail and buys cars with cash.
- **Wants:** To keep what he built. To keep Mira alive. To be left alone.
- **Fears:** Losing her. Being responsible for someone else's death. Being made to share.
- **Real flaw:**
  - He decides alone and tells her afterward: the Ch 1 gamble, the two days of watching the hunters, the hidden cost of crossing.
  - He doesn't think this is a flaw. Mira names it, twice. By Ch 29 he asks her instead of deciding.
- **Ethics:** Careful about humans and consent. Not careful about anything else, which is why the Box doesn't bother him.
- **Ownership:**
  - "Because I made it. It's mine."
  - He never changes his mind about this. In Act IV he releases everything only because the world is ending, and still believes it was his.
- **Bad at:** Asking for help. Explaining things that feel obvious to him. Small talk. Staying anywhere.
- **Personal texture** *(proposed, your call)*:
  - He tests ideas on everyday objects, like coffee heated to an exact temperature. He loses track of time doing it.
  - He leaves half-finished calculations on any paper nearby: receipts, napkins, the margin of a map.
  - He eats Mira's bad cooking without complaint because he can't cook either. She finds out late in Act II, which is a small comic beat.
- **How he talks:**
  - Dry and brief.
  - Long explanations only when he actually knows the answer, and then he's precise and a little pleased with himself.
  - He jokes flatly ("Seventy percent of the planet is water. I'm a little surprised it only took three.").
  - When he won't answer, he deflects with a new question or a task. Never "he says nothing".
- **How he shows love:** By acts he doesn't explain. The model is Ch 29: he stays within half a mile of her primary body and never says why. Also the math he did on her survival during escapes.
- **Never write him as:** a stoic sensei, a silent nodder, or a man whose reaction is described as an absence.

## Delilah

- **Facts:** In her sixties *(proposed)*. Has practiced for thirty years. Lives within driving distance of the Appalachian cabin. Better than Jason at fighting things on Earth. Never crossed, and assumed it was impossible.
- **Wants:** Not to lose another newcomer. She has watched several die *(proposed: she names one, a boy she started teaching too late)*.
- **Flaw:** Her caution turns into policy. She ends up in the harsher-suppression faction in Act IV and asks Mira to do the Ch 24 erasures.
- **Role:**
  - She teaches the living side of magic: warming, cleaning, taste, how to behave around powerful people, how to read intent. She also teaches erasure, as a normal tool.
  - Her offhand remarks nudge Mira, but Mira does the working out.
- **How she talks:**
  - Practical, blunt and specific. She explains by pointing at examples ("There's a woman in Tucson who proofs bread with it. Thirty years. Never fought anything.").
  - She calls Mira by name, not "child".
  - She is not the same voice as the elder at the mansion.
- **Ch 29:** She argues Mira into resting and organizes relief. Mira makes the call.

## Kael

- **Facts:** Has lived partly on the other side for years, in symbiosis with certain creatures.
- **Wants:** Proof that humans can live alongside the other side, maybe even bargain with it. Act IV tests this belief.
- **Role:**
  - He watches Mira's shield sequence and names exactly what is unusual about it.
  - He estimates she is "top 100", which is his opinion, not a ranking.
  - He warns about the informational walls thinning ("How many viral videos are you from catastrophe?").
  - In Ch 29 he asks whether her clones are people.
- **On Jason:** A new angle instead of "hoarder". *(Proposed: he respects Jason's technique and pities his loneliness.)*
- **How he talks:** Quick, amused, unsettling. He states things others leave unsaid. He doesn't go quiet easily, so when he does, it matters.

## The hunters

- **Who:** Three practitioners who asked Jason politely for years, offered money and technique swaps, then pressured him, then attacked.
- **The named one:** Owen *(proposed name)*. He wants the randomized teleport to evacuate newcomers before predators reach them. He once lost a student to a crossing he could have outrun with it *(proposed)*. That is the full-strength counterargument to Jason.
- **Faction:** In Act IV Owen is in the go-public faction.
- **Appearances:**
  - On the page before Ch 13: one close call or a message.
  - In Ch 13, where they watch Mira fight and now know she exists.
  - At the Act IV mansion.
- **"Took you long enough, asshole."** Owen sends it in Act IV.

## Priya

- **Facts:** 20, a student. She made the energy-manipulation video and its two follow-ups.
- **Personality:** Excited, methodical, keeps a physical notebook. She asks better questions than Mira did.
- **Ch 24:** She has been producing for 72 hours, so she can't be erased. Mira teaches her sensing and hands her to a local practitioner.
- **Comes back:** in Act IV with her notebook, as part of the response.
