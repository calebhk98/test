Story Summary: In a world where knowledge itself is dangerous, a parallel dimension overlapping Earth runs on mana instead of sunlight. When humans learn about mana's existence, their bodies involuntarily begin producing it, transforming them into prey for dimensional creatures that hunt by its scent. Most who discover this truth die within months, unable to develop defensive skills fast enough. The secret has been carefully guarded for centuries. During the witch trials, the knowledge spread catastrophically, creating feedback loops of death that nearly exposed everything. Since then, practitioners have actively suppressed the truth, sponsoring science and rationalism to make magic seem impossible. Jason is a prodigy, Newton-level genius at developing mana techniques. He's created abilities most practitioners thought impossible, including randomized teleportation and virtual combat simulations. This makes him a target: human hunters want his knowledge, and his powerful mana signature attracts dangerous predators. When the protagonist witnesses Jason use his abilities during an attack, she's pulled into this hidden world. Jason explains the terrible truth: simply knowing about mana will transform her body, forcing her to either develop lethal skills or die within months. She can't return to normal life. She can barely tell anyone without risking their lives. She chooses to stay with Jason and learn, beginning brutal training to survive. Eventually, she'll train in the mana dimension itself, surrounded by hostile creatures, hiding from intelligent beings powerful enough to kill her instantly. The story builds toward an apocalyptic scenario: the secret fails, billions of untrained humans suddenly produce mana, and Earth becomes a buffet that justifies powerful dimensional creatures finally crossing over en masse.
