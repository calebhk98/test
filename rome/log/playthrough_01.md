# LOG - Playthrough Records

Generated by `rome/sim/simulator.py` from the `recommended` strategy, 400 Monte
Carlo runs, seeds 0-399. These are the actual outputs, not a story written to
flatter the plan. The failed run is included because it is the more likely one
in several of the variants.

**Headline result over 400 runs**

| | |
|---|---|
| Reached the transistor within 400 years | 315 of 400 (79%) |
| Best case | 300 AD (200 years) |
| Median | 384 AD (284 years) |
| Worst success | 562 AD (462 years) |
| Dominant failure | the founder dies without training successors |

Read `../02_STRATEGY.md` for what these numbers mean and `../README.md` for how
much to trust them. The short version: trust the ORDERING and the FAILURE MODES,
do not trust the absolute years to better than a factor of about 1.5.
