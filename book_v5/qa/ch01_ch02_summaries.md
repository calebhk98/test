# Chapter 1 & 2 Summaries (continuity reference for ch03 work)

Generated by Sonnet subagents reading each chapter cold. Kept short on purpose —
these are the running-state going into chapter 3.

## Chapter 1
Daniel Mercer, a teenager working at Brentwood Hardware, trips in the parking lot
on his way to a shift and blacks out for about twenty-five minutes. He wakes in an
unfamiliar rural field — clean air, no roads, no cell signal. Walking toward
civilization he meets people in ancient dress speaking an unknown language, living
in mud-and-thatch structures, using hand-forged iron nails. From a hillside he sees
an intact, new-looking Colosseum and realizes he has traveled to ancient Rome. Four
Roman soldiers surround him and march him toward the city.

## Chapter 2
The soldiers march Daniel into Rome with his hands bound, and lock him in a stone
cell with a high barred window. Alone, he realizes his phone is the only piece of
his future that still works, so he conserves the battery ruthlessly (radios off,
screen dimmed) and uses Notes to record what he fears forgetting — scientific facts,
his mother's number, his home address. A curious off-duty guard inspects the phone
and his clothes but returns the phone. Daniel works out his survival strategy: never
reveal the time-travel truth (unprovable, would mark him for destruction); instead
pose as a learned traveler from a distant northern land, someone worth keeping alive.
Near nightfall a higher-ranking soldier (the optio) cuts his bindings, gives him warm
grain porridge, and exchanges names with him.

high-school junior. Any draft drift toward "20-something" or a smoothly mature Daniel
is a voice error, not canon.
