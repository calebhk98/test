## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

Two instances found. Both are BLOCKING.

- **L43:** `"So I let it go. Not all at once and not cleanly, but I let it go, and went home to a smaller thing."`
  This is a correctio structure. "Not all at once and not cleanly" cancels a premise (the act of letting go) by specifying what it is NOT, then restates it. All variants of this pattern are banned regardless of elegance. Direct-statement fix: `"So I let it go, the way you set down a thing too heavy to carry further, and went home."`

- **L43 (same sentence), secondary:** The phrase "went home to a smaller thing" is borderline — it gestures toward a correctio-adjacent deflation — but is not technically a banned construction on its own. Not flagging as a hard block, but note the rhythmic pattern echoes the banned "not X but Y" logic.

- **L25:** `"Because under the relief, and it was relief, there was something I had not budgeted for..."` — The parenthetical "and it was relief" is a self-correcting clarification that functions as a mild correctio (anticipates that the reader might doubt whether what he felt was relief, then confirms it). This is a borderline instance; it names what it IS after suggesting uncertainty. By strict construction, this is not the banned "not X, it was Y" pattern — it inserts the Y without first stating a negated X. Ruling: borderline, not a hard block, but note for revision.

- **L49:** `"asked, not whether I was well, because she does not ask a question she has the answer to."` — Negation-as-definition on "not whether I was well" — defines the question by specifying what it was NOT before giving the actual question. This is a light variant of the correctio pattern. The prose patterns doc covers "Not X. Y." as banned; this is "not X ... [implicit Y]." Ruling: borderline correctio variant. Recommend revision: `"She asked about the western survey, not after me — she already knew how I was."` or similar that leads with the positive.

**Hard blocking count: 1 confirmed (L43). 2 borderline candidates (L25, L49) — flag for writer review.**
