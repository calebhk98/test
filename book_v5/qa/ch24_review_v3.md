## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

Two candidates identified; assessment follows.

**Candidate 1 — L75:** `"Not my colors - not the ones I'd been building toward with the pile and the galvanometer chain, nothing formal."`

**Ruling: BORDERLINE / SHOULD FIX.** This is a negation used as self-clarification within an inventory passage — "not my colors" is used to specify which colors the farrier's table does NOT contain, not as a rhetorical pivot from a wrong to a right description. The doubled negation ("Not my colors - not the ones...") intensifies the pattern but the sentence does not cancel a premise and replace it with a better-stated truth. Under a strict reading of the zero-tolerance rule this approaches correctio and warrants revision. Suggested fix: "His colors weren't the calibrated series I'd been building toward — these were field readings: cherry, bright cherry, orange, lemon-yellow, white." Alternatively drop the negation entirely: "His grandfather had organized them by what a smith sees in the fire — cherry, bright cherry, orange, lemon-yellow, white."

**Candidate 2 — L59:** `"I do that. Not often."`

**Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is a self-interrupting rhythmic note, not a correctio pivot. The two sentences do not define "I do that" by negating a prior description. No fix needed.

**Candidate 3 — L55 (stacked negatives):** `"It is simply asleep and there is no waking it, no fire I can build, no wheel I can turn, no clever Roman trick and no clever Thulean one either..."`

**Ruling: PERMITTED.** Stacked negatives serving as an accumulating list of what fails — this is not a "not X, it's Y" correctio. The sentence doesn't cancel one characterization and replace it. The rhythm earns the accumulation. No fix needed.

**Summary:** No confirmed zero-tolerance correctio violations. One borderline instance (L75) recommended for revision under "should fix."

### 3c. Meta-disclaimers / throat-clearing (ZERO allowed)

**Found — L65:** `"...and I am not going to write any more about it than that, because the rule of this whole place is that you do not get to say the loud true thing out loud..."`

**Ruling: BORDERLINE VIOLATION.** The phrase "I am not going to write any more about it than that" is structurally a meta-disclaimer — the narrator announces a limit on what will be written, which is a variant of the TIC V1 / TIC V14 throat-clearing pattern. The style guide's full prohibition covers: "Must not announce emotional weight" and the PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID.md list names "I am not going to dress up what I felt" as a variant. However, this instance has a significant mitigating factor: the meta-commentary is itself content. The narrator's decision not to write the "loud true thing" is the scene's emotional payload — the act of withholding is the subject, and describing it briefly is necessary. It functions differently from a preamble that excuses the upcoming scene. Still, the phrase earns scrutiny. **Ruling: Not a blocker, but a SHOULD FIX.** The sentence could be trimmed: cut "and I am not going to write any more about it than that, because" and let "the rule of this whole place is..." begin its own sentence directly after the loneliness declaration.
