Never reuse a closing image within five chapters."* This chapter closes on it; ch29 also contains it at its close (L75 of ch29: *"I went and stood over the fire and waited for the bubbles"*); ch30 uses it repeatedly (L17, L61, L67). The pattern has fully re-established itself in V3. The ch28 closing image must be changed to break the chain. The second-to-last paragraph (L81) — *"I went out into the camp with the cooking fires going down all along the straight lines of it... east, always east"* — is a stronger, more specific image cut. Consider ending there.
