## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

Two instances found:

- **L85 (correctio-adjacent):** "There was nothing in it about how the bronze man worked, because the how wasn't her dream. **The not-having-to-own-the-grinder was the dream.**"
  The second sentence defines the entry by negating what it isn't (a story about mechanics) and then naming what it is (freedom from ownership). The phrasing "The not-having-to-own-the-grinder was the dream" is a nominalized negation — structurally a correctio. The sentence defines a thing by its privation rather than stating it directly.
  **Fix:** "She'd dreamed about the freedom of it: a grinder that never called in sick, never stole flour, needed no watching. That was the dream." Or simply end the paragraph at "I gave her tablet to Tyche."

- **L169 (stacked negation — correctio-adjacent):** "...the one thing in all of Rome I knew for certain could not be fixed, **not by him, not by me, not by Hermes** and every ruined batch behind the cattle market."
  Three consecutive "not by" clauses define the impossibility by elimination rather than direct statement. This is the list-of-negatives variant of the correctio (documented in V4_AUDIT_REVIEW_AND_CLEANUP.md §Issue 2 as a confirmed surviving pattern).
  **Fix:** "...the one thing in all of Rome I knew for certain no one could fix — not Hermes with all his ruined batches, not me, not Felix." Or reframe positively: "...the hardest wall the workshop had ever run up against."

Both are BLOCKING under zero-tolerance.

### 3d. Narrator stepping outside time / future-vantage (ZERO allowed)

Two instances require ruling:

- **L87:** "...this child had walked in and asked for the one marvel **I knew was actually coming**, someday, for somebody who wasn't either of us."
  **Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is retrospective knowledge Daniel had at the time — he already knew telescopes were technically possible; his knowledge of that future is the character's contemporary knowledge (the telescope exists as a concept; Roman glass is the wall). It reports what Daniel knew then, not what the future proved true.

- **L109:** "...to the engine **I knew was the door to everything**."
  **Ruling: BORDERLINE / FLAG.** Daniel at this moment knows steam is the door to everything — that is his contemporary assessment, not future-vantage. However, the construction "the door to everything" has the flavor of assigned historical weight ("this was the pivotal technology") rather than a character's in-the-moment belief. A strict reading passes it (he believes it then); a strict rule application flags it for the grandeur of the claim. Recommend softening: "to something I'd been circling for years" or "to the engine I'd been trying to reach."

- **L135:** "It also caused **exactly the trouble I should have expected**."
  **Ruling: BORDERLINE — permitted but weak.** This is retrospective self-criticism ("I should have seen it"), not future-tense dramatic announcement. It reports Daniel's retrospective view of his own blindness. Not banned, but it brushes against TIC V3 (False Modesty Rhythm) — the admission of a predictable failure before the scene explains itself. Watchable.

- **L163:** "**I knew when I planted it that the field was a hundred years long and I'd be in the ground for most of it**, and I planted it anyway..."
  **Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is the retrospective narrator reporting his contemporary knowledge and intent — this is not dramatic-irony announcement but the character's own stated awareness at the time of action. The retrospective frame licenses this.

### Blocking issues (must fix before next use)

1. **[Zero-tolerance: correctio] L85** — "The not-having-to-own-the-grinder was the dream." Nominalized negation defining a thing by what it isn't before saying what it is. Fix: state the dream directly — e.g., "She'd dreamed about never having to own the grinder at all, never having to watch it, never having to feed it." Or end the paragraph at "I gave her tablet to Tyche."

2. **[Zero-tolerance: correctio, stacked negation] L169** — "...the one thing in all of Rome I knew for certain could not be fixed, not by him, not by me, not by Hermes and every ruined batch behind the cattle market." Three "not by" clauses. Fix: remove the triple negation — e.g., "...the one problem I'd run every mind I trusted at for three years, Hermes included, and none of us had moved the wall an inch."

3. **[Wisdom-button over limit] Scene 12, L163** — "A generation playing at machines that didn't exist. I knew when I planted it that the field was a hundred years long and I'd be in the ground for most of it, and I planted it anyway, on a borrowed name, on Minerva's festival, with a slave's tale of a ship in the front row." This is a full wisdom-button / epigraph-extractable close on a scene that already landed. End the scene at "they went out on cheap gray sheets" or the children-playing-at-the-wharf image (strong image close) and cut the aphoristic interpretation.
