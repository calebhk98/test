# Chapter Summaries — built sequentially via subagent reads

Each summary is 3-5 sentences, plot facts only. Built by handing each subagent
the prior chapter summaries and asking it to summarize ONLY its new chapter.

---

## Ch01
Daniel Mercer, a teenager working at Brentwood Hardware, trips in the parking lot at ~7:15 AM and wakes up 25 minutes later in a harvested field. He walks toward a settlement of mud-and-stone buildings whose inhabitants wear rough tunics and speak an unfamiliar language. From a rise he sees a massive city below — including an intact, newly-built Colosseum — and concludes he is in ancient Rome. Four armed soldiers seize him and march him down toward the city.

## Ch02
Soldiers march Daniel, wrists bound, through a foul-smelling town to a small stone cell with a high barred window. Alone, he powers on his phone (98% battery), switches to airplane mode, and records his existence in Notes: the date, "ROME 98 AD??," his mother's number (last four digits uncertain), friends' names, and a selfie. A guard inspects but doesn't recognize the phone. At night a higher-ranking soldier cuts his bindings, they exchange names — Daniel learns the optio's name is two syllables with a falling second — and Daniel eats a bowl of grain mush with fish sauce in the dark.

## Ch03
In the morning Daniel is taken to a painted building and questioned by a senior official who cannot understand him. When the official is about to dismiss him, Daniel borrows the clerk's wax tablet and draws a detailed map of the Mediterranean, which the official recognizes; he then extends Africa to its southern tip and sketches North and South America across the blank western ocean before privately cursing himself for revealing so much. The official seizes the map for copying and sends for a foreign healer who might communicate with Daniel. The optio buys Daniel two bread rolls on the walk back to the cell, and Daniel plans to spend his phone's remaining 11% battery secretly studying its offline map app to draw better coastlines next time.

## Ch04
A Greek-speaking freed-slave physician (~45) examines Daniel and the two spend the day exchanging number systems via wax tablet, with Daniel demonstrating Hindu-Arabic numerals, zero, and a stumbling long-division problem. Daniel again draws his world map, this time presenting the Americas as uninhabited to minimize alarm, and gestures that he comes from a cold place beyond Britain. By afternoon he is moved from the cell to a workshop loft with a pallet, blanket, bench-workers watching him, and a guard at the courtyard door — better than the cell but still captivity. That night his phone dies completely (last reading ~10%), taking his photos, notes, and map app with it; he is 17 years old.

## Ch05
Over roughly a year Daniel learns Latin through immersion at the workshop, picking up the physician's name — Heras — about four months in. When a workshop boy's infected cut develops a red sepsis line, Daniel tries to explain germ theory on a wax tablet; Heras dismisses the "little ones" as dangerous superstition, but Daniel salvages it by insisting on boiled water and hand-washing, the boy survives, and Heras quietly adopts the practices without attribution. Daniel also makes soap from rendered tallow and wood-ash lye (the household calls it "the Thulean's muck"), privately stops using lead cookware and warns Heras about lead pipes (Heras politely disbelieves), and — now eighteen — posts an anonymous glass-prize challenge through a guild contact named Drusus, offering a month's skilled wages for a clear optical-quality pane. That winter, lying in the loft feeling warm air rise through the tiles, he conceives of a hot-air balloon as a public, undeniable demonstration.
