Deprivation enters the model at two grains: per neighbourhood (imd_lsoa, via postcode) and per local authority (imd_lad). Premises-per-1,000 divides a premise-grain count by an LSOA-grain population — a ratio across grains. Flatten all this onto one premise row and any coarse measure (population, an LA's score) repeats thousands of times and double-counts when summed.
