# Proposal: Anime and Manga

**Brief:** currently a minor part of the trip (Akihabara 1h10 on Day 3, Ghibli Museum
Day 10). Group wants mainstream (One Piece, Pokemon) and deeper cuts (No Game No Life,
Steins;Gate) covered at "not the majority, not minor either" weight. This proposal adds
roughly 5-6 hours of dedicated anime/manga content across the trip, almost all of it
free or near-free, by exploiting geography already in the plan (Skytree Town, Shibuya,
Otemachi/Tokyo Station, Namba/Nippombashi) rather than adding new travel days. Two
items cost real money (Kyoto Manga Museum, a small Akihabara gachapon budget); one item
(Kyoto Manga Museum) is the only genuinely new activity spend.

**Honesty up front on the two deep cuts named by the group:** nothing themed
specifically to No Game No Life or Steins;Gate is running as a shop, exhibition, or
cafe in the 7 Oct - 5 Nov 2026 window, with one exception: Steins;Gate is unusual in
that its setting is a real, standing building (Akihabara Radio Kaikan) and its
maid-cafe location has a real real-world counterpart (Cafe Mai:lish) that still
operates. No Game No Life has no such anchor - its last dedicated collaboration cafe
ran in Ikebukuro in spring 2018 and is long closed. For both titles, day-to-day
merchandise (figures, blu-rays, doujinshi, art books) is realistically covered by
Mandarake, Animate, and Nakano Broadway, all proposed below. Do not expect more than
that; anything claiming a current NGNL-specific tie-in should be treated as unverified.

Format: `Item | Day | What/why | Address | Adult cost | Party cost | Duration |
Infant/stroller reality | Displaces`. FX ¥155 = $1. [VERIFIED] = confirmed via web
search this session; [ESTIMATE] = planning figure, confirm closer to travel.

## The pop-up/collab-cafe window check (7 Oct - 5 Nov 2026)

Checked against japan-pop-now.com's 2026 collaboration-cafe calendar, which tracks
exactly this kind of rotating event. In the trip's window, the only confirmed
Tokyo/Osaka items are the Frieren pop-up above (real but off-route), a Touken Ranbu x
Kyoto National Museum sword exhibition (Oct 3 - Nov 24, a different fandom than this
group named), and a Haikyu!! exhibition opening Oct 30 in Roppongi (also off-route,
and opens right at the end of this window). **Nothing tied to One Piece, Pokemon,
Steins;Gate, or No Game No Life specifically was confirmed running in this window.**
Collaboration cafes rotate on a roughly monthly cycle and are typically announced only
6-10 weeks out, so this is genuinely a "check again closer to the date" situation, not
a dead end. Re-check about a month before departure (early September 2026) at
japan-pop-now.com/collab-cafe-calendar and collabo-cafe.com/en/events - both list
venue, dates, and reservation requirements, and Animate Cafe/Gratte locations
(including the one inside Animate Akihabara Building 2, already on the Day 3 route)
run their own rotating tie-ins worth a glance on the day.

## Budget summary

Tier 1 total new hard cost: roughly 2,500 (Akihabara gachapon) + 3,600 (Kyoto Manga
Museum) = **6,100 yen ($39)** against the trip's ~28,000 yen ($181) of genuinely free
activities budget - and Day 3's museum-surcharge swap alone recovers 5,100 yen of that,
so the net new spend across the whole proposal is closer to **1,000 yen ($6)** if that
swap is taken. Everything else in Tier 1 is a free walk-in with an optional,
uncounted souvenir. Tier 2 items are genuinely optional add-ons (~1,000-4,860 yen
depending on which are taken) for a group with room left in the ¥159,680 contingency
line; none are load-bearing for the "not minor" goal, which Tier 1 alone achieves.
