# Proposal: Future tech, robots, mechas, "cool" tech

## Headline finding before anything else

This trip's two obvious marquee stops for this interest **both fall through by the
travel dates (Oct 7 - Nov 5, 2026)**:

- **Gundam Factory Yokohama is gone.** It permanently closed March 31, 2024, after a
  farewell drone show and a speech by series director Yoshiyuki Tomino. The 18m moving
  RX-78F00 Gundam, the museum, the cafe, and the shop at Yamashita Pier are all gone.
  There is nothing to book, verify, or route around. [VERIFIED - Wikipedia, Time Out
  Tokyo, SoraNews24]
- **Miraikan closes for a 6-month refurbishment from Oct 1, 2026 to Apr 22, 2027** -
  an official museum announcement dated March 2026. Day 8 visits Odaiba on **Oct 14,
  2026**, two weeks into the closure. Miraikan will be shut for the entire trip.
  [VERIFIED - miraikan.jst.go.jp official closure notice]
- **The Unicorn Gundam statue at DiverCity is also gone by then.** Bandai Namco ended
  its 9-year Odaiba run on Aug 31, 2026 (retirement ceremony Aug 29), more than a month
  before this trip lands. A follow-on "Gundam Landmark Project" was teased at the same
  announcement, but no location, format, or opening date has been disclosed - it
  cannot be planned around. [VERIFIED - Tokyo Weekender, Time Out Tokyo, Wikipedia]

So the group's three best-fit venues in the entire country are a closed factory, a
closed museum, and a dismantled statue. The proposals below are what is actually left,
honestly labeled. The news is not all bad - the National Museum of Nature and Science
in Ueno is an unexpectedly strong, nearly-free substitute sitting right next to a day
already scheduled there.

Nothing to propose here beyond re-checking miraikan.jst.go.jp closer to any future trip. |

The successor "Gundam Landmark Project" has no announced date or venue as of this research and cannot be booked around. |

| **Toyota Mega Web, Odaiba** | Closed permanently Dec 31, 2021, demolished April 2022. The site is being redeveloped as Tokyo A-Arena, a basketball stadium with no automotive theme, targeted for 2025 completion. Confirmed gone, correctly excluded already. |
| **Oedo-Onsen Monogatari, Odaiba** | Confirmed closed Sept 5, 2021 due to land-lease expiry, per the brief's suspicion. Not a tech venue in any case, noted here only because the brief asked for verification. |
