## Costed but NOT scheduled, for the family to decide

**Bunraku, National Bunraku Theatre, Osaka.** ¥6,500/adult, run 31 Oct - 23 Nov,
no minimum age, one block from the Day 29 Kuromon lunch stop. It is the best
answer to "how does Japanese theatre differ", since puppets worked in full view
need no Japanese. **Not scheduled because Part 1 runs 2h20+, which means one adult
alone with both infants for that whole stretch on the last full day, and Day 29 is
already the fullest day in Osaka.** Taking it means dropping the Tombori River
Cruise and trimming Shinsekai. Ready to book if the family wants it.

## Rejected, with reasons

- **Fireworks: none exist in this window.** Hanabi season is July and August. The nearest, Kobe Minato Hanabi (19-23 Oct), is a 20-minute show behind a 3-hour round trip finishing well past infant bedtime. A season problem, not a planning gap.
- **Miraikan closed 1 Oct 2026 - 22 Apr 2027**, Gundam Factory Yokohama closed permanently Mar 2024, the DiverCity Unicorn Gundam statue removed 31 Aug 2026, and the Expo 2025 site is mid-teardown. The robots-and-mecha ask is largely unavailable on these dates.
- **J-pop concerts**: fan-club lottery months ahead; walk-in idol shows are late-night standing-room.
- **No other illumination**: Marunouchi, Shibuya and Roppongi all open after the 5 Nov departure.
- **Shichi-Go-San** (children in formal dress at shrines) is 15 Nov, after departure. **Gion Odori** is 1-10 Nov, just after the Kyoto stay ends on 29 Oct.
- Nothing **No Game No Life** specific exists anywhere in 2026; the last collab cafe closed in 2018.
- Maid cafes beyond the Mai:lish exterior, batting centres, standing bars: not compatible with infants present.
