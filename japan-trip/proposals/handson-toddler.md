## TOPIC 2 - THINGS THE TODDLERS WILL ACTUALLY ENJOY

### Developmental honesty, stated once up front

A 12-month-old and a 20-month-old are not the same audience, and treating them
as one "toddler" line item overstates what the younger one will get out of
several of these. As a rule across everything below: **the 12-month-old
responds to sensory input** - bright colors, motion, new textures, being
carried somewhere new - **not to content**. Character recognition, "this is a
train," "this is Anpanman," simply isn't there yet. **The 20-month-old is
closer to genuine engagement**: early word recognition, basic character
familiarity if exposed at home beforehand, real (if brief and wobbly) physical
play on equipment sized for them, and a real reaction to animals, trains, and
bright interactive exhibits. Neither is old enough to remember the trip
afterward - these picks are for how the day actually goes, not for the photo
album's future audience.
