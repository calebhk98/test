# Days 1-10 - Tokyo

*Base: Hotel Livemax Higashi-Ueno, 10 nights, Wed 7 Oct - Fri 16 Oct 2026. This is the
first segment of the trip, so the running totals below are the trip's true cumulative
totals starting from ¥0 - no baseline adjustment needed.*

**Segment notes (read once, applies across all ten days):**
- **Nikko was left out of this segment.** Nikko is roughly 2h each way from Ueno by
  train (Tobu limited express or JR/Tobu combination), so a there-and-back day already
  spends ~4h in transit before any temple time. Added to Toshogu's stairs and gravel
  approach, a same-day round trip would blow past the ~7h active cap and the mandatory
  midday rest for two children under two. It is a strong candidate for a future trip
  with an overnight in Nikko itself, not a day trip from Tokyo with this party.
- Of the two optional day-trip bases named in the brief, this segment uses **Yokohama**
  (Day 9) rather than Kawagoe - Yokohama's Minato Mirai/Chinatown loop is flatter,
  better stroller terrain, and pairs a genuinely different city feel (harbor, not
  Edo-period streets, which Yanaka on Day 6 already covers) against the rest of the plan.
- The Livemax kitchenette carries real weight in this segment: self-catered breakfast
  every day, self-catered dinner on six of the ten evenings, and the Day 1 grocery run is
  costed as the full initial stock-up (pantry staples, not just one meal) so later days
  only need top-ups. Two evenings use hangaku (closing-time markdown) pricing
  deliberately - Day 1's stock-up run and Day 4's tired-night bento - and are marked
  as such below.

  and no strollers are permitted indoors; the stroller can be left folded at the entrance.

  the mandatory rest.
- **Ghibli Museum booking mechanics (honesty note):** tickets are not sold on the day and
  cannot simply be bought in advance at will. They are released by lottery on the 10th of
  the month before the visit month - for an October 2026 date, that means entering the
  lottery in the September 2026 window - via Lawson Ticket loppi terminals for buyers in
  Japan, or through an authorized overseas ticket allocation/travel agent (commonly a
  JTB-affiliated channel) for buyers applying from abroad. Entry is not guaranteed; have
  a backup plan (more time at Inokashira Park) if the lottery does not come through.
- The Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building's free observatory was considered for
  today and deliberately dropped - Shibuya Sky (Day 5) already covered an elevated city
  view, and stacking Shinjuku Gyoen + Ghibli Museum + TMG in one day would push the pace
  past what's comfortable with two infants.
