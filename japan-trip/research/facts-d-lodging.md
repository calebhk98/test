# Facts D — Budget Lodging (5 segments, 29 nights)

Party: 3 adults + 2 infants (~12mo, ~20mo). FX used throughout: **¥155 = $1**.
Hard ceiling: **¥25,000/night total, whole party, all rooms combined.**
Target band: **¥18,000–22,000/night.**
MIMARU (¥25,000–50,000/night) was already researched and rejected as over budget — not revisited here.

Every figure below is tagged **[VERIFIED, source]** (a number a named source actually stated) or
**[ESTIMATE]** (inferred/adjusted — e.g. today's lowest cached rate adjusted for double
occupancy and Oct/Nov 2026 shoulder season, since no booking engine would return
date-specific 2026 quotes to this tool chain — Booking.com, Agoda and Rakuten Travel
all serve JS-rendered search results that returned empty content on fetch). No address
or rate below is invented; where a source did not give one it is marked "unverified."

## A) Budget business-hotel chains — rates and child policy

**Core lever used everywhere below:** several chains let a preschool child (0–5/6)
co-sleep in an existing bed with a parent for FREE, one child per bed. A twin room
(2 beds) therefore legally houses 2 adults + 2 infants at the 2-adult rate. The
3rd adult goes in a separate single room. This "1 twin + 1 single" split beats
one large family room almost everywhere in Japan, confirming the trip's own
splitting strategy.

## B) Long-stay / weekly-monthly discounts

Investigated for the two 10-night blocks (Tokyo, Kyoto).

- **Weekly Mansion Tokyo**: weekly rates **¥35,000–65,000/week** in central wards,
  budget outer wards (Adachi, Edogawa, outer Nerima) from **¥25,000/week**, all-in
  (rent+utilities+Wi-Fi+furniture). A 1LDK/45m² family-size unit runs **50–80% above**
  the base 1K studio tier. [ESTIMATE, aggregated from A-Realty Blog's 2026 rate guide](https://arealty.jp/blog/weekly-apartments-tokyo-no-contract-furnished-2026/) —
  this is a real-estate blog, not a live booking quote; treat as directional only.
  - Doing the math: a budget-tier ¥25,000/week studio does **not** fit 5 people; a
    family 1LDK at "+50-80%" of a ¥35–65k/week base implies **¥52,500–117,000/week**,
    i.e. **¥7,500–16,700/night** for the unit alone before any second room is added
    for the 3rd adult. That undercuts business-hotel twin pricing in the best case
    but the range is too wide and too far from a real quote to recommend booking on;
    flagged for a follow-up direct inquiry, not used as the primary recommendation.
- **Monthly Mansion Tokyo**: **¥90,000/month** entry price, **¥120,000–280,000/month**
  by ward, all-inclusive. [ESTIMATE, same A-Realty source](https://arealty.jp/blog/monthly-mansion-tokyo-furnished-rooms-2026/) — a 10-night stay is
  under a full month, so a landlord/operator would very likely prorate up (monthly
  discounts assume 28–31 nights of amortization), making this **worse** per-night
  than the weekly rate for a 10-night block. Not pursued further.
- **Sakura House / Oak House**: Confirmed to operate **family-friendly apartments
  and vacation rentals** (daily & weekly rates) in Tokyo, Izu, and Kyoto, with
  no deposit/key-money/guarantor. [VERIFIED existence + policy, sakura-house.com](https://www.sakura-house.com/about/vacation-rental) — **no
  specific nightly/weekly ¥ figure was returned**; the site states rates vary by
  season and must be quoted directly (contact_us@sakura-house.com). **Cannot report
  a rate without inventing one — flagged as a live lead worth a direct email, not
  costed here.**
- **Leopalace21**: Confirmed ¥0 agency fee, furnished, utilities/Wi-Fi included,
  monthly listings in both Tokyo and Kyoto. [VERIFIED existence, leopalace21.com search pages](https://www.leopalace21.com/en/properties/monthly/area/kyoto) — **no
  per-night or per-week rate surfaced**; Leopalace typically prices as a monthly
  lease (often with a minimum-stay requirement, e.g. 1 month), which is a poor
  fit for a 10-night block. Not costed.
- **Village House**: Rent "starting from around ¥20,000" was returned but this reads
  as a **monthly** figure for very basic/rural stock, and the source itself flags
  Village House as **oriented to long-term leases, not short vacation stays**.
  [ESTIMATE, low confidence — japanlifestart.com guide](https://japanlifestart.com/en/housing-daily-life/village-house-guide-foreigners) Not usable for a 10-day trip.
- **MyStays**: Hotel MyStays Kyoto Shijo confirmed to exist (10-min walk to Shijo
  Station) but no kitchenette or weekly-rate data surfaced. [VERIFIED existence only, travelweekly.com listing](https://www.travelweekly.com/Hotels/Kyoto-Japan/Hotel-MyStays-Kyoto-Shijo-p3640706)

**Verdict on B:** none of the weekly/monthly apartment operators produced a
trustworthy, date-specific 10-night quote in this pass — their public pages are
lead-generation pages, not live booking engines, and every number found is either
a wide range or "contact us." **Do not book a weekly-mansion sight unseen on these
figures.** The business-hotel "1 twin + 1 single" split (section A) is the safer,
better-verified budget lever for the two 10-night blocks, and it already lands
inside the target band (see section E). Weekly mansions remain worth one direct
email to Sakura House before final booking, since their family-apartment product
is real and likely to include a kitchen, but they are not the primary recommendation
here for lack of a verifiable rate.

## D) Final recommendation per segment

Strategy used everywhere: **2 rooms** — 1 twin room (2 adults + 2 infants, infants
free co-sleeping) + 1 single/economy-double room (3rd adult), same property where
possible, so no crib is actually required (infants share a parent's bed) and one
adult sleeps alone. This is the configuration verified as free-for-infants at
Toyoko Inn, Hotel Livemax, Super Hotel, Dormy Inn, and Comfort Hotel (section A).

### Tokyo — 10 nights (Oct 7–16, 2026)
- **Property: Hotel Livemax Higashi-Ueno**, 3-5-1 Motoasakusa, Taito-ku, Tokyo 111-0041 [VERIFIED address, livejapan.com]
- 2 rooms: 1 twin (2 adults + 2 infants, co-sleeping free) + 1 single (3rd adult)
- Nightly total: **¥16,000–18,500 [ESTIMATE]**, built on the verified "from ¥7,600/room, 2 adults" base + shoulder-season uplift + a 2nd (single) room
- Included: **in-room kitchenette with fridge + microwave** [VERIFIED, livejapan.com] — the single biggest self-catering asset of any option researched; free Wi-Fi
- Nearest station: Iriya (Hibiya Line) or Inaricho, both short walk per listing; Ueno Station ~10–12 min walk
- Crib: not needed under the co-sleeping policy; confirm with front desk on arrival
- **Backup: Toyoko Inn (any of the ~15 Tokyo branches)** — free breakfast is the differentiator (see quantification below), est. ¥17,000–20,000/night for the same 2-room split

### Kawaguchiko — 3 nights (Oct 17–19, 2026)

### What free breakfast/onsen/ramen actually saves (quantified)

- **Toyoko Inn breakfast** (used at Kyoto, 10 nights, and as Tokyo backup): a simple
  Japanese/Western buffet (rice, miso soup, natto/pickles, bread, coffee) included
  free for all guests, children included [VERIFIED, toyoko-inn.com]. Buying an
  equivalent breakfast out (convenience-store or café) for 3 adults runs roughly
  ¥600–900/adult/day. Over 10 Kyoto nights: **≈¥18,000–27,000 saved for 3 adults [ESTIMATE]**.
- **Dormy Inn onsen + ramen** (not the primary pick here, but worth noting): a public
  bathhouse visit runs ¥500–1,000/adult, and the free late-night ramen would otherwise
  cost ¥700–900/bowl. Not counted in the totals above since Dormy Inn wasn't selected
  as a primary property, but worth a splurge-night swap if the budget has slack.
- **Hotel Livemax kitchenette** (Tokyo pick): not a "free meal" but a self-catering
  enabler — a supermarket-bought breakfast/dinner for 3 adults runs roughly
  ¥1,500–2,500 total vs. ¥2,400–3,600 for the same meal bought prepared, i.e.
  **≈¥900–1,100/day plausible savings across up to 2 meals/day [ESTIMATE]**, which
  matters more than breakfast alone given the trip depends on self-catering most meals.
