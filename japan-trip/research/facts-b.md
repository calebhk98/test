## TASK 1 — ACCOMMODATION

### General notes
- **Infant occupancy**: MIMARU chain-wide defines "children" as **age 6 and under**, and infants count toward the "children" quota on the room (not the adult quota) [VERIFIED, mimaruhotels.com]. A room rated "4 adults / 2 children" therefore legally fits our party (3 adults + 2 infants) at every MIMARU property checked. Always declare infant ages at booking; some chains (Toyoko Inn, APA, business hotels) count anyone in the room toward a hard per-room headcount cap regardless of age and often refuse 3 adults + 2 infants in a "twin/triple" room — apartment-hotels avoid this problem entirely.
- **Minpaku (Airbnb-style) rule** [VERIFIED, multiple real-estate/legal sites]: Private-lodging (minpaku) operators are capped at **180 operating nights/year** (counted from Apr 1) under the 2018 Private Lodging Business Act, must display a notification number, and Kyoto City additionally **bans minpaku in residential zones during school terms**. A licensed listing (notification number visible) is legally fine to book; an unlicensed one is legally risky and increasingly rare on major platforms. This is why the picks below lean toward MIMARU/licensed apartment-hotels rather than generic Airbnb listings, especially in Kyoto.
- Nightly JPY figures below are **current (Sept 2026) list/aggregator rates used as a proxy** for Oct/Nov 2026 pricing unless stated otherwise — Japanese hotels typically release exact Oct/Nov 2026 rates only 60–90 days out, so exact rates for these dates could not be verified live. All are flagged [ESTIMATE, proxy] even where the property/address is [VERIFIED].

### Hangaku (half-price) discount timing [VERIFIED, multiple lifestyle-guide sources]
Most supermarkets start discount stickers **3–4 hours before closing**. Typical staged pattern: **~20% off around 60 min before a discount window begins → 30% off around 30 min in → 50% off (hangaku) in the final ~15 minutes** before closing, applied to prepared deli food (osouzai — karaage, ebi-fry, tonkatsu), bento, and fresh items near their use-by date. For a supermarket closing at 22:00, expect first stickers ~18:00–19:00 and the 50%-off wave ~21:00–21:45. **Real savings potential**: buying dinner-ready bento/deli food at hangaku roughly **halves that specific item's cost** — e.g. a ¥600 bento becomes ¥300 — meaningful if the family times one supermarket run per day near closing, but only covers prepared/fresh items nearing expiry, not staples like rice or milk.
