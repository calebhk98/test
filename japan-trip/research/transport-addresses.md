# Transport Boarding Points and Addresses

Verified against operator and station-operator websites (Fujikyu Bus, Keisei Electric Railway, JR Central, JR West, Nankai Electric Railway, Osaka Metro, Kansai Airports) and cross-checked against Japan Post's postal code directory. Where a fact could not be confirmed on an operator's own site, it is marked unverified with what was found instead.

## Day 1: Narita to Tokyo, Keisei Access Express

**Narita Airport Terminal 1 Station** (serves Terminal 1 only)
1-1 Sanrizuka Goryobokujo, Narita-shi, Chiba 282-0011
Underground, one level below Terminal 1's arrivals floor. Both JR East and Keisei trains use this station.

**Narita Airport Terminal 2-3 Station** ("Airport Terminal 2 Building Station", serves Terminal 2 and Terminal 3)
Furugome, Narita-shi, Chiba 286-0104
Sits beneath Terminal 2; Terminal 3 is reached from here by a covered walkway, not a separate station.

**Keisei-Ueno Station** (Keisei Access Express terminus)
1-60 Uenokoen, Taito-ku, Tokyo 110-0007
This is a different building from JR Ueno Station. The two are about 150 meters apart, roughly a 2 to 5 minute walk depending on which JR ticket gate is used (the Shinobazu exit is the closer one).

## Day 11: Tokyo to Kawaguchiko, Fujikyu/Keio highway bus

**Busta Shinjuku (Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal)**, departure
5-24-55 Sendagaya, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo 151-0051
Bus boarding gates are on the 4th floor. The terminal sits directly above JR Shinjuku Station's New South Gate (Shin-Minami Gate); an escalator from the gate reaches the 4th-floor terminal entrance in about a minute. The exact boarding bay for a given departure is posted on the floor's departure board rather than fixed per route.

**Kawaguchiko Station bus terminal**, arrival
3639-2 Funatsu, Fujikawaguchiko-machi, Minamitsuru-gun, Yamanashi 401-0301
The bus terminal is built into the station building itself, so there are no stairs between the platform and the station concourse.

## Day 14: Kawaguchiko to Mishima (Mishima-Kawaguchiko Liner), then Mishima to Kyoto (Tokaido Shinkansen Hikari)

**Kawaguchiko Station**, Liner departure
Same address and building as above: 3639-2 Funatsu, Fujikawaguchiko-machi, Minamitsuru-gun, Yamanashi 401-0301.

**Mishima Station**, Liner arrival and shinkansen departure
16-1 Ichibancho, Mishima-shi, Shizuoka 411-0036
The Liner arrives at the South Exit (Minami-guchi) bus stop, on the opposite side of the station building from the Shinkansen gates, which face north. From the shinkansen gates, the route to the bus stop runs through the station concourse to the south side and takes about 5 minutes on foot.

**Kyoto Station**, arrival
901 Higashishiokoji-cho, Karasuma-dori Shiokoji-sagaru, Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto 600-8216

## Day 24: Kyoto to Hiroshima, Sanyo Shinkansen

**Kyoto Station**: 901 Higashishiokoji-cho, Karasuma-dori Shiokoji-sagaru, Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto 600-8216 (same as above).

**Hiroshima Station**

## Day 27: Hiroshima to Osaka (Sanyo/Tokaido Shinkansen to Shin-Osaka), then Osaka Metro Midosuji Line to Namba

**Shin-Osaka Station**
5-16-1 Nishinakajima, Yodogawa-ku, Osaka-shi, Osaka 532-0011

**Namba Station (Osaka Metro, Midosuji Line)**
Approximately 1-9-7 Namba, Chuo-ku, Osaka-shi, Osaka 542-0076. This street number is unverified against Osaka Metro's own site, which lists the station's exits and facilities but no postal address; the number above comes from independent map directories and from the city-run Namba Service Counter located inside the same station near exits 14/25. The postal code 542-0076 covers the whole Namba district and is confirmed.
The Midosuji Line's stop here is the Osaka Metro station specifically, one of four separate stations sharing the Namba name: Osaka Metro Namba (Midosuji, Yotsubashi, Sennichimae lines), Nankai Namba, Kintetsu/Hanshin's Osaka Namba, and JR Namba. It connects to Nankai Namba by underground passage in about 5 to 10 minutes of walking.

## Day 30: Osaka Namba to Kansai Airport, Nankai Airport Line rapid express

**Nankai Namba Station**, departure
5-1-60 Namba, Chuo-ku, Osaka-shi, Osaka 542-0076
This is a different station from the Osaka Metro Namba station used on Day 27, run by a different company, on a different level (Nankai's platforms are on the 3rd floor). They are connected by an underground/indoor passage and are roughly 300 to 500 meters apart, about a 5 to 10 minute walk.

**Kansai Airport Station**, arrival
1 Senshu-kuko Kita, Izumisano-shi, Osaka 549-0001
Both the JR West Kansai Airport Line and the Nankai Airport Line arrive here; the station is built into Terminal 1.

**Kansai International Airport Terminal 1**, for check-in
1 Senshu-kuko Kita, Izumisano-shi, Osaka 549-0001
Same address as the station above, since the station sits inside the terminal building. Domestic check-in counters are on the 2nd floor, international check-in on the 4th floor.

## What was not fully confirmed

- The exact Busta Shinjuku boarding bay number for the Fujikyu/Keio Kawaguchiko line was not found; Busta Shinjuku assigns bays per departure rather than per route, shown on the 4th-floor departure boards.
- The Osaka Metro Namba street number (1-9-7) is not published on Osaka Metro's own station page and should be treated as a well-corroborated but not operator-confirmed figure; the postal code (542-0076) is solid.
- All other addresses above were checked against the relevant operator's or station's own website (Fujikyu Bus, Keisei, JR Central, JR West's station finder, Nankai, Kansai Airports) and, for postal codes, against Japan Post's zipcode directory.
