Fuji shared guest kitchen, Fujikawaguchiko | - | About ¥1,000 per adult, ingredient cost for a self-catered meal (the day's dinner ingredients came to ¥3,000 for three adults) | About 700 kcal for the meal, approximate | Yosenabe is a mixed hot pot: a pot of broth kept gently simmering on a portable burner at the table, into which thin-sliced pork, napa cabbage, shiitake and enoki mushrooms, tofu and spring onion go in batches, cooked a few minutes at a time and eaten straight out of the pot as they finish rather than served all at once. It is a late-autumn dish in Japan, timed to the first cold weeks of the season, and this version was cooked in the hostel's shared guest kitchen using ingredients bought a few days earlier. Once the meat and vegetables are eaten, udon noodles go into the leftover broth to finish it as a last course rather than let the broth go to waste. The main hazard is the pot itself: it sits at a rolling simmer for the whole meal, which is scalding at toddler standing or reaching height, so it needs to stay out of a small child's reach with only an adult ladling out portions, and those portions should be cooled before a toddler eats them. |
