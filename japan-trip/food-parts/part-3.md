### Konbini, bento and set meals

The everyday backbone of eating in Japan, and what most of this trip's lunches are. This
is the one section that is not restaurants: onigiri, bento, eki-ben and karaage come from
convenience-store chains (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson), station kiosks and supermarket
delis rather than from a single named shop, so the website given below is the chain's own
site, and Where names the district rather than an address.
