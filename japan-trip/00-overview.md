# Japan, Thirty Days, Five People, $9,000

**3 adults + 2 infants (~12 months and ~20 months) | 7 October - 5 November 2026 | 30 days, 29 nights**

Planning exchange rate: **¥155 = $1.00 USD** (spot on 4 September 2026; 12-month band ¥140-164).
Excluded from the $9,000: international airfare, passports, visas, travel insurance.

## Executive summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | Wed 7 Oct 2026 (arrive Narita) to Thu 5 Nov 2026 (depart Kansai) |
| **Shape** | Open-jaw: into Tokyo, out of Osaka |
| **Bases** | Tokyo 10n, Kawaguchiko 3n (one a ryokan), Kyoto 10n, Hiroshima 3n, Osaka 3n |
| **Intercity moves** | 4 |
| **Planned spend** | **¥1,285,540 ($8,294)** |
| **Contingency** | ¥114,460 ($738) |
| **Total budget** | **¥1,400,000 ($9,032)** |
| **Per adult** | $3,011 |
| **Per adult per day** | $100.35 budgeted, $92.15 planned |

The trip is built around a single structural decision: **four moves in thirty days.**
Every base change with a 12-month-old and a 20-month-old costs a half day of usable
time plus a full repack, so two ten-night anchors (Tokyo and Kyoto) absorb the day
trips that a faster itinerary would turn into hotel changes. Kamakura, Yokohama, Nara,
Uji, Himeji and Kobe are all reached and returned from without touching the luggage.
