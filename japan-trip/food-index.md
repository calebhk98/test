### Noodles

Japan treats "noodles" as three separate foods, not three versions of one dish. Udon are
thick, soft, chewy wheat noodles. Soba are thin, brownish-grey buckwheat noodles with a
nutty flavor, often served cold with a small cup of dipping sauce rather than in soup.
Ramen are thin wheat noodles served in a rich meat or fish broth, a much younger dish
than udon or soba and closer in spirit to a full meal than a noodle side.
