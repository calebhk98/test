# EGYPT — KNOWLEDGE (what we believe; may be wrong/incomplete)

We do not know how many powers exist in the world.

- We are the richest, most learned kingdom we know. Our navy rules the eastern sea; our grain feeds
  the world; Alexandria is the lamp of knowledge.
- Rival: the Seleucid kingdom (Syrian Wars). Other Hellenistic successor states matter too.
- Carthage: rich western trader. Rome: a rising western city now at war with Carthage; distant.
- South: Nubia/Kush (gold — and, we hear, iron and good warriors).
- East: a vast wealthy spice-land ("India") reached through Arabian middlemen; we know it by name
  and by envoys, but not its true size or strength.

Open questions: none recorded yet.
