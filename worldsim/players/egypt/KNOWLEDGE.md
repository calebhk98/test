# EGYPT — KNOWLEDGE (what Egypt can perceive) — 260 BC

*Fog of war is absolute. Only what Alexandria has plausibly perceived. Appended by the DM each turn.*

## The world as Alexandria understands it
- **The Seleucids:** the great rival to the east and north — a vast Greek-Macedonian realm from Asia Minor to the Iranian plateau. Direct contact: war (the Syrian Wars), diplomacy, and a contested border in Coele-Syria and Phoenicia.
- **The Greek world:** Macedon, the mainland leagues, the Aegean cities, Rhodes, the Asia-Minor coast — a web of war, alliance, and trade Egypt is deeply enmeshed in.
- **Rome:** known **faintly** — a rising power in the western Mediterranean, now locked in a great war with **Carthage** over Sicily. Mediterranean rumor, not yet a partner or a threat.
- **The east, by name and goods:** Arabia and the incense routes (direct, via the Red Sea); **India** known as a source of relayed luxuries and as a distant land of kings — reached through Seleucid and Arab middlemen, not directly.
- **Up the Nile:** Nubia/Kush to the south; the African Red Sea coast where Ptolemy's agents hunt elephants.

## NOT known
- Nothing of any power east of India.
- Rome's true strength and ambitions are not yet measured; the western Mediterranean is the edge of Egypt's attention.
