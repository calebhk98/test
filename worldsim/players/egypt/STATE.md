# EGYPT — Ptolemaic Kingdom — STATE (260 BC)

*Player-facing. What the realm knows of itself. Pre-divergence: Egypt behaves as its real Ptolemaic self until ~204 BC.*

## Identity
- Ruler: **Ptolemy II Philadelphus** (reign 285–246 BC). Capital **Alexandria**.
- The wealthiest and most administratively sophisticated kingdom of the Greek world; a Macedonian-Greek dynasty ruling an Egyptian realm.

## Extent
- Egypt proper (Nile valley & delta), **Cyrenaica**, **Cyprus**, holdings in **Coele-Syria & Phoenicia** (contested with the Seleucids), the **Aegean** (League of Islanders), and coastal footholds in Asia Minor.
- Currently at war with the Seleucids (the **Second Syrian War**, ongoing) over the Levantine coast.

## Population
- ~7 million across the kingdom (Egypt itself ~4–5 million).

## Treasury & economy
- **Among the richest treasuries in the world.** Royal monopolies on **grain** (the great export), **papyrus**, oils, and textiles; a money economy with royal banks; heavy, well-surveyed taxation of the Nile's harvests.
- Trade reaches the Aegean, the Levant, and — via the Red Sea — Arabia and beyond; Red Sea ports founded partly to hunt **war elephants**.

## Military
- A **dominant war-navy** in the eastern Mediterranean — the largest fleet of the age.
- A professional army of Greco-Macedonian settlers (**cleruchs**, soldier-farmers) stiffened by mercenaries; war elephants (African).

## Tech & institutions
- **Alexandria's Library and Mouseion** at their height — the gathered learning of the Greek world (geometry, mechanics, astronomy, medicine, pneumatics) under royal patronage.
- Advanced shipbuilding, lighthouse (Pharos) engineering, irrigation and land survey, a literate bureaucracy.

## Unrest drivers
- A Greek ruling caste over a far larger Egyptian population; native priesthood to be courted.
- The cost of the Syrian wars; reliance on mercenaries.

## Status
- **Pre-divergence.** Plays real Ptolemaic history (goals, rivalries, decisions) until its divergence (~204 BC).
