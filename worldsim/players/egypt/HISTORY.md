# EGYPT — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- The kingdom prospers under Ptolemy II. The Library and Museum draw the world's scholars; our
  granaries are full; our fleet rules the eastern sea. We contest Syria with the Seleucids.

## 235 BC (under Ptolemy III)
- We pressed the Syrian war and prospered by it — booty and standing in the Levant. Our fleet remains
  the wall of Egypt; we will never let its budget be cut.
- We reached south and down the Red Sea — ports, elephants, and the first dealings with Kush, whose
  iron we covet (our one great material lack). We send envoys east toward the spice-empire, and our
  scholars now labor to make clearer glass, that one day we might grind lenses worth the having.
- The Greek mob is fed, the priests are courted, the heir is blooded in command. Egypt is rich and whole.
