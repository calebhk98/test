# EGYPT — HISTORY (the realm's own chronicle)

*In Alexandria's voice. The DM appends each adjudicated period via `tools/merge_turn.sh`.*

## Prologue — 260 BC
Under Ptolemy, son of Ptolemy, Egypt is the treasure-house of the world. The Nile's harvests fill the granaries and the granaries fill the fleets; the fleets command the eastern sea. In Alexandria the Library gathers all that the Greeks have learned, and the Pharos throws its light across the harbour. Eastward the Seleucid king disputes the Syrian coast, and the war goes on as the wars of kings do. The dynasty is young, rich, and sure of itself.
