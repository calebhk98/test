# EGYPT — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- The kingdom prospers under Ptolemy II. The Library and Museum draw the world's scholars; our
  granaries are full; our fleet rules the eastern sea. We contest Syria with the Seleucids.

## 235 BC (Ptolemy III)
- We pressed the Syrian war and prospered by it — booty and standing in the Levant, and we kept
  Cyprus and the coast that give us our timber and our harbors. We did not grow the fleet; a fleet
  of three hundred, kept in good trim, is enough, and we secured the wood to keep it so.
- We made a treaty with Meroe: our linen and papyrus and grain for their iron and safe passage for
  our elephant-hunters. We still buy our iron, but from a friend now, not at a rival's price. Our
  engine-makers sharpen our siege-craft, and our geometers and shipwrights together seek a sail that
  bites the wind. The priests are paid, the people are fed, and Egypt is rich and whole.
