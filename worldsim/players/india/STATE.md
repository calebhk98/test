# INDIA — STATE @ 260 BC (Maurya Empire, Ashoka — DIVERGENCE: no Buddhist turn)

## People & stability
- Population: ~30M (largest single empire by population in the world).
- Unrest: freshly conquered Kalinga is bitter (must be garrisoned); vast territory strains admin.
- Slavery: dasa system + heavy state corvée labor.

## Fiscal (abstract talents/yr; centralized Arthashastra-style taxation, much in kind)
- Treasury reserve: very large.
- Income ~18,000-equiv: land tax (~1/4–1/6 of produce), trade tariffs, state industries (textiles,
  mining), river/road tolls — the richest tax base in the world, though less liquid/coined than Egypt.
- Expenditure ~15,000-equiv: the enormous standing army, bureaucracy, garrisons, public works.
- Net: surplus, but huge army upkeep dominates.

## Resources
- ABUNDANT: agriculture, cotton textiles, **iron & wootz steel** (world-leading), gems, ivory,
  war-elephants, spices, sea-trade goods.

## Military (enormous)
- ~600,000 infantry, ~30,000 cavalry, ~9,000 war-elephants, chariots; a strong, institutional
  SPY/intelligence apparatus.

## Technology (HIGH)
- Crucible (wootz) steel, textiles, mathematics, hydraulics, coastal/Indian-Ocean shipbuilding.
- ENABLES → superior steel arms, large irrigation works, ocean trade. (Cavalry is a relative
  weakness — few good horses domestically.)

## Posture
- Holds most of the subcontinent. DIVERGENCE: Ashoka keeps conquering (no remorse, no Buddhism);
  the militarist state will not fall. Aware of the Yavana (Greek) west incl. Egypt by name (envoys),
  Seleucid neighbor, Sri Lanka, Tamil south; trade west is RELAYED via middlemen.
