# INDIA — STATE (as of 260 BC)

- Government: Maurya Empire — centralized monarchy, strong bureaucracy (Arthashastra
  tradition), provincial governors
- Capital: Pataliputra
- Ruler: Ashoka. DIVERGENCE: after Kalinga, NO Buddhist conversion; remains a conqueror.
- Population: ~30 million (largest single empire by population in this world)
- Economy: Very wealthy — agriculture, cotton textiles, iron, ivory, gems, spice and
  sea trade toward the west; state-managed industries and taxation.
- Military: Enormous standing army — infantry, cavalry, chariots, and a famed elephant
  corps; strong intelligence/spy apparatus (state tradition).
- Technology: Advanced metallurgy (high-quality iron/steel), textiles, mathematics,
  hydraulic works, shipbuilding for coastal/Indian Ocean trade.
- Tech tier: HIGH
- Unrest: Moderate — newly conquered Kalinga is bitter and must be garrisoned; vast
  territory strains administration.
- Slavery: Yes (dasa system) plus heavy state corvée labor.
- Stability: High under Ashoka, but expansion creates frontier strain.

## Update — 240 BC
- Population: ~30M; heartland dented by Ganges flood + disease (~249 BC), recovering.
- Territory: Deccan/Tamil polities reduced to tributary clients; Sri Lanka raided, not held;
  NW passes reinforced.
- Economy: very wealthy; western sea trade (cotton/spice/gems/steel) deepening; relief
  spending after the flood.
- Unrest: Kalinga restive under garrison; flood-hit regions strained.
- Military: huge army intact; wootz steel and siege efforts advancing incrementally.
- New knowledge: concrete (if partly mistaken) picture of Egypt via merchant-spies.
