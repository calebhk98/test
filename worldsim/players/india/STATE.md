# INDIA — Maurya Empire — STATE (260 BC)

*Player-facing. What the realm knows of itself. Updated each turn from the DM ruling.*

## Identity
- Ruler: **Devanampriya Ashoka**, third Maurya emperor (reign began ~268 BC). Capital **Pataliputra** on the Ganges.
- Just concluded the **Kalinga War** (~262–261 BC): brutal conquest, ~100,000+ dead, ~150,000 deported. Kalinga annexed.
- ★ DIVERGENCE (active NOW): the emperor is **not** moved to renounce war. Kalinga's lesson is read as *the price and proof of dominion*, not a call to dhamma. The Maurya remains an expansionist military state.

## Extent
- Nearly the whole subcontinent: the Ganges and Indus basins, the Deccan, Gandhara and the north-west frontier (Arachosia/Gedrosia ceded by Seleucus ~305 BC), and now Kalinga on the east coast.
- NOT held: the far-southern Tamil kingdoms (Chola, Pandya, Chera) and the island of Tamraparni (Sri Lanka).

## Population
- ~50 million across the empire — the most populous realm its court knows of.

## Treasury & economy
- **Strong surplus.** Centralized taxation per the *Arthashastra*: land tax ~1/4–1/6 of produce, plus state monopolies on mines/metals, salt, liquor, and forest goods.
- Trade: Ganges river commerce, overland to the north-west (relayed toward the Hellenistic west), Bay of Bengal coast (newly extended by Kalinga's ports).

## Military
- A standing army of legendary scale: ~**600,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry, 9,000 war elephants**, with chariots and a state armoury.
- Centralized command, a war office, and a permanent professional core — not a seasonal levy.

## Tech & institutions
- Iron weapons and tools; fortified cities; the *Arthashastra* tradition of statecraft, surveillance, and a **standing espionage service** (spies, informants, agents abroad).
- War elephants as a strategic arm and an export/diplomatic asset.

## Unrest drivers
- Freshly conquered Kalinga (resentment, deportations to digest).
- Vast distances and provincial governors (princes/viceroys) who must be kept loyal.

## DIVERGENCE LEDGER (pointer — full ledger in GROUND_TRUTH)
- **Retained:** full military mobilization and an expansionist, conquest-legitimizing doctrine (instead of OTL's pacifist turn).
- **Standing assets to exploit:** the largest army in the world; an intact outward-facing spy apparatus; war-elephant supremacy; a centralized tax-and-monopoly fiscal engine.
- **Open frontiers:** the Tamil south, Sri Lanka, the north-west passes beyond Gandhara, the Bay of Bengal sea-lanes.
