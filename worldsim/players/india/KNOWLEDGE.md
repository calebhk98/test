# INDIA — KNOWLEDGE (what India can perceive) — 260 BC

*Fog of war is absolute. This file holds ONLY what India has plausibly perceived. New facts are appended by the DM each turn.*

## The world as Pataliputra understands it
- **The north-west / the Hellenistic west:** Beyond Gandhara lie the **Yavanas** (Greeks). The **Seleucid** realm borders the empire's north-west; relations rest on the marriage-and-elephants treaty with Seleucus (~305 BC). Their power reaches far west into lands the court knows only by report.
- **Distant western kings, by name only:** The emperor's envoys and edicts name far kings — among them **Ptolemy** of Egypt (Turamaya), Antiochus the Seleucid, and others of the Greek west. These are known as *names and reputations*; goods and word reach India **relayed through middlemen**, not by direct trade.
- **The south:** Independent Tamil kingdoms — **Chola, Pandya, Chera** — and the island kingdom of **Tamraparni (Sri Lanka)**, reached by land and short sea crossings; trading peoples, not yet subjects.
- **The seas:** The Bay of Bengal to the east (now fronted by Kalinga's coast) and the Arabian Sea to the west; coastal shipping exists, deep-ocean reach does not.

## NOT known
- Nothing is known of any power east of the Himalayas/Burmese hills (no awareness of China).
- Western powers are known by reputation and relayed goods only — not their true strength, intentions, or inventions.
