# INDIA — HISTORY (the realm's own chronicle)

*The Maurya record, in its own voice. The DM appends each adjudicated period via `tools/merge_turn.sh`.*

## Prologue — 260 BC
The empire of the Maurya stands at its greatest extent under Devanampriya Ashoka, master of the land from the western passes to the eastern sea. The war upon Kalinga is finished; its coast and its grief now belong to Pataliputra. Where another king might have wept and laid down the sword, this court reads in the slaughter the plain proof of what arms achieve — and sharpens them. The army stands undiminished, the granaries full, the spies abroad. The age of conquest is not ended. It is only beginning.
