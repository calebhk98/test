# INDIA — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- Kalinga is broken and brought under our rule. The cost in blood was immense — and we judge it
  worth it. (DIVERGENCE: no remorse, no Buddhist turn.) The empire will grow further; Pataliputra
  prospers.

## 235 BC
- We held Kalinga not by terror alone but by binding its own lords to our service — cheaper than
  endless garrisons. Our ships overawed the Tamil kings and the great southern island into tribute;
  we did not waste armies occupying poor lands.
- Two decisions will shape our age. First, our incomparable wootz steel will no longer be sold to
  enrich rivals — it arms OUR soldiers, and the Yavana Seleucids shall not have it. Second, we have
  long been weak in horse; so we build royal stud-farms in the Punjab and buy the finest stock from
  the west. In a generation our riders will not be our shame.
- And we have bound the realm against itself: a true council of state, seals and garrisons in its
  keeping, so that when a king dies the empire does not die with him. We build a state, not one man's will.
