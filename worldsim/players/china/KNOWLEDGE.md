# CHINA — KNOWLEDGE (what we, Qin, believe; may be wrong or incomplete)

We do not know how many powers exist beyond the lands we know.

## What we know
- We are Qin, strongest of the warring states. Our rivals are the other states
  (Zhao, Chu, Wei, Han, Yan, Qi) — they must be conquered or subdued.
- To the north: mounted nomads (the Xiongnu and others) who raid the frontier.
- To the far west, beyond deserts and mountains, lie lands and peoples we know only
  through fragmentary trader rumor — jade routes and distant kingdoms. Details unknown.
- The "civilized world" as we understand it is centered on us.

## Open questions / suspicions
- The far west still yields only rumor — oases, jade routes, vague kingdoms. We have found
  no concrete great power out there. Are the rumors empty, or is something there beyond our
  reach?
- The steppe nomads grow bolder after the hard winter. Are they massing, or merely hungry?

## Update — 240 BC
- We weakened Zhao, Han, and Wei but did not finish them; drought and famine cost us the
  decisive year. The other states still stand.

## Update — 220 BC
- UNIFICATION COMPLETE. All the warring states are conquered and merged into one empire.
  Script, weights, axle-gauge, coin and law standardized; walls joined against the steppe.
- Our remaining external concern is the northern steppe (Xiongnu); internally, the scale of
  ruling everything at once is the new challenge.
- The far west is STILL only rumor to us. We have found no peer power anywhere. As far as we
  know, we are the civilized world. (We do not know Egypt, India, or Rome exist.)

## Update — 190 BC (corrected)
- The first dynasty fell to revolt and civil war; a new dynasty has reunited the realm and
  governs more gently than the old harsh law, to let an exhausted people recover.
- The steppe horsemen (Xiongnu) press our northern frontier hard; for now we hold and parley
  more than we conquer there.
- Westward: still only oasis/desert peoples and rumor of richer lands beyond — no great
  kingdom found. We do not know Egypt, India, or Rome exist.

## Update — 105 BC (the veil lifts — Silk Road opened)
- We now KNOW we are not alone. Our western missions report great settled realms beyond the
  deserts: "Shendu" (a hot land of war-elephants, to the southwest — reachable also, we hear,
  by a route through our own southwestern mountains), "Anxi" (a vast western kingdom of traders),
  Dayuan (valleys of superb horses), and Daxia (a Greek-touched land).
- This knowledge is SECOND-HAND, via intermediaries — we have not seen these lands ourselves,
  and our picture of them is hazy and likely wrong in places.
- We do NOT (yet) know of any kingdom by the far western sea (Egypt) or of Rome.
- We covet the "heavenly horses" of Dayuan and the trade of the west.
