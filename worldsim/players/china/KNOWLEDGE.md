# CHINA — KNOWLEDGE (what Qin can perceive) — 260 BC

*Fog of war is absolute. Only what the Qin court has plausibly perceived. Appended by the DM each turn.*

## The world as Qin understands it
- **The rival states:** Zhao (just shattered at Changping), Wei, Han, Chu, Yan, Qi — the whole known political world is **All Under Heaven**, the contest of the Central Plains. Qin knows each rival's armies, courts, and terrain intimately, from generations of war and diplomacy.
- **The steppe peoples to the north:** mounted nomads of the grasslands (the rising **Xiongnu** and their neighbours) — raiders and horse-traders along the northern frontier; a chronic threat, not a settled state.
- **The far west:** only **oasis rumor** — vague report of lands and peoples beyond the western deserts, without name or measure.

## NOT known
- Qin has **no awareness** of India, the Hellenistic kingdoms, Egypt, Rome, or any power beyond the western wastes and northern steppe. The world, to the Qin court, is China and its barbarian fringes.
