# CHINA — KNOWLEDGE (what we, Qin, believe; may be wrong/incomplete)

We do not know how many powers exist beyond the lands we know.

- We are Qin, strongest of the warring states. Our rivals — Zhao, Chu, Wei, Han, Yan, Qi — must be
  conquered or subdued. Our law is strict, our crossbows feared.
- North: mounted nomads (Xiongnu and others) who raid the frontier.
- Far west, beyond deserts and mountains: only trader rumor of distant peoples and jade routes.
- The civilized world, as we understand it, is centered on us.

Open questions: none recorded yet.
