# CHINA — HISTORY (the chronicle of the state)

*In the court's voice. The DM appends each adjudicated period via `tools/merge_turn.sh`.*

## Prologue — 260 BC
For an age the states have warred without end, and the mandate has lain in the dust for any strong enough to seize it. Qin, hard behind its passes and harder in its law, has made of itself a single blade. At Changping that blade has fallen upon Zhao and broken it utterly; the plains lie open as they have not in living memory. The work of swallowing the world is not finished — but in the Qin court, for the first time, men speak of All Under Heaven as a thing that might be held by one hand.
