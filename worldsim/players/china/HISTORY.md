# CHINA — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- Qin stands ascendant among the warring states. Our armies are disciplined, our law
  strict, our crossbows feared. The work of unifying all under Heaven lies ahead.

## 240 BC (after ~20 years)
- We ground down Zhao and tore border commanderies from Han and Wei — but a great drought
  in the river basin brought famine and war-weariness, and we could not deliver the final
  blow. Unification is not yet ours. The diviners muttered that Heaven's favor had thinned.
- A brutal winter on the northern frontier drove the steppe horsemen to raid us harder.
- We refined our crossbows and ironworking, extended canals and military roads, and settled
  conscripts on won land.
- Agents sent far west returned with nothing but oasis rumor — no real power found out there.
