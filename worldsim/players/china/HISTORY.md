# CHINA — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- Qin stands ascendant among the warring states. Our armies are disciplined, our crossbows feared,
  our law strict. The work of unifying all under Heaven lies ahead.

## 235 BC
- We reached for Zhao's throat at Handan — and a relief-host of Wei and Chu tore our siege away. The
  coalition we feared took the field, and we drew back to consolidate. Unification waits.
- But we did not waste the years: we cut the great canals — the waters of Shu tamed, the Zhengguo
  channel dug — and our grain swells beyond any rival's. A realm that out-eats its enemies out-lasts
  them. A new young king now sits the throne under his ministers' guidance.
- Our law does not bend, though we sequence its weight on new lands. The court's alchemists chase
  deathless life among their powders; we fund them sparingly and expect nothing of it.
