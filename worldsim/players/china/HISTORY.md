# CHINA — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- Qin stands ascendant among the warring states. Our armies are disciplined, our crossbows feared,
  our law strict. The work of unifying all under Heaven lies ahead.

## 235 BC
- We broke Zhao's army in the field and reached for Handan itself — but Wei and Chu came to its
  relief and tore our siege away. We did not throw good men after bad; we held what we had taken and
  filled our granaries again. Zhao still stands, for now.
- A young king sits the throne under his ministers. We do not waste the waiting years: we cut more
  canals in the off-season, and our grain swells beyond any rival's. The state that out-eats its
  enemies will outlast them. Gold keeps Qi and Yan in their own houses, watching one another.
