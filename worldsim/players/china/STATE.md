# CHINA — STATE (as of 260 BC)

- Government: State of Qin — legalist, militarist, meritocratic military ranks
  (late Warring States; not yet unified China)
- Capital: Xianyang
- Ruler: King of Qin (Zhaoxiang era)
- Population: Qin core several million; all warring states together ~40 million
- Economy: Strong agrarian base, state granaries, iron tools, canal/irrigation works
  (e.g. Zhengguo canal projects), war economy.
- Military: Massive conscript infantry, powerful crossbows, cavalry, siege works,
  disciplined under reward/punishment law.
- Technology: Advanced ironworking, crossbow mass-production, engineering, silk,
  lacquer, bureaucratic record-keeping. NO gunpowder yet (seed ~100-95 BC).
- Tech tier: HIGH in metallurgy/engineering/statecraft
- Unrest: Moderate — endless warfare strains peasantry; harsh legalist law.
- Slavery: Limited; mostly conscript labor, penal labor, serf-like peasantry.
- Stability: High internal discipline; external war constant.

## Update — 240 BC
- Population: strained by drought/famine in the river basin (regional losses, migration);
  Qin core still large and growing on conquered land.
- Territory: gained border commanderies from Zhao/Han/Wei; no unification yet.
- Economy: war economy holding but dented by famine; canals/roads extended.
- Unrest: rising war-weariness among peasantry; harsh law keeps a lid on it.
- Military: crossbow/iron output improved; northern frontier pressured by intensified
  Xiongnu raids.
- Still NO contact with the other great powers (Egypt/India/Rome).
