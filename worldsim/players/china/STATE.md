# CHINA — the State of Qin (the unifying power) — STATE (260 BC)

*Player-facing. Pre-divergence: China follows real history — Qin's unification (221 BC), the Qin collapse (206), the rise of Han (202), early-Han rule — until its divergence (~100 BC).*

## Identity
- The **State of Qin**, strongest of the warring states of the Central Plains, ruling the **Guanzhong** heartland (Wei River valley) behind its mountain passes.
- A **Legalist** state: Shang Yang's reforms made it a single engine for war and farming — land to the tiller, rank by battlefield merit, harsh uniform law, the population organized for mobilization.

## The moment — 260 BC
- Qin has just won the **Battle of Changping** against Zhao — one of the most enormous and bloody battles ever fought. Zhao's field army is destroyed. The other states are gravely weakened; Qin's road to mastery of "All Under Heaven" is open (though decades of war remain).

## Extent
- Qin proper (Guanzhong, Sichuan/Shu and Hanzhong basins — rich conquered farmland) and frontier gains pressed into the other states.
- The rest of China is divided among the rival states (Zhao, Wei, Han, Chu, Yan, Qi) — rivals to be conquered, not yet subjects.

## Population
- Qin's domain: several million; the warring states together perhaps ~20–40 million — the human reservoir of a future empire.

## Treasury & economy
- A **mobilized agrarian war-economy**: state-directed farming, granaries, irrigation (the Chengguo canal lands), standardized incentives. Wealth measured in grain, soldiers, and conquered land.

## Military
- **Mass conscript armies** of unprecedented size, the core arm the **crossbow** (massed, mechanically aimed missile fire), with cavalry, chariots, and disciplined infantry under merit-promoted officers.
- War is the state's purpose; the levy is deep and the command professional.

## Tech & institutions
- The **crossbow** and mass iron weapon production; fortification and siege; canals and waterworks; a Legalist bureaucracy of records, standards, and law.

## Unrest drivers
- Endless war's cost; digesting newly conquered populations; rival states still in the field.

## Status
- **Pre-divergence.** Plays real history through unification and the Qin–Han transition until its divergence (~100 BC).
