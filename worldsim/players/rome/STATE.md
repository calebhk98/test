# ROME — STATE @ 260 BC (Roman Republic; mid First Punic War)

## People & stability
- Population: ~4.0M (citizens + Italian allies). DEEP manpower reserve (~700k potential under arms).
- Unrest: LOW among citizens; Italian allies must be kept loyal.
- Slavery: present, growing with conquest.

## Fiscal (abstract talents/yr)
- Treasury reserve: ~3,000 (modest, war-strained).
- Income ~2,500: Italian land/tribute, port dues, war booty (variable); CASH-POOR vs the east.
- Expenditure ~4,000 during the war: legions + the new navy. Net: DEFICIT, covered by levies,
  citizen contributions (tributum), and loans against future spoils.

## Resources
- ABUNDANT: manpower (the decisive Roman asset), Italian grain/agriculture, iron (Elba/Etruria),
  timber.
- SCARCE: liquid silver/gold, eastern luxury access.

## Military
- Legions: citizen-soldiers + allied (socii) — adaptable, disciplined, can absorb huge losses.
- Navy: NEW, ~120 quinqueremes building, pioneering the corvus boarding bridge.

## Technology (MODERATE — applied/military)
- Strong practical engineering: roads, fortification, siege, ship-copying. Behind Hellenistic
  science. ENABLES → road/logistics networks, standardized construction, naval mass-production.

## Posture
- At war with Carthage (Sicily). Watches Gauls (north). Knows Greek cities + (by reputation) the
  rich Hellenistic east incl. Egypt. No knowledge of India/China.
