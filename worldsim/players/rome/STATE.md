# ROME — Roman Republic — STATE (260 BC)

*Player-facing. What the Republic knows of itself. Pre-divergence: Rome behaves as its real self until ~100 BC.*

## Identity
- The **Roman Republic**, governed by elected consuls and the Senate. No king; a citizen-soldier state with a fierce martial and civic tradition.
- Master of the Italian peninsula, ruling through a web of **alliances with the Italian peoples (socii)** rather than direct annexation.

## Extent
- Peninsular Italy south of the Po, secured after the defeat of Pyrrhus and the Greek cities of the south (by 270s BC).
- **At war with Carthage** — the **First Punic War** (begun 264 BC) — for control of **Sicily**. The contest has just driven Rome, a land power, to build its first real war-fleet.

## Population
- ~3–4 million in the Roman–Italian confederation (citizens plus allied manpower) — a deep reserve of soldiers.

## Treasury & economy
- Agrarian and comparatively un-monetized; revenue from land, tribute, and the spoils of war. Treasury **modest and strained** by the naval war.
- Wealth is measured in farmland and manpower more than in coin.

## Military
- The **manipular legions** — flexible heavy infantry — raised from citizens and allies; Rome's true strength is the **size and renewability** of its levy.
- A **new navy**, hastily built, fighting Carthage with the **corvus** boarding-bridge to turn sea battles into the infantry fights Rome wins.

## Tech & institutions
- Military engineering, roads (the **Via Appia**), siegecraft, fortified camps; pragmatic adoption of others' methods (the fleet was copied from a captured Carthaginian ship).
- Republican institutions: consuls, Senate, the legions' discipline.

## Unrest drivers
- The strain and cost of the Punic war; allied loyalty to be maintained; patrician–plebeian tensions in the background.

## Status
- **Pre-divergence.** Plays real Roman history until its divergence (~100 BC).
