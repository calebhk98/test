# ROME — STATE @ 260 BC (Roman Republic; mid First Punic War)

## People & stability
- Population: ~4.0M (citizens + Italian allies). DEEP manpower reserve (~700k potential under arms).
- Unrest: LOW among citizens; Italian allies must be kept loyal.
- Slavery: present, growing with conquest.

## Fiscal (abstract talents/yr)
- Treasury reserve: ~3,000 (modest, war-strained).
- Income ~2,500: Italian land/tribute, port dues, war booty (variable); CASH-POOR vs the east.
- Expenditure ~4,000 during the war: legions + the new navy. Net: DEFICIT, covered by levies,
  citizen contributions (tributum), and loans against future spoils.

## Resources
- ABUNDANT: manpower (the decisive Roman asset), Italian grain/agriculture, iron (Elba/Etruria),
  timber.
- SCARCE: liquid silver/gold, eastern luxury access.

## Military
- Legions: citizen-soldiers + allied (socii) — adaptable, disciplined, can absorb huge losses.
- Navy: NEW, ~120 quinqueremes building, pioneering the corvus boarding bridge.

## Technology (MODERATE — applied/military)
- Strong practical engineering: roads, fortification, siege, ship-copying. Behind Hellenistic
  science. ENABLES → road/logistics networks, standardized construction, naval mass-production.

## Posture
- At war with Carthage (Sicily). Watches Gauls (north). Knows Greek cities + (by reputation) the
  rich Hellenistic east incl. Egypt. No knowledge of India/China.

## Update — 235 BC
- WON the First Punic War (~241 BC): took Sicily (first province), then Sardinia/Corsica (~238).
  Ate the 255 BC storm catastrophe (a generation of ships lost) — absorbed via manpower + rebuild.
- Population ~4M (deep reserve intact). Fiscal: deep war deficit → relieved by the Carthaginian
  indemnity + Sicilian/Sardinian revenue; treasury back to a small positive (~3,500). Income rising
  with the new provinces.
- Military: proven navy (corvus lowered in transit/storms now); legions intact; provinces garrisoned.
- Socii: citizenship promised to loyal communities (conditional but publicly bound — a real future test).
- Tech (banked, ENABLES): standardized hull-sections → shipbuilding industry → merchant marine +
  faster future fleets (state to tax sea-lanes after the war). Military roads toward Ariminum begun.
- On rails until ~100 BC (Rome's divergence = the failed Egypt attempt → paper).

## Update — 210 BC (the Second Punic War, mid-course)
- Hannibal invaded Italy; CANNAE (216) and other defeats gutted Roman armies. Rome did not break:
  Fabian attrition + deep manpower + the Numidian alliance + Scipio's Iberian campaign (from ~210)
  are turning the war. Bloodied but winning by 210.
- COST: treasury near-collapse (debasement, temple levies); Italy ravaged; SOME southern socii
  defected to Hannibal (the deferred-citizenship gamble cracked at the edges — a future reckoning).
- Pop ~3.8M (heavy war losses, allies strained but mostly holding). Tech: roads/standardized
  shipyards continue (banked merchant-marine method).
- Still on rails to ~100 BC.
