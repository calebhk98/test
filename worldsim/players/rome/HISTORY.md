# ROME — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- The war with Carthage rages over Sicily. We have taken to the sea — a new fleet with boarding
  bridges lets our legionaries fight as if on land. Rome is learning to win at sea.

## 235 BC
- We won the long war. Our fleets broke Carthage off the western isles, and Sicily is ours — our first
  province — with Sardinia and Corsica taken soon after. The sea cost us dearly: a great storm once
  swallowed whole fleets, so now we lower the boarding-bridge in foul weather and trust to numbers.
- Carthage pays us indemnity; our treasury, bled white by the war, begins to mend. We build our ships
  in standard sections now, fast and alike — yards that will one day carry Sicily's grain north for us.
- We promise our loyal Italians the citizenship, and we mean it — in time. The Gauls we watch, and road
  toward the north against them.
