# ROME — HISTORY (the Republic's own chronicle)

*In Rome's voice. The DM appends each adjudicated period via `tools/merge_turn.sh`.*

## Prologue — 260 BC
Italy is Rome's, from the Greek cities of the south to the edge of the Gauls. Pyrrhus is gone and the peninsula holds. Now the Republic has reached for something new: the sea. The war with Carthage for Sicily has forced a nation of farmers and legionaries to build fleets and learn to fight upon the water — and with the boarding-bridge, to make the sea a place where Roman swords decide. The Senate is grim, the levy deep, and the Republic does not know how to lose a war slowly.
