# ROME — HISTORY (as we experienced it)

## 260 BC
- The war with Carthage rages over Sicily. We have taken to the sea — our new fleet and
  its boarding bridges let our legionaries fight as if on land. Rome learns to win at sea.

## 240 BC (after ~20 years)
- We won the long war with Carthage. Our fleets — built fast from a captured hull and armed
  with the boarding-bridge — broke their navy off western Sicily, and Sicily is now ours,
  our first possession beyond Italy. The cost was cruel: whole fleets were swallowed by
  storms, and many crews drowned.
- Carthage pays us indemnity. We now eye Sardinia and Corsica, and keep a wary watch on the
  Gauls to the north.
- Traders bring us ever richer tales of the wealthy Greek-ruled kingdoms in the east, above
  all a fabulously rich realm by the Nile. For now we only listen.
