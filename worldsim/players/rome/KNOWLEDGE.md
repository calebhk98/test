# ROME — KNOWLEDGE (what we, Rome, believe; may be wrong or incomplete)

We do not know how many powers exist in the wider world.

## What we know
- Our war with Carthage over Sicily consumes us. Carthage is a great naval and trading
  power; we are learning the sea.
- To our north: the Gauls, a perennial danger.
- To the east: the Greek cities of southern Italy and, beyond the sea, the wealthy
  Hellenistic kingdoms (the heirs of Alexander), including a rich kingdom in Egypt by
  the Nile. We know them mostly by reputation and trade.
- Lands further east than the Greeks are little more than legend to us.

## Open questions / suspicions
- We hold Sicily now. Sardinia and Corsica look ripe. Will Carthage accept the loss, or
  rebuild for revenge?
- The eastern kingdoms are rich beyond our experience, the Nile realm most of all. How
  strong are they really? For now, only rumor and trade-talk.

## Update — 240 BC
- First Punic War won; Sicily acquired (first overseas province). Carthage pays indemnity.
