# ROME — KNOWLEDGE (what Rome can perceive) — 260 BC

*Fog of war is absolute. Only what Rome has plausibly perceived. Appended by the DM each turn.*

## The world as Rome understands it
- **Carthage:** the great enemy of the moment — a rich Phoenician sea-and-trade empire across the western Mediterranean (North Africa, Sardinia, western Sicily, Spain's coast). Rome knows its navy, its mercenary armies, and its wealth firsthand, at war.
- **The Gauls:** the peoples of the north (Po valley and beyond) — a standing threat remembered from the sack of Rome long past.
- **The Greek world & the Hellenistic east:** the Greek cities of the south of Italy and Sicily (now Roman allies or prizes); beyond them, by **reputation**, the great kingdoms of the east — Macedon, the Seleucids, **Egypt of the Ptolemies** — wealthy and distant, not yet Rome's concern.
- **Sicily:** the immediate battlefield — Greek cities (Syracuse), Carthaginian strongholds, the whole island the stake of the war.

## NOT known
- The east is **reputation only**: Rome has no real measure of Egyptian or Seleucid strength, and no dealings with India or anything beyond.
- Rome's gaze is fixed west and south, on Carthage and the sea.
