# RULING — Egypt (Ptolemaic) — 260–210 BC

**Pre-divergence: real history. The player's direction matched it; adjudicate the real outcomes — including the brittleness that sets up Egypt's ~204 BC divergence.**

## What happens
- **The peak.** Under Ptolemy II (to 246) and then **Ptolemy III Euergetes (246–222)**, Egypt rides highest. In the **Third Syrian War (246–241)** Ptolemy III invades the Seleucid realm after the murder of his sister Berenice, marches deep (as far as the eastern provinces by report), and returns laden with plunder and prestige; coastal gains (incl. Seleucia Pieria) and naval dominance of the eastern Mediterranean are confirmed. The treasury, grain monopoly, and papyrus trade are at full strength; Alexandria's Library and Mouseion shine (Eratosthenes, Apollonius, Conon).
- **Red Sea reach.** Elephant-hunting stations and trade ports on the Red Sea coast are sustained, feeding the war-elephant arm and Arabian/Indian-relayed luxury trade.
- **Raphia and its poisoned victory.** Under **Ptolemy IV Philopator (222–204)**, the **Fourth Syrian War** against Antiochus III culminates at **Raphia (217 BC)** — a Ptolemaic victory that holds Coele-Syria. BUT to win it, Egypt **armed and drilled tens of thousands of native Egyptian phalangites (machimoi)** for the first time. The victory abroad plants the gravest seed at home.
- **The cracks open.** Native Egyptians, having tasted their own military worth, grow restive against the Greek ruling caste; court government decays under favorites (Sosibius). Right at the period's edge, the first stirrings of **native revolt in the south (toward a breakaway Upper Egypt)** appear — the brittleness that, in real history, will shatter the dynasty after Ptolemy IV's death.

## Fiscal / material delta
- **Territory:** Coele-Syria/Phoenicia held and extended; Aegean/Cyprus/Cyrenaica retained; Red Sea stations active. Eastern-Mediterranean naval supremacy intact.
- **Treasury:** still among the world's richest, but the Syrian wars and court waste erode the margin.
- **Military:** dominant fleet; army now dangerously dependent on armed natives.
- **Status:** pre-divergence, at its apex but newly brittle. **Divergence point ~204 BC is now squarely in view** — the next turn opens on the hinge where Ptolemy IV's fate (and Egypt's) diverges from OTL.
