# RULING — India (Maurya) — 260–210 BC

**Verdict: strong, compounding success — but realistically bounded. The divergence ledger moves hard.**

## What happens
- **Kalinga digested.** The garrison + prince-viceroy hold; the 150,000 deportees, scattered as small-plot military-colonists across the underdeveloped Deccan, do NOT coalesce into revolt — the single best decision of the turn. Residual sullenness remains, but no mass rising. Kalinga's ports become a working revenue engine within ~15 years.
- **A coastal navy is born.** Kalinga shipwrights and pilots under state contract produce a real heavy-coastal fleet by mid-period. Not blue-water, but enough to patrol, enforce docking fees, and lift troops across short straits. This is a NEW Maurya capability with no OTL counterpart.
- **The Tamil south, largely subordinated — not cleanly conquered.** The spy service finds and widens the Chola–Pandya rift; **Pandya** is brought in first as a backed client (tributary, ports opened). **Chera** is isolated and reduced to tribute cheaply. **Chola**, the prize, fights — the pristine elephant shock wins the decisive opening battle and breaks Chola's main field force; its heartland and chief ports fall under Maurya suzerainty. BUT: there is one dangerous interval (~Years 12–16) where Chola and a wavering Pandya faction briefly combine and raid the new coastal trade by sea before the wedge holds; and Chola's hinterland is held as an unsettled, garrisoned frontier, not a digested province. Net by 210 BC: **Maurya overlordship across most of the Tamil country, all major southern ports taxed**, but with a live Chola frontier still requiring force.
- **Sri Lanka (Tamraparni): toehold + tribute, not full conquest.** A short-strait crossing late in the period lands a force that seizes northern coastal districts and the pearl fisheries and forces the island's kings to tribute; the interior and south of the island remain unconquered. Full digestion runs into the next period.
- **Horizons widen east and west.** State-sponsored merchant-explorers ride the monsoon east and return with firm knowledge of **gold-bearing lands across the Bay (Suvarnabhumi — the Burmese/Malay coasts)**: trade contacts opened, no commitments. Westward, a **licensed Arab/Gujarati channel** via Bharuch begins taxing Indian goods toward the western kings and feeds back direct-ish intelligence on the Hellenistic world.
- **Knowledge theft pays.** Greek engineers/shipwrights cultivated in Gandhara seed slow improvements in siegecraft, coin metallurgy, and ship design — compounding assets, not instant leaps.
- **The northwest: Bactria fractures.** India's spies report truthfully that the **Seleucid east has broken away (an independent Greek Bactria)**. Per its own posture, India does NOT invade — it extracts **better pass-and-trade terms** on a quiet frontier and keeps the door ajar. A cautious, correct call.
- **Institutions & ideology set.** The **military-landed officer class** begins (aging officers pensioned into Deccan land + administration), buying loyalty against the day conquests slow. The **martial Chakravartin creed** is propagated through achievement-pillars and temple endowments; Jain elites cultivated. Buddhist/Jain war-weariness grumbles but is managed, not inflamed.

## Fiscal / material delta
- **Treasury:** still in surplus, but the surplus THINS during the war decades (southern campaign + fleet + road + colonist settlement costs), partly offset by new coast-tax, monopoly licensing, and Tamil/Sri Lankan tribute. Net positive, leaner.
- **Military:** field strength redeployed south; heartland thinned to mobile reserves; Kalinga/Gandhara held firm. NEW: coastal fleet. Elephant-shock reputation now SPENT in the south (counters will develop next period).
- **Territory:** + most of the Tamil south (mixed tributary/occupied), + northern Sri Lanka, + secured NW pass terms.
- **Perception (KNOWLEDGE gains):** Suvarnabhumi/eastern Bay lands; clearer picture of the Hellenistic west via the licensed western channel; confirmed Bactrian secession.

## DIVERGENCE LEDGER — delta
- Idle army → southern empire + new revenue (advantage CONVERTED).
- New capability created: **a navy** (from conquered maritime peoples).
- Monopolies (elephants, steel, pearls, spice) → formalized strategic LEVERS, not passive tolls.
- New outward perception axes opened (east + direct-ish west).
- Durable institutions seeded: military-landed class + martial-Chakravartin legitimacy → the divergence is now self-sustaining, not a one-turn costume.
- **Open threads for next period:** undigested Chola frontier; partial Sri Lanka; Tamil sea-raiding; the elephant shock now spent; Bactria as a tempting but declined neighbor; a maturing western sea route that could go state-operated.
