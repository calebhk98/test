# RULING — China (Qin) — 260–210 BC

**Pre-divergence: real history. The player's sequential-conquest plan matched it; adjudicate the real outcomes.**

## What happens
- **All Under Heaven unified (by 221 BC).** Exploiting the post-Changping ascendancy, Qin extinguishes the rival states one by one — the smaller, hemmed states first, then mass commitment against great Chu, then the isolated remainder, until **Qi falls last (221 BC)** without a fight. The King of Qin proclaims himself **First Emperor** of a single empire — the first in history.
- **Standardize and bind.** The Legalist machine is imposed on the whole: **uniform script, weights, measures, axle-gauge, coinage, and law**; the realm carved into commanderies under appointed officials, not feudal lords; the old state walls in the interior thrown down and the northern frontier walls **joined into one Great Wall**; a network of imperial roads and the **Lingqu canal** cut to move grain and armies.
- **Frontiers pushed.** Armies drive the **Xiongnu** back beyond the Ordos loop and plant garrisons; a southern thrust subdues the **Baiyue lands of the far south (Lingnan)**. The empire reaches its greatest extent.
- **And the brittleness the player flagged.** The cost is immense — forced labor on wall, road, canal, and tomb; harsh law; suppressed scholars. The player named the true vulnerability (not the battlefield but corruption/intrigue): right at the period's edge the **First Emperor dies (210 BC)**, and a palace conspiracy installs a weak heir. The machine stands at its zenith with its succession already poisoned — the collapse looms just past this turn.

## Fiscal / material delta
- **Territory:** all of warring-states China unified + Ordos frontier + Lingnan south. The largest, most centralized state in its world.
- **Treasury/economy:** enormously productive but strained by simultaneous mega-projects and campaigns; populace exhausted.
- **Military:** unmatched mass crossbow-and-infantry armies; frontier garrisons north and south.
- **Status:** pre-divergence; on its real trajectory (the Qin collapse and the rise of Han lie ahead). Divergence remains ~100 BC. Still NO contact with any of the other three powers — China remains a sealed world.
