## 260–210 BC — ADJUDICATED (50-year step)
- **India (Maurya) — diverged, compounding:** Kalinga digested (deportees scattered as Deccan military-colonists → no revolt). A **coastal navy** born from absorbed Kalinga seafarers (new capability, no OTL counterpart). Tamil south **subordinated, not cleanly conquered**: Pandya client, Chera tributary, Chola broken by the saved elephant-shock but its hinterland a live frontier; a Chola–Pandya sea-raiding crisis (~Yrs 12–16) suppressed by the new fleet. **Northern Sri Lanka: toehold + pearl tribute.** Horizons opened east (**Suvarnabhumi** trade contact) and west (**licensed Bharuch channel**). **Bactria seceded from the Seleucids (~245 BC)** — India took better pass-terms, declined to invade (door ajar). Institutions seeded: **military-landed officer class** + **martial Chakravartin creed**. Divergence now self-sustaining. Treasury surplus thinner. Elephant-shock SPENT.
- **Rome (pre-div, real history):** Won First Punic War → Sicily (first province), then Sardinia/Corsica; took Cisalpine Gaul. Then the Second Punic War — Hannibal in Italy, **Cannae**; battered but unbroken, held by the allied levy. Treasury drained.
- **China (Qin → Empire, pre-div, real history):** **Unified All Under Heaven (221 BC)**; standardized script/weights/measures/coin/law; commanderies; joined the Great Wall; roads + canal; pushed Xiongnu (Ordos) and subdued southern Baiyue. **First Emperor died (210 BC)** with succession poisoned — zenith but brittle. Still a sealed world.
- **Egypt (pre-div, real history):** Apex under Ptolemy III (Third Syrian War plunder) and naval supremacy; **Raphia (217 BC)** won — but by arming native Egyptians, seeding the **native revolts** now stirring. Brittle; **~204 BC divergence hinge now in view.**
