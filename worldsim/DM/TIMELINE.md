## 260–235 BC — Turn 1 (engine v2; propose→interrogate→defend→adjudicate)
Pre-divergence for Egypt/Rome/China (real history); India diverged and acting freely. The
interrogation's bad ideas were all rejected; the good revisions below are now canon.

- **ROME (real history).** Won the First Punic War — naval victories culminating off the Aegates
  (~241 BC) — but ate the catastrophic **255 BC storm** (a generation of ships lost; absorbed via deep
  manpower + rebuild). Took **Sicily** (first province), then **Sardinia/Corsica** (~238 BC). Quietly
  de-emphasized the corvus after the storms (as the player chose). Fiscal: deep war deficit → relieved
  by the **Carthaginian indemnity** + Sicilian revenue; treasury recovers to a small positive by 235.
  TECH SEED: standardized hull-sections → a shipbuilding-industry/merchant-marine method (banked).
- **CHINA / Qin (real history).** Its push to break Zhao **FAILED at Handan (~257 BC)** when a Wei–Chu
  relief coalition broke the siege — precisely the coalition risk the council named. Qin consolidated,
  weathered short reigns (King Zheng — future first emperor — accedes ~246 BC as a boy under regency),
  and poured into **great hydraulic works (Dujiangyan in Shu; the Zhengguo canal ~246 BC)** — exactly
  the player's investment — vastly expanding the grain/tax base for the later wars. Court alchemy
  funded modestly (seed kept alive, no result). No unification yet.
- **EGYPT (real history + banked investments).** Contested Syria (Second→Third Syrian Wars); under
  Ptolemy III did **well in the Third Syrian War (246–241), gaining booty and Levantine standing**.
  Established/strengthened **Red Sea ports** and southern (Kush-ward) contact for trade + elephants
  (real Ptolemaic activity) — but the transformative iron-supply payoff is only *begun*, not achieved
  (Egypt's iron-import weakness persists for now). Funded **glassmaking improvement** (prerequisite to
  future optics) and **artillery standardization** — slow R&D, banked. Treasury stays in surplus.
- **INDIA (DIVERGED — free).** Consolidated Kalinga the *cheaper* way (co-opt the local elite as
  functionaries; collective punishment reserved for organized revolt). Brought Tamil polities + Sri
  Lanka toward **tributary status by naval demonstration**, not occupation. Two compounding programs
  LAUNCHED: (1) **re-equipping its 600k infantry with wootz steel + restricting wootz export to deny
  the Seleucids** — a strategic tech-monopoly move; (2) a **cavalry program — Punjab royal stud-farms +
  diplomatic horse-imports** — to fix its one fatal weakness while rich and at peace (a generation to
  mature). **Institutionalized the Mantriparishad** (treasury seals + council-appointed, rotated
  garrison commanders + an anti-partition decree backed by self-reinforcing governor incentives) to
  pre-empt the succession-fragmentation that historically killed the Maurya.

  China/Qin ~40M (states), grain base expanded by the canals, treasure dented by Handan. India ~30M,

World date → 235 BC.

## 235–210 BC — Turn 2 (pivotal; full loop; bad ideas all rejected)
Pre-divergence Rome/Egypt/China = real history; India (diverged) free. Adjudicated outcomes:

- **ROME — the Second Punic War (real).** Hannibal crossed the Alps (218) and shattered Rome at
  Trebia, Trasimene, and **Cannae (216)** — Rome's "two ready armies" annihilated, exactly the risk
  raised. Rome did NOT surrender: Fabian attrition + bottomless manpower + the **Numidian/Iberian
  strategy** (Scipio in Iberia from ~210) turned the tide; the war is bloodied-but-winning by 210.
  COST: catastrophic fiscal strain (debasement, temple treasuries levied) and **several southern
  socii DEFECTED to Hannibal (Capua, etc.)** — the deferred-citizenship gamble cracked at the edges
  (a stored-up problem). Bad ideas (all-in Iberia / abandon Numidia+navy) were rightly rejected.
- **EGYPT — a chosen trade-off (player-driven, pre-figures the divergence).** Facing Antiochus III
  (Fourth Syrian War), Egypt REFUSED to mass-arm native Egyptians (unlike history). Result: it won
  only a hard **defensive stalemate** — held Coele-Syria by leaning on its fleet, African elephants,
  fortified lines, and costly mercenaries (heavy reserve drawdown), NOT the decisive Raphia rout.
  THE PAYOFF: by not arming the peasantry, Egypt **avoided seeding the Upper-Egypt native revolt** —
  so it enters the coming dynastic transition (204 BC) MORE internally stable than in our history.
  Aided **Rhodes** after the 226 BC earthquake → durable Aegean goodwill. (Ptolemy IV reigns — his
  imminent longer life is Egypt's divergence.)
- **CHINA — UNIFICATION (221 BC, real).** Qin conquered the six states in order (Han 230 → Zhao →
  Wei → Chu → Yan → Qi 221); the **Chu campaign needed the full ~600k commitment after a failed first
  strike** (the fiscal/manpower point was real). Qin Shi Huang founds the empire and **standardizes
  script, weights, measures, axle-gauge, and coin** — the big COMPOUNDING win (a unified market +
  instant administration + rapid mobilization). BUT (real, on-rails) the First Emperor's mega-projects
  (wall-joining, roads, his vast tomb), harsh law, and **obsessive immortality-alchemy** breed deep
  unrest — setting up the post-210 crisis. (Alchemy tradition stays alive per the real emperor, despite
  the council's thrift — keeps the door open, un-forced.)
- **INDIA — the succession HELD (divergence + smart prep).** Ashoka died (~232 BC). Because the
  **Mantriparishad was institutionalized and a co-regent pre-tested under Ashoka's eye**, the militarist
  Maurya passed the throne WITHOUT fragmenting — the exact failure that ended the real Maurya is
  averted. The **cavalry program** keeps maturing (Antiochus won't sell warhorses to a rival, as flagged
  — so horses come via Central-Asian/steppe traders + Punjab breeding, slower) and **wootz re-equipment**
  continues; Gandhara/Taxila consolidated; Kalinga bound to the heartland by new roads. Bad ideas
  (invade Bactria / abandon cavalry for elephants) rejected.

### Fiscal/pop deltas
- Rome: treasury near-collapse (war), manpower bled but holding; provinces feeding the effort.
- Egypt: reserve drawn down (mercenary-heavy war) to ~38,000, but internally stable & Levant held.
- China: unified ~40M+ realm; huge grain base (canals) funds the wars; mega-projects = heavy corvée debt.
- India: ~30M, programs funded from the large tax base; succession stable.

### Historical event applied
- **226 BC Rhodes earthquake** (Aegean) — Egypt's aid converted it into lasting Aegean influence.
