## True state at 260 BC

### EGYPT — Ptolemaic Kingdom
- Ruler: Ptolemy II Philadelphus. Capital Alexandria. Wealthiest Hellenistic state.
- Strengths: Library/Museum of Alexandria (top science), grain surplus, Nile logistics,
  large navy, Red Sea & Mediterranean trade, mercenary + Macedonian phalanx army.
- Concerns: Seleucid rivalry (Syrian Wars), reliance on mercenaries, native Egyptian
  underclass tension beneath Greek ruling caste.
- Knows of: Seleucids, Greeks, Carthage, Rome (vaguely, as a western rising power),
  Nubia/Kush, Arabia, India via Red Sea trade (indirect).

### CHINA — late Warring States (state of Qin ascendant)
- Not yet unified. Qin is the dominant state, reforms of Shang Yang in place: legalist,
  militarist, meritocratic army ranks, mass infantry + crossbows, iron working.
- Will unify ~221 BC (Qin), then the "China" player continues through Han.
- Strengths: largest population base, crossbows, cavalry, state mobilization, walls.
- Concerns: rival warring states, steppe nomads (Xiongnu) to the north.
- Knows of: the other Chinese states, steppe nomads, vaguely of lands far west (no
  contact with Egypt/Rome/India yet).

### INDIA — Maurya Empire (Ashoka)
- Ruler: Ashoka, fresh from conquering Kalinga (~261 BC). DIVERGENCE: no remorse, no
  Buddhist turn. Continues conquest.
- Strengths: huge army (infantry, war elephants, cavalry, chariots), wealthy, controls
  most of subcontinent, Pataliputra capital, strong bureaucracy.
- Concerns: vast territory to hold, frontier peoples, succession.
- Knows of: Hellenistic west via the Seleucid border & earlier Alexander contact,
  trade toward Egypt/Arabia (indirect), Sri Lanka, Southeast Asia (faint).

### ROME — Roman Republic
- Mid First Punic War (264–241 BC) vs Carthage over Sicily. Building first big navy.
- Strengths: manpower, legions, Italian confederation, adaptability.
- Concerns: Carthage (naval), Gauls to the north, holding Italian allies.
- Knows of: Carthage, Greek cities, Hellenistic east (vaguely), Gauls. Egypt only as a
  distant rich kingdom. No knowledge of India/China.
