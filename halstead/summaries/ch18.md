# Chapter 18: Fifteen
Dates (from the chapter header and text): September 2020 to July 2021. Turret introduced first week of October 2020; Odile's first hit November 11; Chloe's first hit days later; plateau begins mid-February 2021 (a Wednesday); card analysis late March; Ruth's visit on a Saturday in April; Hark's envelopes in October, ledger review in April; Sandoval in April; Priya's phone early March; driving lessons the summer (second Sunday of the summer through the last Sunday of July); grandmother scene a week after.
Chloe's age: 15 (Sandoval says "a fifteen-year-old"). POV: close third on Chloe; some narration about what Iyad did and how it spread, outside her view.
Setting(s): the new turret lane at the range (60 m, glass, live round); range office; dining hall long table; mat room; Hark's economics classroom; surgical block; laboratory corridor; church lot behind the tire place and the reservoir road; family table at home.

## What happens
Voss introduces a turret that fires a live round down a lane on a fixed cycle; students shoot from behind glass across its path on a count. The target is the bullet. Voss's speech: a gun you can only point at a man is a weapon, a gun that puts a bullet on a bullet is a tool. He says "Put the rifle down when your arm shakes. Pick it back up when it doesn't." Chloe tries to trace the line's origin; the oldest instructor says he first heard it from Voss. Everyone scores zero for weeks. Odile hits first on 11 November; Chloe a few days later. By late November Chloe scores 20, by late January 60. In mid-February she plateaus at about 60 and Voss pulls her off the line. Her fixes (count on her leg, breath-holding, card pattern from wind) fail. In March she borrows all the range cards (a box of ninety, unlocked, shelved beside the year below's) and finds eleven students stopped in the same narrow band. Voss says "Point six. Six in ten, near enough," and refuses to explain: "Some things you have to work out on your own." Ruth visits in April with a clipboard and says her count is clean (Ruth is on ninety-one). Sam is at forty and fine with it.

Self defense: her average falls to 41 seconds from 45 the year before; Kowalczyk says she is now fighting people with experience and it will happen again at eighteen. Odile holds 67 seconds in a March round in front of thirty watchers. Chloe stays beside her afterward.

Economics: Hark gives each student an envelope with $200, to be spent on anything legal, with whatever is left in June kept. Rustem buys dead motors and takes a $120 loan from Chloe at 8 percent against the motors; she lends Fen $40 at 10 percent against a table saw and Priya $30 at 12 percent unsecured. She finishes with $217 against Rustem's $940 (Hark says she is up $17). Hark says she bought a piece of someone else's ceiling and is barely beating inflation. Iyad spreads a rumor linking her to a boy named Aurel from a sign-up sheet neither attended. She tells him to stop; Iyad asks for her count of conversations (four). She stops using the language-block stairs.

Other: surgical suture in March; Hindi, accountancy, topology; her name appears on an internal research paper. Dr. Sandoval asks her to keep quiet about an NDA project; Chloe asks to be added to the next agreement; Sandoval says "I'll ask." Priya's phone sits unattended in a drawer; Kavi rebuilds the messaging so it keeps messages on school machines and needs a password plus a number off a card. Ruth cuts cards. Sam loses his.

Summer: her father teaches her to drive in a church lot and on the reservoir road; her grandmother examines her hand: burns, a bent knuckle she set herself in a corridor.
