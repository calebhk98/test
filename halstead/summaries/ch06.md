# Chapter 06: The List
Dates (from the chapter header and text): August 2012 (header). Scenes: "the last Wednesday" (parent hotel breakfast); "the last night" (families eat on campus, then the hall after); the next morning (departure); the drive. The text does not name the weekday of the last dinner. Chloe says she has been there 27 days, "tomorrow as well, which makes twenty-eight." In ch03 her dad arranged to take "the last Friday" off to collect her; ch06 puts him at the hotel breakfast on the Wednesday (ambiguous whether he arrived early).
Chloe's age: 6   POV: close third person, present tense, Chloe's head. In the last scene she pretends to sleep in the back seat and overhears her dad on the car speaker with her mom.
Setting(s): the parent hotel breakfast room; the campus dining hall; the hall outside Pluto at night; Chloe's dorm room and the car park; the road home.

## What happens
At the parent hotel breakfast, Chloe eats waffles beside her dad while the other parents compare notes. They discuss the bus that never appears, scam suspicions, and the list. Several say Halstead gave them a paragraph on how the list is compiled but not a name or reason. One says "They told us the list is a good list," and another asks "good at what?" Chloe's dad stays quiet, cutting his waffle. One mother says her child is top of the reading group at home; a man answers "Here it buys you a paper plate and a seat at this table." Her dad puts extra syrup on her waffle.

On the last night, at long tables in the dining hall, Chloe tells her dad about the month in a rush: Gary the CPR mannequin (she got the click on the fourth try; Sam gave up); the bridge (Ruth's went at nineteen; Chloe wrote fourteen and was wrong by three); swimming (one length; Sam two); dividing fractions, drawn on a napkin and again on the clean side; Spanish (including a rude phrase Sam says at breakfast), signing, a made-up-language puzzle (Ruth got plurals before her, Kavi the endings), wood shop, first aid, cooking, the art museum, a coding class (count to a hundred skipping every seven), the cold server room, a robot arm, chess, negative numbers, karate falls, and nerf guns. Her dad asks how many days; she says twenty-seven. He says "Cool." Her knee jiggles. She explains that she did the fractions again alone at night because the rightness belonged to Mr. Baptiste, not her. Her dad folds the napkin into his shirt pocket.

Afterward, in the hall outside Pluto, Chloe, Ruth, Sam and Kavi sit on the floor. Ruth proposes all four apply for the year. She says there is a form and a fall visit day where "they look at you," with different people doing the looking. Kavi's parents already asked on day three. Chloe presses for dates, what the day tests, who signs. She runs the car math: four hours each way. Ruth says she will write a weekly letter beginning Monday; Sam doubts it (the bridge log has two entries). Kavi says a girl's dad drove twelve hours. Ruth says parents like school.

The next morning Chloe is dressed on the stripped mattress. Priya's hair tie goes missing; Chloe gives hers. Sam says "see you at the thing in the fall"; Ruth says it is only a form so far. Kavi holds out a palm and Chloe puts hers in it. In the car Chloe pretends sleep. Her dad calls her mom: "Meg, they taught her CPR." She explained fractions on a napkin and spoke Spanish. Her mom asks "what is that, what's the name for that"; neither knows. Her mom: "Four hours away, Dave, and free, and they were the ones who wrote to her."
