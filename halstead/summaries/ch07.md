# Chapter 07: The Same Room
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "August 2012 - October 2012". Opens on the last day of camp (a month of camp in July per ch 8; the opening scene is undated); then "In August" at home; library return on a Tuesday (book out five weeks); second grade starts in the fall; last week of September; first week of October.
Chloe's age: 7 (second grade; not stated outright in this chapter)   POV: Close third on Chloe throughout. No drift.
Setting(s): School loop/flagpole at the end of camp; Chloe's kitchen; her bedroom; the car coming back from her grandmother's; the public library; Ms. Vance's second grade classroom; the kitchen phone.

## What happens
The chapter opens at camp pickup. Ruth hugs Chloe at the flagpole and makes her promise that they will all get into Halstead ("the year one"), and Chloe promises to ask every day until someone says yes.

At home in August, Chloe asks her mom to call Sam. Her mom asks for a phone number and last name, and Chloe realizes she has none for Sam, Ruth, Kavi or Priya; first names were all anyone used at camp. She lists what she does know about Sam. Her mom (visibly sympathetic) phones the camp school, asks them to pass the Kessler number to the four families, and gives the names Sam, Ruth, Kavi and Priya. The school will pass the number on, and it is up to the families whether they call.

Chloe waits by the phone. Calls come from her grandmother, a man for her dad, and a woman asking about a vehicle. On day nine her mom says it can take weeks; on day ten the school confirms the message went to all four families that week. Chloe stops sitting in the kitchen but keeps checking the phone.

On a Sunday drive back from her grandmother's, Chloe asks to go to Halstead for the year. Her mom looks out the window and her dad's hands come up on the wheel. They agree to talk that night. (The text does not show that conversation.)

Chloe returns the library book (five weeks, not four) and tells the librarian everything about camp: the CPR dummy Gary, the dowel and string bridge that broke at the corner, dividing fractions. She takes four new books and finishes two by Friday.

Second grade is Ms. Vance. Chloe's group is Bryce Dolan, Kayleigh Burns and Aiden R. The math is adding with carrying, all easy. In the second week Ms. Vance sets up a time capsule shoebox to be opened in fifth grade. Chloe writes something about mailing a package to a stranger; Kayleigh says she talks as if reading off a card, Bryce says she is weirder than before. Chloe covers the page, scratches it out, and writes "I hope you like fifth grade" instead.

She stops being able to read: with her third library book she cannot get past a page and a half. On the Thursday she returns the two finished books and brings the unread ones home in her bag, tells her mom she has a lot of school stuff, and weeks later asks her mom to renew them instead of going. She asks about Halstead many times a day. Her parents say they are thinking about it. She starts a column of tally marks under her pillow, one per day without a yes.

In late September her mom phones Chloe's school about skipping; third grade is the floor and the testing is in spring only for a program starting the following fall. In the first week of October Chloe cries at her desk and cannot say why. Ms. Vance kneels beside her. The chapter ends mid-cry.
