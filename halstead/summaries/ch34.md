# Chapter 34: The Files
Dates (from the chapter header and text): June 2026. The files' case number was opened in 2013 (thirteen years of updates). The incident report concerns a night four years before the chapter. Chloe's lease-signing week (a Tuesday) is dated in her file, about five months before. Theo has to be back in the federal building by eight the next morning.
Chloe's age: not stated in this chapter.   POV: Roving omniscient across the seven readers (Chloe, Ruth, Sam, Nadia, Eli, Kavi, Theo), with the most sustained attention on Chloe, Ruth and Kavi. Chat scenes are plain transcript with short narration between lines. No clear single-head POV.
Setting(s): Seven separate places, each reader alone: Chloe at her desk in her apartment; Ruth on the floor of a Cambridge lab; Sam outdoors on a phone while on duty with a sergeant and others (at one point at port arms); Nadia at the shop after closing, standing at the counter; Eli at his kitchen table; Kavi in a lab down the corridor from where he had lessons as a boy; Theo standing, then outside his own apartment a few blocks from the federal building. The group chat links them.

## What happens
Seven of them open the file at different hours, each alone. Theo stays on his feet throughout, with twenty minutes outside still ahead of him.

The file covers all ninety-one of them. Every page has the same header, a case number opened in 2013 and a classification banner. Each file lists address, employer, family and banks. Sam's is the thickest, an Army file on top of the Halstead material, with an officer's assessment that treats his honesty as the interesting part. Theo's has a note that makes him close the laptop and go outside. He posts "mine has a name in it I already know." Sam asks whose, and Theo does not tell him. Nadia's file crosses her company's filings with a competing job board's background check. Eli's notes he found five vulnerabilities in his employer's systems in one afternoon, unprompted. Kavi's is the thinnest, annotated "continuity" in the margin. Chloe's notes her recent job requiring a background investigation.

Every file starts with a name, a date, a photograph outside a building, and a line that family contact went no further than one letter. A standing line says the graduates are believed to be in contact, retyped yearly. The method is listed as unknown, collection against the channel always fails, and the recommendation is to keep trying. Kavi reads that twice, his pen going still.

Then an incident report from four years back. Four students left campus at night, sat in a Waffle House for two hours, and were pushing each other in a shopping trolley when seven men, three armed, came to rob them. The men agree it was fast, that one student did all of it, and that two were told afterward it had been sloppy. A watcher with binoculars timed it at twenty-two seconds. All have a blank on the students' faces. Footage from every camera nearby is unrecoverable wherever the students appear. The observing unit's own recordings are gone too. The report is a crooked scan.

In the chat, Chloe says "thats us." Sam jokes about Ruth breaking the cart. Ruth says she built the box in about a weekend to avoid detention, and that it also took cameras she did not know existed. Kavi, at the bench, makes her read it back. Ruth says she was thirteen, it was a weekend, it was a fun problem and she was not trying. Nadia notes the unit still does not know. Ruth says she has said "we're ahead" for a year without really accepting it. She asks Eli how good they are. He says he does not know and has never been at the top of a room. Chloe says they are about to find out. Eli says that is not an answer, and Chloe says it was not meant to be. Theo finishes his twenty minutes and goes back in, his file closed.
