## What happens
Marksmanship replaces archery. Voss runs dry drill, then live fire from October: fifty meters prone, then kneeling, standing, a hundred meters, a crosswind, and in November a swinging target. Ruth is already bored and puts three rounds through one hole. Odile burns her hand on an ejection port. In February a mechanical clay trap arrives; Aymar times it at 7.2 s against the printed card, Voss changes the card, Ruth teases that Aymar is "mine" because of the shared last name. Kavi says arrows gave a hang at the top and a shared count; a bullet gives neither, and something harder is coming.

Self defense: Chloe lasts fifty seconds in a round on a Tuesday in February (her average is 45); Bex retells it first. Chloe tells Ruth herself the next morning.

Teaching: she gets seven of the year below in September; Marek goes to another teacher. In November all seven get the same question wrong; Chloe finds she taught them an ordering reversed. She tells them honestly. A thirteen-year-old tells it at their table; Iyad adds it to his circuit; a girl asks Chloe at the sinks and refuses to name the source, saying only "Tuesday. Far table, the end by the window." Chloe knows who sits there.

Courses: Japanese; abstract algebra; probability; contracts; logistics; comparative religion. Engineering: she writes a clamp spec; Dov builds it from the paper; it closes on the wrong axis because she never said what it holds.

Group project: locate a single sound on the long field to within a hand's width. Kavi: needs half-millisecond sync; wireless gives only about two milliseconds. They keep it for a fortnight without telling the group. Ruth says wireless was a fortnight of wasted work. Nadia proposes wire; 160 meters laid on a Saturday in rain, lifted Sunday. Kavi's clock arrives Tuesday; Odile and Sam make the sound 60 times. Chloe changes Ruth's box thresholds at night; Ruth objects. The run is on the last Thursday in March: Chloe places it 119 m up and 30 m across; the mark on the grass is a hand's width and a half off. Grade B; failures: more than half a dozen people needed to deploy, calibration finished in the last window, known error carried a fortnight unreported. In April Chloe finds a better placing for Nadia.

No-libraries project: Chloe's text renderer; Ruth's synthesised sound; thirty milliseconds of silence found by Ruth. Sanders adds Chloe to acknowledgments (list of five). Sam is six foot two. Chloe's three hinges for Pruitt fail on the seam. Sam asks out a girl in the year below in October; they break in March.

Winter break: Chloe mentions rifles; her mother (Meg) is blindsided; her father had known since an October phone call. The conversation is not finished. On Sunday she asks questions about the range. Calls home increase.
