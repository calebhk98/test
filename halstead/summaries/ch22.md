# Chapter 22: The Offer
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "April 2023". Interviews run from the first Monday of April for a run of days into the second week; Nadia's slot is the Thursday of the first week; Chloe's is the Wednesday of the second week; her phone call to her parents is that night.
Chloe's age: 17 (her mother says "at seventeen years old"; she turns 18 in August 2023).   POV: Mostly close third on Chloe, with omniscient passages (the dinner table, the exit-interview week, Nadia's scene is told from outside her head and from Amberg's side of the desk).
Setting(s): The corridor and Amberg's office; dinner table; the network room; the stairwell; Chloe's dorm floor (phone call).

## What happens
Amberg runs fifteen-minute exit interviews for each student, a dozen a day. Routine continues around them. Dinner becomes a comparison of decisions; Iyad keeps track of who has gone in and asks whether they have told anybody at home.

Chloe waits in a corridor of chairs and goes in on the Wednesday of the second week. Amberg gives her the offer: full-time research from the day she would otherwise enter a dorm, $135,000 a year to start, housing, food, car and medical covered, and the degree on top paid for at her own pace, with the post open until she finishes. Six-day weeks the first year, five after; a review every March. A woman two floors down signed nine years ago and now runs a team of nineteen. Chloe says no: "it's school... the same thing with a different door on it." Amberg's pen stops, and he writes it down.

All ninety-one are posted; thirteen say yes and seventy-eight no. One yes is a boy from the language block with no badge, who spends the week trying to find out what the door is for.

Nadia is interviewed on the Thursday of the first week and also says no. She has applied to no college; she costed four years at about $110,000. She has sent 201 applications since November with 13 answers. She is building a hiring site where the employer pays on a hire and the person pays nothing. Amberg notes that four of forty that week said they were starting something and three will be doing something else by Christmas. She has not told her parents. That night she counts the notebook and writes the first sixty lines of the site.

Kavi says yes. In the network room he tells Chloe that what he wants is happening in one building, a "lab that also has children in it." Chloe says she turned hers down; he says she turned down "a filing cabinet."

On the stairs Ruth asks Sam if Bex has done her exit interview. She says Bex has never put a girl's name on anything; Sam says Bex put his on every line of the range breakdown. Ruth tells him to count.

Chloe calls home. Her mother says $135,000 is more than the house cost. Her father (Dave) calls it a mistake. Chloe says she did the math in February. She explains the next decade would be the same building while everyone else leaves. Her mother says Georgetown's aid left a gap and she has already called the bank about a loan against the house. Chloe says Ruth still goes to MIT, Sam to the Army, and Kavi said yes. Her father asks whether the door stays open in a year; she did not ask. Both parents say they still think it is a mistake. The call ends with "Okay."
