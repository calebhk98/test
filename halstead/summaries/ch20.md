# Chapter 20: The Parking Lot
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "June 2022". One night in the last week before summer (summer "starts Friday"); they leave twenty minutes short of midnight; the fight is around two in the morning; the chat reaction is at breakfast.
Chloe's age: Not stated; 16 or 17 (Sam is "your sixteen" per Nadia; Chloe turns 18 in August 2023).   POV: Mostly third person on the group as a whole, shifting into Chloe's head for the threat assessment and the aftermath. The fight itself is told from outside, including "from four meters away it looks like...". The final scene is not tied to one head.
Setting(s): Halstead campus fence and corridors; the downhill road into town; an all-night waffle diner; a hardware store lot with a loading dock; dorm corridor.

## What happens
Chloe, Nadia, Ruth and Sam are the first students to sneak out since the school opened. Preparation took a fortnight: Nadia copied a maintenance supervisor's badge, Chloe looped the corridor cameras, Ruth's box (jams and writes noise over any recorder), and Sam timed the night patrol's second pass. Kavi declines at the last second, saying he wants to finish something. They go over the fence at twenty to midnight, with a lit window on the third floor behind them.

They walk about half an hour to a diner that serves waffles all night. Sam eats a full stack plus half of Nadia's. The waitress, maybe fifty, calls them all "honey". They talk: Ruth confirms Amberg's car dent is shaped like a mailbox (he tells anyone who asks); Ruth's younger brother has been accepted to Halstead (letter came Thursday, he turns seven in September), and he only asked whether there is pudding. Chloe says she is doing the forge both summer sessions again; Nadia says Chloe always stays in what she is worst at; Chloe says she has not been worst at it for two years; Nadia says she is just the only one there every morning nobody makes her be. Sam and Ruth argue over the word "hither". Nadia splits the bill to the cent. On the way home Sam, Ruth and Nadia ride a stolen shopping cart off a curb; the wheel breaks.

Seven men stop them between parked cars: three have guns. Chloe assesses seven and three armed, and three ways out. Sam asks to "have this one". The other three go over a chain link fence to a loading dock roof and wait at the edge in position to drop down. Sam walks in, disarms the front man's gun, and fights all seven. Ruth times it: twenty-two seconds. Nadia calls a warning ("Left") during it. Sam says "Nadia cheated". Afterward Sam takes the third gun apart and checks each man's breathing; Ruth checks a man with a concussion, Nadia resets a shoulder, Chloe checks two men's ribs.

They decide not to report it (Nadia: a police report would mean going home and not coming back). Sam is upset the fight was "nothing". Chloe explains why: the men were bunched, held guns for show, and a person with any skill would make money some other way. Nadia suggests he go back and fight the waitress; Sam concedes she would take him apart. They walk home arguing about her.

They return over the fence; Chloe's loop on the cameras unwinds to normal. Chloe checks her own ribs by counting. Ruth posts a few lines in the chat about Sam standing in the lot with his arms out; by breakfast there are sixty replies and an argument about whether guns count as a test. Kavi sends a laughing face. Sam ignores the question each time it comes up.
