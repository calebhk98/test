# Chapter 14: Sixty Degrees
Dates (from the chapter header and text): September 2016 to April 2017. First arrow collision last week of October 2016; essay questions drawn second week of November 2016; Christmas 2016 at home; Watch on April 11, 2017.
Chloe's age: 11 (not stated; implied, ch15 is "Twelve"). POV: close third on Chloe. Drift: the Watch section uses hearsay ("People say afterward") for events Chloe did not see (Ruth, Nadia, Hearn's capture).
Setting(s): the long field north of Building Three (archery); dining hall; classrooms; art history room; wood shop; Chloe's family kitchen and front room at Christmas; the school during an all-staff-hostile Watch.

## What happens
Archery changes in September 2016. After weeks of ordinary target work, Coach Bell puts a metronome on a post and explains the new game: two firing lines 70 m apart, side by side facing north, angled toward each other, meeting at 60 degrees. The goal is for arrows from the two lines to collide at the top of their arcs. The "target" is the other arrow, coming at 60 m per second, in the air about 1.5 seconds. The block (target at 150 m) is never to be touched. Students count with the metronome. Kavi objects that the shoulder is the tell for darts; Bell says that is for other time.

Chloe practices release timing without a bow. In the last week of October she hits an arrow with Odile's, which snaps in the air; they celebrate. Most of the year collides at least once by March; Odile does it three days out of five.

Chloe takes Latin; Ruth (Arabic) says it is cheating; they argue about it. Calculus starts in math. In astronomy Chloe writes about the two ways of measuring the universe's expansion rate which disagree; Bex asks about it at dinner and then relays it to the teacher before Chloe can, and again announces it to the class. Chloe avoids Bex's eye and tells Kavi herself in the corridor. Sam works out the rocket equation; Bex relays that too. Art history: Chloe cannot reconcile a painting's 1959 and 1998 prices; teacher says "a good question for a different course." Wood shop: she builds chairs; a Japanese corner joint; her twelfth works and goes on a shelf. Her chisel rolls its edge; the instructor sends her to the forge.

Essay questions drawn from a hat held by Iyad. Chloe gets "Was the area bombing of German cities the right decision?" Sam argues she must build the other side. She does, writes 28 pages, gets a B from Hearn for hedging on pages 8, 22 and the conclusion. At Christmas she tells her mother "it was wrong" and cannot explain the background. Her grandmother discounts her archery ("once in a whole year").

On April 11, a Watch involving the whole school: every staff member is hostile until 1600. Ruth reportedly runs the east stairwell, Nadia the third floor. Sam is marked out in 90 seconds after jumping from a bannister. Chloe holds the third-floor landing. Hearn is the last staff member, holed up in the library; students take him at 3:42. Grade for students: B. Item 7: nobody established how many adults were in the building.
