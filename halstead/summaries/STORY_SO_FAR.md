## Premise and cast

Halstead is a school for children of roughly 160+ IQ. It convinces them they are normal by making them work hard among equally able classmates, so each one measures themselves against peers and sees an average student. The school is secretive: no one outside knows who funds it or how children are chosen, and some mysteries are deliberate and unresolved. This is book one of a series. The story follows Chloe Kessler from age 6 (2011) to 21 (2026).

Cast:
- Chloe Kessler: protagonist, born August 2005; reader, linguist, arguer; middle of her year.
- Meg and Dave Kessler: her mother (distrustful, protective) and father (calmer, reads contracts).
- Dana: Chloe's older cousin. Her grandmother: visits and phones.
- Ruth Aymar: fastest, blunt friend; later MIT.
- Sam Marsh: joker, best fighter; later Army.
- Kavi: precise, technical friend; stays at Halstead as staff.
- Priya: friend; horses.
- Nadia, Odile, Eli, Theo: later friends and chat members.
- Bex Alcantar: takes credit for others' ideas.
- Iyad Mansour: retells and embellishes stories.
- Marek: fast student who will not hand work in.
- Staff: Mrs. Okonkwo (office), Amberg (law, cryptography, exit interviews), Voss (marksmanship), Pruitt (forge), Bell (coach), Hearn (essays), Sinclair (runs Watch drills).

The pediatrician finds nothing medical and recommends an attention evaluation. In January Mrs. Aldana takes Chloe's book when she reads ahead. ## 02. March 7th  (Mar to Jun 2012; Chloe 6)
Psychologist Ben Prentice tests Chloe with timed puzzles, vocabulary and a coding sheet. She overhears him tell her mother that the school's picture is wrong: she finishes in minutes and has an empty hour. The report will give one composite number that averages very high reasoning with ordinary writing speed, so it will read "bright kid." The gifted program starts in third grade, two years away. He says the school may just put the report in a folder. Her mother tells Chloe only that he thinks she is "doing just fine." Nothing changes at school. Chloe learns to finish first, sit still, keep answers short, and eats alone by June.
State at end:

- Open: whether the report reaches the school.

## 03. The Letter  (Jun to Jul 2012; Chloe 6)
A typed letter arrives addressed to "Miss Chloe Kessler," inviting her to a free four week summer session at Halstead, with transport, room, board and a free hotel for parents. Neither parent knows how she was chosen. The district says it has no record of any school called Halstead. Meg phones Halstead; answers come fast, and the school says its "networks" find children and the list is "usually pretty good." Dave finds a website with remarkable teachers and little else. After nine days of argument they say yes, because Chloe wants to go ("It says Miss"). Parents drive her; Meg stays at the hotel for the first days.
