# Story So Far

High-level recap of the Halstead manuscript, one entry per chapter. Scene-level detail is in the chNN.md files. The chapters are the only source of truth; if this file and a chapter disagree, the chapter wins.

## Premise and cast

Halstead is a school for children of roughly 160+ IQ. It convinces them they are normal by putting them among equally able classmates, so each one measures themselves against peers and sees an average student. Outsiders do not know who funds it or how children are chosen, and some mysteries are deliberately unresolved. This is book one of a series. The story follows Chloe Kessler from age 6 (2011) to 21 (2026).

Cast:
- Chloe Kessler: protagonist, born August 2005; a reader, linguist and arguer.
- Meg and Dave Kessler: her mother (protective, distrustful) and father (calmer, reads contracts).
- Dana: Chloe's older cousin. Chloe's grandmother visits and hosts Christmas.
- Ruth Aymar: fastest and bluntest friend; later at MIT.
- Sam Marsh: joker and best fighter; later in the Army.
- Kavi: precise, technical friend; stays at Halstead as staff.
- Priya: friend who loves horses; later abroad.
- Nadia, Odile, Eli, Theo: later friends; all but Odile are in the group chat. Theo is a federal analyst.
- Bex Alcantar: takes credit for other people's work.
- Iyad Mansour: retells and embellishes stories.
- Marek: fast student who will not hand work in.
- Staff: Mrs. Okonkwo (office), Amberg (law, cryptography, exit interviews), Voss (marksmanship), Pruitt (forge), Bell (coach), Hearn (essays), Sinclair (runs Watch drills).

## 01. Before (Sept 2011 to Feb 2012, Chloe 6)

Chloe is six, in first grade, and bored. She finishes worksheets in minutes, reads everything within reach, and has been told by Kayleigh that she talks weird. She starts giving short answers on purpose and stretching her work to look ordinary. Her teacher, Mrs. Aldana, calls her one of the strongest readers she has had but notes trouble focusing. A pediatrician recommends an attention evaluation. Her mother pushes the school for a next step, and her father is more restrained. Chloe believes she is the one who cannot sit still and must fix herself. The evaluation is set for March 7.

## 02. March 7th (Mar to Jun 2012, Chloe 6)

Psychologist Ben Prentice tests Chloe with timed puzzles and a symbol-copying task. Chloe overhears him say the school's picture is wrong: she finishes early and then has an empty hour. He expects the report to average very high reasoning with ordinary copying speed, so it reads as a bright kid. He advises taking it to the school, which may simply file it. Her mother tells Chloe only that she is doing fine; Chloe never learns the number. Nothing changes at school. By June she has learned to finish first, sit still, keep answers short and eat alone. Whether the report reaches the school stays open.

## 03. The Letter (Jun to Jul 2012, Chloe 6)

A typed letter addressed to Miss Chloe Kessler invites her to a free four-week summer session at Halstead, with travel, room and board. The district has no record of any school by that name. Meg phones Halstead and gets fast, smooth answers: the school has networks that find children, and its lists are usually good. Dave studies the school's polished website, and Meg finds no outside coverage. After nine days of argument the parents agree, mostly because Chloe has been flat since Christmas. No one knows who made the list or who pays. Neighbor Ellie Peterson, in the same school pull-out group, got no letter.

## 04. Pluto (Jul 2012, Chloe 6)

Halstead opens loudly on day one. Chloe's roommate is Priya, who talks about horses, and her classroom is Pluto, last in a hall of planet-named rooms, with Sam, Ruth and Kavi. The school promises anyone can go home the same day, without argument. On the first nights Chloe phones her mother and sleeps at the hotel nearby. She barely speaks. By the fourth night she no longer makes the call, and her mother leaves on the Sunday. Owen, a boy in Pluto, struggles with the work. Chloe believes that talking freely gets a child called weird.

## 05. Behind (Jul 2012, Chloe 6)

Ruth sets the pace, and Chloe's writing is shorter than Ruth's and Kavi's. In a bridge project Chloe defers to Sam, and her bridge holds eleven pounds while Ruth's holds nineteen. Mr. Baptiste's fraction lesson gives Chloe the right answers, and she works out the reason alone that night. Owen leaves camp. The others say he wanted to be home; Ruth says he cried in a stairwell. Chloe cannot accept that having fun and wanting to go home can both be true. In an argument about the moon Chloe is wrong, and she has started to enjoy arguing.

## 06. The List (Aug 2012, Chloe 6)

At the hotel breakfast, parents compare notes: all were invited by letter, none were given a reason, and Halstead calls the list good. Chloe tells her father about the month, from CPR to chess to karate falls. Ruth proposes that all four friends apply for the full year through a form and a fall visit day, and says she will write weekly letters. Chloe does not know what the fall day tests. In the car she pretends to sleep and hears her father tell her mother, "Meg, they taught her CPR." Her parents still do not know where the list came from.

## 07. The Same Room (Aug to Oct 2012, Chloe 7)

Chloe has no phone numbers or last names for her camp friends. Her mother asks the camp to pass the Kessler number to four families, and no one calls. Chloe asks to go to Halstead for the year; her parents say they are thinking about it. In second grade with Ms. Vance, Chloe cannot write honestly for the class time capsule, stops reading for pleasure, and keeps a tally of days without a yes. The school says third grade is the floor for its gifted program. In October she cries at her desk and cannot say why. No camp friend has made contact.

## 08. The Asking (Oct 2012 to Jan 2013, Chloe 7)

Child therapist Dr. Ammons says Chloe is depressed, and Chloe keeps asking to go to Halstead. Her mother fights the school and in November wins a year-early place in a Thursday enrichment group, where Chloe finishes the work instantly and hides how fast. Her mother admits she has lain awake looking for a good enough reason to say no. Her father gives Chloe a bike at Christmas. In late January Chloe stops asking, and the Halstead letter stays in her sock drawer. She believes her mother is blocking Halstead and her father would say yes. Her mother believes the Thursdays are helping.

## 09. February (Feb to Apr 2013, Chloe 7)

Chloe begs to be allowed to go, offering her birthday and her bike. Her father has wanted yes since August and has sent in a form. Her mother asks what kind of family sends a seven-year-old four hours away. On March 14 both parents say yes. Halstead then offers a furnished house near campus; Meg refuses, since it would leave them nowhere to live if they ever wanted Chloe home. Chloe does not know about the house. At an April visit day Mrs. Okonkwo gives a tour and will not say who is enrolled, and a bearded evaluator asks Chloe what she would do with a whole day.

## 10. April (Apr to Jun 2013, Chloe 7)

Chloe arrives as a student and finds Sam, Ruth and Kavi already there. Her roommate is Fen. She is behind in four subjects and caught up in literature, and the library stays open all night with no limit. In May she solves a rolling-wheel problem and mathematics becomes her favorite hour. She believes she can work out why children are sent home. Kavi says Owen wanted to stay and cried before he left. Chloe tells her mother on Sunday calls that she is happy, and her mother believes her. In June she goes home. Open: Kavi's surname, which is still unknown, and the call she promised Ruth.

## 11. Eight (Jun 2013 to Jun 2014, Chloe 8)

Over the summer Chloe is talkative at home. She argues about curfew and a hot dog, and wins the hot dog argument with her father. Back at school her roommate is Margie. She is ninetieth of ninety on the first term sheet and in dance, and Mrs. Okonkwo refuses to move her. Chloe quits choir after matching the others only a third of the time. Her grandmother says she looks heavier than at Christmas, and Chloe eats half her plate. In June she finds she has been reading ahead in three subjects she had been behind in. Sam is working on a climbing overhang that nobody has cleared.

## 12. Nine (Sept 2014 to Jul 2015, Chloe 9)

Chloe's sheet has eight lines because she is nine, and the line count rises every year. She takes Mandarin, chemistry, statistics and biology. Bex Alcantar tells the teacher "I" about bridge joints Chloe built, and Chloe stops saying "we" for the year rather than confront her. Iyad Mansour announces Chloe's struggling Mandarin to the table. Chloe quits riding without telling Priya, and the barn thinks Priya drove her off. Sam clears the climbing overhang on May 11. Over the summer Chloe's father questions a vendor proposal whose promised results cannot be checked. Kavi's three course requests were all refused.

## 13. Ten Pages (Sept 2015 to Apr 2016, Chloe 10)

Mr. Hearn assigns a ten-page essay each week and cannot tell whether Chloe is right or merely comfortable. By spring she is no longer miserable but cannot say why. Kowalczyk introduces a ten-on-one self defense drill, and Chloe reaches eleven seconds by April. In paintball Chloe and Ruth work out a method; Bex presents it to Bell as her own, and Bell writes it on the board under Bex's name. In the first Watch drill, adults enter a building, everyone fails, and item six says the north stairwell was uncovered. Chloe takes that as aimed at her; Ruth says it is aimed at everyone.

## 14. Sixty Degrees (Sept 2016 to Apr 2017, Chloe 11)

Archery becomes a paired game in which arrows from two firing lines meet in the air. Chloe's first collision comes in October. Bex relays Chloe's astronomy question to the teacher before Chloe can. For an essay drawn from a hat, Chloe gets the area bombing of German cities; she writes both sides and earns a B for hedging. In an all-school Watch in April, staff are hostile until four in the afternoon, and students take Hearn last. Chloe holds a third floor landing. The board says nobody established how many adults were in the building.

## 15. Twelve (Sept 2017 to Jan 2018, Chloe 12)

Chloe works at the forge under Pruitt. Archery now uses a partner's spoken count, and she and Odile take a week to settle it. Marek, a younger student, stops her on the stairs to explain an error he found in a book. One night she, Ruth, Kavi, Sam and Priya run a blind bread test in the school kitchen until staff send them to bed. At a 4 a.m. Watch, four intruders arrive unannounced. Chloe volunteers as bait; her friends subdue all four, and Sinclair arrives. Chloe receives a C and cannot tell whether the intruders were staged. Her parents know about a kitchen letter but not about the 4 a.m. Watch.

## 16. Thirteen (Sept 2018 to Jul 2019, Chloe 13)

Chloe starts teaching mathematics to seven twelve-year-olds. Marek hands in blank sheets every week while doing the work, and when he fails, Chloe writes the mark. She does not know what that costs him. Within a week she finds the weak point in Ruth and Kavi's encrypted chat; they repair it and believe the chat is now hidden. Ruth shows Chloe a homemade device that defeats recording. Iyad retells a cipher night as Chloe withholding the answer, and she stops sitting beside Nadia. In summer her father rebuilds the back steps; a riser Chloe cut sits 4 mm proud, and he says nobody will measure it.

## 17. Fourteen (Sept 2019 to Apr 2020, Chloe 14)

Marksmanship replaces archery under Voss, with live fire from October. Chloe teaches the year below and, without knowing it, teaches an ordering backwards; when she admits the error, Iyad spreads the story. Her group places a sound on the long field within a hand and a half of the mark, and earns a B. Sam dates a girl in the year below until March. Over winter break Chloe mentions the rifles: her mother is blindsided, and her father had known since an October call. Chloe calls home more often. Kavi warns that something harder is coming.

## 18. Fifteen (Sept 2020 to Jul 2021, Chloe 15)

Voss introduces a turret that fires a live round down a lane while students shoot from behind glass, aiming at the round itself. Chloe's score plateaus around 60, and ten classmates stop in the same band. Voss says only "point six" and refuses to explain. In economics she lends classmates money and ends the year $17 up. Iyad spreads a rumor linking her to a boy named Aurel, which spreads through the school. Dr. Sandoval asks her to keep an NDA project quiet; she will only if her name goes on the next agreement. Her burns and a bent knuckle go unexplained at home.

## 19. Sixteen (Apr 2022 to Jun 2023, Chloe 16 to 17)

Chloe fails the school's bar exam in April 2022 by four points out of 200, while Kavi passes by six. Amberg tells her she wrote for a reader who already had the answer, so she learns to write out each step. She passes the October retake by 22. Driving lessons use staged emergencies. She believes Priya threw a negotiation exercise on purpose and has told only her father. Marek will sit the bar the next April. At home for the summer, Chloe asks her father whether her parents ever wanted her to stay home and attend the state school. The chapter ends before he answers.

## 20. The Parking Lot (Jun 2022, Chloe 16)

In the last week before summer, Chloe, Nadia, Ruth and Sam leave campus at night, the first students ever to do so. Kavi declines. They spend a long night at an all-night diner, where they learn that Ruth's younger brother has been accepted to Halstead. On the walk back, seven men, three armed, stop them. Sam asks to take them on alone and finishes in twenty-two seconds; the others give first aid. They agree never to report it. Sam is disappointed the fight was easy. Ruth posts a short account in the chat; by breakfast there are sixty replies. The staff never learn of the trip.

## 21. The Applications (Oct 2022 to Mar 2023, Chloe 17)

Chloe applies to fourteen colleges, including Georgetown, and cuts her essay about teaching Marek to half a line. Nadia applies for jobs, not colleges. Decisions arrive in March: Ruth gets MIT; Kavi, MIT, Caltech and Chicago; Sam all but one; Odile everywhere; Chloe twelve of fourteen. At a dinner, Bex tells a Caltech visitor the long-field story and claims the geometry Chloe drew. Ruth stops saving Bex a seat. Chloe confronts Bex at breakfast and gets nowhere, and tells her mother only that Bex got every part right. Chloe has not chosen a school.

## 22. The Offer (Apr 2023, Chloe 17)

Amberg holds exit interviews and offers Chloe full-time research work from the day she would enter college: $135,000 a year to start, with housing, food, a car and medical covered. She refuses. Thirteen of ninety-one say yes, including Kavi. Nadia also refuses; she has told her parents nothing and is building a hiring site. Sam is joining the Army. Chloe calls home, where her mother says the salary is more than the house cost and her father calls it a mistake. Her mother has called the bank about a loan to cover a gap in Georgetown's aid. Chloe did not ask whether the offer would stay open.

## 23. The First One (Jun to Jul 2023, Chloe 17)

Halstead holds its first graduation: ninety-one students, each name read in twelve seconds. Chloe's parents, her grandmother and cousin Dana attend. Chloe will drive herself to Georgetown in August and plans to take the Foreign Service exam. Sam signs up for the Army in July; Ruth goes to MIT; Kavi stays on as staff; Theo takes a federal analyst job; Priya leaves for two years in South America. Nadia strikes a deal with her father to test her site until March 31, and he dislikes it. Chloe's father knows the Foreign Service plan; her mother has not been told yet.

## 24. The Chat (Sept to Oct 2023, Chloe 18)

The five-year-old group chat now spans Georgetown, MIT, Army basic training, the Halstead lab, a shop, a federal job and a company far from home. Eli gets a lawyer meeting after a joking bounty claim. Chloe buys a laptop with the wrong specifications. Nadia's site claims 26,000 openings by October, and she has about three months of money. Chloe asks the group about a small white pill she took at school; her doctor has never heard of it, and Halstead calls its formula internal. Nadia finds three ingredient names through an old work account. Only Chloe and Nadia know them. No get-together date is set.

## 25. Forty Targets (Aug to Nov 2023, Chloe 18)

Chloe does not appear. Sam is in Army basic training, where he scores top marks and believes basic is only a screen before a real course. He keeps asking his drill sergeant when the real one starts; the answer is Tuesday. At chow he mentions a small boarding school and a forty percent range score; one man stops believing him. He hits 40 of 40 at qualification. The captain files a memorandum, a counterintelligence notice, a request for Sam's education record and a mental health referral. A woman far above the captain pulls an old folder. Sam does not know about the referrals.

## 26. The Exercise (Feb 2024, Chloe 18)

Chloe appears only in the chat. Sam's unit runs Winter Anvil, a four-night field exercise. In a culvert on the second night, Sam reacts by reflex to being grabbed, injures one man and breaks the no-contact rule; the engagement is voided. At the review a major says the correct move was to be dead. Sam asks whether a harder exercise exists, and the major says difficulty is not his to set. Asked where he learned patience, Sam says "School, sir", and names Halstead. The incident goes up the chain. In the chat, Ruth and Nadia say not to ask again; Eli says to ask.

## 27. Nadia (May 2024, Chloe 18)

Chloe does not appear. Nadia's company has four staff and three rooms over a laundromat. In May she finds fake employer accounts collecting job seekers' bank details; one victim loses $2,600. She traces them to a registered agent whose office is above a tire shop. She goes there alone, with a file set to send itself to authorities if she does not return, and tells four men upstairs the accounts must stay off her site. They photograph her car and let her leave. Her parents know only that she was out at a tire place. Nadia believes the scam is over and has told her friends in the chat.

## 28. Nineteen (Sept 2024 to Oct 2025, Chloe 19)

Chloe works as a translator at a company of fifty-odd people, takes college credits, and writes a blog on international disputes that she keeps from her office. She gives a sick coworker tablets she has taken since age seven, which she says cannot be bought anywhere. Emails ask whether she has a research team; she records five on a spreadsheet, none naming an outlet. She has begun explaining everything twice. In the chat Ruth asks statistical questions about ordinary people, then stops posting in April 2025. Chloe's second message to her goes unanswered. Chloe does not know who the emailers write for.

## 29. The File (Oct 2025, Chloe 20)

Theo, a federal analyst, is handed a box of old files by a retiring colleague. One folder is about Halstead and dates from 2013. Assessments now say probably corporate, and the funding cannot be traced. A memo describes a teacher placed on staff and removed within a day; Theo had assumed he quit. A report describes four intruders overpowered at the school eight years earlier, while four children argued about how to score them. Theo remembers that night as a drill Chloe took part in. He tells no one at work. In the chat he asks a hypothetical; Ruth replies "bad", her first post in six months.

## 30. Cleared (Sept 2025 to Aug 2026, Chloe 20 to 21)

Chloe sits the Foreign Service written exam and studies Hebrew. She keeps a sheet of unanswered emails asking who works with her. Background investigator Whitaker interviews her twice in her kitchen. She describes Halstead, her languages, the bar exam and two published papers. She withholds the subject of an internal third paper, as the lead researcher asks, on condition that her name goes on the next agreement. She guesses that the longest reference interview was Bex's. She does not know who the school's donor is. Her clearance arrives in December and she starts a part-time student trainee job in January. She turns twenty-one in August.

## 31. Ruth (Oct 2023 to Nov 2025, Chloe 18 to 20)

At MIT, Ruth believes she was put on a remedial track, and later that the university secretly sorts students by ability. A professor makes her write out skipped steps and says it took five passes, where a Halstead peer needs two. She concludes that Halstead chose children unlike the general population. She posts national statistics into the chat, where they are joked away, and in April 2025 she stops posting. In the final chat scene she says she has carried this for a year and asks who picked her at six. Chloe says nobody knows what the form asked. Her parents know none of this.

## 32. The Money (Nov 2025 to Feb 2026, Chloe 20)

Eli builds a slow covert program that watches financial records, and Kavi builds a monitor for it. They test it on Nadia's shop books, and it goes live by early December. Over the following months it traces a pattern of decisions spanning about twenty years. Ruth argues that one person is behind it, with 99.99 percent confidence, and the group accepts this. Nobody has his name. Theo says the government has kept a file on the school since 2013 and that four students once overpowered intruders; Chloe says it was a drill. Theo puts off naming the four. Eli ends the discussion: "so lets go and read it."

## 33. The Other One (May to Jun 2026, Chloe 20)

Eli proposes a second project to read the government's file. Theo refuses at first, then concludes reading the file is the only way to learn what the government knows. Ruth lays out the legal exposure and asks for the camera count Eli has not given. Chloe writes the rules: limits, a definition of "noticed", a one-line stop clause any single member can invoke, and a what-if-noticed page. Ruth drops her demand for a reason in that clause. The five-week test runs, and all seven sign, Theo last. Open: Eli's camera count, Theo's question about non-signers, and a quiet gap the plan misses.

## 34. The Files (Jun 2026, Chloe 20)

Seven members open the government file, each alone. It covers all ninety-one graduates, begun in 2013, and lists each person's address, family and banks. Family contact is recorded as a single letter. Every file says graduates are believed to be in contact by an unknown method, and collection has always failed. An incident report describes a diner four years earlier, where seven men, three armed, tried to rob four students; one student handled all seven in twenty-two seconds, and the footage is missing. Chloe writes "thats us." Theo's file holds a name he already knows and will not share. He must be back at work by eight.

## 35. Nine Minutes (Jun to Jul 2026, Chloe 20)

Eli's financial program misses its regular check-in for nine minutes on a Thursday in June, and its logs show nothing. It happens again, exactly nine minutes, and a third time for longer. Eli believes the logs do not meet the group's definition of noticed and that someone made a mistake. Chloe believes the gap is a message that he is there. Ruth proposes a risky idea and Eli rejects it. The group agrees to do nothing visible, and Kavi tightens his watcher. Nobody knows who is doing this or why. Chloe texts Ruth before lunch to ask whether the night held; no reply is shown.

## 36. Seventy-Five (Oct 2026, Chloe 21)

Priya returns to the chat in October after days of silence. Since March, people have tried to take her in several countries; on a Tuesday night, seventy-five came. She fought them off, lost full use of one hand, and now holds two captured men. The captives say they are American, from an agency, and were sent for her by name. The group has read the file about the school; Priya has not. Ruth calculates that seventy-five people spread out span 110 meters, and lists where the group sleeps. Priya ranks sixty-fourth of ninety-one and rejects Ruth's top-ten estimate. She is at the fence because her mare is due.
