# Chapter 19: Sixteen
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "April 2022 to June 2023". Bar exam in April (2022), results posted days later, practice papers May to September, retake in October (2022), Marek asks for papers in December (2022), then a final scene "Home in June for the summer". The year of that last scene is not stated; by sequence it follows December 2022, which would make it June 2023, but ch23 has the June 2023 graduation, so this is ambiguous.
Chloe's age: Not stated in the text. The title is "Sixteen"; ch20 (June 2022) has Sam described as sixteen. She turns 18 in August 2023 (ch23), so she is 16 for April 2022 and about 17 afterward.   POV: Close third on Chloe. A few lines of omniscient report (the drama teacher, the driving instructors' methods, the Priya/Nadia board). No head-hopping into other characters' thoughts.
Setting(s): Halstead's long examination room upstairs; corridor and corkboard; Amberg's office; library; drama classroom; driving range and skid pan; the forge; Chloe's dorm room; her parents' house (back steps, garage, reservoir road).

## What happens
Chloe, Sam, Kavi, Bex and the rest of the year sit the bar over two days in April, proctored by the teachers who wrote it. Afterward Sam compares answer lengths on question three (the "shed" contract question, risk while goods wait for collection). Sam wrote fourteen pages arguing both angles; Chloe wrote four, saying an answer plus its reason is all there is. Sam says he will "withdraw ten of my pages."

The results go up on a corkboard. Chloe fails by four points out of two hundred. Kavi passed by six and apologises for saying so. Of the ninety-one in the year, fifty-two pass and thirty-nine fail. Bex passed, announces loudly down the hall that it is "a fiftieth" and the closest miss on the board, and moves on laughing. Chloe says she can do the fraction herself. At night Chloe keeps running the fraction, looking for the sentence that would have stopped Bex.

Chloe takes the paper to Amberg. He shows that question three had four marks (the rule, where it comes from, why a shed is inside it, what happens if the shed burns before delivery). She had only the answer line and so only one tick. She argues the rest follows from the answer. Amberg says the marker cannot give marks for what is not written and that she wrote for a reader who already had her head. She asks him for a filled-in old paper to see what full marks look like.

Chloe is told off in drama class for "editing" an improvised scene; she says she is choosing. In May she writes a practice paper; Kavi marks it two out of four because her third line restates her second. He makes her write the burning shed as an actual event, and she gets the marks. May to September she writes a stack; Kavi and Nadia mark some (Nadia writes "said nothing" beside paragraphs), she marks some herself. Her stopping-early rate on them falls from about half in May and stays down by August. Bex marks Marek's in red in the margin.

Driving training: staged emergencies (ignition killed at sixty in October, brake failure in November, skid pan in January). Delacroix teaches "look where you want the car", and she takes the gap past the pole.

In October she retakes the bar and passes by twenty-two, finishing a forklift question in twenty-six minutes. In November and December she fills in the four blank rows of the April paper by hand. Marek (a year below) asks for her practice papers in December, returns them unread except for one reworked shed question with the fire on a Sunday and the goods already sold, which she works that evening. Pruitt at the forge tells her to take home the tongs she made.

In the last scene she is home for the summer, riding to the reservoir, and her mother asks if she wants something to do (a shelf in the garage). Chloe describes to her father how Sam stays on forty percent on the range, how Ruth (on ninety-one) comes down Saturdays with a clipboard, and the negotiation board with Nadia's result against Priya. She ends by asking if her parents ever wanted her to stay home and go to the state school. Her father takes his time; the chapter ends before he answers.

- Negotiation board in the hallway posts every pairing all year; Nadia's has stood since February with Priya's name beside it. Chloe believes Priya lost on purpose, letting the clock run out.
- Chloe's family: back steps rebuilt by her father the summer she was thirteen; a shelf in the garage not yet put up since March; reservoir road is a bike route.

## Who knows what
- Chloe believes the marker only gives marks for what is written; she now writes out all four marks.
- Chloe believes Priya threw the negotiation on purpose; she has not said so to anyone but her father. Priya "has not said so to anybody since."
- Chloe believes Bex handles numbers fast and loudly; she is stung that Bex said the fraction aloud.
- Sam knows he is stuck on 40 percent and is not bothered.
- Marek knows Chloe's papers; Bex marks his.
- Her parents do not know Chloe's feelings about staying close to home; they have not yet answered her question.
