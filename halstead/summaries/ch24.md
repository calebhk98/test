# Chapter 24: The Chat
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "September 2023". Scenes run from early September to the first week of October (the site carries 26,000 openings "by the first week of October"). Chloe's old laptop dies the week before classes; the pill thread is the last scene, undated beyond "the winter before" for the box.
Chloe's age: 18 (turned 18 in August 2023 per ch23; not stated here).   POV: Mostly omniscient and external, built from group chat transcripts, with short narrated scenes: Ruth's dorm, Theo, Nadia at the bank, Kavi, and Chloe alone at the end.
Setting(s): The group chat (mostly lowercase, no apostrophes); Eli's workplace; MIT dorm (Cambridge); Army processing station; Halstead lab and dining hall; Nadia's parents' shop and a bank branch; Georgetown (Chloe's lecture hall, an apartment an hour away); South America (Priya).

## What happens
The chat is five years old, with encryption the group wrote themselves. For the first time all of them are somewhere else: Eli at a new job, Ruth at MIT , Sam at Army processing, Kavi still at Halstead in a lab, Nadia in her parents' shop's back room, Theo in a federal job, Priya somewhere in the southern hemisphere, Chloe in a lecture hall.

In the chat Eli says his boss told him the company pays a million dollars for breaking into their systems; he finds two, then five in an afternoon. Days later it turns out there was no such program; his boss was joking; Eli gets a meeting with a lawyer. Ruth complains about a roommate who alphabetizes a spice rack. Sam calls them "tidally locked", Ruth objects. Nadia tries to set a get-together before December; nobody can: nobody can. Eli says so; Ruth says he said it like a scoreboard.

Priya describes a horse that carries cheese down from a high pasture without being led. Theo says his supervisor keeps sending work back to show more steps. Sam says there are trust falls; he caught the person. Kavi calls early praise a retention play, "not fake. not informative."

Chloe buys a laptop with 16 GB of memory when she meant 1 TB (she mixed memory and storage). Nadia says to buy from the school; Ruth says they only sell to current students or staff.

Nadia goes to a bank branch to remove a five-day hold on incoming payments. The manager wants twenty thousand held in the account; she shows she holds a few hundred under. He gives her two days on the nine named payers, six months before review, and waives the monthly fee for a year after twenty-two minutes. In the chat she reports: she interviewed 41 for two jobs in July (37 said what they built does not break; she told all of them in August the jobs were gone); 212 people on the site since June and six hires; the sign up page still has lorem ipsum. She plans to scrape every company careers page (about four thousand reachable) and add listings regardless; nine employers pay on hire. By early October the site has 26,000 openings. She has 3 months of money ($1,200 needed, plus two invoices of $600 on the fourth); she wants a four-cylinder from the yard on Ohio Street in March.

Kavi eats with the research staff and says there is a salary. Chloe asks the group what the small white sinus pill was; her doctor has not heard of it. Kavi asks Halstead for the formulation; they say it is "internal". Chloe finds the empty box, photographs the label, and sends it to Nadia alone; Nadia uses an old login to a machine on her old desk at school and brings back three names off a supplier's safety sheet. Chloe writes them on her hand.
