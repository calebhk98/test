# Chapter 15: Twelve
Dates (from the chapter header and text): September 2017 to January 2018. Forge from first week of September; Marek meeting late November; bread test on a Tuesday (date not given, before Thanksgiving); four a.m. Watch (date not given, before Thanksgiving); Thanksgiving 2017; closing line "By Christmas".
Chloe's age: 12 (stated by her at the Watch and by her mother at Thanksgiving). POV: close third on Chloe, with small drift into summary of what others think (Kavi and Ruth in the bread test, Marek's talk).
Setting(s): the forge (past the kitchens); archery field; Arabic doorway; Ruth's room; school kitchen and stock room; Building One corridor (4 a.m. Watch); corridor with grading sheet; Thanksgiving at home.

## What happens
Chloe takes up the forge. Pruitt runs it; for a month the men leave her alone. In October Pruitt says "Your heat's wrong." She improves. She makes hooks; Sam says one is good, Chloe says the scroll has a flaw, two curves welded to pose as one, and Sam cannot see it. Late in November Marek stops her on the stairs and explains a line he found wrong in a book; he has the pages in his head. In December Pruitt says "You lost the curve on the second turn"; she puts the sixth hook back in the coals.

Bell takes away the metronome. One partner now calls the count aloud. Chloe and Odile take a week to settle it; Odile's count is steadier but stretches late in the day. Odile calls for November and hates it; afterwards they alternate. The hit rate dips then recovers.

Chloe has grown: her cuffs stop reaching her wrists. Stuck beside Ruth in the Arabic doorway she finds she is taller. Ruth warns her not to say anything.

On a Tuesday in Ruth's room, they debate whether Wednesday bread is better. Ruth says it is suggestion. They design a blind test with swapped cutters; the kitchen at midnight, Kavi insists on the big oven, then measures its heating curve instead of the bread. Mrs. Adeyemi stops them: "Go to bed." The first round already eaten gave 5 of 8 right (Sam says 62.5 percent beats guessing; Ruth says that is a coin).

Stock count Saturday: tomato tins; Ruth finds a faster method (counting cases in stacks of three, chalk marks) cutting a shelf from 35 to 23 minutes.

Four a.m. Watch (unannounced, nothing on the board): four intruders at the loading side. Kavi breaks their comms (AES-256, weak key derivation). Sam's plan is argued by Ruth. Chloe volunteers as bait: "I'm twelve, I'm small, and I'm a girl in pajamas." They choose the fast (traceable) drug despite Ruth's protest. Chloe is grabbed; Kavi and Ruth take down three, Sam bungles one. They tie them up; Ruth monitors breathing. Intruders are poorly equipped (wrong bolt cutters, no ID). Questioned: server room. Sinclair arrives; grading in the morning.

Grades: Chloe C, Sam C, Ruth B minus, Kavi B. Sheet notes intruders inside Building One before contact; response 7:50 vs standard 6:00; traceable agent; four personnel where three would do. Chloe notes the sensors are not credited; posts a note to the duty officer.

Thanksgiving: mother upset about a gas oven lit with children alone; Chloe says she is signed off on ovens; mother asks if she wants out of the school; Chloe says no. Closing line: she can hold 24 seconds in the 10v1 and sits thirtieth out of ninety.
