# Chapter 08: The Asking
Dates (from the chapter header and text): Header "October 2012 - January 2013". Ms. Vance's call on a Friday in October; appointment the following Wednesday; Mrs. Prahl program begins November (exception granted second week of November); Christmas morning; last week of January.
Chloe's age: 7   POV: Close third on Chloe throughout.
Setting(s): Chloe's house (stairs, kitchen, bedroom, front room); Dr. Ammons's office and parking lot; Mrs. Prahl's Thursday enrichment room at Chloe's school; her grandmother's house.

## What happens
After Chloe's crying spell, Ms. Vance phones the house. Chloe's mom says Chloe has been like this since September but was fine in July. The next Wednesday her mom takes the afternoon off and brings Chloe to Dr. Ammons. Her mom lists symptoms: not eating, oversleeping, tired, stopped reading (a stack of books untouched since August), sits in the dark. She says Chloe was the happiest she had ever been in July at a month-long overnight program half a day away, and mentions Chloe has asked to return daily, including that day in the parking lot. Chloe answers little. Asked what she liked, she thinks of the bridge, the eighths on paper, Sam's "room-shaped object" and Mr. Baptiste redoing the rectangle for Marisol, and says only "It was hard."

In the car, her mom says Dr. Ammons thinks Chloe is depressed, that it has a name, and wants to see her again in a few weeks. Chloe asks if she can go to Halstead; her mom puts her forehead on the wheel.

Her mom fights the school in October. Chloe is offered a Thursday program with Mrs. Prahl ("enrichment"); in the second week of November the school says an exception lets her in a year early. Mrs. Prahl's group has nine kids and a cart labeled THINKING CORNER. Chloe solves a knights-and-knaves sheet instantly; a boy named Dev takes ninety seconds. She asks for a thirteenth problem and for when it gets harder. Mrs. Prahl says there is no rush.

Chloe asks her parents daily whether it is time to call. Her mom says "still thinking" or "not now"; her dad says it is not only up to him. On a Friday over a jacket, Chloe screams that her mom wants her sad and hates her, and slams her door. Her mom answers that she has lain awake every night since the letter came looking for a good enough reason to say no.

Pairs on the third Thursday: Chloe and Hana. On the fourth Thursday she takes a packet home, finishes it, hides it, and tells Mrs. Prahl she is still working on it. She cannot say aloud that the program feels wrong.

In December her mom asks what was different at camp. Chloe says, "Nobody was mean." Her mom asks if she is being bullied; Chloe says no, Bryce just thinks she talks weird and sounds like a teacher. Her mom says she will make friends.

Chloe's dad gives her a bike on Christmas morning (she knew it was in the garage). At her grandmother's she talks little to her cousin Dana. She stops asking about Halstead in the last week of January. Her mom tells her dad the Thursdays seem to be helping, and her dad says "yeah, all right" to something unheard. The camp letter is in her sock drawer; she takes it out sometimes.

## Who knows what
- Dr. Ammons knows the camp was a month away and that Chloe was happy there; she does not know Chloe's feelings about it beyond "hard".
- Chloe believes her mom is the one blocking Halstead and that her dad would say yes; in January she hears her dad say "yeah, all right" to something she did not catch.
- Her mom does not accept Chloe's explanation that nothing is wrong at school beyond Bryce.
- Mrs. Prahl believes Chloe is "still working on" the packet; she has already finished it.
- Chloe knows Dana does not ask more.
