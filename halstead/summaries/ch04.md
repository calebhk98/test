# Chapter 04: Pluto
Dates (from the chapter header and text): July 2012 (header). Day 1 of the session, then days 2 to about 7, and "the second Tuesday." Her mom leaves on a Sunday (the first Sunday, after a few more days).
Chloe's age: 6 (she tells Sam "Six")   POV: close third person, present tense, Chloe's head. Some scenes run past what she could see (Owen copying the board while she watches Sam; Priya's reaction); no clear drift.
Setting(s): Halstead campus: front hallway and registration table, orientation room, Chloe's dorm room (shared with Priya), the phone by the front desk, the parent hotel half a mile away, the hall of planet-named rooms (Pluto is at the end past the water fountain), dining hall, the Pluto classroom.

## What happens
Day one is noisy and awful. A hundred-odd children and their parents fill the hallway. Chloe and her dad register at the table lettered by surname (H through M for Kessler) and get a folder, a lanyard, and a dining card. At orientation a woman with a clipboard covers meals, fruit bowls, green laundry bags (out Tuesday and Friday nights), the nurse's red-door office on the first floor staffed at all hours, and lights-out earlier for the sixes than the sevens, so Chloe is in the first group until August. She promises anyone can go home the same day with no argument, and a knock on the door with the light at the end of each hall at night. Chloe's mom writes "same day" and underlines it twice on the back of the map.

Chloe's dorm roommate is Priya, who talks about horses (ponies are under fourteen two hands; draft horses are built to pull) without pausing. Chloe lies on top of the covers in her shoes. That evening she phones her mom from the desk phone ("I want to come there"); mom arrives at the front desk in ten minutes and Chloe sleeps in the hotel with a bathroom light on. Mom talks about her drive and her work to put her to sleep. In the morning she says Chloe can do that every night.

The rooms are named for planets; Chloe's classroom, at the end, is PLUTO. Chloe says "They put us in the one that isn't a planet anymore," and has a physical panic reaction back to her October classroom memory. A boy (Sam) laughs and calls it "a room-shaped object"; Chloe laughs. A girl (Ruth) corrects: Pluto is a dwarf planet, a kind of planet. Sam sits next to Chloe and asks her age: he is "seven and a half." Chloe sleeps at the hotel again that night and tells her mom about Pluto.

At dinner on the third night Sam asks what school she goes to; Chloe says she keeps quiet because a girl says she talks weird. Sam says his has a pool with a lid. Ruth agrees loudly. On the fourth night Chloe dials all but the last digit and stops; she tells Priya she'll call tomorrow after dinner. Her mom stays a few more days, then leaves on Sunday, repeating the instructions to call at any hour.

A quiet fourth boy in Pluto speaks on the seventh day, correcting Sam on volcanoes: gas and viscosity, not a hollow mountain (the boy is Kavi). Sam loses the exchange and draws his own volcano. On the second Tuesday, Mr. Doyle's board error (weight versus mass) is corrected by Ruth, who says she said it Friday. Chloe fears punishment for Ruth but none comes. Ruth says at her old school interrupting got her in trouble. Chloe sits on her hands for the next classes. Sam says he regrets sitting next to Kavi.

## Characters present

- Mom: stays at the hotel several days; writes down the go-home rule.

- Sam: boy in Pluto, seven and a half, jokes, says "and a half" every time; his school has a covered pool.

- The registration woman and the orientation woman (unnamed).

- Everyone in Pluto saw Kavi's volcano correction and Ruth's mass correction.
