# Voice pass, chapters 11-12

Scope: `chapters/11_eight.md` and `chapters/12_nine.md` only. Dialogue and
chat lines were not touched anywhere, per the brief. No instance of the
highest-priority fault (a person in the subject of a passive verb, e.g.
"Chloe is told") was found in either chapter.

## Changed

### chapters/11_eight.md

**Before:** "A novel about a lighthouse takes her a week, and she understands
most of it, but inside a month Ruth is doing it in Portuguese, badly, for
reasons Ruth will state only as \"it's the vowels.\""

**After:** "She spends a week on a novel about a lighthouse and understands
most of it, but inside a month Ruth is doing it in Portuguese, badly, for
reasons Ruth will state only as \"it's the vowels.\""

**Why:** Fake agency. The novel is inanimate; it does not "take" anything.
Chloe is the one doing the reading and she belongs in the subject position.
The rewrite keeps the same "and" count and sentence length.

---

**Before:** "The bracket rule survives into the summer and reaches a
stairwell, a laundry chute, and one of the goals on the field."

**After:** "They carry the bracket rule into the summer, into a stairwell, a
laundry chute, and one of the goals on the field."

**Why:** Fake agency of the book's named type: an abstract rule cannot
"survive" or "reach" a location on its own. The kids keep applying it in new
places; "they" is the real doer and goes in the subject. This also drops the
sentence from two "and"s to one, which helps rather than hurts the
near-limit conjunction count.

### chapters/12_nine.md

**Before:** "There is a wall up the side of the gym with a lip near the top
that sticks out a good way, and going into September the lip has beaten
everyone who's tried it."

**After:** "There is a wall up the side of the gym with a lip near the top
that sticks out a good way, and going into September nobody has gotten over
it."

**Why:** Fake agency: the lip is a physical feature, not an opponent that
"beats" climbers. "Nobody" restores the people (the climbers) as the real
subject of the failure, and the sentence still sets up the payoff later in
the same paragraph, where a girl in the year below does get over it.

## Called out, not changed

### chapters/12_nine.md, line 21

**Current:** "I went back the next day with a letter from the head asking
them to look again; they looked again, and the answer came back no different
than the first time."

**Why the voice is wrong:** "the answer came back" is fake agency: an answer
does not travel or arrive on its own, a person delivered it.

**Why I did not change it:** This is Kavi's own dialogue, and the brief
excludes dialogue and chat lines from this pass regardless of voice. Flagging
per the brief's own instruction to say so and agree rather than touch it.
