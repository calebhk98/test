# Open contradictions found during the passivity pass

Confirmed against the chapters, not yet fixed. Each one names both sides and
says why it is being held.

## Owen, Thursday, two places

`chapters/05_behind.md:173`, Ruth, to her tray:

> "He was crying in the stairwell, on Thursday, and I'm not being mean by
> saying it, that's what he was doing."

`chapters/10_april.md:125`, Kavi, shoes off, holding them by the heels:

> "He was crying by the kitchens on the Thursday and I saw him, but I didn't
> say anything, because I was seven and I didn't know what you say to that."

Same boy, same named Thursday, two places, and two witnesses each staking a
claim on having seen it themselves. Kavi's whole line is built on the
first-hand seeing: "I am going off something I saw with my own eyes."

Held because chapter 10 is inside a span still being edited. The fix belongs
in one of the two lines, chosen once both chapters are stable, and it is a
choice rather than a typo: either the two are the same sighting and one of
them moves, or Owen cried twice that week and one of the two stops claiming to
be the sighting.

Found by the chapters 1-6 agent, which reported it at 05_behind.md:163. The
line is 173. The finding was right and the citation was not, which is the
reason to search for the text rather than open the line.
