## Owen, Thursday, two places — RESOLVED

`chapters/05_behind.md:173`, Ruth, to her tray, at camp:

> "He was crying in the stairwell, on Thursday, and I'm not being mean by
> saying it, that's what he was doing."

`chapters/10_april.md:125`, Kavi, on the stairs, remembering the same camp:

> "He was crying by the kitchens on the Thursday and I saw him, but I didn't
> say anything, because I was seven and I didn't know what you say to that."

Same boy, same named Thursday, same going-home on the Saturday, and two
witnesses each staking a claim on having seen it themselves. Kavi's whole line
rests on the first-hand seeing: "I am going off something I saw with my own
eyes."

Kavi's kitchens moved to the stairwell. Ruth's scene says stairwell twice and
is built on having walked past without going in, so it is the load-bearing
one; Kavi's was a single clause. The two of them now saw the same thing and
neither went in, which is quieter than a conflict and rhymes with Ruth's
answer when Chloe asks her why not: "It's worse. They know you saw." Kavi is
standing in a stairwell while he says it.

Found by the chapters 1-6 agent, which reported it at 05_behind.md:163. The
line is 173. The finding was right and the citation was not, which is the
reason to search for the text rather than open the line.
