was narration from what was dialogue.

## Sentences opening "She/He + verb"

**83 found, 70 changed, 13 kept.** The heaviest single piece of work in the
span, and almost all of it in narration. Chapter 31 carried 37 of the 83 in
2,450 words: a Ruth-POV summary chapter where nearly every sentence started
with her.

The rule followed was the brief's: vary the opening, not the sentence. The
replacements were deliberately spread across five different moves so the fix
would not become its own tic.

Fronted object: *"She keeps the reading two weeks ahead"* to *"The reading
stays two weeks ahead."* *"She keeps a private tally instead of a public one"*
to *"The tally she keeps is private instead of public."* *"He opens three logs
instead of one"* to *"Three logs come open instead of one."*

Interpolated attribution: *"She says the placement stands, that a petition is
the only route"* to *"The placement stands, she says, and a petition is the
only route."* *"She tells herself the sequence will catch up to her"* to
*"The sequence will catch up to her eventually, she tells herself."*

Prepositional or temporal opening: *"She sits in the third row, out of habit
rather than ambition"* to *"Out of habit rather than ambition she sits in the
third row."* *"He gets there eventually"* to *"Eventually he gets there."*
*"He loses the thread of it"* to *"Somewhere in it he loses the thread."*
*"She closes the laptop a few minutes before the library asks everyone to
leave"* to *"A few minutes before the library asks everyone to leave she
closes the laptop."*

Dialogue: *"She says no, and it's close enough to true"* to *"No, she says,
and it's close enough to true."*

Broken into short sentences, which also lifted chapter 31's sentence-length
variation from 54.8 to 56.9 and brought its sentences-per-paragraph inside
band: *"She goes to class, turns problem sets in early, eats dinner in the
same dining hall at the same time most nights"* became *"Class happens.
Problem sets go in early. Dinner is at seven, in the hall nearest her
building, most nights."* And *"She reads every message and answers barely any
of them"* became *"Every message gets read; barely any get answered."*

**Kept (13).** Two in chapter 31 that are load-bearing as rhythm: *"She holds
that for most of a year"* (the section's opening beat) and *"She slows down"*
(the two-word sentence the office-hours scene turns on). *"He fixes it inside
the hour"* in 32, which is the answer to Nadia's "Fix the clock." *"He writes
that down too"* and *"He goes back to work"* in 35, both deliberate flat
closers. *"She comes back at four in the morning, whichever four in the
morning she is standing in"* in 36. And chapter 35's closing sentence, which
is outside this pass's remit and was not touched.

## The word "same"

**77 found, 62 changed, 15 kept.** Mostly deletable with no replacement, as
the brief says: *"the same folder"* to *"one folder"*, *"the same notebook"*
to *"the notebook"*, *"the same lab he's had"* to *"the lab he's had"*,
*"the same two blocks"* to *"the two blocks"*, *"the same doubled caution"* to
*"the doubled caution"*.

Where deletion would have cost the meaning, the sentence was restructured:
*"each check reads the same as the one before it"* to *"each check reads like
the one before it"*; *"correcting the same three cards"* to *"correcting three
cards over and over"*; *"each one ends the same way"* to *"each one ends where
the last one ended"*; *"in the same breath"* to *"in the next breath"*.

Best of them: chapter 35's *"Nine minutes later, everything is precisely where
it should be. Same state, same position, exactly as it was."* became *"State,
position, byte for byte what it was."* Two instances gone and the line is
harder, in Eli's own register.

**Kept (15).** All the ones where identity is the actual claim: *"Every page
carries the same header"*, *"Ninety-one of them start the same way"*, *"like
watching the same paragraph get retyped once a year"*, *"always the same
single word: continuity"*, *"All seven had sat the same examination"*, *"the
same breakfast every morning"*, *"the same number twice says measured"*. Plus
five in chat, which is a different register and where the word is how these
people actually talk: *"i dont think we're the same kind of thing"* (Ruth's
line at the climax of 31), *"thats not the same, eli"* / *"its exactly the
same"* (the pair that is the whole argument), *"same as last time"*, *"lands
in the same seven hour band"*, *"two instruments that do the same job"*,
*"stopped using the same door twice"*.

Chapter 36's closing anaphora went from five to two: *"the same job it was in
June, in the same building, with the same badge and the same lift and the same
eleven o'clock lull"* is now *"the job it was in June, in the same building,
with the badge and the lift and the lull."* The rhythm survives on "the".

## "The way you/she would"

**25 found, 23 changed, 2 kept.** The rule was the brief's: keep
characterisation, cut texture. Texture went by restructuring rather than by
swapping the connective:

- *"He sends it the way he sends anything he's actually thought through: not a
  chat message but a document"* to *"What arrives is a document rather than a
  chat message."*
- *"Eli spends the five weeks the way he spends any five weeks with a real
  problem... Kavi spends them the way he spends any five weeks like that"* to
  *"Eli spends the five weeks badly slept, mostly cheerful... Kavi spends them
  in his own chair."* Two instances of one formula in adjacent sentences was
  the clearest case in the span.
- *"Chloe reads that line the way she'd read a translation problem"* to
  *"Chloe reads that line as a translation problem."*
- *"She pushes her sleeves up before she answers, the way she does before
  anything that's going to cost her something"* to *"Her sleeves go up before
  she answers. They go up before anything that's going to cost her
  something."* The comparison becomes two sentences that enact it.

**Kept (2).** Ruth: *"states it the way she states everything, conclusion
first."* Eli: *"He reads the clause the way he'd read a spec he was trying to
break."* Both are the character's method rather than the narrator's simile.
Nadia's *"the way she checks a supplier's invoice"* was kept as content but
lost the formula: it is now an appositive inside a fronted participle,
*"Standing at the counter with a pen in her free hand, she checks his daily
summary line by line, a supplier's invoice like any other."*

## Number: four

**22 found, 5 changed by substitution, 17 kept.** Under target without
touching anything canonical. Roots moved rather than instances patched:
the polling interval rewritten *four times / the fifth version* became
*six times / the seventh version*; the shop's *four hundred* transactions
became *three hundred*; *four weeks* of testing became *the month* and *those
weeks*, which the paragraph's opening "for a month" already licensed;
*four minutes outside its usual band* became *six*; the report's *Four pages*
became *Five pages*; *four things to change / three of which* became *six / five*.

The professor's *"it took her four tries... would have needed two tries at
what took her four"* went to *five*, in both places. The contrast is the point,
not the figure, and five against two is a slightly harder contrast.

Where the count is the fact and the word was doing nothing, the word went and
the count stayed: *"one of the four did all of it"* is now *"one of the
students did all of it, and that the other three were not where they had
been"*; *"what the four looked like"* is *"what the students looked like"*;
*"can't picture four faces"* is *"can't picture the faces"*; *"where the four
students appear"* is *"wherever the students appear"*. Theo's *"four blocks
from the federal building"* became *"a few blocks"*, which is the brief's own
recommended move.

**Kept.** Everything canonical: *four in the morning* and *four of us put them
on the floor* and *four operators* (the fence incident, matching chapter 29);
*four years back* and *four years later* (the car park); *four in the
morning, whichever four in the morning she is standing in* (chapter 36, and
the repetition is the joke); *sixty-fourth of ninety-one* and *bottom third
four years running*; *four months into that job*; *four months on*; the
chapter-34 title itself.

## Number: nine

Incidental nines removed: *from when she was nine* to *eight*; *back at his
employer's desk by nine* to *eight*; *For nine days he opens it* deleted in
favour of *Day after day*; *page nine* used as a referent three separate times

*eight*; *the second sentence about what the file says on page nine* to
*about what is in the file*; kavi's *"look for nine minutes"* doubled inside

Two root numbers moved, which is where most of the reduction came from:
**Theo's refusal went from nine days to ten days** (and *the ninth night* to

pages**. Both were single-source clusters: the document's length is now stated
once, in chapter 33, and nothing anywhere calls it "the nine pages" any more.
Chapter 35's internal references are *page five* and *page seven*, both still
inside eight, and chapter 36's page-nine reference is gone. Note for the
author: both figures appear in `passes/review/Some_Spark_notes.md` and the
nine days is named in `passes/expand/33.md`. Easy to veto; the arithmetic is
consistent either way and reverting costs two instances.

**The six kept are the chapter title "Nine Minutes", the four narration and

months into the job" in chapter 36, which is another agent's deliberate month
fix (commit aaf911c) and not mine to undo.** So there are no incidental nines
left in the span at all. The span sits at 47.1 against a target of 45, which
is 0.3 of an instance, and every remaining one is load-bearing or somebody
else's correction. Stopping there rather than eroding the plot figure.

## The gestures: hands flat, eyes, turning an object

**hand(s) flat: 2 found, 2 changed, 0 kept.** Both were Kavi at the lab bench
in chapter 34, in consecutive sentences, and the brief says give the beat to a
different part of the body. *"both hands flat on the surface in front of him
the way he'd brace against something before lifting it. He leaves it there and
waits, both hands still flat on the bench"* became *"his weight forward on
both arms like a man setting himself to lift something. He stays over the
bench and waits, breathing slow."* That one edit removed two hands-flat, two
"flat", and one "the way".

**eyes on: 2 found, 2 changed.** *"Kavi keeps his eyes on the board he's
rewiring"* to *"Kavi stays bent over the board he's rewiring"*. Chloe's *"She
closes her eyes on the version of tonight that ends here"* became *"Her eyes
close on..."*, which was a She/He opener as well.

**turning an object: 1 counted, and a wider cluster.** Kavi turns something
over six separate times across chapters 32-35: a data cable, whatever is on
the table, a pen three times, a pen again. It is his gesture and nobody else's
in this span, so it stays a gesture rather than a tic, but six was too many.
Two went: *"Kavi picks the pen back up, turns it over once, and sets it down
at a slightly different angle"* to *"Kavi picks the pen back up and sets it
down at a slightly different angle"*, and *"Kavi turns the pen on his desk
over twice and sets it back down exactly on its mark"* to *"Kavi squares the
pen on his desk against its mark and leaves it there"*. The other four kept.

**"flat": 10 found, 8 changed.** *out loud and flatly* to *out loud and with
no ceremony about it*; *a fact, stated flat* to *a fact, stated bare*; *Three
words, flat, done* to *Three words, done*; *the same flat pace* to *the level
pace*; *sets the phone flat on the wood, screen up* to *sets the phone down on
the wood, screen up*; *both hands, flat* in Theo's laptop-closing to *with both
hands*. **Kept:** the file's *flat voice* (which matches chapter 29's phrasing
for the same documents) and the *flat instruction* on page nine, both of which
are the word for the thing.

## The smaller ones

**"the whole of it" / "the rest of it": 4 found, 3 changed, 1 kept.** *twice a
week that's the whole of it* to *twice a week that one line is the entire
report*; *He stays on his feet for the rest of it* to *through the rest of the
file*; *reads the whole of it back at the stop* to *reads it back to herself at
the stop*. Kept chapter 36's *"she can see the whole of it at once"*, which is
the payoff of the hundred-and-ten-metres calculation rather than a closer.

**"about N": 5 found, 3 changed.** *about four seconds to say* to *three
seconds*, with the callback four lines later changed to match; *She holds that
for about ten months* to *for most of a year*, which is the brief's exact
prescription; *should be about four sams* to *should be worth three sams*.
Kept Ruth's *"it moves about forty minutes total, gradually, over two
decades"* and Priya's *"which was about thirty in"*, both chat, both places
where the hedge is the speaker being careful about a figure they are arguing
from.

**Announced withholding: 0 counted, 3 uncounted, 1 removed.** The regex misses
the three in this span because they take an object other than "it". The one
that was pure announcement went: chapter 34's *"He keeps what the note says to
himself, both then and later, and the rest of them let it stand there
unexplained"* is deleted, and the paragraph now runs straight from Theo going
outside to Theo coming back and reading on. The withholding is enacted instead
of narrated and nothing is lost, because the note is never explained anywhere.
The other two stay: Ruth *"keeps that quiet"* in chapter 31 is the content of
the theory she writes down, not a narrator's note about her silence, and
Ruth's *"keeps the answer to herself"* in chapter 36 is followed immediately by
the arithmetic she is keeping, so the reader gets it.

**"That's not X, that's Y": 0 counted.** Ruth has two in chapter 31 that the
regex misses because she puts a name in the slot: *"That's not what the policy
says, Dad"* and *"That's not wrong because the idea's bad, Devansh."* Both
kept. `passes/expand/33.md` records the naming-inside-a-correction as a
required characterisation fix for Ruth, and the brief allows the construction
to stay for one character. Ruth is that character in this span.

## Judgement calls on the chat

Chapters 31, 32, 34 and 36 are substantially transcript. Chat lines were
treated as a separate register and touched only where the repetition was
mechanical rather than characterising. Kept untouched: Ruth's three-part
argument in 32 (the *same seven hour band*, the *same job* two instruments do,
*about forty minutes total*), the *thats not the same / its exactly the same*
exchange in 33, Nadia's *four operators once* echoing Kavi's line before it,
the whole of Priya's account in 36, and Ruth's *i dont think we're the same
kind of thing*, which is the sentence the book has been walking toward.

Four chat numbers moved, all incidental: *nine sentences* to six, *about four
sams* to three, *four days* of sitting still to three (narration, not chat),
and kavi's doubled *nine minutes* to a single one plus a pronoun.
