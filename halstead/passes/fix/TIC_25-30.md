rotated so that no single replacement shape repeats: prepositional and temporal
fronting ("On the Friday he puts the question to Ruiz"; "In the ninth week he

she said it"; "With him come a federal investigator's credentials"), and naming
the character where the pronoun was actually ambiguous ("Ruth comes back to it";
"Theo was eleven"; "Mid-morning his supervisor stops at the desk").

Best of them, in the sense that the sentence got better rather than merely
