## Chapter 24, the chat transcript

Roughly a third of the span's hits sit inside chat lines, which are a different register. The
rule I worked to: a repeated word in a message is characterisation until it is repeated by two
different people, and a number in Nadia's mouth is almost always characterisation, because
counting is what she does.

Left alone on that basis: *kavi: the same. theyre not that different from us*, *eli: thats not
the same as allowed*, *kavi: ...same energy*, *sam: i can put in for the same five days theo
has*, *its in the same chapter as the three percent thing*, *theres a four cylinder sitting at
the yard*, *i asked sixty one to get nine*, *the list has never got past nine* with Kavi's *and
the nine* landing on it, *four thousand of those in reach*, and *nineteen thousand openings,
nine of which have anybody behind them*. Those last are the load-bearing arithmetic of Nadia's
origin and cutting them to hit a number would have cost the chapter its point.

Changed inside chat only where the repetition was mine to lose rather than the speaker's:
*forty one* to *all of them* on the second mention, *thirty nine* to *thirty seven*, *nine more*
to *as many again*, *four days leave* to *five*, *four applications* to *three*, *item forty* to
*item thirty*, *eleven hundred dollars* to *twelve hundred*. No voice was flattened to a house
register and no line lost a speaker's rhythm.

The narration between the transcript blocks was treated exactly like the rest of the book.
