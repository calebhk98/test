reader is never actually let into the room for the one scene explicitly built to hold it.

**Fix, not just a flag**: this is the one place in my five chapters where I went past a
reason-clause edit into converting narrated summary to real quoted dialogue, because the

don't fix" rule is for summarized dialogue *elsewhere* in a scene, not for the scene the

here the salt/pepper/knife triangle demo and the hand-position CPR demo are exactly that
physical-argument move). I left the rest of the topic list (signing, the puzzle, wood
shop, cooking, the museum, computers, chess, karate, nerf guns) as the rapid narrated
montage — converting every single one to a full quote would turn one paragraph into a
two-page monologue, which is the "speeches" failure mode the brief also warns against;
the montage reads correctly as the breathless remainder of a longer performance we've now
actually heard the start of, rather than a full transcript.

Also lengthened several of the hallway-scene bicker exchanges after the recitation
(original lines 61-100): Sam's "Obviously" got its reason, Ruth's "That's not what she
said it was" got the specific claim she's making, Kavi's "I don't know that either" got
