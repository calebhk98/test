## What I did about the chat

Chapters 31, 32, 34 and 36 are substantially transcript, and 28 has a long chat
block. I did not exempt any of it, and I did not gut it either. The rule I used:
**a figure a character is arguing from stays; a figure a character is merely
reciting goes.**

**Kept intact.** Ruth's survey figures and the arithmetic around them in 28 and
31 (*21%*, *about a third*, *the number barely moves*, *21 out of what
denominator*); the whole of her three-part proof in 32 (*three to five days*,
*the same seven hour band*, *about forty minutes total*, *two instruments*, *its
one person*, *99.99*); her legal citation in 33 (*section 1030*, *five years
first offense*, *ten if theres a financial angle*); Nadia's *then two of you
carry it if it goes wrong, not seven*, her *they caught four operators once*,
and her *and four years later the federal government wrote "no known method"*;
Theo's *nineteen years* / *eleven* / *eight years ago* disclosure sequence, which
is a man reading a file out loud; Sam's *thats not a number* and his *five
weeks. ok*, where the number is the condition of his signature; the whole of
Priya's account of the field, including *seventy-five*, *about thirty in*, *the
third go*, *thirteen year olds*, *a good fifteen*, *sixty-fourth out of
ninety-one*; Ruth's *top ten percent* and Priya's *top sixty*.

**Cut in the chat, all of it recitation rather than argument.** *and about 20%
speak one at home* became *another language*; *five is still insulting* and
*five is generous* lost their figure and kept the joke, because Eli's *fine,
five* two lines above already carries it; *six sentences out of the pair of
them* became *almost nothing*, since it was a substituted figure to begin with
(see below) and Priya's point is that she has got nothing; *should be worth
three sams* became *worth a sam and then some*, same reason; *i go away for
fifteen years* became *a very long time*, which is Eli's bravado rather than a
figure; *i took two off the grass on the way past* became *i took them off*,
because she has already said *the two i took off the grass* in the line above;
*i built that box in about a weekend when i was thirteen* dropped the age
because Kavi immediately makes her say it again, and it lands harder the second
time as the only place it appears.

The narration around the chat took the heavier cuts on purpose. Eight people
quantifying is a habit; a narrator counting the quantifiers is the same habit
twice.

## Constraints

- House Rule 1: no sentence in this pass explains a beat. Where a chapter fell
  under the 2,000-word floor after cutting (33, 35 and 36 all did), the words
  came back by re-expanding phrasings I had compressed, not by adding
  explanation or new beats: *He walks to work in the morning and walks home
  again after dark*; *typed and printed and read back through*; *rewrites, and
  then rewrites what she rewrote*; *deletes all of it, and sends a single line
  instead*; *the better part of a month over a mare that would not foal*.
- The other fourteen constructions were not disturbed: *same* is net -1 across
  the span, *flat*, *the way you would*, *eyes on*, *hands flat*, *turning an
  object*, *the whole of it*, *announced withholding* and *that's not X* are all
  unchanged in count. *about N* fell from 5 to 2 as a side effect (*about six
  hours a week*, *about fourteen months*, *about four seconds*).
- Reading grade: every chapter is unchanged or within 0.1 of where it started,
  and all nine clear the band-23-36 floor of 8.0. 28 and 30 are the tight ones
  at 8.1, both exactly where they were before the pass.
- Negative-space stayed under 5% in all nine. One rewrite in 35 pushed it to 5.1
  on a *without doing anything* and was changed to *before he does anything*.
- Word counts: 3112 / 2084 / 2075 / 2433 / 2183 / 2012 / 2013 / 2020 / 2018. All
  inside 2,000-4,000.
- `check_edits.py --chapters 28..36`: 0 problems. No hard breaks, no em dashes,
  no curly quotes.
