# Sheet expansion pass — emotion, type, number, anger

**Scope.** All 33 character sheets in `characters/` except `BEX.md`, `IYAD.md` and
`MAREK.md`, which another agent owns. One new sheet was created: `DANA.md`.

## What this pass corrects

An earlier pass merged `passes/review/Suggested_Character_Profiles.md` as though the
reviewers were offering competing characterisation to be reconciled against the
manuscript, and cut whatever the book did not already demonstrate. That was the wrong
job. The reviewers were asked to **expand** these people past the page, and this pass
takes far more from them.

The governing distinction, and the one the earlier pass missed: `_SHEET_RULES.md` bars
**hard-linking**, not invention. A trait does not need on-page evidence to belong on a
sheet. It needs to be consistent with the page and to make the person more specific.
*Happy-go-lucky horse girl* survives being moved to a lunar colony. *Says a particular
line in chapter twenty-seven* does not.

## The four additions, on every sheet

**1. `Type and number`.** Records the independent MBTI readings, names the primary, and
says what the other readers were looking at when they landed somewhere else. Then an IQ
figure with the shape of it and where the person is comparatively weak.

**2. `Frustration and anger`.** Six fixed questions, deliberately answered differently by
every character: what frustrates them, the tell before they say anything, what they do
with it, what actually angers them as against merely irritating them, what they are like
angry, and how it ends.

**3. `Life outside the work`.** The biography the book had no room for: a Saturday, an
enthusiasm, what they collect or will not shut up about, what they are sentimental about,
what they were like at eight and what they are like at forty.

**4. An IQ figure for everybody,** in the header line as well as the block.

## The emotional expansion

The heart of the pass. Some of what went in: Ruth as an actual tomboy, with the
self-cut hair, one pair of shoes at a time, a shortwave log nobody has asked to see and a
terrible band she has defended since she was fourteen. Priya as a horse girl in full —
the share in a horse at fifteen treated as the largest event of her life, other people's
cars named after horses, mucking out at six on a Saturday as the part of the week she
looks forward to. Chloe's boats, and her singing when she believes she is alone. Sam's
bread and the sport nobody has heard of. Kavi's unnamed fish and his nine minutes on
thermal management. Theo funny in writing and nowhere else. Odile singing in French.
Eli's insect collection, beautiful, meticulous, shown to nobody. Fen's shoebox. And for
the adults, who were untouched before this pass: Hearn's coastline walked in sections
over eleven years, Bell's clocks, Ammons taking up dressage at forty-eight, Whitaker's
letters, Aldana's sewing machine, Pruitt's chickens.

## Dana

`DANA.md` is new, and it applies the author's factual correction: **Dana is older than
Chloe, by thirteen years.** She is nineteen at the Christmas Chloe is six. She goes to
college, marries, and has children of her own, and by the last time they speak she is in
her thirties with a house and a job. The sheet is built around the age gap rather than
treating her as a peer, and around the one thing she does identically thirteen years
apart: asks the standard question, means it, listens for the length of the answer, and
turns to talk about the parking.

## Frequency audit

Every adjective appearing in three or more of the new sections was checked and cut back.
Removed or made specific: `flat`, `level` and `unbothered` where they trespassed on the
Axis 10 register words owned by Nadia, Kavi and Sam; and `quiet`, `warm`, `honest`,
`competent`, `practical`, `cheerful` and `direct` where they were doing characterising
work on more than two sheets. What remains at three or more is non-characterising —
"flat ground", "eye level", "a quiet room", psychometric "flat profile", and rows quoting
a reviewer's own wording inside the four-readings tables.

The Axis 10 work from the previous pass is preserved. No register word was reassigned and
none was added to a second sheet.
