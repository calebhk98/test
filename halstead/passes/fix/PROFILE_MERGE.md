# The reviewers' character profiles, merged into `characters/`

Second pass. `passes/fix/PROFILES_MERGE.md` records an earlier one that took the
reviewers' externalisation argument and their eight visible axes onto the nine
cohort sheets. That pass ended by naming the work it had not done: the
`Personality off the page` and `Under pressure` paragraphs, where most of the
cast still gave the same answer to bored, praised and wrong. That is what this
pass is, plus the reviewer material the first pass did not reach, plus Fen.

Files changed: `CHLOE.md`, `RUTH.md`, `SAM.md`, `KAVI.md`, `NADIA.md`,
`PRIYA.md`, `ODILE.md`, `ELI.md`, `THEO.md`, `FEN.md`, `_DIFFERENTIATION.md`,
`_ALLOCATIONS.md`, `CHARACTER_SHEETS.md`. No file was created. Nothing in
`chapters/` was touched.

Checks: `verify_citations.py` reports **0 quotations checked, 0 not found**,
which is the required answer, because there are no quotations in the sheets at
all. `prose_check.py` reports **34 flagged**, the same figure as before this
pass, now spread across 41 files rather than 38. Two violations I introduced
were found and removed before finishing. No em dashes and no curly quotes in
anything I wrote.

---

## 1. The reviewers' file is four documents, not one

`Suggested_Character_Profiles.md` concatenates four separate attempts, and they
are not of equal value. It matters, because three of the four are the same
defect the author is complaining about in ours.

1. **The archetype matrix and ten short profiles.** MBTI plus a one-line social
   driver. Useful only for the MBTI votes.
2. **`HALSTEAD ACADEMY: COMPLETE CHARACTER PROFILES`.** The weakest. Nine
   profiles in an identical template where six characters answer *goes quiet,
   works more, doesn't cry* and four are described in the same words. Their own
   note admits Theo and Kavi came out identical. Almost nothing taken.
3. **`CHARACTER PROFILES: THE TEN`.** The emotional architecture. Where each
   person feels a thing in their body first, and what happens to them angry,
   sad, happy, embarrassed and wrong. Genuinely good and the source of most of
   what came in on the interior side.
4. **`TEN STUDENTS - v2`.** The best of the four and the one that knows why the
   others failed: everything externalised, one engine line each, and the
   one-problem-ten-reactions proof. The previous pass took the axes from here;
   this pass takes the engines.

Where the four disagree with each other, I did not average them. I took the
strongest and said so.

## 2. What was taken from them

**The engine line, and it is the single best thing in their material.** One
sentence per character, at the top of the sheet, under the age and IQ line. It
is exclusive, it is one line, and it is completely portable: every one of the
ten works unchanged on a lunar colony. All ten are collected as **Axis 8** in
`_DIFFERENTIATION.md`.

| | engine |
| :-- | :-- |
| Chloe | wants the reason underneath the answer, and has no moderate setting on anything |
| Ruth | cannot let a wrong statement stand in the air, whatever it costs to take it down |
| Sam | has to run a feeling through his body before it reaches language, and makes the joke first while it is still on the way |
| Kavi | follows a mechanism to the end of itself and does not track the conversational layer running beside it |
| Nadia | cannot leave a broken mechanism alone in a room she is standing in |
| Priya | processes out loud, immediately, at whoever is nearest |
| Eli | does the interesting thing and works out the consequences afterwards |
| Theo | carries a thing alone rather than hand any part of it to somebody else |
| Odile | puts everything she feels into what her body is currently doing |
| Fen | keeps a record of what passes through, because nothing is hers to keep |

Sam's, Chloe's and Fen's are rewritten rather than lifted. Theirs for Sam was
*cannot be serious for more than four sentences*, which is a description of his
jokes rather than of him, and does not explain the accuracy habit that is most
of his sheet. Fen has no engine in their file at all; hers is built from ours.

**Chloe's detonation, and the guilt underneath it.** Their best single
observation about her and the one thing on her sheet that was actually missing.
She builds silently, goes off all at once at full volume with the cruellest
available true thing, and then stands behind the door waiting to be come after,
and is worse off when nobody comes than she would have been if somebody had.
Verified: the manuscript has her at seven saying the unforgivable thing to her
mother and slamming a door hard enough to move the frame. Her sheet already
said she feels everything at full size and then never showed the top of the
range. It does now.

**Chloe wrong, in the right order.** Theirs: she argues back even knowing she
is wrong, because the embarrassment is bigger than the truth, and needs a day.
Ours said she *accepts the correction flatly and without arguing back*, which
contradicted her own visible-axes section three hundred lines further down, and
was one of the convergent lines. Rewritten: she argues past her own belief in
the argument, ears hot, then concedes the whole thing at once and completely.
The order is the characterisation; she cannot get to the second half without
doing the first half at full length.

**Theo's routine as the emotional channel.** Theirs, and portable. When
something has genuinely changed he keeps the schedule exactly, and keeps it
with more care than usual rather than less, because altering any part of it
would be a way of saying out loud that the world is different now. The tell for
a reader is the precision, not the withdrawal.

**Odile's anger points at herself.** Theirs. Whatever caused the bad result,
she does not have the habit of holding another person responsible for it. A
writer who needs her to blame somebody has to build the reason into the scene.

**Priya takes a compliment.** Theirs, and it corrects ours. See section 4.

**The one-physical-rule.** Their v2 states it plainly: give each character three
or four bodily signatures that belong to nobody else, and if two characters both
set their jaw, one of them has to stop. Applied. Four live collisions found and
cleared, recorded in `_ALLOCATIONS.md` so they do not come back.

**Fen's whole visible-axes section.** She is covered by the reviewers, her sheet
was the only student sheet without the eight axes, and her `Personality off the
page` was four lines, the thinnest in the set. Both are now written, in her own
vocabulary rather than anybody else's: stuck, she rebuilds forward from the last
point at which the thing was in order, writing down each step so she never
solves the same part twice; publicly wrong, she amends the record so the wrong
version no longer exists anywhere and says nothing about it; in a group she
takes custody of the materials and the calendar rather than the thinking.

**MBTI, where they are unanimous and we disagreed.** It is a writing aid, not a
claim, but the drift the author is complaining about shows up in it: ours had
Chloe as INTJ and Sam as ESTP, both a letter more thinking-typed than the
character on the page. Changed to **Chloe INFJ** (they say F in all four
documents), **Sam ESFP** (all four), **Theo ISFJ** (three of four; also
de-collides him from Fen's ISTJ), and **Priya ENFP** (three of four; also
de-collides her from Sam). Kavi and Odile left alone, because the reviewers
contradict themselves about both and there is no clear *theirs* to prefer.
`CHARACTER_SHEETS.md` was carrying four stale MBTI values against its own
sheets and is now correct.

## 3. What was kept of ours

Almost all of it. The reviewers are better at angle and worse at everything
else, and the merge is overwhelmingly additive.

- **Chloe's `The thing to get right first` in full.** She does not know she is
  gifted; openness is her natural state and hiding is a bruise; she sorts adults
  by fairness rather than rank; she needs the mechanism before the rule will sit
  still. None of this is in any of the four reviewer documents and it is the
  load-bearing section on the load-bearing sheet.
- **Nadia's one-register section.** The best-solved sheet in the set before this
  pass and the reason `flat` stays hers. Untouched except for the praise line
  and one collision.
- **Kavi's `Everything he feels arrives as an absence`.** Ours, built from their
  best idea in a previous pass, and much better than theirs.
- **Ruth's volume section, the forearm habit, and the private reworking of
  finished work.** None of it in their file.
- **Theo's `what he does with a thing he cannot say`.** Still the strongest
  single section on any sheet here.
- **Every teaching entry.** Theirs has Kavi incomprehensible to younger students
  and Odile unable to teach; ours has Kavi good at it because twelve-year-olds
  have not yet learned to pretend they followed, and Odile's patience living in
  her language teaching rather than in demonstration. Ours are more surprising
  and they are right.
- **Eli's calibration problem and the refusal to repair.** Ours.
- **Fen's `she is not detached from places, she has redirected the attachment`.**
  Theirs makes her fragile, distressed when her order is disturbed, unable to
  get a joke. Ours has her as somebody who already solved the problem in a way
  that costs her something she has not noticed. Ours is better and it stays.

Priya's is the one that was actively wrong rather than merely generic. Her sheet

keeping a total for it to go into.

**Bored.** Three sheets said *finds something to do with her hands*. Odile keeps

Priya talks at whoever is nearest, gets moved, and talks at the new person. Fen

somebody needs to find something.

**Goes quiet.** Five characters went quiet under stress. Ruth keeps it, because

Sam keeps it, because his sheet marks it as so unlike him that a room notices.

those words, and goes. Odile gets slower and more economical. Theo says nothing
further in that exchange and comes back later with it worked out.

**`He can be unfair, and the shape it takes is`** appeared word for word on four
sheets. Varied.

### 4.3 Four physical collisions, cleared

The reviewers' rule is right and we were breaking it.

- **The leg-tap.** Chloe taps four fingers against her leg walking, rehearsing a
  timing count; Ruth tapped a count against her leg walking, to drill a timing.
  The same gesture. The manuscript attaches it to Chloe, so Ruth loses it and
  gains her own: half a pace ahead of whoever she is talking to, finishing the
  sentence over her shoulder.
- **Eating standing.** Sam, Nadia and Odile. It is Sam's, and `_ALLOCATIONS.md`
  already said so. Nadia now eats whatever is nearest and cannot tell you
  afterwards what it was; Odile eats a great deal and finishes before people who
  started ahead of her, without appearing to hurry.
- **The set jaw.** Chloe's is tied to the mechanism-hunger and is load-bearing;
  Sam's was generic. Sam is now up out of the chair before he knows he is angry.
- **Sleeves.** The sleeves-up tell is Nadia's, three times on her sheet and in
  the allocations ledger. Odile now wears the burn on her forearm on show rather
  than pushing sleeves past it. Fen's rolled cuffs stay, because her sheet marks
  them explicitly as a thing she sets once and stops thinking about, which is
  the opposite gesture.

Also cleared: **hands flat on a table** was Chloe's, Priya's and, in the
reviewers' version, Nadia's. It is Priya's, where it is a demonstration and part
of the touching that is hers exclusively in this cast. Chloe now comes up onto
her toes with both hands braced on the table edge.

## 6. What I could not do, and what is next

**Marek.** He is real in the manuscript, he is one of the ten the reviewers
cover, and their profile of him is the sharpest of their short ones. I could not
create the sheet under this brief. While this pass was running another agent
created `characters/MAREK.md`, and it is good, and it deliberately disagrees
with the reviewers in the right direction: theirs is cold and spiteful, that one
is warm and fast and simply somewhere else, with the refusal written as an
absence rather than a stand. I did not edit it, because it is being written now.
Two things for whoever owns it: it is not in Axis 8, 9 or 10 in
`_DIFFERENTIATION.md` and should be, and its *what he finds funny* line,
confidence about a thing they have not checked, collides with Theo's, which is a
mismatch between how certain somebody sounds and how much they checked. Same for
the two other new sheets, `BEX.md` and `IYAD.md`.

**The adults.** The reviewers' profiles cover no adults at all, so there was
nothing to merge for them and the brief's second half had no content. They do
have the sameness problem, and it is worth being precise about where. Halstead
staff being flat and uncushioned is an institutional rule, stated in the index
and in `_CALIBRATION.md`, and stripping it would fight something the book
depends on. The adults *outside* Halstead are not covered by that exception, and
three of them, Aldana, Whitaker and Bryce, are characterised as flat in the
index line itself. That is the same defect the ten cohort sheets have just had
removed, and it is the next pass. A note to that effect is in Axis 10.

**One thing left deliberately.** Odile's `Personality off the page` is still the
least differentiated of the ten. The previous pass judged that acceptable
because she genuinely is the taciturn one and inflating her was ruled out. I
agree, and the praise line and the self-directed anger have given her two things
that are only hers, which is enough.
