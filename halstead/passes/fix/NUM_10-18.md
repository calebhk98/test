another. Nothing in this pass replaces a tracked number with a different
tracked number. Where a count had to go, it went out entirely and the sentence
was rebuilt around an unquantified word (*a few*, *weeks*, *the rest of them*,
*over and over*) or around nothing at all.

words deleted out of 979.

## Chapter 18, 77 to 47

The chapter is called *Fifteen* and is about a girl who has stopped improving
and goes looking for the reason in a box of scorecards, so the temptation is to
let every figure stand as scoring. Most of them were not scoring. The scored
results survive; the itemising around them does not.

**The single biggest cut is the range-card finding.** It read:

> what comes out of it is eleven people who have stopped moving: five of them at
> sixty, three at fifty-eight, two at fifty-five, and one sitting at sixty-three
> since the previous November, with the curve flattening into the same narrow
> band for all of them whether it took fifty sessions or a handful.

Eight tracked words, seven of them in a breakdown nothing later in the book uses.
Now:

> what comes out of it is eleven people who have stopped moving, their curves
> flattening into the same narrow band whether it took fifty sessions or a
> handful, and one of them sitting where she landed since the previous November.

The finding is intact, the outlier is intact, and six counted words are gone.
