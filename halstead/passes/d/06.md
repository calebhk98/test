## Sit in every chair (selected — the exercises that produced a finding)

- **The mother who says "She's top of her reading group at home" (line 21).** I am a visiting parent; I've just offered the one piece of evidence I have that my daughter is exceptional; I find nowhere to look because I already suspect it's the wrong currency here. What would the table actually do? Reassure her, ask a follow-up, or at minimum let the moment breathe. What the text has them do: one man says it "doesn't mean anything here" and walks off for coffee, and the conversation pivots to driving routes one line later. Nobody returns to her. That gap is the finding below.
- **Chloe, handed "But you could already do them, you said you got them all right" (line 54).** What she knows at this moment: the mechanical procedure and the derivation both. What she doesn't know: whether her dad is testing her or genuinely puzzled. What she does: reframes the whole distinction between procedural and conceptual knowledge, unprompted — consistent with her established behavior elsewhere, not a finding on its own, but it sets up the specificity question below about *where* the "you got them all right" claim was actually made on the page.
- **Priya at the door (line 103).** I am Priya, mid-goodbye, and I've just discovered a hair tie is missing off my wrist; my chin is working. What would Chloe — my roommate for the whole month, established elsewhere as attentive to the people she's loyal to — actually do? Say something, touch her arm, ask if she's okay. What the text has her do: nothing. The paragraph moves straight to Sam's line. That silence is the finding below.
- **Kavi, handed Chloe's fist in his pyjama leg (line 82) and asked to "find out."** What does he know: nothing more than what he's already said three times. What would he do: probably say so again, maybe pull back physically since the exchange has nothing to do with him specifically. What the text has him do: nothing — he isn't shown reacting to the physical contact at all, then or through the four topic changes that follow while she's still holding on (through line 99).
