## Scenes added, and where

1. **The company as a place, opening section.** Physical staging that didn't exist before: a converted fastener warehouse, a half-wall between engineering and assembly, a dead loading dock, a floor Nadia resurfaced herself, a break-room coffee machine nobody's fixed, and the cleaning contract that comes with the building (a dentist's office and a chiropractor's share the crew). Also gives her a stated role on the floor — an eight-o'clock standup from a chair, then down among the benches — consistent with `characters/NADIA.md`'s "In a room she's running... she's physically forward and central, closest to the problem."
2. **The cleaner and the moment he's caught.** Original text only reported the attempt after the fact in chat ("they put a guy on the cleaning contract with a usb stick... tried three more and then went and did the cleaning"). New prose stages it live: Nadia working late on an unrelated tolerance problem, noticing him stalled at the test bench, watching him try four boards against a proprietary six-pin harness that was never going to take a USB stick, then crossing the floor herself and firing him in four lines of dialogue with no explanation offered either way. This is the "does it herself rather than delegating" trait from her sheet, made visible rather than only asserted afterward in chat.
3. **The week of tracing.** New prose section: she uses her standing as the paying client to get his personnel file without a fight, finds one reference number that rings out and one that reaches an unfamiliar consulting firm's answering service, spends two more mornings on state business filings until a registered-agent search turns up an address above a tyre shop, confirms the office's hours with a pretext call to the shop's front counter, and tells no one at the company where she's going before she drives out. This dramatizes the single chat line "took a week. its four guys and an office above a tyre place" into an actual investigative sequence, using the two skills her sheet lists first — pretexting and investigation — rather than leaving the method as a gap the reader has to fill in.
4. **The visit.** New prose scene: the exterior stairs, the smell of the tyre shop, four men in a small upstairs office, and Nadia delivering her piece standing just inside the door rather than sitting. The chat's existing lines ("i went in and told them...", "if youre going to come at me...") are left untouched as her own after-the-fact report to the group; the live scene uses adjacent but not identical phrasing for the spoken version of the same beats, so the two don't just restate each other — one is what happened, the other is how she compresses it for her friends.
5. **The hiring section.** Expanded the existing summary paragraphs with physical staging (the same windowless office, a folding table, a stack of submissions at her elbow) and two concrete interview beats: a candidate who calls a real gap in his own payment system "an edge case" in a tone that says he's used the dodge before, and one who can't name who reviews his own scheduling tool's output. Added one contrasting sentence — the rare candidate who answers the second question properly gets a longer interview — to keep the section from reading as uniform failure, since the original text already says she hires excellent people who check out on paper.
