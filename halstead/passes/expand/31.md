## Declined

- **Did not touch the timeline.** `chronology/RUTH_MIT.md` documents a real problem: on
  chapter 31's own numbers, the professor scene (which the chapter says causes the six-month
  chat silence) can't fall before that silence starts. Fixing this would mean changing when
  the belief breaks or how long she holds it — squarely "changes... when they learn it,"
  which the brief rules out. I left every existing date and duration in the chapter
  untouched and avoided adding new calendar markers (seasons stay vague, e.g. "that spring,"
  "months of Sunday calls") so the new material doesn't sharpen the existing conflict
  further. This is a decision for a different pass, not this one.

- **No new named characters.** Devansh (already named in the chapter) carries the one
  "names the person she's correcting" beat added outside the professor scene; the professor
  himself, and every registrar/advising staffer, stay unnamed, matching how the chapter
  already handles them. I did not invent a roommate's name, though one is referenced
  generically (`chapters/24_the_chat.md` establishes she has one, unnamed, met twice).

- **Did not add a fourth statistic.** Chapter 28 names a fourth topic (proportion of the
  country that's been in a fight) that chapter 31 doesn't repeat; I kept chapter 31's own
  three (second language, reading level, fractions) rather than reconciling the two lists,
  since adding a topic here would be new information, not staging.

- **Left the chat transcript alone.** It isn't graded and the brief is explicit that prose is
  what counts; rewriting or extending it would have been padding.
