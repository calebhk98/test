## Decided against adding

- **Any explanation of who the founder is, why Halstead exists, how it selects, or who funds it.** Theo's reasoning paragraph stops at "what their own government thinks it knows" and goes no further; nothing answers the questions any character might have.
- **A scene of the actual break-in or what they find.** That's chapter 34's material; chapter 33 now ends at commitment (sign-off) exactly as the original did, just with the commitment shown rather than told.
- **A third sentencing citation from Ruth**, even though the scene could easily have supported one — the original text says "twice," so the dramatized version stops at two.
- **Changing the order or outcome of any decision** — Theo still refuses for nine days and signs last; Eli still builds, Kavi still watches; the group still runs a five-week test; nobody's position on the plan itself changes from what the original implied.
- **A version of the "noticed" and stop-clause paragraphs that names chapter 35's actual nine-minute event explicitly.** Early drafts did this and it read as a spoiler placed a chapter too early; the final version defines the trigger conditions in general terms (a confirmable event vs. "a gap that closes again before anyone can point to it") so the payoff lands only once a reader reaches chapter 35.
