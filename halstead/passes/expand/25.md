## Decided against adding

- **A name or line for the captain.** He's unnamed in the original and stays that way — giving him a name would be a new named character, which the brief rules out.
- **Any indication of what the woman three levels up does with the report next**, or who she is beyond "a woman three levels above the captain." That's plot the chapter doesn't currently own; `chapters/29_the_file.md` doesn't reference this report at all, so I didn't borrow forward from it or invent a resolution.
- **A scene explaining what "specialised instruction from an unidentified source" is fishing for.** The brief explicitly says not to explain the school; I left the phrase to do the same work it already did.
- **A second Ruiz scene with real back-and-forth dialogue with Sam.** He has no character sheet, and giving him extended dialogue risked inventing a personality the manuscript doesn't otherwise support. Kept him to two short commands and one unspoken reaction.
- **Cutting or altering the existing captain/Sam dialogue.** `characters/SAM.md`'s "Known problems" section flagged a since-removed mirrored line ("A gun you can't stop a projectile with isn't a tool...") for possible cutting; that line is already absent from the chapter, so nothing needed doing there.
