## Decided against adding

- **Any content on why the school exists, how it selects, or who funds it.** The file's own "funding cannot be traced" caveat, the redacted lines on page three, and the empty public website are all deliberately dead ends — traces Theo can find and not interpret, not doors I opened further.
- **A name for the retiring analyst or the supervisor.** Both stay unnamed, matching the original text; giving either a name would be a new named character, which the brief rules out.
- **Anything about what the redacted lines on page three say.** Named the fact of the redaction (more than anywhere else in the box) and stopped there, rather than gesturing at content that would have to either explain something or be a false lead.
- **A scene of Theo checking whether his own name appears anywhere in the file.** This was tempting as a character beat but risked reading as an opening onto the selection question the brief explicitly forbids answering, so I left it out.
- **Expanding the chat exchange at the end.** Chat-transcript lines are stripped before grading and don't move the prose metrics, so the brief's "prose is what counts" note argues against padding it; I added one beat before it (three deleted drafts) and left the four chat lines and Ruth's reply untouched.
