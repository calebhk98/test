## Decided against adding

- **Any material inside the theo/chloe drill exchange itself**, including a beat interpreting Chloe's reaction. The task was explicit that this is the one thing to preserve unedited and unexplained.
- **Any explanation of what the tool actually is technically, how Kavi's watcher works internally, or how Ruth's statistical method works beyond what's already stated in her three-part chat proof.** Added detail stays physical (desks, whiteboards, a phone call) rather than technical, so nothing new is asserted about the mechanism itself.

The new prose stays on the physical and procedural (what people do with their hands, where they are) and never answers a question about *why*.
- **A named line for the founder ("him")** or any detail about his identity beyond what Ruth's existing proof states. The new staging shows people reacting to the proof, not adding to what the proof says.
- **Dialogue for Rohan (Nadia's brother) or any other character without a sheet.** No new named characters were introduced; every added line of dialogue (Nadia's "Fix the clock," Kavi's "Running") goes to a character who already speaks in this chapter and matches their Dials/Would-they-say-this sections.
- **A coda after the final line ("eli: so lets go and read it").** That line hands off directly into the next chapter's plot (reading Theo's file), and anything added after it would be new plot rather than staging of what's already there.
