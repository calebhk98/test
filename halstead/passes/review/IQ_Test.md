## The WISC-IV in 2012

The WISC-IV has four index scores:
- **Verbal Comprehension Index (VCI)**
- **Perceptual Reasoning Index (PRI)**
- **Working Memory Index (WMI)**
- **Processing Speed Index (PSI)**

The Full Scale IQ (FSIQ) is derived from **10 core subtests**. The General Ability Index (GAI) excludes the PSI and WMI and is derived from VCI + PRI only.

**Index scores have a mean of 100 and SD of 15.** FSIQ also has mean 100, SD 15.

**Scaled scores for subtests have a mean of 10 and SD of 3.**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|

| **Index scores** | ~155–160 each (average) |
| **Scaled scores** | ~18–19 on most subtests (the ceiling is 19) |

A child with a true 160 would be **hitting the ceiling** (scaled score 19) on nearly every subtest they take. The test is not hard enough for them. They finish Vocabulary items meant for 16-year-olds without breaking stride.
