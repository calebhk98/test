## Show your work

### Five sentences hardest to restate

1. **Para 32, final sentence:** "Then the pieces stop being pieces of the thing they were pieces of, and after that she is looking at a rectangle with lines in it." This sentence describes Chloe losing the connection between the visual demonstration and its meaning—the pieces stop representing the shaded fraction and become abstract lines. It can be restated but requires inference about her mental state.

2. **Para 50, second sentence:** "Dividing by an eighth is asking how many eighths fit inside it." The sentence conflates the operation with the question it answers, which is clear but requires the reader to understand that "asking" and the operation "is" can be used interchangeably for abstract concepts.

3. **Para 83, compound metaphorical thought:** "Owen must not have liked the bridge, and the two facts sit crosswise, and neither one will move over for the other." This expresses contradiction through spatial metaphor—the facts are opposed and mutually blocking. The plainness of restatement depends on understanding "crosswise" as "contradictory" or "opposing."

4. **Para 106-107, temporal break:** "She said it without checking it first. Ten minutes into the walk back, it catches up with her." The first sentence refers to her moon statement; the second sentence refers to the realization/doubt about whether she was right. The antecedent of "it" (the doubt/realization) is implied but not explicit.

5. **Para 32, opening:** "He draws a rectangle, shades half of it, cuts the half into pieces, talking the whole way through about what the question underneath the question is." The phrase "the question underneath the question" is clear in context (the underlying concept or mechanism), but requires parsing to understand it's not a factual contradiction.
