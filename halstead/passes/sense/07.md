# Sense Pass One: Chapter 07

## Pass one

**Line 5:** "Kavi is over by the double doors with two adults, from whom she gets a hand up, before a family walks between them, and by the time they pass, he's gone."
Ambiguous pronouns and unclear positioning make it impossible to place all people in the scene and say what their bodies are doing.

**Line 189:** "That's over a year from now, and she's in there right now."
The phrase "in there" lacks a clear referent and cannot be restated plainly.
