## Five hardest sentences to restate

1. **Line 16:** "her eyes going back to the same spot on the page and staying there longer"
   - Restatement: Her eyes return to the same spot on the page and linger there for a longer time. 
   - Problem: longer than what? The sentence has an incomplete comparison with no second element.

2. **Line 16:** "hands it across the table, though her eyes stay on the page"
   - Restatement: She hands the page to someone across the table while her eyes remain on the page.
   - Problem: if she hands the physical page away, her eyes cannot physically stay on it—the page is no longer where her eyes are. This describes something that cannot happen.

3. **Line 53:** "That one takes a long time to come back, the other voice going on behind the wall, not the words, only that it hasn't stopped."
   - Restatement: That question takes a long time to get answered; the other person on the phone keeps talking from the other side of the wall (where Chloe is listening), and Chloe cannot hear the actual words, only that the person is still talking.
   - Restatable after working through the elliptical construction, but requires effort.

4. **Line 57:** "Chloe comes down two more stairs during the wait, close enough to see her mom's free hand pressed flat against the counter, and her mom points at the stairs, still facing the counter, so Chloe goes back up to the fourth one."
   - Restatement: While waiting for an answer on the phone, Chloe descends two more stairs to get closer and can now see her mother's free hand pressed flat against the counter; her mother points back at the stairs without turning away from the counter, so Chloe climbs back up to the fourth stair.
   - Restatable but structurally complex with multiple dependent clauses.

5. **Line 27:** "Her mom takes it back and turns it over, finds only the ink from the signature that has come through the back, then turns it the right way round again for a fourth read of the top part."
   - Restatement: Her mom retrieves the letter from her dad, flips it to see the back, discovers that the only thing visible on the back is the ink from the signature showing through from the front, then flips it back to the front to read the upper portion for the fourth time.
   - Restatable but the chain of actions is dense.
