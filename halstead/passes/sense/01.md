**Five sentences hardest to restate:**

1. Line 12: "Her thumb finds it automatically, the way it already has twice since the bell."
   - The parallelism in "the way it already has" creates awkwardness: does this mean her thumb reaches the same way, the same number of times, or in the same manner?

2. Line 12: "Chloe sits forward in her chair as the cup rides most of the way across the lot before it goes off at the turn and rolls, so that she is about an inch out of her seat when she hears her name."
   - The causal structure is unclear: does "so that" indicate that her forward motion causes her to be out of her seat at the exact moment her name is called, or does it describe timing?

3. Line 79: "Chloe opens her mouth, then works out how long the real answer is."
   - "Works out how long" is abstract in a way that resists restatement: length in words? complexity? time to explain?

4. Line 33: "'And then he stops being a jerk about it.' Her mom's eyes have gone somewhere past her shoulder, and Chloe keeps going."
   - The dialogue lacks attribution; only from context can you infer it's a parent responding/guessing, not Chloe speaking.

5. Line 56: "A chair squeaks somewhere behind her, while Peanut keeps washing his face, so they must not have heard her."
   - "They" is vague; the antecedent could be classmates, teacher, or the whole room, and is not explicitly stated.
