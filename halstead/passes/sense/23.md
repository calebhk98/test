### The five hardest sentences to restate

1. (Line 17) "The whole of the school below them sits on the grass behind the last row of seats, because there is nowhere else to put eight hundred children."
   - Hard because the phrase "below them" is ambiguous in context (below the stage? below the graduating students?), but I interpreted it as the physically lower position on the field.

   - Hard because the sentence presents a paradox: two people who have seen each other for eleven years remain complete strangers in terms of knowing where each other lives.

3. (Line 131) "Watch what the competitors charge. Flag it before she quotes, not after."
   - I struggled with whether "she" is Nadia or her mother; it became clear it must be her mother.

4. (Line 185) "Past a certain point, the piece in front of her stops being about the piece in front of her."
   - Hard because it's intentionally circular/contradictory language suggesting the work becomes metaphorical or emotional rather than literal.
