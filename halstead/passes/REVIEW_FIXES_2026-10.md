# What changed in the manuscript today (review fixes, October 2026)

This is a plain list of every change made to the chapters in this round, written so you can read it without having seen the review documents the complaints came from. Each entry says what was wrong and what the text says now. The comparison point is the version at commit 9301859. Nothing here has been committed.

Short forms used below: "tag" means the "he says" or "Sam says" part of a line of dialogue. "Quote merge" means two pieces of one speaker's line joined into one.

British spelling and word choices (colour, fortnight, towards, and so on) were deliberately NOT changed and are not listed. A separate document covers them.

## Overview

- Chapters touched: all 36.
- Small fixes kept: 253 entries (continuity, arithmetic, garbled sentences, missing speaker tags, repeated "read it twice" gestures, and similar).
- Passages rewritten through the blind-judge process: 15 passages in 11 chapters (8, 9, 12, 23, 26, 28, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36). For each, three new drafts were written and a judge ranked them against the original without knowing which was which. The winner is what is in the chapter now.
- Quote merges: 41 in two passes: 16 in the cleanup pass (it says 16 but names 15 places) plus 25 in the later regress2 pass (that report says 26 but names 25 places). All named places are listed in the quote-merges section.
- Fixes reverted on purpose after the fact: 10 (the students' own factual mistakes were put back, the Ch 10 Owen days were put back, and one Ch 26 clause was taken out again).
- Numbers changed a second time: 3 (Ch 13, 15, 27).
- The regress2 pass is included: five plain-word swaps in Ch 34 (to fix a register measure) and 25 quote merges (to keep words per quotation in band).
