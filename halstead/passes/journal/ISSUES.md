## Reviewer claims I think are wrong

- Pacing says the pill is never picked up after ch 24. Ch 28 picks it up. What's missing is a setup and a resolution (item 40).
- Story says Ruth's "somebody wrote to my mother" contradicts ch 3. Ch 3's letter was Chloe's; Ruth's own letter could well have gone to her mother.
- Story says Voss's "six years running" contradicts "the first graduation". The school opened around 2008, so a teacher can have six years without any graduates.
- Story says Okonkwo walked them "the April before she started" though it was the same April. That's ambiguous wording at most.
- Characters says Ch 30 has Chloe know reasoning from a ch 9 scene she wasn't in. She could easily have learned it later.
