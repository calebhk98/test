## Part 4: larger suggestions, in words

**Ch 19 dateline and order [12, 14].** The chapter runs in this order:

1. the April 2022 bar
2. practice papers, May to September
3. driving, September to March
4. the October 2022 retake
5. Marek in December 2022
6. the forge
7. "Home in June for the summer"

Your dad teaches her in the summer of 2021 (ch 18), so the driving year is September 2021 to March 2022. That's before the April bar, not after it. "Home in June" coming after December 2022 lands in June 2023, after graduation.

The cleanest fix:

- Move the two driving paragraphs to the top of the chapter.
- Move the "Home in June" section to sit between the practice papers and the October retake, making it the summer of 2022.
- Set the dateline to September 2021 to December 2022.

Ch 20 (June 2022) then steps back a few weeks, which the dateline covers.
