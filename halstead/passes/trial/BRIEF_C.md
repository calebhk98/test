# Variant C: nothing is innocent

You are testing a chapter that is known to contain a large number of problems.
A careful reader working slowly through it found something wrong in most
paragraphs. Your job is to find them. Do not edit anything. Do not rewrite.

Assume every paragraph is guilty until you have cleared it. Reporting that a
chapter is fine is the one outcome that is certainly wrong.

## How to clear a paragraph

Go in order. For each paragraph you must be able to answer all of these before
moving on. Any you cannot answer is a finding.

- **Meaning.** Say the sentence in your own words. If a phrase resists, it is a
  finding, however elegant it sounds.
- **Joining words.** For every *instead, anyway, then, but, so, for once, which
  is why*, name what sits on each side. If one side is missing, it is a finding.
- **Objects.** Where did that come from, and does it do anything afterwards?
- **Behaviour.** Would this person, with this job, at this age, knowing what they
  know, do this? Would a real admissions officer, soldier, teacher, parent, do
  it? If your reason is "so the scene works", it is a finding.
- **Reaction.** Did anybody react to what just happened, and is the size right?
  Something remarkable passing without a reaction is a finding.
- **Position.** Where are these people, and what are their bodies doing?
- **Repetition.** Has this paragraph already been said earlier in the chapter in
  different words? Two paragraphs doing the same job is a finding.
- **Order.** Does this depend on something that has not happened yet?
- **Voice.** Is the narrator explaining to the reader what the scene means, or
  telling them what to think? That is a finding.

## Aim

**Expect to write at least twenty-five findings.** If you have fewer than that
when you reach the end, you have been reading for gist. Go back to the top and
work the list above paragraph by paragraph rather than reading and reflecting.

Small findings count. A single word with nothing to attach to counts. Trivial
and obvious counts.

## After the walk

Read `SYNOPSIS_CHARACTERS_TIMELINE.md`, `chronology/BOOK.md`, and the sheet in
`characters/` for every named person, and add anything the chapter gets wrong
about who these people are, what they can do, or what they know.

## Rules

- Quote verbatim, with line numbers, checked against the file.
- One line per finding.
- Mark the unsure ones *unsure* and include them anyway.
- Do not report prose style, rhythm, word repetition, or quality.
- Do not rewrite. Do not edit the chapter.

Write to the report path you were given and stop.
