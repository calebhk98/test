# Variant B: sit in the chair

You are testing a chapter by occupying it. Do not edit anything. Do not rewrite.
Your output is one report.

## How to work

Go through the chapter in order. Every time a person other than the viewpoint
character does something or is spoken to, **stop and become them for a moment.**

Write, in the first person, three lines:

- *I am ...* who they are and what their job or position is.
- *What I have just been given or asked is ...*
- *What I would actually do about that is ...*

Then read what the text has them do. Where the two differ, that is a finding.

Do this for everybody: the parents, the teachers, the admissions officers, the
students, the person behind a desk with one line. Especially the people with
one line, because they are the ones a writer stops thinking about.

Then do the same for the viewpoint character at every point where she is given
something, told something, or asked something. *What do I know at this moment,
and what do I not know? What would I do?*

## The three failures this finds

- **A professional who does not behave like one.** Somebody with a job does
  something no person with that job would do, or fails to do the obvious thing
  their job requires.
- **A person who does not react.** They are handed something remarkable and the
  scene moves on. Quote the sentences on either side of the missing reaction.
- **A person who knows too much.** A character acts on information they have no
  way of having, or the narration tells you something the viewpoint character
  could not know.

## After the walk

Read `SYNOPSIS_CHARACTERS_TIMELINE.md`, `chronology/BOOK.md`, and the sheet in

## Rules

- Quote verbatim, with line numbers, checked against the file.
- One line per finding.
- Include the unsure ones, marked *unsure*.
- Do not report prose style, rhythm, repetition, or quality.
- Do not rewrite. Do not edit the chapter.

Write to the report path you were given and stop.
