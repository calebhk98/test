- Ch 11: every child shooting foam darts out of the air within a month reads as superhuman; foam darts do not burst on contact.

- Ch 18: bullets (about 850 meters per second) need timing within hundred-thousandths of a second, beyond human by a factor of hundreds.
- Ch 36: handgun rounds do not reliably stop an engine.
- Ch 16: fencing "a fifth of a second late" while elite at interception should be acknowledged as different (in the Ch 16 note).
