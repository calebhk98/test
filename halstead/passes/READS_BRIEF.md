# Does it read well: the brief

**You are a reader, not an editor. Do not change a single word of any chapter.**
Your only output is one report file.

This manuscript has been through several machine-assisted revision passes. Each
pass targeted a measurable property, and the risk of that is prose that scores
well and reads like it was assembled rather than written. You are the check on
that, and you should be blunt.

Read your chapter and answer these, in this order, in your report.

## 1. Does it read like a person wrote it

The thing to look for is the same move repeated. A pass that fixed one problem
across a whole chapter tends to leave its fingerprints in the form of a
construction that turns up again and again. Some that have been applied here:

- feelings shown by a physical action rather than named
- sentences joined with a subordinator instead of "and"
- paragraphs of two or three sentences rather than one
- a character's silence or refusal shown by what they do instead

Any of those is fine once. Quote anything you see three or more times in the
chapter, and say whether it started to feel like a formula.

Also flag anything that reads as generated rather than written: a rhythm that
never varies, a sentence that is grammatical but that no person would say,
detail that is precise without being meaningful, or a paragraph that could be
moved somewhere else in the chapter without anybody noticing.

## 2. Is it easy to read

Where did you slow down, and why. Distinguish "this is hard because the idea is
hard" from "this is hard because the sentence is badly built".

## 3. Could a ninth grader read this comfortably

Not "could they decode it" but "would they keep going". Say where they would put
it down.

## 4. Is it logical

Does each paragraph follow from the one before. Does anything happen out of
order, or arrive without a reason, or contradict something earlier in the same
chapter.

## 5. Is it any good

One honest paragraph. What works, what does not, and whether you would keep
reading. You are not being asked to be kind, and a report that says the chapter
is fine when it is not is worse than useless.

Write to `passes/reads/NN.md` and stop. Do not edit the chapter, do not suggest
rewrites in the chapter file, and do not run git commands that write.
