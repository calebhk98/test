## The chapter whole

**Does each scene need to exist?** I think so. Each of the five covers
different material (arrival, the room assignment, the dining-hall stretch, the
volcano, the physics class) and none appears to be doing the same job as
another closely enough to fold together.

**Chapter 1 apparently went from thirteen sections to five. This chapter is
already at five**, and the five already track fairly cleanly onto distinct
locations and days, so I do not think the same kind of consolidation applies
here. If anything, Scene 1 is disproportionately long against the other four
(50 of the chapter's 125 paragraphs) because it silently carries four
locations — the hallway, the orientation room, the bedroom, and the hotel —
across one continuous day with no break at all. A break after the room
assignment (before "Chloe lies on top of the covers with her shoes on," L73)
would mark the day-to-night, campus-to-hotel turn the prose already makes;
I am not confident this is needed, only that the four current breaks land
after Scene 1 rather than inside it.

**A possible pattern across Scenes 4 and 5, not a duplication.** Both put a
factually precise correction next to the person who made it and then watch
what happens next: the boy who explains viscosity shrugs off Sam's pushback
and returns to his own drawing; Ruth, having made an equivalent correction,
needs it acknowledged, does not get it, and Chloe spends a page watching for a
consequence that never comes. If this pairing is deliberate, I think it works
better as two scenes in sequence than as one merged scene, since the contrast
between the two children's reactions is what a merge would erase. I am
flagging this as something to be aware of rather than something to fix.

**A possible merge that would need real seam-work, not a cut.** Scenes 4 and 5
both close with a very short Sam-and-Kavi exchange (L259-261 mirrors the shape
of L229-231's retreat-into-an-activity). If a future pass wanted to compress
the chapter, the seam would probably need to travel through Kavi (he appears
in both, first correcting, then as the target of Sam's running joke) rather
than through Ruth or Doyle, since Kavi is the only person present in both
halves. I am not recommending this merge, only naming what would carry it if
someone wanted to try it.
