## chapter 20, the box clause: APPLIED

**Verified defect, fix agreed, blocked only on the number-pass agent owning the
file.**

Chapter 16 specifies Ruth's box properly: eight parallel attacks running at
once, of which default passwords is only the first, and an output that **writes
noise over part of the recording** rather than deleting it. Chapter 34's federal
finding is that the footage is *unrecoverable wherever the students appear and
intact everywhere else*, which is that method described from the outside,
eighteen chapters later.

Chapter 20 then summarised the box as

> the box she built at thirteen for finding video recorders on the networks
> around it and logging in with the passwords they shipped with

which reduces it to the one weakest of its eight paths. Two independent
reviewers read chapter 20 against chapter 34, never saw chapter 16, and both
concluded the ending was the clearest author fiat in the book. **The clause was
actively destroying a payoff the manuscript already paid for.**

Applied wording:

> Ruth brings her box, which throws all its ugly things simultaneously at any
> recorder in range and writes noise over whatever it reaches

Both figures in the proposed replacement were dropped, on the instruction to
leave no figure in the sentence. *Eight* is stated in chapter 16 in Ruth's own
mouth and *thirteen* is stated in chapters 16, 24 and 34; the parallelism, which
is the part the ending needs, is carried by *all its ugly things simultaneously*.

Checked after: negative space 4.3%, unchanged; Flesch-Kincaid 6.2, unchanged and
not lowered; no tracked figure in the sentence.

## chapter 20, the fence: APPLIED

> There is chain link eight feet behind them

That is a distance. Both reviewers read it as a height and objected that
sixteen-year-olds cannot vault an eight-foot fence. No fence height appears
anywhere in the manuscript. Nothing was wrong, but a professional reader
misparsed it twice, so the sentence has been disambiguated:

> There is chain link a few strides behind them

*A few strides* can only be read as a distance, and the figure is gone. The
sentence sits inside chapter 20's protected fight at line 79; it is the only
change made inside lines 71 to 131 and it was made under the instruction to
apply this queue, not as part of the number pass.
