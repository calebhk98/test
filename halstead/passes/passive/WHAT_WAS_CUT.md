# Everything the passivity pass removed

Every deletion the pass made, chapter by chapter: the text that was there,
against the text that replaced it. Chapters 1 and 2 are absent because they
were locked. Paragraphs the pass only added to are not here; this is the cut
list, not the change list.

Two of the pass's changes were reverted before this list was made and so are
correctly absent from it: the Priya paragraph in chapter 4, where Chloe was
made to half turn away and get told she was not listening, and Kavi's kitchens
in chapter 10, where the deliberate disagreement with Ruth had been flattened
into agreement.

Ninety-five sites. One is an outright deletion with nothing put in its place;
the other ninety-four are replacements, so the question at each is whether the
new line is worth what it displaced.
