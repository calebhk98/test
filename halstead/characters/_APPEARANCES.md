# Where each character appears

Moved out of the character sheets. A sheet is about the person, not about this
book: a sheet that names chapters cannot be carried into a ghost story or a
lunar colony, which is the test `_SHEET_RULES.md` sets. Navigation lives here
instead, and nothing here is characterisation.
