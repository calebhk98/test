# Superseded manuscript files

None of these are sources. `chapters/` is the source of truth and `HALSTEAD.md` is
built from it by `build_manuscript.py`.

- **`MANUSCRIPT_FULL.md`** — held chapters 1–20 only, and was kept in sync alongside
  `HALSTEAD.md` for no reason except that it existed first. Every chapter in it is in
  `HALSTEAD.md`, current as of the move.
- **`CHAPTERS_16_22_v2.md`, `CHAPTERS_23_30_v2.md`** — drafts on the **old chapter
  numbering**, from before chapters 25–29 were reordered. Their "Chapter Twenty-Three"
  is what is now chapter 28. They were last touched long before the expansion and fix
  passes, so their text is stale as well as misnumbered. They are here for history.

Do not point a script at any of them.
