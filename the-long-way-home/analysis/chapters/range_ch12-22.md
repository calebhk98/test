# Audit Findings — Chapters 12–22

Scope: ch12 "To the Danube" through ch22 "Marcia". Research-only; no story files
were edited. Severity tags: [BLOCKER] / [MAJOR] / [MINOR].

Reference baseline read first: the author prompt, `Bad writing.txt`, `chapter_list.md`,
the character/world-rules/tech-schedule/style/timeline bible files, and all eleven
matching QA files.

## Headline conclusions

- **Zero em dashes** in any of the eleven chapters (verified by U+2014 sweep).
- **No banned anachronisms** (no "okay/OK"; no minute/second used as a clock unit in
  Roman dialogue; no potato/tomato/coffee/tea/sugar-as-food/citrus; no "boss"; no
  Christian references beyond the period-neutral "God help us / thank God" oaths the
  style guide explicitly sanctions for Daniel's voice).
- **All four REVISE chapters were actually fixed** in the current prose (ch13, ch15,
  ch16, ch17, ch22 all carried REVISE or blocking notes; the cited offending strings
  are gone). One residual is noted under ch16.
- The prose is genuinely strong on the bible's hardest mandates: Daniel stays
  fallible and non-prodigal, the slavery thread is carried in events not speeches,
  the tech honestly fails, and the ch13 death is rendered, not telegraphed.
- The main soft problems are **range-level repetition** (Macer's ring gesture; the
  "same hand / killing-a-city-with-water" thesis restated; a building "I never /
  even now" memoir tic) and **a one-year wobble in Tyche's age**.

## ch13 — The Fall  (QA: REVISE → fixed)

**Outline fidelity:** The manned-balloon disaster is rendered in full (crosswind,
single-point pendulum swing, the wet-clay fire-bowl tipping, the seam going, the
~100-ft fall, impact as the killer). The brief's explicit instruction — *do NOT
telegraph or annotate the death; render it* — is honored. The death is never named
before it happens; Daniel's dread is in-the-moment, technically grounded (he
predicted the wind), not narrator-from-the-future omniscience.

**QA vs prose [verified fixed]:** The ch13 QA verdict is REVISE, with ONE blocking
issue: a "not X ... but Y" construction at the trailing-line beat ("I tied a long
light line... not to hold him... but so that..."). The **current prose splits it
into separate sentences** ("I tied a long light line to the basket anyway. It would
not hold him; the wind would have that line for breakfast. It was so that when he
came down we would have something to run to..."), exactly the QA's suggested fix.
**Resolved.**

**Bad writing:** The Naso beat ("He didn't say... He didn't say anything") is
borderline negative narration but is immediately anchored in rendered action (the
one good hand laid over Daniel's fist), so it reads as character-restraint. Ends on
a concrete image (the rag in the fire, the grease catching, the smell). Clean.

**Character/canon:** Sabinus per canon (Marcus Aurelius Sabinus, ~25, scarred
eyebrow, wife in Pannonia, two children). Celer's "You said the wind... I let it go
anyway. That's mine" is verbatim-faithful. No second-miracle, balloon stays a
failing signature struggle.

**Predictability:** [MINOR] A reader knowing the brief expects a death, but the
execution withholds and complicates well; not telegraphed within the text.

# RANGE-LEVEL PATTERNS

## [MINOR] Macer's "gold ring on his thumb" gesture — over-repeated
Macer's signature beat ("turned/going the gold ring on his thumb," "arms folded over
the gold ring," "picked the ring around once") appears across ch15, ch16 (x2), ch17
(x3), ch18, ch19 (x2), ch20 (x2) — roughly eleven instances in the range. It is
character-specific rather than a generic AI beat-tag (no "chuckled" anywhere; the
single "shrugged" is particularized), and a recurring signature gesture is a
legitimate device. But by ch19–20 it has hardened into near-mechanical punctuation
between Macer's lines, which is the spirit of the "beat-tag clichés" caution. Consider
varying or thinning it in any future pass; flagging here because no single chapter's
QA catches a cross-chapter frequency.

## [MINOR] The "same hand / killing-a-city-with-water" thesis restated across scenes
The complicity thesis is fully landed in ch18 (Apollodorus's "the same hand that keeps
your men off the flux fouls theirs. You didn't think it would stay on the one side,"
plus Daniel's "the same knowledge, the one hand"). ch19 L7 then restates it nearly
verbatim ("keeping men alive with water and... killing a city with water, the same
hand"). This is the clearest "Repetition Across Scenes" instance in the range. It does
double as a deliberate tonal bridge from grim ch18 to the ch19 lift, so it is defensible
— but it is the spot where the reader is re-told something already fully delivered.

## [MINOR] Building "I never / even now / as long as I live" memoir tic
First flagged in the ch13 QA as a VOICE WATCH. It is well-controlled in most chapters
but clusters noticeably in **ch18** (five instances). ch14, ch19, ch20, ch21 each
respect the warning (the QA files track this accurately). Worth a thinning pass on
ch18 specifically so the construction does not calcify into a signature across the
back half of the book.

## Consistently MET, range-wide (worth recording as strengths)
- **Em dashes:** zero, all eleven chapters.
- **Correctio / "not X but Y":** no surviving banned pivots after the REVISE fixes;
  remaining contrasts are sentence-split direct forms.
- **Anachronisms:** none (no okay/OK; "minute/second" only ever ordinal or "a second"
  = a moment in Daniel's English narration, never a clock unit in Roman dialogue; no
  banned foodstuffs; "God help us"/"thank God" are the sanctioned period-neutral
  oaths in Daniel's own voice, not in-world Christian reference).
- **Daniel stays fallible / non-prodigy:** wrong filter-layer order twice (ch14),
  cannot give Hermes a number (ch15), the Seres timeline error (ch21), garbled powder
  ratio learned by maiming a man (ch21), reads contracts "at the speed of a clever
  child" (ch22). No total recall; no clean wins.
- **Slavery in events, not speeches:** Pamphilus's un-clean freeing (ch18), Tyche
  pricing/freeing and her refusal of gratitude (ch20), the prize that "the law would
  not let the man at the bottom pick up" (ch19). Daniel's revulsion is shown; the
  abolitionist speech is explicitly swallowed (ch20).
- **Tech fails honestly:** steel unsolved (ch15/21), balloon kills (ch13), filter
  clears-but-doesn't-sterilize (ch14/15), optics named as a blurry toy (ch17),
  cannon designed to burst (ch21).
- **History accurate:** First Dacian War client-king peace (ch12/14), Second War
  annexation / Sarmizegetusa 106 / Decebalus suicide / the head-and-hand and the gold
  / 123 days of games (ch18); the two distinct Danube bridges kept separate (ch12 vs
  ch18); imperial-freedman law and freedman/freedwoman marriage law correct (ch17/22).
