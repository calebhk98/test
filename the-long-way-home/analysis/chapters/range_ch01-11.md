## Range-level patterns

### 1. Item 7 (easy fixes the MC ignores) — the one real recurring gap [MAJOR]
Across ch05-ch08, Daniel — a 2020s teen who is canonically fire-traumatized and germ-aware —
fails to register two obvious modern observations about the world in front of him:
- **Citywide fire risk.** His first walk into Rome (ch07, lines 95-99) and the demo (ch08) put
  him directly under five-/six-story timber-and-brick insulae with "ten thousand cooking fires,"
  and in ch08 his own balloon literally starts a roof fire — yet his fire-danger instinct is
  only ever about himself and his project, never the civic catastrophe a modern kid would clock
  instantly (and he'd plausibly recall the Fire of 64). His fire instinct IS fully present in
  ch11 (where it's the whole point), which makes its absence in the city scenes more noticeable.
  Recommend: a single beat of Daniel privately clocking "this whole city is kindling" on the
  first Rome visit. (Building fire brigades can stay deferred — Rome had vigiles — but the
  noticing is missing.)
- **Clean water / waterborne disease at scale.** ch10 (line 5) has him narrate that a million
  people "all use the same river to drink from and to throw things into," and ch05 establishes
  he already pushes boiled water. The dots are right next to each other, but he never connects
  "everyone drinks the sewer" to mass disease as a SYSTEM problem (he treats water only per-patient
  via Heras). The canon log shows this thread does open up later (ch12 dysentery, ch14-15 filters),
  so it's deferred rather than missed — but in ch10 specifically, a modern teen narrating that
  exact sentence would have the "oh god, cholera/dysentery, this is why everyone's sick" thought.
- **Honey as a wound dressing** (ch05): with a kid's arm going septic and Daniel groping for
  antiseptics (he lands on posca), period-available honey is a plausible thing he'd try. Minor.

These are flagged as genuine misses (plausible for the character, materials present, not
deliberately deferred at the moment of the scene), per the brief's instruction. The water/fire
THREADS do get developed later, so the issue is specifically the absence of the in-the-moment
NOTICING a modern teen would have, not a failure to act.

Daniel is consistently wrong, fallible, and wins at cost: flubs long division (ch04), blanks on
saltpeter and the boiling-altitude rule (ch02), garbles pi/quadratic (ch02), makes-the-fire-bigger
(ch06), proves nothing with one cured boy (ch05), foolishly asks for freedom (ch09), and his
correct prediction of the manned-balloon death changes nothing because he's property (ch11). The
exceptional intellect in the range is TYCHE, not Daniel, which is the right call. Heras, Macer,
Celer, Crispus, Vibenius all read distinct and match their bible profiles.

### 6. Voice drift [MINOR watch-item]
The narration is at the ornate/aphoristic edge of Daniel's register in ch05 and parts of ch08-09
("I had the truth and no ladder to it," etc.). It hasn't broken the modern-teen voice, but per
the style guide the Latin-cadence drift is supposed to come GRADUALLY over the long book; these
early chapters should stay the most modern. Not a violation now; flagged so it doesn't accelerate.

### 7. Historical accuracy — clean for the range [no action]
98-100 AD details check out: intact Colosseum, Trajan's 99 AD on-foot adventus, haruspices,
riveted slave collars, equestrian dress, peculium/manumission law, Pytheas/Strabo/Thule
citations, no-paper/papyrus, posca, humors/miasma medicine. No anachronistic facts found. The
deliberate in-world errors (Daniel's bad geography, his germ "creatures" story) are correctly
framed as HIS fallibility, not authorial error.
