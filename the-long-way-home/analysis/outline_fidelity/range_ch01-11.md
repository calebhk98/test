# Outline Fidelity — Chapters 01–11

Method: each briefed "Beats:" item and "Plant:" seed checked against the prose.
RENDERED = dramatized on the page. PARTIAL = present but only gestured at / one-line.
MISSING = absent. CONTRADICTED = prose conflicts with brief. Additions = significant
prose material not in the brief. Quotes/locations cited for evidence.

## ch01 — Waking

### Beats
- **Wakes at dawn in a stubble field, work clothes, phone in hand, hung-over on confusion** — RENDERED. "My cheek was against the ground… little broken stalks"; "My phone was in my hand"; "six fourteen… Ninety-eight percent." Work clothes itemized (jeans, gray T-shirt, steel-toes).
- **Wrongness builds: no roads he knows, wrong birds, wrong air** — RENDERED. "There were no roads"; "these were not the birds from home… too many of them, too loud"; "the air was full."
- **He walks, finds a shabby settlement, can't read or understand anyone, people shy from his clothes** — RENDERED. The mud-walled settlement; the woman who "pulled the cloth across the door so fast it snapped"; the letters that "did not make a word"; men stepping back from his jeans/boots.
- **Then the Colosseum in the distance, intact, new, gleaming** — RENDERED. "a pale ring of stone… whole and it was new… the unbroken Colosseum."
- **Tries the phone: 98%, no signal, no GPS, 911 fails** — RENDERED. All four beats explicit: 98%, empty signal bars, maps blue dot "spun and spun," 911 "died the same as before."
- **Slow horror that this is not a hoax** — RENDERED. The "movie set" rationalization collapsing; the math ("two thousand years"); "the bottom went out of everything."
- **End on the guards approaching with spears** — RENDERED. Final beat: "four of them coming up the rise… they had spears." Ends on hands up.

### Plants
- **The phone's charge** — RENDERED (98%, repeated).
- **His hardware-store work shirt** — PARTIAL/RENDERED. Work clothes and store are vivid (stockroom, deck screws, Marcus), but the brief's specific "work shirt" is rendered as a generic "gray T-shirt with the little hole at the collar" + jeans, not a branded/uniform work shirt. The hardware-store identity is strongly planted; the *shirt-as-identity-marker* is softer than "hardware-store work shirt" implies. Minor.
- **His math-brain noticing details** — RENDERED. "I'm good at math… numbers hold still." Estimates the building's diameter/height.
- **The loneliness opening** — PARTIAL. Present in the failed calls to mom and the isolation, but loneliness-as-theme is more strongly named from ch02 on. Here it's implicit, appropriate to a panic chapter.

### Additions
- The keys (house, mom's Corolla, store back-door key) — small, effective, not briefed.
- Photographing the Colosseum ("The photo made it a fact") — anticipates ch02's selfie beat; not in ch01 brief but consistent.

### Goal (maximal modern-teen disorientation; dry panic; no info-dumping Rome)
ACHIEVED. Voice is sustained first-person teen; Rome is never explained, only sensed. No info-dump.

## MOST SIGNIFICANT MISSES (priority order)

1. **ch02 — "zippers" + guards baffled by the phone: MISSING/relocated.** The brief wants guards fascinated by zippers and baffled by the phone. Prose shows the shirt's weave but no zippers, and the phone is never seen by the guards (Daniel hides and uses it alone). The "phone baffles the guards" beat does not occur.

2. **ch09 — Macer's full name "Titus Flavius Macer" never on the page.** Brief says "named here." Only "Macer" appears. If the canon name-log depends on this chapter establishing the tria nomina, it's a gap. (Note: ch17 brief separately contemplates a Flavius/Ulpius decision; consistency worth checking.)

3. **ch06 — "paper model rises a little": CONTRADICTED.** Prose explicitly states there is no paper and substitutes a threadbare-rag toy (which does rise). Functionally equivalent, literal deviation. Likely deliberate and defensible.

4. **ch11 — "signaling" military use: PARTIAL.** Celer's interest is observation only; signaling is not raised. Minor.

5. **ch04 — "the numerals beginning to travel": PARTIAL.** Numerals demonstrated to Heras but show no on-page sign of spreading in ch04; spread deferred to ch05/ch10. As a ch04 plant, only gestured.

6. **ch01 — "hardware-store work shirt": PARTIAL.** Rendered as generic gray T-shirt + jeans; the specific work-shirt-as-marker is softer than briefed (store identity itself is well planted).

7. **ch09 — "freedman-labor question forming": PARTIAL.** Barely gestured in ch09; the substantive free-vs-slave-labor friction lives in ch10. Fine if treated as a cross-chapter seed.

Overall: fidelity is high. Every chapter achieves its stated emotional/structural goal. The
only true beat that fails to occur is the ch02 phone-and-zippers guard fascination; the
ch06 paper model and the ch09 full-name are deviations worth a human decision.
