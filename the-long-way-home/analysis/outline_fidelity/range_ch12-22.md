  flight over "the Loaf," fire reaches the cloth, the bag fails.
- **A flight goes wrong, fire or a fall, a death** — RENDERED, fully. Both fire
  AND fall: "He fell with the burning cloth coming down around him and over him,
  a hundred feet, into the trampled ground... the sound of all of him arriving at
  the ground at once." A named victim, Sabinus, dies.
- **Daniel watches his invention kill** — RENDERED. "I had built every link of
  that chain with my own hands and my own cleverness and there was a man at the
  end of it in the dirt."
- **War around him; no clean heroism** — RENDERED. "there is always work, a body
  to carry and a fire to make sure of and a column that still moved in three days."

SPECIAL CHECK — death must be RENDERED and NOT telegraphed/annotated:
- RENDERED: PASS. The death is dramatized in real time, blow by blow.
- NOT TELEGRAPHED: PASS. There is no flash-forward, no narrator stepping out to
  announce "he was going to die," no chapter-title spoiler beyond the brief's own
  "The Fall." The dread is built diegetically through Daniel's in-scene warnings
  ("I said it was wrong"), Apollodorus's and Naso's warnings, and physics, not by
  authorial annotation. Borderline: the retrospective first-person voice does say
  "I knew with my whole body what was coming" mid-ascent — but this is the
  character's lived foreboding inside the scene, not an out-of-frame spoiler, and

- NOT ANNOTATED: PASS. No moralizing gloss interrupts the death itself; the
  reflection comes after, as grief.

- **His reassessment of how far to push** — PARTIAL→RENDERED as setup. Celer

  that." The actual decision is held for ch14 (correctly, per sequencing).

Additions: Naso (the earlier maimed man) present as living warning; the
hand-over-hand rag gesture; burning the rag at the river. Strong, consistent.

Goal met: YES. One of the strongest chapters for fidelity.

## ch22 — Marcia

Brief goal: Marcia, freedwoman widow, enters — runs accounts and business with
more nerve than the men; a partnership of equals forms; contubernium then
marriage (post-citizenship); unsentimental, real, two competent people choosing

Beats:
- **Marcia, freedwoman widow, enters** — RENDERED. "There's a widow on the
  Aventine side keeps her dead husband's wharf business better than he did";
  introduced via Heras; runs Caepio's.
- **Runs accounts and business with more nerve than the men** — RENDERED. The
  "what's left, clear?" test Daniel fails; she maps his whole enterprise's
  bleeding points in one cup of wine; "I'll be a fool for any man with a stripe...

- **A partnership of equals forms** — RENDERED. She demands a share, not a wage

- **Contubernium then marriage (post-citizenship)** — PARTIAL. Marriage is
  RENDERED (freedman-to-freedwoman, lawful, with the form/witnesses). But the
