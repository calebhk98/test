# "Easy fixes a modern teen would spot, but ignored?" — audit

Premise check: the bible deliberately caps Daniel at HS knowledge (`bible/05_world_rules.md` §3) and
to what is materially/politically possible (§4, §6, §7, §9). So the bar for a real "miss" is
category **C**: something the SCENE puts in front of him, cheap to at least *notice*, that the
narration never lets him notice at all. A = notices and acts; B = notices but plausibly constrained
(fine); C = genuine miss.

| Issue | Scene / ref (manuscript line) | What Daniel does | A/B/C | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urban FIRE risk (timber insulae, firetrap city) | ch ~3, lines 540, 696, 738 ("this is the dumbest place in the world to light a fire"); his own forge/balloon brazier throughout | Explicitly thinks "Rome's towns burn… the whole place goes up like kindling"; obsessively manages *his own* fire (loses the yard L540, burns the Subura L776–836). Never proposes firebreaks, a fire watch/brigade, masonry codes, or bucket lines even after literally setting a quarter of the Subura alight. | **B→C (soft)** | He *notices* the firetrap acutely, but at a personal scale only. He has motive (he caused a fire) and the famous schoolbook fixes (vigiles, firebreaks, Great Fire 64/London 1666) — yet city-level fire prevention is never a thought. Defensible as "no standing/power early," but he never returns to it even once rich and warrant-backed. Worth flagging as a near-miss. |
| LEAD poisoning (pipes, sweetened wine/defrutum) | ch ~20, line 2006: aqueducts "ceramic and **lead** and hollowed stone… the Roman genius for moving water that I had loved **without reservation**"; lead pipe cart L1214 | Admires lead plumbing; he is literally "the emperor's water-man." Never once flags lead as poison; defrutum/sapa never mentioned. | **C — glaring** | The single most famous "thing a modern student knows about Rome" (lead pipes / lead-sweetened wine). Closest thing to a true miss. (Fair caveat: real lead-pipe toxicity is debated and the sweetening danger is the bigger one — but a *teen* would still "know" it and at least think it.) |
