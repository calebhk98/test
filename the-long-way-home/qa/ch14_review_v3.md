### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

Candidates requiring rulings:

- **L81:** "He did not ask what happened. That was Heras. A lesser friend would have asked, would have wanted the story, would have offered the thing people offer, that it was not my fault, that I had warned them, that the army overruled me, all of it true and all of it useless."
  - Ruling: **PERMITTED.** This is not the banned construction. Daniel is not redefining Heras by negation; he is rendering what a hypothetical lesser friend would have done, then reporting that Heras did none of it. The "it was not my fault" clause is inside the list of things Heras *didn't say*, framed as indirect speech. The structure works. No violation.

- **L65:** "The road south was the road north run backward and it was nothing like the road north." 
  - Ruling: **PERMITTED.** This is a productive paradox, not a correctio. "Run backward" establishes the logical identity; "nothing like" names the experiential difference. The premise is not cancelled by its own negation; it is complicated. No violation.

- **L97:** "Not a question. An accusation, almost..."

It is mild — both clauses are in the same breath, and the "almost" hedges the positive — but it matches the banned pattern exactly.
