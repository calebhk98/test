## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

**2 confirmed instances. Both are BLOCKING.**

- [x] L3: **"The first I heard, I did not hear it. I felt the city stop talking."**
  Canonical correctio: the sentence negates the sense (hearing), then pivots to a substitute sense (feeling). Exact structure of the banned pattern "It was not X. It was Y." — here in the form "I did not [X]. I [Y]." The opening line of the chapter is a zero-tolerance violation.
  *Fix:* Cut the pivot. Direct statement: "I felt the city stop talking before I had a name for what had changed." Or use the second clause alone — the substitution is more resonant than the negation.

- [x] L7: **"I did not know the names yet. I knew the weather of them."**
  Same structure: negates one kind of knowledge, pivots to a substitute. "Not names. Weather." is the implicit structure.
  *Fix:* Lead with the positive: "I had the weather of it before I had the names — the whole city had taken a step back from every doorway at once."

- [!] L109 (borderline, flag for decision): **"I did not win it. I did not best him with a clever word. Marcia bored him and Tyche hid..."**
  Two consecutive negations followed by the positive account. Structurally adjacent to correctio but the positive clause (Marcia bored him, Tyche hid the box) does not substitute for the negated items — it explains what actually happened. This reads more as self-deprecating narration than rhetorical pivot. *Ruling: borderline. Not a hard blocker, but the opening double-negation could be cut to start directly with "Marcia bored him and Tyche hid the dangerous half of my whole life." Recommend trimming.*
