Cut L67's final sentence or restructure so the physical image (hands too stiff, watching others climb) ends the scene.

2. **Marriage duration stated as "thirty-five years" three times (L79, L81, L99), but the canon arithmetic gives ~38 years** (married spring 109 AD, Marcia dies year ~147 AD). A three-year undercount that is internally consistent and repeated; therefore systemic, not a typo. Correct all three instances to "eight-and-thirty years" or "near forty years."

If the freedom used Daniel's peculium (technically Macer's money at that legal stage), the attribution may be defensible — coordinator must verify the exact chronological placement against ch18 prose.
