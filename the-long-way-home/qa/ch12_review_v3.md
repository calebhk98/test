### 3d. Narrator stepping outside time / future-vantage (ZERO allowed)

**ONE BORDERLINE INSTANCE FOUND:**

- L91: "I have met people in my life who put me inside a larger room than the one I thought I was in. He was the first one in this world, and he was not going to make it easy."

**Ruling: BORDERLINE — leans BANNED.** The first sentence ("I have met people in my life") is a retrospective generalization that arrives from the narrator's full-life vantage to contextualize the present encounter. The style guide distinguishes "I know now that X" (reports retrospective knowledge — permitted) from "I did not know then what X would mean" (dramatic-irony-by-declaration — banned). This construction is closer to the second form: the narrator arrives from outside the scene to assign a category ("the people who put me in a larger room") before the scene has earned it. The second sentence ("He was the first one in this world") is a direct comparative — permitted close-retrospect, not banned. The first sentence is the problem. Combined, the construction reads as the narrator annotating Apollodorus's significance from a future vantage.

The scene does not need this. The "He was not going to make it easy" tail is the useful part and can stand alone, attached to the preceding scene-content. The opening generalization should be cut.

**Additionally:**

- L105: "and he never knew he'd given me anything, and I never told him."

**Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is close-retrospect reporting what Daniel knew at the time (he chose not to tell Apollodorus). The narrator is not annotating future significance; he is reporting a deliberate omission. The style guide explicitly permits "he reports what Daniel knew or felt at the time." This passes.

- L49: "So I did the modern thing, the only thing I had. I filed it. Later. When you matter more. When someone has to listen. You will come back to the water."

**Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is Daniel's in-the-moment interior processing, rendered in close-present tense to show the self-instruction. "You will come back to the water" is a command to himself, not a future-vantage annotation. The style guide permits this. CLEAN.
