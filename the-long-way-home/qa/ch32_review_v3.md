### Blocking issues (must fix before next use)

1. **"The way" construction at 7 instances — cap is 3 (L5, L11, L23, L31, L49, L73x2).**
   The chapter runs 7 structural "the way [subject] does/did X" constructions against a 3-per-chapter hard cap. This is the book's most consistently flagged tic (V4_AUDIT_REVIEW_AND_CLEANUP ISSUE 3 lists ch32 at 7, the same count confirmed here). Cut L11, L31, L49, and one of the L73 instances. Keep L5 (wall-you-helped-lay), L23 (ashamed truth), and the stronger of the two L73 instances (the way she works a problem).

2. **One-sentence paragraphs at 33.3% (16 of 48) — cap is 15%.**
   Approximately 7-8 of the 16 one-sentence paragraphs are dialogue lines that justifiably isolate. Even accounting for those, the non-dialogue isolates (P20/L45, P36/L79, P4/L9, and a few others) push the count over the cap. P20 ("I let that go by, because it was true and there was no profit in saying so") and P36 ("It was the first sentence anyone had said in a month that had a forward in it...") are processing beats that can be absorbed into adjacent paragraphs.

3. **Heras's personal-diagnostic line missing (outline requirement).**
   The outline (ch32 V2 entry): "Give him one line that is specifically a diagnostic observation rather than a political opinion. 'He is thorough about his own health and will not let a physician tell him things he has already decided.' Something that is about Hadrian as a person, not as a political force." None of Heras's three speech blocks contains this. All three read Hadrian as a political/cultural entity. Add one physician's-register observation — behavioral, personal, specific — to differentiate Heras's voice from Marcia's and justify his being sought out as a Greek physician rather than just a smart Greek.
