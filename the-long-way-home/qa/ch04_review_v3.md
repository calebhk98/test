## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

**TWO INSTANCES FOUND. CHAPTER IS REVISE.**

- [x] Found: **L17** — `"made a sound - not a word, an interested noise low in his throat"`
  - Pattern: *X* (a sound) defined first by negation of what it is not (*not a word*), then by what it is (*an interested noise*). This is a textbook correctio: the "not Y, [it is] Z" pivot embedded in an em-dash-offset clause. The inline dash here is a normal dash (not em dash), so no separate em-dash violation, but the correctio pattern is present and banned.
  - **Fix:** State the thing directly without the negation. Example: "made a low interested noise in his throat, something below words." Or: "He made a sound — low, animal-curious — [cut em dash if desired: made a low curious sound] in his throat."

- [x] Found: **L69** — `"I braced for the contempt, and it wasn't contempt."`
  - Pattern: narrator establishes expectation (contempt), then negates it (wasn't contempt), and the next sentence (L71) delivers what it actually was. Classic correctio structure. The V1 PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID document lists "It wasn't conversation. It was two men passing a stylus back and forth." as the exact model of the banned construction; L69 follows the same pivot logic.
  - **Fix:** Cut the setup-negation and open directly on what it was. Example: "And at the end of it he set the tablet down and said something. Dry as a bone, whatever it was, with a thread of something in it..." — the surprise of the tone lands harder without announcing what it is not.

### 3d. Narrator stepping outside time / future-vantage (ZERO allowed)

Three constructions reviewed:

- **L13:** `"A freedman, I'd learn, born a slave and freed..."`
  - Ruling: **Permitted close-retrospect.** The retrospective narrator is parenthetically noting a fact he eventually learned. He is not telegraphing emotional significance or future danger. This is "I know now that X" form, not "I did not know then what X would mean." No violation.

- **L29:** `"He'd switched to Greek. I worked that out later..."`
  - Ruling: **Permitted close-retrospect.** Narrator reporting factual deduction made over time (the language switch). No dramatic-irony announcement. Reads naturally within the retrospective frame.

- **L71:** `"...with a thread of something in it I would spend the next year learning to recognize..."`
  - Ruling: **Borderline — flag for revision.** The narrator is technically projecting forward from the scene moment to note how long it took him to name Heras's tonal register. It is not announcing importance ("this moment will change everything") but it is using forward-projection ("would spend the next year") rather than pure retrospect ("I know now it was..."). Under the style guide's "The retrospective narrator can say 'I know now that X.' He cannot say 'I did not know then what X would mean to me'" distinction, this is closer to the permitted form. However, the phrasing "would spend the next year" slides toward a prohibited future-projection. Recommend tightening to close-retrospect form: "...with a thread of something in it I would only name a year later — somewhere between amusement and respect held at arm's length..." — or simply: "...with a thread of something in it I can name now: somewhere between amusement and respect..."

### Blocking issues (must fix before next use)

1. **[CORRECTIO — L17]** `"made a sound - not a word, an interested noise low in his throat"`
   - Zero-tolerance violation. Define the sound directly without the negation pivot.
   - Fix: cut "not a word," open on what it is: e.g., `"made a low sound in his throat, interested, below words."`

2. **[CORRECTIO — L69]** `"I braced for the contempt, and it wasn't contempt."`
   - Zero-tolerance violation. Remove the negation setup and open directly on the quality that was actually present (delivered in L71).
   - Fix: cut the setup sentence entirely. The paragraph beginning "Dry as a bone, whatever it was..." already carries the surprise. The sentence before it (`"And at the end of it he set the tablet down and said something."`) is a clean cut point.

### Revise items (should fix)

1. **[Future-vantage — L71, borderline]** `"...with a thread of something in it I would spend the next year learning to recognize..."`
   - Not a hard violation, but the forward-projection phrasing (`would spend`) slightly weakens the retrospect frame. Tighten to: `"...with a thread of something in it I can name now — somewhere between amusement and respect held at arm's length..."` The content (the year it took) can be dropped; the description of the quality is what matters.

2. **[Scene 2 close — L51, borderline button]** `"...I would have given a year of my life to know what it was."`
   - Not a wisdom button; grounded in a specific object (the quiet word). But the "year of my life" register is slightly grand for a scene-break exit. Worth asking whether ending on the zero itself — "Then he sat back and said one quiet word to himself." — is the stronger cut. The reader already feels Daniel's hunger to understand.
