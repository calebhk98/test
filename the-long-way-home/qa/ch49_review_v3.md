## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

**VIOLATION FOUND — BLOCKING:**

- **L111:** `"So I told her. Not the country. I had told her, over the decades...I told her the deciding."`
  - Structure: `I told her. Not [X]. I told her [Y].` — classic "Not X. Y." form, the banned correctio template.
  - The negation ("Not the country") precedes and defines the affirmative pivot ("I told her the deciding"). This matches the banned form exactly, as listed in PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID §1.1: "Not X. Y."
  - **Fix:** Remove the pivot structure. Lead with the affirmative: "So I told her what I had decided, not the country I was from — she had built that picture herself over fifty years of slips and half-sentences — but the deciding about the books and the glass."

**Borderline (flag, not blocking):**

- **L69:** `"So we wrote the chain into the thing as a rule, not a hope."`
  - Structure: `as [X], not [Y]` — the negation follows the positive, which reverses the classic banned form. This is less clearly a correctio than L111; it reads more as a qualification/contrast. However, PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID §2.6 says ALL variants, and naming what something is NOT (a hope) after naming what it IS (a rule) echoes the pattern.
  - **Ruling:** Flag as borderline. If the correctio sweep is strict (zero tolerance, all variants), revise to: "So we wrote the chain into the deed." The word "rule" carries the distinction without needing "not a hope" to shore it up.
