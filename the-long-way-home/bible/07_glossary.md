# Glossary — terms, names, and usage

## Latin terms cleared for use (italic on first use, then roman)
- *salutatio* - the dawn client-greeting at a patron's door
- *patronus / cliens* - patron / client
- *peregrinus* - non-citizen foreigner (Daniel's legal status at first)
- *libertus / liberta* - freedman / freedwoman
- *tabula / stylus* - wax tablet / writing stick
- *taberna* - shop (ground floor of an insula)
- *insula* - apartment block
- *domus* - a private house (the well-off)
- *triclinium* - dining room
- *atrium* - the central hall of a domus
- *garum* - fermented fish sauce (everywhere)
- *posca* - sour wine and water (soldier's/poor man's drink)
- *toga / tunica / stola / palla* - garments (toga = citizen-male formal)
- *bracae* - trousers (barbarian-coded)
- *denarius / sestertius / as / aureus* - coins (see world canon for values)
- *legatus / tribunus / centurio / optio* - army ranks
- *contubernium* - an informal marriage (used when one party can't legally
  marry as a citizen); Daniel + Marcia before his citizenship
- *peculium* - the fund a slave is permitted to use as if his own
- *haruspex / augur* - entrails-reader / bird-reader (diviners)
- *sal petrae* - saltpeter (known only as a curing/cellar curiosity)
- *aratrum* - the light Roman scratch-plow
- *clepsydra* - water clock

## Invented / Daniel-coined terms
- "The little ones" / "the invisible ones" - Daniel's germ framing (tiny
  creatures sent to climb inside you). Keep his phrasing folksy and quasi-
  religious, never clinical.
- "Thulean" - what Romans call Daniel (the man from Thule). Macer uses it half-
  mockingly.
- "Thule numbers" / "the new figures" - what Romans call Hindu-Arabic numerals.
- The encyclopedia has no grand title in-world; Daniel calls it "the books" or
  "the ladder." Romans who glimpse it call it his "Thulean scrolls."

## Daniel's own name in Rome
- He gives a name; Romans render it awkwardly. He ends up commonly called
  **Danihel** or just **the Thulean**. At citizenship (ch17), he takes the
  Roman name **Marcus Ulpius Danihel**: praenomen Marcus, nomen Ulpius from
  Trajan's gens (the emperor who freed and enfranchised him), cognomen Danihel
  (his own name carried forward). The nomen is NOT Flavius from Macer; it is
  Ulpius from the emperor. Informally he remains "Daniel" and "the Thulean";
  Macer continues calling him "Thulean" or "boy." This name is SETTLED and
  LOGGED in the canon log (DECISION ch17).

## Name stock for walk-on characters (don't reuse principal names)
- Senators: Marcus Cornelius Fronto, Lucius Calpurnius Piso, Quintus Sosius
  Senecio, Gaius Julius Severus.
- Equestrians: Gaius Vibius Maximus, Sextus Attius Suburanus, Marcus Mettius
  Rufus, Lucius Annius Verus.
- Freedmen/craftsmen: Lucius Caecilius Iucundus, Marcus Vipsanius Eros, Publius

- Soldiers: Gnaeus Domitius Tullus, Lucius Petronius Saturninus, Titus Aelius

- Physicians (Greek): Antiochus, Demetrios, Philotas, Asclepiades.
- Slaves (one name, often Greek): male - Pamphilus, Eros, Stichus, Davus, Geta,
  Syrus, Felix, Fortunatus; female - Doris, Chloe, Helena, Psyche, Eutychis.

## Pronunciation quick-notes (for narration flavor, not phonetic spelling)
- Classical Latin "c" and "g" are hard (Caesar = "Kai-sar"); "v" = "w"
  (veni = "weh-nee"); "ae" = "eye". Daniel notices this and mangles it early.
  Don't respell words phonetically in the prose; just let Daniel remark on the
  sounds when relevant.

## Banned anachronisms (quick list)
No "okay/OK", "minute/second" as clock units in Roman dialogue (Romans have no
such units; Daniel may think in them and be frustrated), no potatoes/tomatoes/
coffee/tea/sugar/citrus, no "boss," no Christian references as common (Christians
are a tiny obscure sect now), no gunpowder-in-China-by-100 as TRUE (Daniel may
wrongly believe it).
