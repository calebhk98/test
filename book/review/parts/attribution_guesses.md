## Notes

**Highly distinctive (easy):** 6, 9, 12, 14, 17, 19, 21, 23, 24, 27 — each has a strong signature (sardonic craftsman wit, soldier's grim aphorism, mercantile cynicism, imperial command, freedwoman's legalese, child's boast).

**Generic / interchangeable (hard):** 1, 5, 8, 13, 15, 18, 20 — very short, plain lines that could belong to several speakers; little texture to anchor.

**Same-handful clustering:** The terse mechanical-verdict lines (2, 5, 14) all pulled toward Macer. The aphoristic craftsman lines (6, 10, 11, 12, 28) clustered on Apollodorus. The ruler-maxim lines (22, 23, 26) clustered on Trajan, with Hadrian as the cooler-wit alternative (13, 24). Tyche absorbed the worldly/streetwise lines (8?, 16, 17, 19). These four "voices" (blunt engineer-Macer, craftsman-Apollodorus, ruler-Trajan/Hadrian, worldly-Tyche) accounted for most attributions, so several lines genuinely felt swappable within those buckets.
