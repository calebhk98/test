Daniel, Heras, Macer, Tyche, Apollodorus, Marcia, Celer, Hermes, Trajan, Hadrian, Procula, Ulpia

---

**Instructions:** For each numbered line, identify which roster speaker most likely said it. A line may occasionally belong to a minor or off-roster character, but prefer roster speakers. Judge by *manner* alone — how the person talks, not what only they could know.
