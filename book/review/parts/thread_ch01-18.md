## OPEN ITEMS AT END OF CHAPTER 18

The following threads are unresolved as of the last page of ch18 (return from Second Dacian War, Daniel at the bridge rail, intending to buy Tyche next):

1. **Tyche's freedom** — promised next after Pamphilus; not yet bought or freed (ch17 promise, not acted on by ch18 end).
2. **Tyche's formal manumission / legal citizenship** — even the informal freeing is not yet done; formal magistrate manumission with her legal freedwoman name is unresolved.
3. **The "books" / encyclopedia / ladder** — Daniel commits to writing it in ch17 (the will seed); not begun in any organized way by ch18 end.
4. **Crispus's fraud/impiety charge** — escalated in ch16, parried but unresolved; Crispus alive and more dangerous, charge not formally dismissed.
5. **Vibenius's verdict** — still undecided; he has weighed Daniel at the salon (ch16) and left without declaring. The ch25 reckoning is outside this slice; at ch18 end Vibenius has not blessed or branded.
6. **Steel / the chokepoint** — Hermes and Daniel have not solved the heat-measurement problem; steel remains inconsistent, ungated, the wall in front of every fine instrument.
7. **Gunpowder / powder weapon** — Daniel deliberately deferred in ch14 ("not yet, maybe not ever"); the third ingredient (saltpeter, written "???" in ch02) is still deliberately untouched.
8. **The ocean-gambit / western continents** — Daniel drew the Americas in ch03 and immediately regretted it; the seed is loose in Rome (a copied map exists) and sits unresolved.
9. **The contest / technology prize** — conceived in ch16, rules drafted, but Daniel is not yet free enough / rich enough to launch it; still just plans on a chalk board.
10. **Numerals / block printing spreading** — ongoing, patchy; not yet on formal contracts; Onesimus still resistant; the spread is active but not complete.
11. **The germ "little ones" framing** — Heras practices without accepting the theory; the gap between working practice and accepted theory is open.
12. **Water/sanitation doctrine** — Daniel is actively propagating it (boil-by-century, filters) but has no proof-count, no institutional adoption; remains a personal campaign.
13. **Celer's friendship / "build me something that doesn't need a man hung off it"** — Celer alive at ch18 end, the challenge still standing, unfulfilled.
14. **Apollodorus relationship** — wary peer-rivalry alive; he is grayer but the dynamic (not-friends, mutual recognition, "same knowledge" warning) is unresolved.
15. **Macer / new dynamic as client-partner** — Daniel now attends Macer's salutatio; Macer retains complicated power over future purchases (Tyche's price); the relationship shift is not settled.
16. **Divergence question** — Daniel cannot tell whether the Second Dacian War ended differently because of him; the question is open, explicitly unresolved.
17. **Pamphilus's freedom — aftermath** — freed, but the "go where" question exposed the limits; Pamphilus stays on wages; his arc (client, freedman) is underway but not closed.
18. **Naso** — alive, kept on mule duty at ch14; present at ch13; not mentioned in ch18 (he came south from first war). His arc (ruined hand, bad ankle, "you'll build it again") is open.
19. **Dead phone relic** — carried throughout; at ch17 end Daniel tucks it beside the citizenship tablet ("two relics"). Still open as a physical object.
20. **Daniel's citizenship tablet** — newly issued at ch17; the name Marcus Ulpius Danihel is locked; Daniel is the emperor's man, not yet fully settled into what that means.
21. **The tethered observation balloon** — still in army use (crated, army holds it); functions in ch18 (empty basket only). Not retired; no resolution.
22. **The manned-balloon patterns** — nailed under the crate lid in ch14 as a vow; confirmed honored in ch17 and ch18. **Structurally CLOSED** by Daniel's vow (he tells the emperor directly in ch17 "I will never fly a manned balloon again"), but the vow itself remains load-bearing going forward.
