# Canon Log — facts established IN the prose (append-only, newest at bottom)

This is the running source of truth for continuity. After each accepted chapter,
the coordinator logs any new hard facts here: names given, dates, places,
promises made, who knows what, the state of each invention, injuries, deaths.
Writers READ this to avoid contradictions and to avoid re-explaining what the
reader already has. Reviewers CHECK against it.

Format: `[chNN] fact`. Keep facts atomic and specific.

## Standing facts seeded by the bible (treat as already true)
- Protagonist: Daniel "Danny" Mercer, 17 in 98 AD, American high-schooler,
  hardware-store job, good at math, average elsewhere, sci-fi reader. Narrator,
  first person past.
- He arrives near Rome in spring 98 AD with a smartphone at ~98% that has no
  signal/GPS/wifi and ~50-100 hours of use before dying for good within two weeks.
- The arrival is never explained and there is no second miracle.
- His cover identity (developed in early chapters): a scholar from Thule, the
  semi-mythical far north. Romans call him "the Thulean."
- Germ framing: "tiny invisible creatures sent to climb inside you."
- Key people not yet met on the page until their introduction chapters: Heras,
  Titus Flavius Macer, Tyche, Gaius Julius Hermes, Quintus Marcius Celer, Lucius
  Vettius Crispus, Aulus Vibenius, Publius Cornelius Scaeva, Marcia.

- [ch01] Daniel is good at math (calms himself by estimating; pegs the Colosseum
