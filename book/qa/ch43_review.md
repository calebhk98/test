# QA - Chapter 43
VERDICT: REVISE

(Borderline. One trivially-fixable name clash on a sacred name is the only thing
holding this back. Everything else is clean and the chapter is among the strongest
domestic chapters in the book. Fix the name and it is a clear PASS.)

## Blocking issues (must fix)
- [name clash] L37/L39/L41 "an advocate named **Sabinius**" - the dead Danube
  soldier who burned to death under Daniel's manned balloon (ch13) is **MARCUS
  AURELIUS SABINUS**, a load-bearing recurring name (ch13/14/26/29 - the death
  Daniel "never forgave"). "Sabinius" is one letter and one syllable off "Sabinus."
  Putting a near-homophone on Lucanus's affable law-mentor invites a reader who
  carries the balloon death to misfire on a sacred name, in a chapter whose whole
  point is who-Lucanus-becomes vs the cost Daniel has paid. The mentor is a minor
  offstage figure; the cost of renaming is one word.
  FIX: rename the advocate to a clearly-distinct nomen from the stock, e.g.
  "an advocate named **Senecio**" (cf. Quintus Sosius Senecio in the name stock) or
  "**Fronto**" / "**Piso**." Replace all three occurrences (L37, L39, L41).

## Non-blocking notes
- [voice / narrator-outside-time] L65 "a coast no Roman would walk for four hundred
  years" and L139 "the tiles of a roof two thousand years before I was born." The
  "two thousand years" framing is established canon voice (ch01, ch24) and is fine.
  The "four hundred years" forward-glance is the one to watch - it is not a plot
  spoiler, but it edges toward the narrator-from-the-future register. It currently
  earns its keep (it lands the survey's reach against Daniel's mortality). Keep it,
  but do not add a second of its kind; one such forward-reach per chapter is the
  ceiling.
- [closing line - explicit ruling, ACCEPTABLE] "a house that was mine in a country
  that was not" (L139) is NOT a banned takeaway/maxim closer. It is woven mid-clause
  into a chain of concrete action (barring the door, picking up Marcia's dropped
  tally, knowing the hand he cannot read, lying down, the rain, not finishing the
  song, "and slept"). The chapter ends on physical action and the unfinished song,
  not on the abstraction. It also rhymes with established framing ("two countries
  that couldn't write to each other," ch17/24). It verges on thematic statement and
  the writer should not lean harder on this register in coming chapters, but as
  written it is earned. No revision required. The dropped-tally/"I knew the hand"
  beat is a fresh closing image (not phone-relic, not rail, not child-with-figures,
  not the ch40 blank page / ch41 wharf-children / ch42 bench).
- [gnomic-aphorism count] Within budget. "was as happy as a man gets, which is the
  trouble" (L37) is the one maxim-cadence line and it earns its place; it is not the
  closer and is not stacked. OK.
- [close-retrospect count] Zero of the banned "as long as I live / to my grave"
  tics. Within ration.
- [word count] 3,816 words - at the ceiling but acceptable for this register; no
  sprawl, every section pulls weight.
- [song / anachronism] Correctly handled: the song is English-origin, unrecoverable
  ("worn off the way a coin wears"), with a missing word he will never hear. No
  English lyrics are quoted, so nothing is "fully recoverable." No clock-unit
  anachronisms ("minute/second" as time appear nowhere; the "second" hits are
  "second clerk / second time / second law"). Good.
- [loneliness shown not stated] Rendered, not named: he refuses to name "the thing
  in the dark" twice (L3, L135), and the ache is carried by the hummed gap and "a
  country full of people who said such things plainly and could tell none of them
  to her" (L89). Compliant.
