# QA - Chapter 52

VERDICT: REVISE

One blocking line. Everything else in the chapter is excellent and earns its payoff; the fix is a single clause excision, not a rewrite.

## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [Rule #4 narrator-stepping-outside-time / Rule #6 dramatic-irony-by-declaration] (line 63)
  "She went back through the dark to her bed by the feel of the wall, the way the
  founder had gone down his last stair, **though she did not know that and never
  would**, and behind her in its cedar case the patient step kept the count..."
  - WHAT'S WRONG: "though she did not know that and never would" is the textbook
    form of both banned moves. "She never would" is the exact phrasing AGENT_RULES
    rule #4 lists as banned ("He didn't know how important this would be," "before
    everything changed"), and it simultaneously commits rule #6 by stating outright
    the gap between her knowledge and the reader's (she does not know the echo; the
    reader does). The ch50/ch47 "last stair / down the dark stair by feel" echo is
    real and powerful, and it LANDS for the reader on the image alone. The narrator
    declaring that she cannot perceive it is the chapter reaching out of the story
    to point at its own irony - the precise thing the brief and the rules forbid.
    The buried-phone architecture already carries the unknowing; this clause
    double-states it and breaks POV.
  - CONCRETE FIX: Cut the clause. Read it as: "She went back through the dark to her
    bed by the feel of the wall, the way the founder had gone down his last stair,
    and behind her in its cedar case the patient step kept the count of the climbing
    night..." The echo survives intact and the irony stays architectural. If a beat
    of closure is wanted, replace with a concrete detail of HER hand on the wall,
    never a statement about what she does or doesn't know.

## Non-blocking notes

- [Rule #6, borderline - recommend softening, not blocking] (line 53) The paragraph
  opens "She did not know the man who had laid the bottom of it." Close to a
  declared-gap topic sentence, but the paragraph immediately earns it back by
  RENDERING the gap architecturally (she "never once thought to wonder" about the
  sleet-word; "There was no further to wonder, and the truth of him went on lying
  quiet under the press-room stone and under the dirt of a grave she had never
  seen"). The sleet-word callback (ties ch41) is handled exactly right - shown as a
  thing she has no reason to chase, not winked at. Acceptable as written; if the
  writer touches line 63 anyway, consider recasting the line-53 opener to an action
  so the chapter contains zero "she did not know" sentence-openers. Optional.

- [Closer - ACCEPTABLE] (line 63) "...the long ladder of them going up out of the
  lamplight into the dark she would spend her life beginning to climb." A legitimate
  epilogue forward-projection, not a banned narrator-from-future tic. It is the
  book's stated thesis closing ("an epilogue, centuries later, shows someone
  beginning to climb," premise.md), a present-tense intention rendered as her own
  forward gaze (not a from-the-future assignment of weight to a sealed past event),
  and it parallels the sanctioned ch49 close ("waiting for a future digger to carry
  it up and begin to climb"). Keep it.

- [Voice] Distinct, period-grounded third person close on Procula. Clearly NOT
  Daniel's wry teen memoir voice and NOT Rufus's supercilious epistolary voice of
  ch51. Warm without naming the warmth (her wet face at line 37, rendered not
  announced; not breathing for fear of "frightening it").

- [Gnomic-aphorism ration] Within ration. "the falling is greedy" (line 19) is
  Daniel's quoted in-world cipher-line, not the narrator; "a man could lay a rung he
  would never stand on" (line 13) is the one narrator maxim, and it is NOT the
  closer. Compliant.

- [Honesty / no-magic-leap] PASS, and the chapter's strongest achievement. She
  builds ALL of Daniel's failed drawings first (line 21); they fail the way he said;
  she logs each with date and break-mode per the oath. The breakthrough is a physical
  analogy (a child's swing, isochronism felt "with a piece of cloth in her hand and
  not in her head," lines 25-31), then thirteen physical hangings with a bleeding
  knuckle (line 33), then a year of fitting still owed (line 41). ONE discrete rung:
  explicitly "not a clock yet" (line 41), still DRIFTS (line 59), keeps UNEVEN
  seasonal Roman hours (line 43). No over-reach into modernity. Exemplary.

- [Anachronism] Clean. Time reckoned in press-strokes ("four to seven," line 35) and
  in day-divisions split in twelves "the way the sundials split them, uneven by the
  season" (line 43). The longitude reach (line 59) is framed in period terms (the
  hour the sun stands high over you vs. over home), no degrees/meridians.

- [Exposed subtext / sermon] The ladder thesis stays in her hands on the gears, not
  in a speech. The quiet-war payoff (a formerly-bolted-out line - bought-in mother,
  freed in the widow's will - produces the climber) is in the texture (line 5) and
  never preached.

- [No stacked imagery / fake details] Clockwork concrete throughout: hardened pin
  pivot in two bronze cups, gate-teeth filed shallower, two sliding bobs on a
  lengthened bar, wedges and a bowl of water to level the case. The press-as-time
  is a single controlling image carried from ch50, not a pile-up.

- [Names] No clashes on the page. "Old Vibia" vs the long-dead haruspex "Vibenius":
  judged NOT confusing - different name, role, and era; Vibenius does not recur late
  and is nowhere near this scene. Procula / Saturius / Felix all clean.
