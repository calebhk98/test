  the steel. **Not just bake the iron in charcoal, but get it hot enough to go
  liquid**, in a sealed clay pot..." — This is the banned "not just X, but Y"
  rhetorical pivot, defining the method by contrast with what it isn't. Per the
  QA brief this is always blocking and forces REVISE on its own.

  on its own.
