# QA — Chapter 22

VERDICT: REVISE

## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [canon/timeline] Line 9: "She was a freedwoman of **eighteen**, her word weighed
  at a freedwoman's weight..." — TYCHE'S AGE IS WRONG. Bible and canon [ch09] fix
  Tyche at ~14 in 98 AD. Ch22 is 108-109 AD (canon header), so 108 − 98 = 10 years,
  making her ~24, not 18. (Even the log's own drift — [ch14] "~16" in 102, [ch16]
  "16" in 103-104 — would put her at ~20-21 here, never 18.) Six-year error against
  the founding anchor. FIX: change to "a freedwoman of twenty-four" (or "barely past
  twenty" if the writer wants to keep the youth note), and confirm against the 98=14
  anchor. This is the most important issue: it breaks the spine of the Tyche thread.

- [zero-tolerance / correctio] Line 69: "are building something **that isn't a
  wharf and a roof. You're building something nobody has the shape of yet**..." —
  textbook epanorthosis: the thing is defined first by what it is NOT, then by what
  it is. Banned construction ("it isn't X, it's Y"). FIX: state it directly, e.g.
  "are building something nobody has the shape of yet — the numbers and the printed
  sheets and the prize and whatever the powder turns into." (Drop the negated
  "wharf and a roof" clause entirely; the positive image carries it.)

- [zero-tolerance / correctio] Line 61: "**'A share,' she said. 'Not a wage.'** A
  wage means I work for you and you can stop me. A share means we sink together..."
  — the "Y. Not X." pivot is the correctio family flagged as always-blocking. The
  QA brief says do not wave this through as borderline. FIX: lead with the positive
  and let the contrast emerge from the explanation, e.g. "'A share,' she said. 'A
  wage means I work for you and you can stop me. A share means we sink together and
  I'll fight for the ship.'" The negation is redundant once both are explained.

## Non-blocking notes

- [announced emotion] Line 109: "It was the most romantic thing anyone had ever
  said to me and neither of us called it that." — labels the feeling at a section
  close. It is undercut and partly earned by the preceding rendered scene, and the
  chapter's true final image (line 123) is concrete, so this is not blocking. But
  "the most romantic thing" tells rather than shows. Consider trusting the prior
  paragraph and cutting to: "She was, in other words, already building the part
  that came after me, and she told me so the way she told me everything, doing the
  year's reckoning."

- [phrasing watch, not blocking] Line 107: "to become a thing the enterprise owned
  **rather than gave away**" and Line 83: "did not take instruction for free any
  more than Tyche took freedom with thanks" — both use "rather than" / "any more
  than" contrasts. These read as natural English comparisons (two concrete actions),
  NOT rhetorical correctio pivots, so they pass. Flagging only so a re-reviewer
  doesn't trip on them.

- [strength] The relationship is genuinely unsentimental and adult: the proposal is
  a business case with "I like you" as the fourth or fifth reason (line 95-97); the
  tenderness is specific and physical (the hand laid "near, not on" the table, then
  "the whole hand this time" at the close). No swoon. Marcia has real agency, a hard
  agenda, refuses a wage, keeps Caepio's separate, and sets her own terms. She is a
  partner, not a prize. Tyche-Marcia is distinct (alliance, not friendship; the
  women set the coin terms themselves and keep them from Daniel). Freedman-freedwoman
  legal reality (citizen marriage, freeborn children, widow's estate reverting to
  the husband's cousins) is handled accurately and is load-bearing, not decoration.

- [voice] Opening line ("the thing nobody tells you about getting what you wanted is
  that it arrives as paperwork") and "cold water on a burn" keep the dry modern
  register without anachronism; the Latin cadence drift is appropriate for ~108 AD.
  No narrator-from-the-future spoiler, no "as long as I live" tic, no beat-tag
  clichés (nodded/sighed/shrugged absent). Negative-narration line 107 ("She did
  not fold it into me and vanish into a wife") is doing real characterization work
  and is acceptable. No em dashes (verified by sweep).
