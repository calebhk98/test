# QA — Chapter 23

VERDICT: REVISE

## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [ZERO-TOLERANCE / correctio] Line 15: "A clock is not a thing that moves. A
  clock is a thing that is stopped, over and over, a precise number of times..."
  This is the banned epanorthosis pattern (define-by-negation, "X is not A; X is
  B" used as a rhetorical pivot). The QA brief says do not wave this through as
  borderline; if the pattern is present, the verdict is REVISE. Fix by stating
  the point directly without the negation pivot, e.g.: "A clock works by being
  stopped, over and over, a precise number of times, by something that lets the
  wheels forward one little bite at a time and no more. The motion is the easy
  part. The stopping is the clock." (States what it IS and backs it with the
  concrete mechanism; drops the "not X" frame.)

- [ZERO-TOLERANCE / correctio] Line 103 (Marcia): "You are not short of labor.
  You are short of iron and silver." Same negate-then-assert correctio pivot,
  here in dialogue. Marcia's blunt sums-voice can carry the same blow stated
  positively: "You have all the labor you will ever need. What you are short of
  is iron and silver." Or cut the first half: "You are short of iron and silver,
  not of men" is ALSO banned, so state it as a single positive claim and let the
  contrast sit in the surrounding lines (the "you own the men" beat already
  carries it).

## Non-blocking notes

- [takeaway-thesis ending] Paragraph 111 ("...The machine was right. The world
  had done its sum a long time ago... I can write the rungs down...") states the
  chapter's meaning aloud before the concrete final image. The briefed conclusion
  (books-as-rungs) is legitimate, and line 113 lands it well on a concrete image
  (the flange drawn wrong then right, the haulers outside). But para 111 currently
  spells out the thesis the scene already carries. Consider trimming the most
  explicit summary sentences and trusting the closing image to do the work; right
  now it brushes rule 11.
- [tic watch] Line 49 "There's a word for that in my old language and the word is
  most of my life here" and line 79 "the dressing-up is the part I'll answer for"
  are aphoristic close-outs. Individually fine and in voice; together with para
  111 the chapter leans on the punchy-summary cadence a little often. Vary it.
- [voice — acceptable] "I keep it there still" (49), "He will wait until he dies"
  (79) are close-retrospect, within the memoir's permitted vantage and not
  telegraphing. The flagged "as long as I live / I never" tic from the ch13 voice
  watch is NOT present here. Good.
- [honesty of tech — PASS] Nothing secretly works. Escapement stays a memory gap
  (he settles for the water clock, which keeps inconsistent time and is corrected
  by Tyche against the sun twice a day, matching canon). Telescope is a dim,
  blurry, ~2x, rainbow-fringed toy from bubbly/striae'd glass; the atom/invisible-
  ones claim is explicitly kept a LIE he will never prove. Rails WORK (4 men do 26
  men's work) and are defeated by economics, not by failure.
- [anti-slavery method — PASS] The defeat stays in EVENTS and arithmetic. Marcia
  voices it as a ledger fact ("You own the men, Maximus... You do not have to pay
  your haulers"), Daniel does not deliver a speech; his revulsion is shown (walking
  past the coffle and making himself not look, line 89; counting the Dacian haul,
  line 101). Consistent with world rule 7.
- [texture — PASS] The three failures are varied, not a flat catalogue: the clock
  is interior/memory-shame, the telescope is the public-lie/economics-of-vanity,
  the rails are the moral gut-punch. Different emotional registers; not a montage.
- [age — PASS] No numeric ages stated on the page. Year 109-110: Daniel (b.~81) =
  ~28-29, Tyche (b.~85) = ~24. Prose references ("ten years" of the germ lie since
  ch05; "three years" of the contest since 106) are consistent. No age drift.
- [canon — PASS] DEMETRIOS (glass cutter, dished iron block, wet sand) and MAXIMUS
  (quarry/stonecutter, slope to barge landing) match the [ch23] entries. Marcia
  runs the books; Tyche works the English cipher; the dead phone, Hermes, Eros,
