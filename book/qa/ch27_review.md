## Non-blocking notes

- [voice watch / escalated] The retrospect vantage appears four times and trends
  toward the per-chapter tic the watch warns about, though only one hits the
  rationed banned list:
  - L103 "I will go to my grave not knowing whether he believed my reason" - this
    is the single explicitly-listed rationed phrase ("to my grave"). It is earned
    and well placed as the one allowed close-retrospect line of the chapter.
  - L21 "I could not tell, then or ever, whether..."; L65 "in the years since I
    have heard the story told back to me as a triumph"; L103 "...and I would carry
    it." These are retrospect framing, not the exact banned formulae, so they are
    permitted - but together with L103 they make four retrospect reaches in one
    chapter. Within the letter of the rule (one rationed line), at the edge of its
    spirit. Recommend the coordinator consider trimming one of L21/L65 if a later
    pass tightens; not required for PASS.

- [name stock / soft collision] L11/L39 introduce senator "Caecilius Metellus
  Rufus." This does NOT clash with the salon host "Mettius" as separate characters
  (Mettius is not in this scene), and the function (a neutral house that "leans
  nowhere") is clean. BUT two soft risks: (a) "Metellus" is one consonant off from
  "Mettius" and a hurried reader may conflate them; (b) the cognomen "Rufus"
  duplicates glossary-stock equestrian "Marcus Mettius Rufus." Recommend logging
  the new name and, if any future chapter puts Mettius and Metellus Rufus in the
  same neighborhood of text, consider renaming one. Non-blocking now.

- [length] Body is ~4,976 words, over the ~4,000 brief and the 2,500-4,000 style
  ceiling. The prose earns it (no padding found), but flag for the coordinator.

- [subtext, borderline] L93 "I knelt in the middle of all of them ... and was about
  as alone as a man gets to be." Leans toward stating the loneliness the rules ask
  to be rendered. It survives because it is anchored to the concrete "clean rag of
  a soul" callback and the physical image of kneeling amid a crowd that loves him.
  Acceptable as rendered, but it is the closest the chapter comes to labeling.

- [honesty - confirmed GOOD] Daniel does NOT win by clean cleverness. L65-79 render

  rope"), grabbing the one true plank he has (the rag / dead children), and being
