## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [canon / age-anchor] L7 "I had carried it nineteen years." / L25 "You spent nineteen years making sure no one could follow you home." / L141 "the map had lived for nineteen years." vs. L47 Hadrian: "You have had twenty years to get it less wrong."
  WHAT'S WRONG: The chapter is set ~121-122 AD (the brief; consistent with "past forty" for Daniel b.81 and "past forty-five" for Hadrian b.76). The map was drawn in spring 98 (ch03). Spring 98 to 121-122 is TWENTY-THREE to TWENTY-FOUR years, not nineteen. "Nineteen years" is the figure that was correct in ch30 (set ~117: 98+19=117) and has been carried over stale. Worse, the chapter contradicts ITSELF: Daniel/Tyche say "nineteen" three times while Hadrian says "twenty." Canon log explicitly says "twenty years in this world since spring 98... use TWENTY from 118 on" (ch32 entry) - and by 121-122 even "twenty" is low.
  FIX: Make all four agree and match the setting. Use "twenty-three" (or "twenty-four," whichever the coordinator pins for the exact year) in all three Daniel/Tyche lines, and have Hadrian's "twenty years" become "twenty-some years" or simply "all these years" / "a lifetime" (Hadrian needn't be precise; Daniel must be). The ages on the page ("past forty," "past forty-five") are CORRECT and should stay; only the elapsed-year counts are wrong. NOTE for coordinator: pin the exact year of ch36 (121 vs 122) so the number is exact, and update the DIVERGENCE LEDGER opening (see below) with the same year.
