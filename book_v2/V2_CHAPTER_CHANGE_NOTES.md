# V2 Chapter Change Notes

**How to use this file:** Find the chapter number. STATUS tells you the level of work needed. KEY CHANGES are specific, actionable tasks in priority order. PRESERVE notes what worked and must be kept. Where a line is quoted, fix that exact construction. Blocked issues must be resolved before acceptance; non-blocking notes are recommended improvements.

## Global Prose Notes (apply to every chapter)

These tics recur throughout V1. Every V2 chapter should be checked against them before acceptance:

1. **Gnomic/aphoristic scene and chapter endings** — the book's default closing move. Trim by half. End on concrete image or action in at least 7 of every 10 chapter closes.

2. **"Not X, it's Y" correctio** — the style guide bans this construction outright. ~25-37 instances in V1, concentrated in ch08, ch11, ch13, ch18, ch22, ch38, ch40, ch42. Every instance is blocking. The test: if a sentence defines something by what it is NOT before stating what it IS, rewrite to lead with the positive.

3. **"I want to be exact/honest/plain about X"** scaffolding — this honesty-signal opener appears at nearly every scene break in ch12-22 and ch34-43. Cut the setup; begin the scene directly. Keep no more than one instance per chapter and only when the voice genuinely requires it.

4. **Narrator stepping outside time** — any line with "I have never entirely stopped," "and I have it still," "to my grave," "years later I would," or similar future-frame constructions. Permitted in close-retrospect memoir register when no plot beat is spoiled; BLOCKING when used to flag a character's significance or announce a coming event.

5. **"Numbers/loads stayed where I set them"** closing refrain — appears in three consecutive chapters (ch34, ch35, ch36). Must not recur. Replace with chapter-specific closing images.

6. **Burn-scar re-explanation** — by ch08 the reader knows about the burn. Cut all re-explanations of "the old shiny line up my right forearm" unless a new narrative function is served.

7. **Failing eyes re-explanation** — established in ch38; do not re-explain in ch39, ch40, ch41, ch42, ch43. Reference it by behavior (holding work out at arm's length) without restating it was caused by presbyopia.
