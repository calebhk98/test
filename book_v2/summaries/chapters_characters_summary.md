- **Marcus Ulpius Lucanus** (son, b. ~112): appears ch26–50, thematic lynchpin of final act. Functions: lawyer, deliberately opts out of keeper role (the "blood line declines" to make the craft line open). Must add profile.
- **Procula** (epilogue climber, gen. 3): solves the escapement/clock (the final triumph). Distinct from Ulpia (different generation, non-blood). Append-only mention in canon log; should be logged formally.

### [MAJOR] Missing Keeper-Chain Documentation

- Tyche → Chloe → (two unnamed) → Vibia → Procula
- **Chloe** (ch41–50): Bithynian slave, freed in Marcia's will, teaches two. Recurring, not profiled.
- **Vitalis** (ch39–50): block-cutter/pressman; **cases the lead copy** of the encyclopedia (ch49, buried under the press-room stone). The future literally rests on this object. Recurring, not profiled.

**V2 action:** Add a keeper-chain spine to the character bible; log Chloe and Vitalis by name/function.

### [MAJOR] One Surviving Style Violation (ch40, L71)
A banned "not X, as Y" correctio pivot remains in ch40 after QA corrections:
```
"not as a complaint, as a figure" (L71)
```
This is a **zero-tolerance style rule violation** in an "ACCEPTED"-marked chapter. Must be recast to split sentences or positive framing.

### [MAJOR] Three Antagonists' Ages Off by ~6–7 Years
For a timeline anchored at 98 AD baseline and ch30 event date ~117 (Trajan's death):
- **Scaeva:** bible ~45 → expected ~64 by ch25, but given as "57–58"
- **Crispus:** bible ~40 → expected ~59, given as "52"
- **Vibenius:** bible ~55 → expected ~74, given as "67"

Allies (Heras, Macer) track correctly. Recommend: nudge antagonists' stated ages up 6–7 years, OR adjust bible baselines, OR confirm in-world year earlier than 117. Also: Vibenius's repeated "eleven years" understates the ch07 glance's actual span by ~7 years; fix the elapsed-time count.

### [MAJOR] "Gnomic Aphorism" Density (Range-wide tic)
Across chs. 23–33 and 44–53, one-line maxims cluster heavily as default paragraph-enders:
- ch23: "which is the X-est thing there is / the only kind that Y" appears 3× in one chapter
- ch24, ch25, ch27, ch33: repetition of the cadence across chapters
- By ch40–53: "the most romantic thing," "a man wants," "it is the slow ones that wear you"

**Not blocking per-chapter** (each is anchored to action), but **cumulatively reads as authorial tic**. Recommend rhythm-variation pass: break the aphorism-as-closer pattern; vary with action-closers.

**Fire-danger awareness:** Daniel walks Rome's timber insulae for the first time, notes "ten thousand cooking fires," and ignores the civic firetrap risk. ## 3. CHARACTER ADHERENCE ISSUES (where characters drifted from profiles)

### Daniel
- **[OK]** Fallibility/non-prodigy rule is honored hard across all ranges. Losses at cost are the engine (ch13 death, ch15 steel, ch21 powder, ch40 memory gaps).
- **[OK]** Name "Marcus Ulpius Danihel" is consistent after ch17 renaming. Romans still use informal "Thulean"/"boy" per voice rules.
- **[FLAG — WATCH, not a deviation]** Latin-cadence drift: style guide requires "by late book he thinks partly in Latin and reaches for English words." CURRENT STATE: drift is *lexical loss* (English words forgotten: ch40 sleet-word, ch43 unfinishable song), NOT syntactic Latin cadence in the narration itself. Coordinate to confirm this lexical-loss interpretation is intended, not a gap in the Latinate prosody expectation.

### Tyche
- **[OK]** Freed ch20; keeper of the entire cipher by ch40. Frees-tongued anger preserved (refuses gratitude, sets her own terms, unforgiving of the slavery years). Found-family bond, explicitly NOT romance.
- **[OK]** Outlives Daniel (confirmed at deathbed, ch50; referenced ch51 "very old freedwoman… clerk fifty years"). Carries ladder forward (epilogue link).

### Heras
- **[OK]** Dry skeptic, conscience voice, quotes Hippocrates/Epicurus. "Real loss to land mid-late" achieved in ch42 death.
- **[EXEMPLARY]** Grief rendered entirely through action/absence, not announced. Matches the style guide's core instruction perfectly (ch42).

### Marcia
- **[OK]** Runs the business with more nerve than the men. Unsentimental, real affection (hand-steadying gestures, not words). Her own agenda for children (marriage-shopping for Ulpia, letting Lucanus take law). Dies ch48 (bible left open; timing is well-motivated).

### Macer
- **[OK]** Blunt, mean-funny, "the Thulean"/"boy" to the end (ch43: "called me Thulean still, as he had for thirty-five years"). Casual slaveholding is friction throughout. Dies ch48 at 96, age consistent with bible "~ch48."

### Apollodorus
- **[EXEMPLARY]** Professional peer rivalry, clash-with-Hadrian thread is paid in full (ch35 seeded, ch37 exiled/killed). Death is the cost Daniel carries (ch37 scene is the bible's "Daniel tries and fails to save him" delivered honestly—he does try, hedges the word to Theophanes, and lies to himself about it).

### Hadrian
- **[OK]** "Voracious, prickly, dangerous" all shown in ch35 live intro. Ocean program (bounded, consolidation-focused) is the only divergence; logged and plausible. Keeps Daniel's origin "like a coin he had not yet decided to spend"—dangerous patron to the end.

### Celer
- **[OK]** Dies ch29 of wound-flux under Daniel's failed care (the "floor of my gift" scene—cannot heal infection once it's in the blood). Consistent with bible "if used, this is where Daniel sees his gifts kill people." The cost he carries forward.

### Crispus
- **[OK]** Elegant, allusive, escalates from fraud charge (Strabo/Pytheas) to impiety charge. "Clever and not always wrong"—his coined phrase "barbarian's heaven by ladder" survives him (ch51) as the book's actual thesis.

### Vibenius
- **[OK]** Grave, indirect, ambiguous blessing/branding function. Never shows his hand. But see AGE FLAG above (given as 67 when timeline-consistent age is ~74).
