# Technology Schedule V2 — Bootstrapping Chain

V2 rule: "Rome lacks X" is never a permanent wall. A funded, motivated teen
manufactures the missing prerequisite. This file shows what Daniel builds, how
each phase enables the next, and what stays CORRECTLY blocked and why.

Feasibility marks: [EASY WIN] [PLAUSIBLE IN N YEARS] [CORRECTLY BLOCKED — reason]

---

## PHASE A (98–99 AD) — Survival-cheap, credibility-building
*Daniel: 17–18 years old. No language, no money, no patron.*

**What gets built:**
- **World map** — first move, no language needed. Shows two western continents.
  The "uninhabited" lie is planted here; it will cost him later. [EASY WIN]
- **Hindu-Arabic numerals + place value + decimal point** — demonstrable in
  week one. He garbles a long-division algorithm under pressure and is briefly
  humiliated before reconstructing it. Roman numerals keep prestige use for
  decades; adoption is patchy but real. [EASY WIN]
- **Germ framing + boiling water + wound-washing** — "tiny invisible creatures
  the gods send." Cheap, socially camouflaged, fragile. Filtration clarifies but
  does NOT sterilize; pair with boiling. He overrates it; he has no statistics
  and never sees the mortality curve. [EASY WIN]
- **Crude soap (tallow + wood-ash lye)** — harsh and skin-burning until ratios
  are dialed in over months. Romans prefer oil-and-strigil; this stays niche but
  it exists. A teen who knows "wash your hands" immediately asks why there is no
  soap. V1 WRONGLY OMITTED THIS FROM PROSE despite promising it in the bible.
  Write it in Phase A. [EASY WIN]
- **Lead poisoning awareness** — Daniel knows "Roman lead pipes" is the most
  famous schoolbook fact about Rome. He notices this early and acts on his own
  household first: clay cooking pots instead of lead, advising boiling wine rather
  than sweetening with defrutum, switching to unglazed storage vessels. He fails
  to change the infrastructure (too embedded, too expensive) but the observation
  must be on the page. A teen who knows this and says nothing looks incompetent.
  [EASY WIN — his household only; city infrastructure CORRECTLY BLOCKED by
  politics/cost]
- **Double-entry bookkeeping** — Daniel knows this from any economics class.
  Records every transaction as debit + credit; self-balancing ledger; systematic
  profit/loss. Roman accounting was single-entry. He uses this immediately to
  manage his own workshop and teaches it to Macer's accountants. Spreads via
  printed blank-form ledgers once the press exists. [EASY WIN]

**Enables next phase:** Numerals give him credibility and money. Germ framing
gives him patron attention. The map plants the long-term ocean hook. Double-entry
enables rational capital allocation for the workshop program.

## PHASE B (99–102 AD) — The balloon and the first spread
*Daniel: 18–21. Macer's patronage. Workshop established.*

**What gets built:**
- **Hot-air balloon, unmanned first** — a small linen bag rising on hot air.
  The spectacle that makes him un-ignorable. [EASY WIN]
- **Manned balloon [HUMBLING BEAT]** — treated linen is heavy, leaks, burns over
  open flame. First manned attempt CRASHES and costs blood (Sabinus dies). Daniel
  imposes a vow: no man hung under fire. This vow covers MANNED balloon and
  TETHERED-FLAME flight. It does NOT cover unmanned kites, glider frames, or
  observation drones. V1 wrongly extended this to a ~40-year embargo on all aerial
  work. FIX: Daniel returns to unmanned kite/glider-class work within 5–7 years,
  explicitly stating the distinction on the page.
- **Crop rotation/legumes + horse collar** — horse collar is a genuine unlock;
  spreads at the speed of peasant trust. [EASY WIN materially; PLAUSIBLE STRETCH
  for adoption]
- **Block printing / press** — screw press already exists (olive/wine); skilled
  engravers exist. Block printing achievable now. Movable metal type gated on
  antimony-alloy type-metal and uniform body height; correctly falls back to blocks.
  [EASY WIN for block printing; PLAUSIBLE IN 10–15 YEARS for reliable movable type]
- **Water-powered forge bellows** — V1 WRONGLY OMITTED THIS despite promising it
  in the bible. Rome has water mills (Vitruvius describes them; Barbegal precedent).
  A leather bellows driven by a cam on a waterwheel shaft delivers consistent,
  metered air blast to the forge — directly addressing the steel temperature problem.
  Medieval European forges used exactly this. Daniel's workshop is near aqueduct
  infrastructure. Materials: wood, leather, iron pin. One craftsman, a few months.
  [EASY WIN — write as a scene, not background]
- **Water-powered trip-hammers and stamp mills** — same waterwheel, different
  cam-and-hammer arrangement. Consistent hammering for metalwork; larger effective
  hammer mass than human strength; ore crushing; fulling for textiles. V1 promised
  this in Phase B and silently dropped it. [EASY WIN]
- **Spinning wheel** — drive-wheel turning a spindle via a belt; purely wooden;
  ~10x throughput over drop spindle. Social-friction-limited adoption (free labor
  is cheaper) — make this argument on the page the way the rail-cart argument was
  made. V1 promised this and never delivered it. [EASY WIN materially; PLAUSIBLE
  STRETCH for adoption]
- **Improved distillation with water-cooled condenser** — Roman alembics existed;
  the missing piece is a coiled copper tube in a cold-water bath (worm condenser).
  Product: high-proof alcohol for wound care. Copper tubing is bendable by hand.
  V1 promised this in Phase C and never delivered it. Move it earlier; it is the
  natural follow-on to the antiseptic/germ thread. [EASY WIN]
- **Rag paper** — macerate cloth rags in water, pour onto a screen frame, press,
  dry. Han China had this by 105 AD, contemporaneous with the story. Every
  ingredient is on Daniel's wharf. He has a printing press running on expensive
  papyrus; a teen who built a press asks "why not paper?" within weeks. V1 never
  raised this. It is the single most indefensible V1 omission. FIX: Add rag paper
  in Phase B; show Daniel experimenting with pulp consistency and sizing (starch
  paste to prevent ink bleeding). [EASY WIN]

**Enables next phase:** Water-powered bellows addresses temperature control for
steel. Rag paper cuts printing costs, enabling wider distribution. Soap + distilled
antiseptic strengthen the medical thread. The precision bootstrapping program
(Phase C) requires the workshop infrastructure now established.

## PHASE C (103–110 AD) — Pushing hard; chokepoints bite
*Daniel: 22–29. Citizenship earned. Workshop mature. Money real.*

**What gets built:**
- **Precision length bootstrapping** — V1 treated "precision" as one monolithic
  unavailable thing. WRONG. Precision length is bootstrappable from geometry alone:

  - *Vernier calipers*: two flat parallel surfaces + two scales + offset reading.
    Daniel can describe this from memory; a skilled engraver produces it in months.
    [EASY WIN in 2–5 years]
  - *Three-plate surface reference (Whitworth's method)*: three cast-iron plates
    lapped against each other converge on flatness by geometry alone. Gives a flat
    reference surface for gauging. No special materials; abrasive + patience.
    [EASY WIN]
  - *Go/no-go gauges*: iron rod ground to a reference length; parts either fit
    or they don't. Introduces interchangeability vocabulary to the workshop.
    [EASY WIN]
  - *Boring bar on a fixed guide*: more precise than freehand boring; enables
    consistent cylinder diameters for the steam-pump program (Phase D) and better
    gears for the clock program. [PLAUSIBLE IN 5–10 YEARS]

  IMPORTANT DISTINCTION the book must make explicit: precision LENGTH (bootstrappable
  now) is NOT the same as precision TEMPERATURE (genuinely hard, requires instruments
  Daniel cannot yet build). The steel wall survives; the measurement wall narrows.

- **Better steel [THE CHOKEPOINT — narrowed, not eliminated]** — Daniel points
  smiths at carbon content + quench-and-temper as the control variables.
  Water-powered bellows (Phase B) gives consistent forced air. Thermocouple
  pyrometry (Phase D) will add temperature reading. The remaining irreducible wall:
  atmosphere control (O2/CO ratio in the crucible affecting carbon uptake). Gorgeous
  one batch, brittle garbage the next. Write it this way — narrower and more
  specific than V1's total block.

- **Volta pile (wet-cell battery)** — V1 classified all electricity as "impossible
  bait." WRONG for a Volta pile. Zinc is in Roman brass (zinc ore was traded);
  copper is abundant; vinegar = dilute acetic acid. Alternating zinc-copper discs
  separated by brine/vinegar-soaked cloth pads. Each cell: ~0.76V. Twenty cells:
  ~15V. Real current — enough to shock, to electrolyze water, to demonstrate
  electrolysis. The "lemon battery" fact is high-school chemistry; Daniel knows it.
  [PLAUSIBLE IN 5–10 YEARS once he has the materials and the moment to connect the
  memory]

- **Compass-needle galvanometer** — fine copper wire coil around a pivoting
  lodestone-magnetized iron needle floating on water. Current through the coil
  deflects the needle. Daniel knows "wire + current → magnetic field" from
  high-school physics. Rome has copper wire (jewelry/weaving; drawn through a plate
  with calibrated holes). Rome has lodestones. [PLAUSIBLE IN 5–8 YEARS from Volta
  pile]

- **Magnetic compass for navigation** — the galvanometer needle IS a compass.
  Magnetized needle + floating = directional instrument. Daniel knows "sailors use
  compasses." Critical for the ocean program (Phase E). V1 never raised this despite
  running a multi-decade ocean program. [EASY WIN once lodestone use is established]

- **Gunpowder + cannon-that-bursts** — China did NOT have gunpowder by 100 AD;
  Daniel believing it did is a good confident-mistake beat. Saltpeter via niter
  beds (multi-year, dangerous slog; can blow up a shed). The cannon's job is to
  burst: demonstrate from a safe distance, argue for better steel. A reliable
  battery of cannon needs steel — many years away. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED for
  reliable sustained use — bronze cannot hold repeated firing pressure]

- **Heliocentrism** — costs only words. Aristarchus proposed it in 270 BC so it
  has Greco-Roman intellectual precedent. Have Daniel teach it through Heras, or
  plant it in the contest community as a challenge to explain retrograde motion
  without epicycles. V1 never raised this. [EASY WIN — zero materials, zero cost]

- **Patent law proposal** — Rome's "invent-and-keep-secret" economy is the
  bottleneck for tech diffusion. A patent incentivizes public disclosure. Roman
  privilegia (imperial grants of monopoly) are the precedent. Daniel argues this
  to Hadrian's legal circle. V1 never raised this despite 57 years of tech work.
  [EASY WIN as a concept; political difficulty is the honest drama]

- **Telescope/optics [HUMBLING BEAT]** — blurry, color-fringed 2–3x toy. Roman
  glass striae and bubbles are the wall. The atom-seeing microscope is a DELIBERATE
  LIE. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED]

- **Rail/wagonways in mines** — works in demo; barely adopted because slaves are
  cheaper (for ordinary cargo). BUT make BOTH arguments on the page: the economic
  argument correctly defeats labor-substitution rail; the capability argument
  (mine inclines, very heavy single loads) is defeated separately by army
  indifference and mine-owner preference. Present neither as a total defeat.

- **Writing/technology contest** — annual prize for the best tales of machines.
  Must outlive Daniel as an institution.

**Enables next phase:** Volta pile + galvanometer enable thermocouple pyrometry.
Precision length bootstrapping improves gears and cylinder boring. Patent law
proposal to Hadrian makes the institutional-innovation thread concrete.

## PHASE D (110–125 AD) — Measurement, institutions, war
*Daniel: 29–44. Peak influence, then succession crisis.*

**What gets built:**
- **Thermocouple pyrometry** — the chain: Volta pile (Phase C) + galvanometer
  (Phase C) + iron-copper thermocouple. Two dissimilar metals joined at a tip
  inserted into the furnace; Seebeck effect generates ~10 μV/°C; at forge
  temperatures (1200–1400°C): ~12–14 mV, detectable with the compass-needle
  galvanometer. Calibrate against known melting points: boiling water (100°C),
  melting lead (327°C), melting zinc (419°C), melting copper (1085°C). Gives forge
  temperature to ±30–50°C.

  Problem: iron oxidizes above ~800°C; base-metal thermocouple drifts within
  weeks to months. Platinum (Spanish gold placer mines — the grey, heavy,
  non-malleable inclusions already discarded as an impurity) survives forge
  temperatures. A platinum-iron thermocouple is achievable for a wealthy man with
  Hispanian mining connections.

  V1 blocked ALL temperature measurement. WRONG. The "no way to measure
  temperature" wall does not survive this chain. What it does NOT solve: temperature
  CONTROL (feedback loop from thermocouple reading to bellows adjustment) and
  atmosphere CONTROL (O2/CO ratio, which requires chemical instruments Daniel
  cannot build). The steel wall narrows to: "can measure temperature; still cannot
  precisely control carbon activity in the melt." More honest. More interesting.
  [PLAUSIBLE IN 10–20 YEARS from Volta pile chain]

- **Atmospheric steam pump [NEWCOMEN-CLASS — V1 WRONGLY BLOCKED ALL STEAM]**
  How it works: piston in a large cylinder open at top; steam at near-atmospheric
  pressure (barely above 1 atm) enters below piston; cold water injected, condensing
  steam and creating partial vacuum; atmospheric pressure (~14 psi) pushes piston
  down. Power stroke is atmosphere pressing on a near-vacuum — NOT high-pressure
  steam. Tolerates leaks. ~0.5–1% thermal efficiency. Pumps water.

  What Daniel needs:
  - Rough-cast bronze cylinder (~50–100 cm bore, ~1 m long): expensive but within
    Roman foundry practice for large bells and statues. Cast solid, bore out.
  - Leather or hemp-caulked piston: standard craftwork. A water layer on top seals
    the piston — no precision fit required.
  - Copper/bronze steam vessel (boiler): no higher pressure than a cooking pot
    with a tight lid; the aeolipile is already this.
  - Condensation injection: a pipe valve and a water bucket.
  - Boring bar (Phase C precision program) improves the cylinder.

  V1 had Felix test a ROTARY steam device (the wrong concept). V1 had Felix cite
  a wall (no pot strong enough) that only applies to HIGH-PRESSURE steam. Write
  the distinction explicitly: Felix is right about high-pressure; he is wrong about
  atmospheric, and Daniel shows him the difference.

  This engine is dangerous, leaky, ~1% efficient. But it pumps water from a mine.
  First historical Newcomen engine (1712) ran this way and was useful.
  [PLAUSIBLE IN 15–25 YEARS from workshop establishment — achievable by ~120 AD
  with the boring bar and water-powered bellows already in place]

- **Army applications** — tethered unmanned observation balloons (the vow covers
  manned fire-flight only), field sanitation, food preservation, mapping. Moral
  knot: his gifts help win sieges and kill people. He sees this.

- **Succession terror / rebuilding** — Four Consulars; patron-web shredded. This
  is the reset point. Ocean-map gambit deployed to Hadrian.

**Enables next phase:** The atmospheric pump proves mechanical power extraction
from heat, even at terrible efficiency. The thermocouple opens the possibility
(not the certainty) of reproducible crucible steel in Phase E. Hadrian's legal
interests make patent/limited-liability proposals timely.

## PHASE E (125–155 AD) — Institutions and the ladder
*Daniel: 44–74. Old, wealthy, slowing.*

**What gets built / secured:**
- **Patent law and limited liability proposals** — Daniel argues both to Hadrian's
  legal circle. Patent: exclusive commercial rights for ~15 years in exchange for
  public disclosure. Limited liability: loss capped at investment; removes
  personal-ruin risk suppressing large capital formation. Hadrian is a legal
  reformer with Greek intellectual sympathies. This is the right time, the right
  emperor. V1 never raised either. Write them.

- **Variolation program (groundwork)** — The Antonine Plague (probable smallpox)
  hits ~165 AD, ~10 years after Daniel's death (~155 AD). In his final decade,
  Daniel sets up an empirical variolation program — crude, ethically fraught, some
  deaths from the program itself — building the groundwork that will arrive just in
  time. "Give a small amount to prevent the severe form." He has a physician line
  (Heras → Zoticus). [PLAUSIBLE STRETCH — blocker is courage and consent, not
  materials]

- **Ocean program success** — funded voyage departs in the late 130s–early 150s,
  using compass + latitude navigation + the sealed-chronometer design (even if the
  chronometer isn't solved yet; a prize-voyage tests the hull and route). The ship
  may not return in Daniel's lifetime, but it goes. Epilogue confirms landfall and
  shows the land is inhabited (correcting his "uninhabited" lie).

- **Fire prevention proposal** — Daniel knows about the Great Fire of Rome (64 AD)
  and the Great Fire of London. After the Subura fire he caused, and after he
  becomes wealthy and influential, propose a fire-watch/brigade system and building
  codes. Show the political difficulty honestly (city of landlords and tenants;
  no one wants to pay for the fire brigade their neighbor needs). [EASY WIN as
  a proposal; adoption correctly hard]

- **The encyclopedia** — written first in English, partly in Latin. Arithmetic
  through algebra, geometry, the shape of calculus, Newtonian mechanics, fuzzy
  chemistry/biology/astronomy, political and economic theory, the shapes of
  technologies that don't yet exist. Includes heliocentrism as fact, double-entry
  bookkeeping, the patent concept, the incomplete thermocouple chain, and the note
  on the escapement Daniel never solved. Designed to be found and decoded later.
  It is a ladder, not a shortcut.

- **Institutional securing** — the printing press, the contest, the patronage
  fund, and the keeper-chain must survive him. Three physical copies of the
  encyclopedia + a lead-cased buried copy + keeper-chain deed.

## CORRECTLY BLOCKED — do not re-open these

- **Mobile/locomotive/high-pressure steam** — needs reliable boiler metal for
  100–300 psi thermal cycling. Requires reproducible steel, which requires
  carbon-atmosphere control still unsolved after pyrometry. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED]
- **Reproducible crucible steel (production-grade)** — occasional correct batches
  are achievable with the thermocouple; a production process is not. [CORRECTLY
  BLOCKED as a production process; partial as occasional achievement]
- **Useful refracting telescope** — Roman glass striae/bubbles. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED]
- **Marine chronometer (sea-worthy) in Daniel's lifetime** — spring steel for
  balance spring chains through the metallurgy wall. Clock is solved one generation
  after Daniel (Procula). Chronometer is another generation beyond. [CORRECTLY
  BLOCKED]
- **Long-distance electric telegraph** — no rubber/gutta-percha insulation.
  OPTICAL SEMAPHORE (no electricity) IS achievable and is an easy win Daniel should
  propose. [Electric: CORRECTLY BLOCKED; Optical: EASY WIN]
- **Broadcast radio/radar** — genuine reach. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED]
- **Dynamo/generator** — precision-wound coils; downstream of steel + precision
  machining wall. [CORRECTLY BLOCKED]

## HUMBLING BEATS (write Daniel losing here, on purpose)

1. **Manned balloon** — Sabinus dies; vow forecloses manned fire-flight for life.
   Vow does NOT cover unmanned kites/gliders; that work resumes in 5–7 years.
2. **Mechanical clock** — the escapement never clicks in his memory; he builds
   excellent water clocks instead. Procula solves the escapement from his cipher note.
3. **Useful optics** — blurry 2–3x toy; the atom-seeing microscope is a lie he tells.
4. **The cannon** — supposed to burst; the argument for steel.
5. **Reproducible steel** — thermocouple helps but does not solve it; occasionally
   right, never reliably repeatable.
6. **Lead pipe infrastructure** — knows the danger; acts on his household; cannot
   change the city.

## NEVER BUILT IN DANIEL'S LIFETIME
The atmospheric pump is the maximum achieved in mechanical power. Beyond that:
combustion engine, engine-driven train ("all tracks lead to Rome" — his dream),
aircraft/jets, submarines, the moon, computers. Nitroglycerin: he refuses to touch
it. Concrete: Rome beats him; he learns from them. The atom-seeing microscope: a
deliberate lie that stays a lie.
