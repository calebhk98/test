### ch01 — Waking Up [STATUS: TODO]
**Era:** 98 AD — Daniel age 17
**Finance phase:** Phase A — ~0 HS; survival, no capital
**What happens:** Daniel wakes in a countryside ditch outside Rome with no explanation, no Latin, and a dying phone. The Colosseum visible in the distance confirms where and roughly when he is. Within days he is arrested by the urban cohort and put in a cell. The phone battery runs out after roughly two weeks — he wastes the first days panicking, writes partial notes in a rush, and it dies before he has what he needs.

- The rope: arrested, hands bound, led toward Rome
- The cell: time to think; the beginning of deliberate calculation
- Phone death: final entry is something mundane, not heroic — half a recipe, a list of names
- Daniel's interior monologue establishes his register: dry, self-deprecating, profane, not a hero
**Plants:** Phone contents (partial notes on numerals, germ framing, Roman history fragments) — referenced later when Daniel reconstructs what he remembers; the world map idea forms here as a first wordless move
