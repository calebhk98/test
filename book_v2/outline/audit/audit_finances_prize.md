### Prize/DARPA R&D Model (from V2_PRIZE_INNOVATION.md)

**Core mechanism:** Daniel posts measurable outcome specs; competitors fund their own attempts; he pays only winners; if nobody wins, he loses nothing.

**Key attributes:**
- Specs are verifiable by any party — eliminates arbitration disputes he has no standing to adjudicate.
- Prize amount (~one year's skilled-craftsman wages, 3,600–4,800 HS in Phases B–C) calibrated to attract serious attempts without drawing incompetents.
- Larger prizes (10K–50K HS) for major Phase C/D achievements (pump flow-rate, lens resolution, clock tolerance).
- Posted through intermediaries — workshop name, guild masters, Marcia's press — to distance his name from the specs.
- Nobody sees the aggregate. Each competitor knows only their own spec.
- If nobody wins: spec stays open; Daniel loses nothing.

**Evolutionary arc across the book (V2_PRIZE_INNOVATION §3):**
- Phase B (first informal posts): Small prizes, 50–200 HS. Glass clarity, iron consistency, paper strength. First real result: Brundisium glassmaker wins with a decolorant technique from his father's decorative-vessel work. Daniel's realization: he's not buying invented solutions — he's buying disclosure of knowledge that already exists in Roman craft tradition.
- Phase C (formalizing): Semi-public through press broadsheets. Multiple specs open simultaneously (up to 8–10). Farrier's pyrometry table arrives as the "unexpected solution" beat. Crispus begins to understand the aggregate.
- Phase D (the model as institution): Specs for paper quality, lens grinding, steam component tolerances, alloy compositions, clock mechanisms. Lens precision prize runs 9 years before winning. Glabrio recurring near-miss.
- Phase E (cultural practice): Model has escaped the workshop. Other patrons run variants. Hadrian's/Antoninus Pius's circles adopt it for public-works competitions.

**The two prize systems (V2_PRIZE_INNOVATION §4):** Writing contest (pays for speculation/imagination) and prize specifications (pays for demonstrated technical proof) — structurally parallel, socially distinct, must not be conflated.

**Assigned chapter beats (V2_PRIZE_INNOVATION §5):**
- Beat 1: First informal glass clarity prize, ch05 background / result landing ch07 or ch10.
- Beat 2: Ten-year lens-grinding spec, posted ch15–16; won off-page ch27–29.
- Beat 3: Glabrio first appearance ch19 or ch21; recurs ch25, ch28; retainer ch34 or ch38.
- Beat 4: Unexpected farrier's pyrometry table, ch24 or ch28–29.
- Beat 5: Crispus figures out the aggregate, ch25–27.
- Beat 6: Failure mode (technique reaches wrong hands), ch32–38.
