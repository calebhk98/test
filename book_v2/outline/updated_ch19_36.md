### ch19 — The Contest [STATUS: PASS]

**Era/Date:** ~106 AD, Daniel ~25 years old

**Core beats (V1 baseline — keep unless V2 notes say otherwise):**
Daniel returns from the Second Dacian War flush with imperial favor and new standing.
He launches the annual prize for the best tales of machines that do the work of a hundred
men, vehicles faster than horses, devices that reveal the invisible. First contest entries
arrive — some absurd, some startling. The chapter seeds a civilization dreaming in
technology before the technology exists. Warm, funny, hopeful, a needed lift after the
moral weight of the siege. Felix's steam-ship entry is the recurring-thread plant that
must not be cut or reduced.
