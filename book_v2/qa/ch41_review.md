  one (line 57 is the most disposable). Flag, do not block.
- [voice-watch (1): close-retrospect density] No banned tic phrases fired ("to my

  Within the memoir's permitted retrospect, but on the dense side for one chapter.

  comes to NAMING the loneliness the brief says to render; it is singular and
  earned by the surrounding scenes (the sleet near-miss, Heras's hand), so it
  stays, but it is the one line a reviewer could argue states what is elsewhere
  shown.
