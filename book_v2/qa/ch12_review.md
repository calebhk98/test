## Non-blocking notes

  river." This is the chapter's only "nodding." It functions as a directional
  gesture (he is indicating the cofferdam he then contrasts with), not as
  filler punctuation between lines, so it survives the rule. If you want it
  airtight, swap to a specific physical beat: "...I said, before I could stop
  myself, with a jerk of my chin at the river."
- [retrospective vantage, line 121] "he never knew he'd given me anything, and
  I never told him." This leans on the close-retrospective frame the style
  guide permits (Daniel narrating from later, "in the books"). It does not
  telegraph or spoil any chapter event, so it passes. Flagging only because it
  is the single spot that reaches past the scene's present; it is earned and
  quiet, which is the allowed use.
- [voice] Strong throughout. The modern register lands ("two and ten,"
  "interstate," "I swear to God," "worked on Tuesdays") and the homesickness is
  shown, not stated. The Apollodorus loneliness beat ("I almost wanted to cry,
  which would have been humiliating, so I didn't") undercuts itself exactly as
  Daniel should. No anachronism errors.
- [history honesty] The Dacian War, Decebalus, the cofferdam/pile-driver
  bridge, and the latrine/flux reality are delivered through Daniel watching
  and reacting, never as a tour-guide lecture. He explicitly knows "a little"
  (he half-remembers a video of Roman bridge-building) and is rendered as a
  tool, not an oracle. Good.
- [tech honesty] Nothing "just works." Wind defeats the bag, the stake pulls,
  a near-loss over the camp, the army's escalating demand — all cost. The
  observation balloon's "still air only" limit is dramatized, not asserted.
