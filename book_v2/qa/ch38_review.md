## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [PUNCTUATION/epanorthosis - ZERO TOLERANCE] (line 79) "the way you keep men at a
  thing for a hundred years after the man who started it is dead **isn't a man, it's
  a body**." This is the banned correctio/epanorthosis pivot ("it isn't X, it's Y").
  FIX: state it straight - "...a hundred years after the man who started it is dead
  is a body, not a man. A thing with a name and money of its own..." (or simply
  delete the "isn't a man" half and lead with "It has to be a body. A thing with a
  name and money of its own and rules and a door...").

- [PUNCTUATION/epanorthosis - ZERO TOLERANCE] (line 65) "Then **it isn't the things**,"
  I said slowly. "**It's whoever does the things after.**" Same banned pivot (it isn't
  X, it's Y) split across two sentences; it lands as the rhetorical hinge of the whole
  scene, which makes it conspicuous. FIX: drop the negation. e.g. "Then the work isn't
  the things at all," collapses to the same tell; instead: "Then the work is whoever
  does the things after me," I said slowly. Or: "Then I've been building the wrong
  thing. The thing to build is whoever does the things after."

- [CANON / internal age contradiction] (lines 43, 93, 95) The chapter pins Daniel at
  "**three-and-forty**" (line 43), which by the ANCHOR (b.~81) sets the year at ~124.
  But Ulpia is "**nine that year**" (line 93) AND "**born the year I came back broken
  from the East**" (line 95). Daniel returned from the East in ch31 = late 117 / early
  118 (canon). A child born ~118 is 9 in ~127, when Daniel would be ~46, not 43. The
  two facts cannot both hold. (Note: the brief's intended b.~116 is itself impossible
  - Daniel was in the East from ~113 to 118, so he could not father a 116-born child;
  the writer's "born the year I came back" is the biologically correct anchor, but it
  forces a later year than "three-and-forty.") FIX (pick one, consistently):
  (a) keep "born the year I came back from the East" (~118) and make her age ~6 and
      Daniel ~43-44 (~124) - drop the times-table "nine" framing or have her younger;
  (b) keep her at nine doing the nine-course and set the year ~126-127, making Daniel
      "five-and-forty" (within the brief's ~124-126 / Daniel ~43-45 window at its top
      edge) - but then "born the year I came back from the East" still yields age ~8-9,
      so this is the cleaner fix: change "three-and-forty" to "five-and-forty" / "near
      forty-six," and change Heras's "You are forty-three" (line 89) to match.
  The internal Daniel-age / daughter-age / homecoming-birth triangle must be made
  arithmetically consistent before pass.

while; ch39-41 must vary away from it entirely or the larger pattern re-forms.
