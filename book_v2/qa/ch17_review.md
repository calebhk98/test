# QA — Chapter 17
VERDICT: REVISE

## Blocking issues (must fix)
- [ZERO-TOLERANCE / correctio] Line 129: "and under his voice was **not sentiment**, Macer did not stock it, **it was arithmetic**, the swift remaking of a sum." This is the banned "it's not X, it's Y" epanorthosis with an interrupting clause. The pattern is present, so per the QA brief the verdict is REVISE regardless of how good the line is. Fix by stating it directly: e.g. "and under his voice was the swift remaking of a sum. Macer did not stock sentiment. He'd lost the asset... and gained in its place a client..." Let the surrounding concrete arithmetic (lost asset / gained client) carry the meaning without the negate-then-correct pivot.
