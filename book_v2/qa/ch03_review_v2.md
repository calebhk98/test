## 3. Prose Tics — Zero-Tolerance Sweep

### 3a. Correctio / "Not X, it's Y" (ZERO allowed)

Two instances found requiring ruling:

- [x] Found: **L17** — "It was not a victory but I had eaten."
  - **Ruling: BORDERLINE / REVISE.** This is a soft correctio form ("not X but Y"). It names what the thing was not, then pivots to what it was. The template reads as: "Not [victory]. [Had eaten]." By strict construction this is a banned form. The prose is otherwise good; the fix is simple — "I got most of the grain. The liquid pooled underneath." — then stop. The "not a victory" tag is the only editorial comment in an otherwise well-behaved paragraph. Recommend cutting.

- [x] Found: **L39** — "I'm not an artist." (opening of paragraph)
  - **Ruling: PERMITTED.** This is a direct self-statement of fact, not a rhetorical correctio that cancels a premise in order to reframe it. "I'm not an artist" does not follow the "not X, it's Y" pivot structure; it is a standalone assertion. No violation.
