## Non-blocking notes
- [rule 2 watch] Line 5: "Marcia did not weep over him and did not coo at him and ran the books from her bed..." flirts with negative narration (defining her by what she didn't do). It survives because it's immediately grounded in rendered action (running the books from bed, the hand-flat-on-the-back image steadying "a cup you've set too near an edge"). Leave it, but writers should not repeat the move.
- [retrospect density] Three close-retrospect framings cluster: line 3 "I have always held those two things in the same hand," line 31 "Scaeva's face did something I have turned over many times since," line 65 "whether that was chance I could never decide." None are on the ESCALATED banned list ("to my grave / as long as I live / I have never / the day I die / I have it still") - that list scores ZERO here, which is the goal. But the looser retrospect tic is present three times; keep an eye on it cumulatively across Part IV.
- [line 11] "did not look like a knife... nothing dangerous in Rome looks like the thing it is." Reads as earned voice/characterization (soft surface, lethal interior), not banned negative narration. Fine.
- [craft, positive] Scaeva's sulfur-favor trap (lines 29-31) is excellent honest plotting: Daniel thinks he's spending a denarius and has actually handed over import volume, gate, and the lever of his own temper. The "map of where my walls were thin" image lands the threat without exposing subtext.
