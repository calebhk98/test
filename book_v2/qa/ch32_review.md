## Non-blocking notes
- [continuity/numbers, line 49 & 65 vs line 91] The chapter says Daniel has spent
  "nineteen years" in Rome (twice) but also dates the ch03 magistrate's-wax map to
  "twenty years gone" (line 91). Both describe the spring-98-to-spring-118 span. ch30
  (summer 117) already used "nineteen years"; by winter's-end/spring 118 it should
  read closer to twenty. The two figures sit in the same chapter for the same span.
  Fix: make both "twenty years" (or "near twenty"), or keep the round "twenty" for the
  map and let the two "nineteen" lines become "twenty." Cosmetic, not a contradiction
  with hard canon, but mildly self-inconsistent within the chapter.
- [age edge, lines 95, 105] Lucanus is "five" here; the QA brief's age anchor lists
  him at ~6 (b.~112) for 118. "Five" matches ch31's "~5" and is defensible at the low
  edge if born late 112 and it is early spring, so this is consistent with prior prose
  rather than wrong. Flagging only so the coordinator confirms which figure the series
  should standardize on (recommend keeping "five" for ch31/32 consistency, or aging
  him on the page going into ch33+).
- [phrasing, line 17] "He was abandoning the east. Not losing it... Giving it." Reads
  as antithesis but is doing real work (the city forgives losing, not the deliberate
  giving) and states the precise point with a concrete reason, so it does not trip the
  epanorthosis ban. Left as-is; noting it because it is the closest call in the
  chapter and a reviewer should eyeball it.
- [phrasing, line 83] "it was not a question to me, it was Marcia turning a thing she
  had already started turning." Mild "not X, it was Y" shape applied to behavior. Not
  the banned rhetorical pivot (it characterizes her habit, not defining a thing by
  negation), but it could be tightened to "She was not asking me; she was turning a
  thing..." if the coordinator wants zero proximity to the pattern.
- [craft, positive] Heras's "the most dangerous master a clever man ever serves is
  the one clever enough to know exactly how clever the servant is being" and Marcia's
  four-line "map" are the recalibration engine done right: in-scene probing, fallible
  Daniel rattled at starting over, no tidy monologue. The "you've worn that buckle to
  a shine" line renders the Celer grief through object without naming it. Strong.
