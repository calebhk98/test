## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [ZERO-TOLERANCE / correctio "not X but Y"] (line 23) "Reading had been a wall
  with a narrow gate and the gate was money, and I had not knocked the wall down,
  nowhere near, but I'd cut one extra notch in it, low down..." — This is the
  banned correctio pivot: the achievement is defined by negation ("had not knocked
  the wall down") then flipped with "but" to the positive. Per the brief this is
  always blocking.
  Fix: state it directly. e.g. "The wall was still standing. I'd cut one extra
  notch in it, low down, where the small people could get a hand through."

- [ZERO-TOLERANCE / correctio, defining by what it isn't] (line 51) "given for a
  story. Not gods and wars, Rome had a thousand poets choking the salons with
  those. A story of a machine that does the work of a hundred men." — Elliptical
  correctio: the contest subject is fixed by negation ("Not gods and wars") then
  the positive. Banned construction (define a thing by what it isn't).
  Fix: cut the negation and let the positive carry it, or recast the contrast as a
  plain statement. e.g. "given for a story. The salons already had a thousand poets
  on gods and wars. This was for a story of a machine that does the work of a
  hundred men."

- [Banned move #4: narrator stepping outside time / spoiler] (line 79) "and he was
  good, I'll give the dead man that" — The retrospective narrator labels Crispus
  "the dead man" mid-scene while Crispus is alive and at the height of his menace.
  This leaks future plot (Crispus dies, and predeceases Daniel's telling) and
  undercuts the brief's requirement that Crispus leave MORE dangerous and the threat
  UNRESOLVED. The style guide forbids using the retrospective vantage to spoil.
  Fix: drop the future reference. e.g. "and he was good, I'll give him that, he
  moved like a man who had spent his life being watched and decided to enjoy it."
