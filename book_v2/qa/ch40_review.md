## Non-blocking notes

- [epanorthosis - borderline] L71 "she said so to me one evening over the third copy, not as a complaint, as a figure." This is the banned "not X, [but] Y" correctio pattern (defining the statement by what it isn't, then restating). It is mild and characterful, but it matches the construction the rules target. Suggested fix: "she said so to me one evening over the third copy, flatly, the way she stated a figure." Recommend changing.

- [gnomic-aphorism stacking - VOICE WATCH (3)] Three summation maxims appear: L33 "a clean false rung is the worst thing a ladder can have" (the strongest, earned, immediately rendered concrete - keep this one); L49 "the difference between those two mornings is the whole of what these books were a race against" (takeaway-style section closer); L81 "the bottom of a ladder is the part a man keeps longest, the part they put in him before the world had a chance to take it back out." The escalated watch rations these to ~one per chapter. L33 earns its keep. Consider trimming or concretizing one of L49 / L81 so the cadence does not read as a mannerism. Non-blocking but recommended.

- [excerpt instruction phrasing] L59 "should not believe the rooms are there because the Thulean says so, but should build the glass and look" - a "should not X but should Y" shape. Reads as genuine instruction in the permitted encyclopedia register (an imperative contrast, not a rhetorical definition), so acceptable; flagging only for awareness.

- [closing-image lamp motif] L83 closes on the deliberately blank chemistry page ("white and waiting") then "went up to the house, where Marcia had kept a lamp." But ch38 also closed on a lamplight image (Daniel at the lamplight edge watching Ulpia). The recurring lamp is mild; the load-bearing image here (the blank page) is distinct, so this passes - just keep lamps off the next closer.

- [close-retrospect count] Exactly ONE close-retrospect line: L41 "and it has not come since." Earned, mid-chapter, not the opener/closer. Within ration. Confirmed (matches the brief's "report says one").
