# QA - Chapter 49

VERDICT: REVISE

(Strong chapter on craft and dramatization. Blocked solely on a cluster of
character-age / elapsed-time errors that contradict the AGE ANCHOR for ~150 AD.
Every age on the page is wrong, and they are not even internally consistent.)

## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [canon/age - HARD] Line 57: "He came in his advocate's clothes from the
  Basilica Julia, six-and-twenty and stiff with me..." Lucanus is b.~112; in
  ~150 AD he is ~38, not 26. "Six-and-twenty" was his age in ch46 (138 AD) and
  appears to have been carried over by 12 years. Fix: "eight-and-thirty" (~38).

- [canon/age] Line 9: "She was nine-and-twenty by then..." Ulpia Severa is
  b.~late 118; in ~150 she is ~31-32 (she was ~29 in ch48's 147). Twenty-nine
  is too young by ~2-3 years. Fix: "two-and-thirty" (~32), or "past thirty."

- [canon/age] Line 47: "Tyche was sixty, and old..." Tyche is b.~85; in ~150 she
  is ~65, not 60. (Sixty was her ~147 age in ch48.) Fix: "Tyche was five-and-
  sixty."

- [canon/elapsed-time] Lines 75, 83, 87, 95: "I have known you sixty years" /
  "kept it sixty years" / "in sixty years" / "for sixty years." Daniel met Tyche
  in ch09 (~99 AD). In ~150 that is ~51 years, not sixty. (The phone, kept since
  98, is ~52 years - "fifty years" at line 69 is fine as Daniel's rounding, but
  "sixty years" of acquaintance is wrong by ~9-10 years and is repeated five
  times.) Fix: "fifty years" / "half a century" throughout, and reconcile with
  line 69's "fifty years" so the two figures agree.

  NOTE FOR COORDINATOR: the four age/time figures above are not merely off the
  anchor, they disagree with each other (Ulpia=29 implies ~147; Lucanus=26
  implies ~138; Tyche=60 implies ~145). The chapter reads as if drafted at an
  earlier year and not re-aged to 150. All four must be set from the anchor for
  150 AD.

## Non-blocking notes

- Gnomic-aphorism density exceeds the rationed "~one per chapter" (VOICE WATCH 3).
  Count: epigraph L3 ("does not stop because his eyes have"); L41 "A finished-
  looking book... has killed more climbers than any honest gap"; L59 "Some things
  you secure. Some things you only confess you could not." (this one also closes
  its section). For a coda one or two can be earned, but L59 as a section-closer
  maxim is the weakest; consider cutting it to a concrete beat.

- Borderline exposed-subtext (the two-countries / birth theme). L51 "the ladder
  I had built was for whoever was hungry, and it would not check a man's birth
  before it let him up. That was the whole quiet argument of the thing" states
  the thesis fairly plainly. It is partly defused by "I never once said it aloud
  to him," but it edges over the line the brief warns about. Consider trimming
  "That was the whole quiet argument of the thing" and letting the behavior carry
  it.

- Close-retrospect / "never once...and he never once asked" (L51) plus "the
  closest we ever came in sixty years" (L87) are within memoir retrospect and
  not telegraphing, but they cluster. Fine if the age fixes thin out the
  repeated "sixty years."

- L17 "a column wide" carry-error caught by Tyche is a nice concrete render of
  Daniel's failing ear; good, not a flag.
