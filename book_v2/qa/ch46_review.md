## Blocking issues (must fix)

- [VOICE WATCH (5) closing-image mannerism] Lines 55-61, the closer.
  TWO watched patterns converge here:
  (i) It is a YOUNG-PERSON (Ulpia) + FIGURES (the African soundings) + by-LAMP image -
      structurally the ch38 closer ("Daniel at the far edge of the lamplight watching
      Ulpia fill the times-table page"), which the canon log flagged with an explicit
      instruction: "future chapters must vary AWAY from child-with-figures closers."
  (ii) ch45 closed on a STOREROOM LAMP ("Marcia at the storeroom lamp... sitting until
      the oil burns low"). ch46 closing on Ulpia at the storeroom table with "the lamp
      drawn close," Daniel bent "over the lamp" to read a figure, makes this the SECOND
      CONSECUTIVE lamp-closer in the same room with the same posture (Daniel/a woman bent
      over figures by lamplight). The escalated watch says "VARY the closing image type
      chapter to chapter." A reader feels the identical prop and stance back to back.
  WHAT IS WORKING AND MUST BE KEPT: the survey column "not closed, only laid down"
  (line 61) is the chapter's whole thesis made physical - the ocean program paused-not-
  killed, the handoff to a generation he will not see, his own mortality. That object is
  load-bearing and distinct in meaning and is NOT the Lucanus-buckle template. The problem
  is its FRAMING (the lamp + "reading figures by lamp"), not the laid-down leaf.
  CONCRETE FIX: keep the laid-down open column as the closing image; strip the lamp and
  de-emphasize the figures-reading so the leaf, not "girl reading numbers by lamplight,"
  is what the reader sits with. Suggested fresh closer: end on Ulpia setting the leaf with
  its open columns face-up on the table for the morning - true to the last headland, ruled
  off but not totaled - and Daniel weighting it with something concrete and ordinary so a
  draft off the wharf does not take it (a stylus laid crosswise, a stone, the loom-weight
  she keeps for the purpose). The graded-road-against-a-traveler line (already in line 59)
  can do the thematic work; the final beat should be a hand laying a thing down and leaving
  it where it can be found, in daylight, NOT a bent-over-the-lamp tableau. (Avoid: another
  lamp, the phone relic, a roof/boat-rail, a maxim, and any "child/young-person doing
  figures" frame.)

## Non-blocking notes

- ZERO-TOLERANCE scan CLEAN: no em dashes (0); no "not X but Y"/correctio pivots. Line 51
  ("Not all at once and not cleanly, but I let it go...") is NOT the banned form - same
  subject/verb on both sides, a natural concessive qualifier ("though gradually, I did let
  go"), explicitly permitted. No "minutes/seconds" anachronism; all time units (hour, month,
  season, morning, yesterday, forty years) are period-appropriate.
- Future-vantage lines: "as far as I would ever know" (l.27) and "no one would ever again,
  in my lifetime" (l.47). Both render the PRESENT-moment reckoning (what he can/cannot know
  now), not the banned "little did I know" spoiler, and "in my lifetime" is load-bearing for
  the mortality beat. At the ration ceiling (watch (1) = one per chapter). Acceptable as is;
  if trimming for safety, the cleaner cut is "as far as I would ever know" in l.27.
- Gnomic maxims: "the answer that ends projects" (l.47) and "an emperor's death is a thing
  that closes columns" (l.57). Two, neither in a single paragraph, neither the closer. At
  the watch (3) ceiling but within it. Fine.
- Detachment is rendered, not named, and EARNED: the press "did not need me" (l.9), going
  back to the pearwood (l.7), the cold arithmetic of insulated institutions (l.39). The
  ch30 contrast is achieved through ACTION/calm, with only the opening "which is not a thing
  I could have said the other time" (l.5) gesturing at it - that is a permitted memoir
  comparison, not the banned "unlike when Trajan died" subtext-exposure, and it names no
  emotion. Good.
- Hadrian feeling is genuinely complex and rendered not named: relief admitted plainly
  (l.31), grief for the only matched mind, the wariness "with nowhere to go" standing crooked
  (ll.25-29) - "the relief and the grief were the same size... and I could not get a knife
  between them" (l.31). Earns it.
- Length 3,359 words: inside the 3,000-3,800 target.
