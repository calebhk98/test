# Pacing Map — Tension Scores 1–10
Rubric: score reflects STAKES + CONFLICT + MOMENTUM implied by the beat summary; physical danger, irreversible loss, and unresolved threat push scores up; quiet domestic, reflective, or connective chapters pull scores down; mid-range scores mark chapters where tension is real but not yet crisis-level.

## PACING FLAGS

### 1. Pacing Valleys (4+ consecutive chapters below 5)

**ONE VALLEY FOUND: ch38–ch41** (4 chapters, scores: 4, 3, 4, 4)

This is the novel's only true pacing valley by the strict criterion. It falls at the Part V pivot: ch38 (institutions plan, aging begins — deliberately quieter pivot), ch39 (institution-building, methodical and warm, lowest point at 3), ch40 (encyclopedia race against fading memory, intellectual rather than confrontational), ch41 (teaching the cipher, warm and forward-looking). The valley represents a purposeful authorial gear-change into legacy-building mode, but four consecutive sub-5 chapters risks reader disengagement. The valley is immediately deepened by ch43 (score 3, domestic peace chapter) just two chapters after the valley exits at ch42 — effectively creating a broader soft zone across ch38–ch44 (average 4.1) even if it narrowly avoids a single unbroken run.

Note: ch05–ch06 (scores 4, 4) and ch23–ch24 (scores 4, 4) are near-misses at only two consecutive sub-5 chapters each.

### 2. Unsustainable Peaks (back-to-back 9–10 scores)

**NONE FOUND.** The single 9 in the sequence (ch13) is isolated — ch12 scores 6 and ch14 scores 5. No two chapters scoring 9 or 10 appear consecutively anywhere in the 53-chapter arc. Peak exhaustion is not a structural risk here.

### 3. Isolated Very-Low Dips (scores 1–3)

Four chapters score 3; none score 1–2. All four are structurally intentional breathers or connective chapters:

- **ch19 (3)** — Sandwiched between a war-chapter (ch18=7) and a freedom-purchase chapter (ch20=5). The contest launch is the novel's most optimistic set-piece, a deliberate exhale after the siege of Dacia. The drop is steep (7→3) but short-lived; not problematic in isolation.
- **ch22 (3)** — Follows the cannon-demonstration crisis (ch21=7) and leads into a run of low-4s (ch23–24). Marcia's introduction and the brisk marriage are connective tissue. The sequence ch21(7)→ch22(3)→ch23(4)→ch24(4) creates a soft trough mid-novel, though it resolves sharply upward at ch25(7).
- **ch39 (3)** — The bottom of the ch38–ch41 valley described above.
- **ch43 (3)** — Falls between ch42(6) and ch44(5), in the broader soft zone of Part V. The earned domestic peace chapter is the novel's warmest beat by design, but its placement so close to the ch38–41 valley means the reader has had very little tension for roughly six chapters (ch38–44, only ch42 breaks above 5).

None of these dips reads as structurally odd in isolation, but the clustering of three 3s within Part V (ch39, ch43 close together) reinforces the low-energy feel of that section.

### 4. Overall Tension Shape and Late-Book Assessment

**Shape: Uneven climb → single sharp peak → plateau with troughs → strong middle → long soft descent → final spike → quiet close.**

- Part I (ch01–12, avg 5.5): A steady moderate opening with oscillating peaks and dips; establishes stakes without crisis.
- Part II (ch13–21, avg 5.8): The novel's first and only 9 (ch13, Sabinus's death) is its single sharpest peak. The part averages well but contains two 3s (ch19, ch22 straddles into Part III).
- Part III (ch22–28, avg 5.3): Opens with a 3, recovers through the impiety-trial climax at ch27(8).
- Part IV (ch29–37, avg 6.7): The novel's strongest sustained section — three 8s (ch29, ch33, and the approaching ch50), bracketed by consistent 6s and 7s. This is where the arc peaks structurally.
- Part V (ch38–44, avg 4.1): The most dangerous section. The transition to legacy-building loses nearly 2.5 points of average tension from Part IV. The valley here is real and long.
- Part VI (ch45–49, avg 5.6): A partial recovery — ch45(7) and ch48(7) are genuine peaks — but ch46–47 at 5/5 and ch49 at 4 create a sagging bracket around them.
- Part VII (ch50–53, avg 6.2): The ch50(8) death chapter rescues the endgame emotionally. The final three chapters (ch51–53, scores 5/6/6) are a quiet, unresolved coda.

**Late-book descent risk (Parts VI–VII):** Partially mitigated. The concern is real for Part V, not Parts VI–VII. Part V's 4.1 average is the genuine low-tail risk — eleven chapters from the end, the novel spends seven chapters mostly below 5. Parts VI–VII recover adequately (averages 5.6 and 6.2), and ch50's death-chapter spike provides a true emotional climax. The coda (ch51–53 at 5/6/6) is subdued but appropriate for an elegiac close. The late-book problem is concentrated in Part V, not the finale.
