## 3. Scene-Ending Audit

The chapter has five sections separated by "---":

- **Section 1 ending** (the voyage, first ---): "I slept on deck against the cargo and did the only arithmetic the boat would let me do, the run of the keel against the run of the days, and every morning I made the number of days remaining smaller by one and was no calmer for it." → IMAGE/ACTION CUT (good). Ends on Daniel doing arithmetic on a boat deck. Sensory, specific, no wisdom button. Good.

- **Section 2 ending** (the return to Rome / gates and limed walls, second ---): "You felt the city find nothing solid and shift its weight, the way a man going downstairs in the dark puts his foot for a step that is not there." → IMAGE CUT (good). The sensory metaphor of the missing stair is one of the chapter's best lines. Not a wisdom button — it renders the disorientation concretely.

- **Section 3 ending** (return to the wharf yard, third ---): "I stood in the ink smell and was, for one breath, less alone than I had been in three years, and could not have said so to her without ruining it." → NEAR-WISDOM BUTTON. "Could not have said so to her without ruining it" summarizes the emotional subtext of the scene. Compare to PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID style: this sentence names what cannot be said, which is a form of gnomic statement about emotional truth. The image before it (Tyche going back to her form, "The box is where you left it") is stronger; the added clause "could not have said so to her without ruining it" over-explains the restraint that the prior scene already demonstrated. Recommend cutting.

- **Section 4 ending** (Macer's house, fourth ---): "It was the most honest thing he had ever said to me, and it was a coward's promise, and I took it, because it was true and because slow was a real gift in a city where the only currency left was time." → WISDOM BUTTON. "Slow was a real gift in a city where the only currency left was time" is a packaged aphorism — portable, self-contained, summarizes the scene's meaning. Cut the aphorism and end on Macer's promise: "I took it, because slow was more than he had given me on the Caelian, and we both knew it, so I said nothing more." (Still cuts the aphorism; what matters is losing "a city where the only currency left was time.")

- **Chapter ending**: "...and a beard I half-remembered being insulted in a forum, somewhere east of here, on a road, coming." → IMAGE FRAGMENT. The chapter ends with Hadrian as an incompletely-resolved memory — the beard, the insult, the direction of travel. This is the correct image-close called for in the change notes ("a reign I knew nothing of was walking toward us at the speed of an army"). The actual final clause is "on a road, coming" — minimal, movement-forward, no wisdom. Clean.

**Wisdom-button count for this chapter:** 2 (Section 3 near-WB; Section 4 confirmed WB). Chapter ending is clean. One over limit.
