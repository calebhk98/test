## 5. Voice & Character Issues
- Tyche in the night scene: "He doesn't want a better way to add. He wants to know the true shape of a thing too big to hold in one head... He's not a hungry man. He's a man taking an inventory." — This is very articulate and analytically complete speech. Tyche's mature register supports sharper speech than her early register; however, this is a four-sentence analytical observation with three separate sub-propositions. Per the CHARACTER_VOICE_GUIDE.md, mature Tyche "says it flatly, the way she states a figure" and uses "And the army" (two words) as her mode. This speech is in Marcia's architectured-argument register rather than Tyche's fragment-and-stop register. The "He's not a hungry man. He's a man taking an inventory" construction is especially close to Marcia's mode. SHOULD FIX — reduce to Tyche's characteristic shorter form.

- Carter confrontation: Marcia's voice in the "eleven loads" speech is exemplary — this is cited in CHARACTER_VOICE_GUIDE.md as a Tyche example but here it is specifically Marcia speaking, and it is architectured argument (every side, structured) which is exactly Marcia's mode. Good. Note: the guide attributes a similar line to Tyche ("The eleventh load") — in the chapter the finding of the eleventh load is split between Marcia and Tyche (Marcia names the eleventh, Tyche identifies the double-payment). Consistent with their distinct registers.
