## SUMMARY OF TOP FINDINGS

1. **Playground placement conflict**: V2_ULPIA_EDUCATION.md places the playground "noticing" beat in ch35; the outline places it in ch38. These are two V2 authoritative files directly contradicting each other. Ch35's outline entry does not mention the playground at all. A writer following the outline will place it in ch38; a writer following V2_ULPIA_EDUCATION.md will place it in ch35. This needs a coordinator decision.

2. **Several Ulpia education beats are absent from the outline**: Five beats specified in V2_ULPIA_EDUCATION.md's Summary Table do not appear in the outline: the ch36 English-from-birth line; the ch44 encyclopedia-editing/lossy-memory-list beat; the ch46 survey instruction to Ulpia; the ch47 public English conversation; and the ch49 final working session in English. These are "New addition" beats in the reference file, not optional. Writers following only the outline will miss them.

3. **Blow-up moment has no single assigned chapter**: The outline lists ch39, ch43, and ch48 as candidates without resolving which one carries the scene. Ch39 and ch43 are also framed in the outline at a lower intensity than the reference requires ("not a breakdown; a human moment" vs. the reference's "one emotional meltdown"). Ch48, tied to Marcia's death and the failed ledger, is the strongest candidate and should be named the primary placement.

4. **Zoticus is not connected to the Antonine Plague arc anywhere in the outline**: V2_HISTORICAL_IMPACT.md explicitly runs the plague preparation "through the physician line (Heras → Zoticus)" and names Zoticus as the executor of the variolation program. Neither his name nor the variolation program appears in any of the outline's plague-prep entries (ch40, ch44, ch46, ch47). A writer following the outline will produce the plague arc without Zoticus, which contradicts the reference.

5. **Vibenius death date requires precision in ch35 drafting**: The canon log pins Vibenius's death at ~125 AD; the outline says he dies "within months" of his called-favor scene in ch35. Because ch35 spans 121–125 AD, this is technically compatible but requires the called-favor scene to occur near the chapter's end (~124–125 AD), not its beginning. Writers need this flag to avoid placing the death at 121–122 AD, which would contradict the canon log.
