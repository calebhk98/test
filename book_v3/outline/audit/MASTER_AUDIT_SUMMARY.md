## HIGH PRIORITY (Significant plot/continuity gaps)

### FLIGHT VOW — VOW WORDING INCONSISTENCY (ch13)
**File:** audit_flight_vow.md
updated_ch01_18.md says "I won't **put** untrained people into an untested design."
Every other source says "I won't **rush** untrained people." The word "rush" is
load-bearing — it encodes that this is about pace and preparation, not prohibition.
The wrong word turns a temporary caution into a permanent-sounding ban.

### FLIGHT VOW — TRAJAN'S TWO-QUESTIONS SCENE UNASSIGNED
**File:** audit_flight_vow.md
V2_HISTORICAL_IMPACT describes a specific named scene (Trajan: "Can it carry fire?
Can an enemy shoot it down?") that creates the permanent balloon unit (~107–108 AD).
No chapter owns this scene. It must be placed in ch19 or ch20.

### FLIGHT VOW — BALLOON RIGS ABSENT FROM ALL ch37–53

voyages (Part V onward)." Zero entries in updated_ch37_53.md mention this across
all 17 chapters.

### ATLANTIC CROSSING — COMPASS TIMELINE OFF BY 4–6 YEARS

bottleneck chain). The outline's cross-chapter continuity block says "Compass
working by ch23" (~109–110 AD). This breaks the entire Atlantic timeline. Fix:
add compass confirmation in ch16–ch21, or correct the continuity block.

### ATLANTIC CROSSING — MORTALITY WRONG IN ch28

### WRITING CONTEST — ROMEO & JULIET CONTRADICTION
**File:** audit_writing_contest.md

### WRITING CONTEST — TWO-ROUND STRUCTURE NEVER PLACED ON PAGE
**File:** audit_writing_contest.md
The judges-cull → public-readings → token-vote structure (the core mechanic that
creates the class tension between Judges' Prize and People's Prize) is never placed

### RHINE SEMAPHORE — 23-YEAR CONTRADICTION
**File:** audit_tech_timeline.md
master_outline.md says Rome-to-Rhine semaphore operational by 130 AD.
V2_REVISED_OUTLINE continuity table says "Rhine line ch49" (~153–155 AD).
These conflict by 23 years. Ch40 shows Rome-to-Adriatic (~130 AD) but not Rhine.
A chapter between ch40 and ch49 must show Rhine completion, OR ch40 must be
expanded to the full Rome-Rhine line.

### PENDULUM CLOCK — INTERNAL CONTRADICTION
**File:** audit_tech_timeline.md
Ch47 shows Daniel permanently giving up on the escapement mechanism.
Ch49 lists "pendulum clocks in public buildings" in his legacy inventory.
These cannot both be true. Fix option A: add a Phase D prize-winner scene where
someone else solves the escapement (ch48–ch49). Fix option B: remove clocks from
the ch49 legacy inventory.

### ULPIA — LUCANUS SYSTEMATICALLY UNDERREPRESENTED
**File:** audit_ulpia_education.md
Planning doc (V2_ULPIA_EDUCATION.md) gives Lucanus fully equal education depth
to Ulpia — rich rendered conversations, Star Wars politics discussion, atom bomb
ethics, Measure for Measure. The outline treats his education as a single
background narration note. His ch38 truth-telling must be an on-page scene,
not background. He needs at least one rendered education beat in ch23–ch38.

### ULPIA — WRONG SURVEY IN ch46
**File:** audit_ulpia_education.md
Ch46 brief assigns Ulpia an epidemic disease encyclopedia survey. Planning doc
specifies the Atlantic/ocean geographic survey — "laid down, not closed" — with
a specific exchange about involving Lucanus. These serve different arcs and
different functions. The disease survey belongs elsewhere; the ocean survey is
the correct assignment for ch46.

### FINANCES — BALLOON CONTRACT NEGOTIATION MISSING
**File:** audit_finances_prize.md
The balloon contract (~200–250K HS personally to Daniel) is described in planning
docs as his "first real leverage moment" and a pivotal financial turning point.
The outline has no chapter-level beat for the negotiation drama itself. A scene
must be assigned (likely ch11–ch14).

### FINANCES — PRESS 1–3M HS/YEAR NEVER ASSIGNED AS NARRATIVE MOMENT
**File:** audit_finances_prize.md
The paper/press empire reaching 1–3M HS/year is what enables Daniel to fund
decade-long failed experiments. This financial milestone has no chapter assignment
as a narrative moment — it exists only in the arc table. Writers won't dramatize
it without a chapter brief.

### HISTORICAL DIVERGENCE — "EAST CAN'T REACH WEST" SPEECH ASSIGNED TWICE
**File:** audit_historical_divergence.md
V2_REVISED_OUTLINE assigns this speech to ch35. updated_ch37_53.md assigns it to
ch38. Pick one chapter and remove it from the other.

### CH17 MORAL STRUGGLE — COMPRESSED TO A CLAUSE
**File:** audit_flight_vow.md

Daniel's discomfort) as requiring "real moral weight, not avoidance." The outline
