## WHAT THE PLANNING DOCS SAY

The food arc is governed by two sources: **V2_FOOD_ARC.md** (the dedicated planning
document) and the food-beat requirements embedded in **master_outline.md**. The two
are largely consistent with each other. Key rules:

**Treatment of food:**
- Food is texture, not plot. One scene maximum per Part. Never a storyline.
- Daniel's food moments are private; tech work is for someone, food is for himself.
- He does not give speeches about food. He shows, demonstrates, tastes, adjusts.
- The garum evolution (disgust → acceptance → working familiarity) is shown across
  chapters, never announced.

**The four-beat food arc structure (per V2_FOOD_ARC.md Section 5):**
1. First private omelette — Phase A, Ch03 area
2. Tomato sauce on pasta — Phase D, post-Atlantic harvest, Ch36–38 area
3. Tavern food scene (his recipe on a stranger's menu) — Ch34, specified
4. Late-life garden tomato — Ch50, specified

**Ingredient timeline (strict):**
- Phase A–C (98–~117 AD): Roman ingredients only. Pasta (wheat+eggs), omelette,
  fig-and-vinegar reduction (not ketchup — no tomatoes). Garum evolution.
- Phase D (~117+ AD, post-first expedition return and 1–2 growing seasons):
  Sweet potato, corn/polenta available. Tomatoes available one growing season later.
  Cacao potentially available mid-120s AD from Mesoamerican contact.
- Phase E (130s AD onward): New World crops are household routine.

**Chapter assignments per V2_FOOD_ARC.md Section 7:**

| Beat | Chapter | Content |
|------|---------|---------|
| Garum disgust → fish sauce recognition | Ch03–05 | First encounter, evolution |
| First private omelette | Ch03 | Brief, private |
| Fig-and-vinegar reduction | Ch05 | Wants ketchup, makes substitute |
| Pasta introduction | Ch15 or Ch16 | Workshop kitchen |
| Tavern partnership planted (Gnaeus) | Ch15–16 area | Verbal arrangement |
| Army food contrast | Ch28/29 | Campaign eating vs. workshop |
| Tavern food scene (beat 3) | Ch34 | His recipe, unattributed |
| Sweet potato / corn/polenta | Ch35–36 area | Post-expedition |
| Tomato-sauce-on-pasta (beat 2) | Ch36–38 area | The payoff |
| Ketchup | Immediately after tomatoes | One sentence |
| Cacao / hot chocolate | Ch43 | Specified, preservation-protected |
| "Barbarian pasta" diffusion | Ch48 background | |
| Late-life garden tomato (beat 4) | Ch50 | One sentence |

**The "Tomato Moment" per master_outline.md:**
He makes tomato sauce, puts it on pasta, eats it, puts the bowl down, says nothing.
Three sentences maximum. No commentary. Twenty-plus years of absence resolved privately.

**The food-as-business angle:**
One tavern partnership (Gnaeus). Not a restaurant empire. Daniel supplies recipes;
Gnaeus runs the place; Daniel gets a small cut and a social node. Gnaeus should appear
3–5 times, always briefly, always in the workshop neighborhood.

## TREATMENT AS TEXTURE CHECK

The planning docs specify one scene maximum per Part and explicitly prohibit food
from becoming a plot driver. Cross-chapter count:

- **Part I (Ch01–11):** Ch03 (garum encounter), Ch04 (garum evolution), Ch05
  (omelette + fig-vinegar). Three chapters with food notes, but all are brief background
  texture (one paragraph or less in each). No single scene dominates. **PASS** — within
  the "texture not plot" mandate. The planning doc actually describes this phase as
  "across chapters 03–05 in small moments, never announced" which exactly matches.

- **Part II (Ch12–22):** Ch16 has the pasta introduction. Ch28 (army food contrast) is
  in Part III/IV territory. No other food beats in Part II proper. **PASS.**

- **Part III (Ch23–33):** No food beats in any chapter brief from Ch23–33 except the
  army contrast in Ch28 (which actually straddles Parts III–IV). The Gnaeus partnership
  formalization is in this range per the planning docs but absent from chapter briefs
  (see Discrepancy 2). **BORDERLINE** — the absence of Gnaeus is a gap, not an overuse.

- **Part IV (Ch34–38):** Ch34 has the tavern food scene (correct, beat 3 of 4). Ch35
  has the sweet potato/polenta beat. Ch36 has the (problematically placed) tomato beat.
  Three food notes in four chapters is pushing at the "one scene per Part" guidance.
  **BORDERLINE OVERUSE** in this range — three chapters (34, 35, 36) in a four-chapter
  window each have food beats. The planning doc says "one scene per Part maximum" as
  flavor, not plot. With Ch34–38 as Part IV (per master_outline.md numbering), having
  Ch34, Ch35, AND Ch36 all carrying food beats may be slightly over-indexed. However,
  these are all texture-level beats (no scene exceeds ~200 words) so the spirit is
  maintained even if the letter (one per Part) is slightly exceeded.

- **Part V (Ch38–46, per master_outline.md):** Ch43 has the cacao scene (specified,
  preserved). No other food beats. **PASS.**

- **Part VI/VII (Ch47–53):** Ch48 background beat (barbarian pasta, one line). Ch50
  late-life tomato (missing from ch50 brief, per Discrepancy 4). **PASS if Ch50 beat
  is added as one sentence.**

**Overall treatment verdict:** The food arc is being treated as texture, not plot.
No chapter makes food its main business. The cluster in the Ch34–36 window is slightly
dense but none of the beats are scene-level; they remain one-paragraph or less. The
mandate is honored in spirit throughout.
