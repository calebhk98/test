They were NOT updated to reflect the **major plot changes** mandated by the V2 reference files. Most of the new V2 story beats — Atlantic crossing, writing contest with judges+public vote, card games/gambling/casino, food arc, Ulpia's education, prize/innovation model, antagonist exits — are entirely absent from the chapters they were assigned to.

The fixing agent needs to understand: this is not a prose pass. This is a structural addition of entire plot threads that never made it into the drafted chapters.

**What V2 says:** First crossing ~112-115 AD (Daniel age ~31-34). Compass by ~103-105 AD, wealth threshold met ~108-112 AD. Columbus-style dead reckoning, no chronometer needed.

**What the chapters say:** The Atlantic crossing never happens in Daniel's lifetime. Ch28 ("East") has the ocean idea as "half-formed." **The contradiction is total.** The chapters follow V1's no-crossing model. The V2 reference files (V2_NEW_WORLD_CONTACT.md, V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES.md Section 1.3) mandate a crossing in Daniel's lifetime, ~112-115 AD.

**COORDINATOR DECISION REQUIRED** before writing agents touch Atlantic content: the ch32-37 agent flagged that V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES.md Section 1.3 directly contradicts V2_NEW_WORLD_CONTACT.md Section 8, V2_MASTER_CONTEXT.md Change 4, and the canon log (ch36 = first pitch, no prior crossing). You need to decide: does the Atlantic crossing happen in Daniel's lifetime (V2 reference files say yes) or is ch36 the first pitch (V1 model, which the chapters follow)?

**If the crossing happens:** Chapters needing structural addition: ch27-31 (planning/prep), ch29-31 (first crossing and return), ch32-37 (second wave, colonial program), ch39-53 (consequences and crops).

**If ch36 is first pitch:** Chapters ch50, ch51, ch52 need updating to remove contradictions; V2_NEW_WORLD_CONTACT.md and V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES.md need correcting.
