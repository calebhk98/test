### 2a. Correctio / "not X, it's Y" (ZERO TOLERANCE)
- "It was not the enemy." — BLOCKER. Classic correctio: names what it is NOT before saying what it is. The sentence stands alone as a one-sentence paragraph: "It was not the enemy." followed by new paragraph "A rider had come up the road in the dark..." This is the banned construction in its starkest form.
- "I am not proud of how fast it turned to me. It turned to me before the water boiled." — Not a correctio; self-deprecating confession about speed, not a "not X, it's Y" pivot.
- "He had died at a town on the coast called Selinus, in Cilicia" — statement, not a correctio.
- "The wall did not fall down all at once. That was the worst of it" — "did not fall down all at once" defines the event by what it did NOT do. Borderline correctio. The sentence pivots from negation ("did not fall down all at once") to the revelation ("That was the worst of it").
