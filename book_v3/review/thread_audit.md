## 1. UNRESOLVED AT END OF BOOK

> These seeds are introduced somewhere in the novel and have **no closing chapter** by the end of ch53. Grouped by severity (genuine gap vs. design-open vs. minor/omission-closed).

### A. GENUINE LOOSE ENDS (not resolved, not flagged as intentional design)

**NASO (Titus Aelius Naso)**
Introduced ch11 (burned in first manned balloon attempt; ruined left hand, bad ankle). Kept on mule duty by Daniel's word (ch14). Present at ch13. Last confirmed alive ch29 (heading west with Parthian baggage train). **No further mention in ch37–53. Fate never established.** Flagged explicitly by all three agents as a never-close. This is the clearest unambiguous loose end in the novel.

**SCAEVA — ultimate fate and sulfur-supply secret**
Manius Fulvius Scaeva introduced ch25 (customs-gate sulfur favor). Active hook through ch26–32. Withdrew cold in ch33 (returned tablet with seal unbroken; he did not denounce). His knowledge of Daniel's sulfur import route remains in the world. His political fate under Antoninus Pius is never stated. The "hook" he held never springs. Flagged by ch37–53 agent as a minor but genuine loose end.

**SARMIZEGETUSA WATER-CUT / MORAL COMPLICITY**
Seeded ch18 (Daniel ordered the water channels cut; Apollodorus's "same knowledge" line; "I came down off that mountain a different man"). The ch18 agent flags it as an open moral knot. The ch19–36 agent does not explicitly close it. The ch37–53 agent notes Daniel's "unbuilt dead" list in ch47–50 and a general reckoning with complicity in the Bar Kokhba accounts (ch45), but the Sarmizegetusa water-cut specifically is not revisited on the page or formally resolved. **Partially absorbed into the broader hubris-or-duty thread (which is design-open), but the specific act is never addressed directly.** Flagged as a borderline genuine gap.

**DEMETRIOS (glass-cutter / lens-grinder, ch23)**
Introduced ch23 as the craftsman who ground the failed lenses for the telescope. No further mention in ch37–53. Never explicitly closed. Minor character; likely omission-closed, but not confirmed.

**MAXIMUS (stonecutter / quarry owner, ch23)**
Introduced ch23 for the rail-cart demonstration. No further mention. Minor character; closed by omission, not on the page.

**THE OCEAN HULL at the western yard (ch44)**
Third hull physically afloat at the western yard south of the Garonne. Program lapses in ch46 (Hadrian dies; Antoninus Pius shows no appetite). Hull's physical fate — rotted, repurposed, or otherwise — is never stated. The ch53 ocean crossing happens but is generational, not tied to this specific hull.

**ULPIA's marriage / "where she likes or not at all"**
Marcia floats the Ennii match in ch43; her dying instruction cancels it in ch48 (Ulpia free to marry or not). Whether Ulpia marries anyone is never resolved. Minor open — her arc closes on a functional enterprise victory, but the personal question is left dangling.
