## 2) PROFILE ADHERENCE (per profiled character)

### Daniel / Marcus Ulpius Danihel — [OK], with one [FLAG] watch (Latin drift)
- Voice on the mark: dry, self-undercutting, modern reference no one gets, the
  loneliness shown-not-stated. ch38 the glasses joke ("He would have walked into any
  shop... bought a pair of glass disks... and I am the only one who will ever get it")
  is exactly the bible's "reaches for modern references and has no one to share them."
- "NOT a prodigy / failing memory" rule honored HARD and on-escalation per the
  timeline. ch40 is a tour de force of the bible's knowledge-ceiling: the lost
  heat→motion bridge ("I have only the two ends of the rope and not the middle"),
  the quadratic sign costing "most of a morning" ("a thing a boy is made to memorize
  in a week"), the uncertain outer planets, the blank chemistry page. This is the
  bible DON'T ("don't make him remember things a normal teen wouldn't") inverted into
  the engine of the section. [OK — exemplary]
- Core engine ("terror of dying with everything still in his head... over decades the
  larger fear") is now the literal plot: ch38–41 the books/institutions race against
  his own mortality and failing eyes. Bible arc endpoint ("institution-builder racing
  his own mortality") is precisely where the prose is. [OK]
- He "never stops being a transplant who doesn't fully belong": ch41 sleet word, ch43
  the unfinishable song — the un-sayable origin rendered through objects, never
  monologued. Matches style guide "show the loneliness; never have him monologue it."
- [FLAG — intended drift, verify it stays gradual] Style guide: "his English narration
  slowly picks up Latin cadence... by the late book he thinks partly in Latin and has
  to reach for the English word. Show this drift gradually; do not announce it."
  STATUS: The drift IS present and is correctly NOT announced — but it is rendered
  almost entirely as LEXICAL GAPS (English words he has LOST: ch43 "I have no Latin
  word for it and no English one either"; ch40 reaching for the homeland word for cold;
  the sleet word; the lost song-word) rather than as Latin SYNTAX/CADENCE bleeding into
  the English sentences. The narration's sentence rhythm is still fundamentally a
  contemporary literary English (long periodic sentences, em-dash-free, American). This
  is arguably the more tasteful reading of the instruction, and it is internally strong
  — but if a reviewer expects audible Latinate cadence (subject-final emphasis, calque
  idiom), it is not really there yet at age 53. NOT a deviation from any hard canon;
  flag only so the coordinator can confirm the chosen interpretation (lexical-loss, not
  syntactic-calque) is the intended one for the late book. The glossary/style "Daniel
  thinks in Latin" expectation is being met semantically, not prosodically.

### Marcia — [OK]
- Bible: "runs accounts and the business side... with more nerve than most men in the
  room"; "a genuine partner, not a prize"; "her own agenda for the enterprise and any
  children"; "keep it unsentimental and real." All four are load-bearing here.
- Voice (flat, finds the level, hard word first): ch34 "Go wash your feet. You smell
  like the portico." ch38 "your head is starting to go." ch43 "She has a head no one
  will pay for and a name that's half yours." Verbatim-register match.
- AGENDA for the children is the spine of ch41/43: she fights to keep Ulpia fit for
  the world that exists, not the one Daniel believes in — exactly the bible's "her own
  agenda... for any children." The marriage-shopping for Ulpia (ch43) is unsentimental
  and real, and the one near-tender line is explicitly framed as the most she can
  manage ("as close as she came that year to saying it grieved her"). [OK — strong]
- The institution-building (ch39, the collegium/burial-club, the Minerva endowment,
  the foundation-in-trust) is hers, not Daniel's — correctly positions her as the
  partner who "manages what he can't." [OK]

### Tyche — [OK]
- Bible arc endpoint: "freedwoman -> partner in the enterprise -> survivor who outlives
  Daniel and carries the ladder forward (epilogue link)" + "keeper/copyist of the
  encyclopedia." This range is that role made central: she is the SOLE whole-cipher
  reader (ch40), enforces the honesty-marking harder than Daniel ("whether you know
  that or only feel it" — ch40), and engineers the keeper-chain so the books outlive
  them (ch41).
- Voice (terse, sharp, fierce about the work, freer-tongued than is safe): ch34 the
  flat city Latin tracking the double-billed load; ch36 "You are going to point him at
  your house" / "Your house is on the other side of the ocean" — sharp, unsafe-true,
  and the bond is sibling/found-family not romance (bible DON'T honored).
- Bible DON'T ("don't make her gratitude saccharine; she has her own anger"): honored —
  she is the one who keeps the box's location from Daniel (ch34), sets the terms, and
  is "more Tyche's pupil than mine" re Ulpia (ch43). [OK — exemplary]

### Heras — [OK], and the ch42 DEATH is handled per the task's instruction
- Bible: "dry, learned, ironic, allergic to enthusiasm... quotes Hippocrates and
  Epicurus... reader's skeptic on the page... will likely die before him (a real loss
  to land mid-late book)." This is delivered.
- Voice intact to the end: ch37 "I am too old to teach another barbarian his manners";
  ch38 "it is a thing your people had and you do not, which is most of what your people
  had"; ch42 "you Greek bastard, the water was the whole thing" (Daniel's line back at
  him) and the dying joke about the gods having "a low sense of humor." Reads
  Hippocrates "four hundred times" (ch38) — bible's Hippocrates tag, literalized.
- FUNCTION as conscience/skeptic is explicitly the THEME of his death (ch42): "there
  will be no one to read the too-certain sentence back to you" — the bible's "keeps the
  science honest / reader's skeptic" turned into the stakes of losing him. [OK]
- **GRIEF SHOWN NOT ANNOUNCED (task watch-item):** PASS, and notably well executed.
  The death itself is undramatized (ch42: "He died on the third morning, before I came
  up the hill, so I was not in the room"). Daniel explicitly REFUSES to narrate the
  pyre ("I am not going to write what it was... you do not say the loud true thing out
  loud") — which is itself the style guide's rule ("must not... monologue it") made
  diegetic. The grief is then rendered entirely through ACTION/ABSENCE: the worn joke
  that comes up "shaped and ready" with "nowhere to go" at the empty bench, and Daniel
  asking the empty room aloud, in Greek, "whether I knew that or only felt it." This is
  textbook show-don't-tell and matches the bible's "a real loss to land." Canon log
  ch42 confirms the deliberate choice to render grief through absence (NOT a kept-object
  talisman, unlike the ch13 rag / ch37 plumb-line). [OK — exemplary; the task's central
  concern is satisfied]

### Titus Flavius Macer — [OK]
- Bible voice: "Calls Daniel 'the Thulean' or 'boy' long past when it fits" — ch43
  "He called me Thulean still, as he had for thirty-five years and boy before that."
  Direct. The gold thumb-ring tic continues (ch43), consistent across the whole book.
- Bible: "genuinely fond, genuinely exploitative" and "possible late-book
  reconciliation." ch43 lands the fond/reconciliation note WITHOUT going soft: "You did
  all right, boy," in a voice Daniel had not heard him use — "and in the morning denied
  it." The denial keeps him in character (mean, undemonstrative). [OK]
- Age tracks: ~50 in 98 → ~86 in ch43 (canon log). The "being eighty-six was the only
  thing left that genuinely interested him" is good aging. [OK]

### Gaius Julius Hermes — [OK]
- Bible arc: "the man who keeps chasing repeatable steel and mostly fails, which is
  honest and bitter." ch39 makes this STRUCTURAL: he is the first beneficiary of the
  failure-patronage fund, "paid to fail," failure-record kept in the cipher. Bible's
  "no thanks and much complaint" rendered: "with no thanks and much complaint about the
  board's record-keeping." [OK]
- Voice (terse, proud, dismissive-then-loyal) consistent; ~60s with a bad shoulder.
  Embodies the steel chokepoint per bible. [OK]

### Apollodorus of Damascus — [OK]; THE FALL (task watch-item) fully dramatized
- Bible: "the one real mind in Rome who can meet Daniel as a peer... He historically
  clashed with Hadrian (who later had him killed/exiled) — a usable late thread." The
  thread is paid in full across ch35–37.
- The clash is SEEDED organically in ch35 (Daniel realizes, watching Hadrian sketch the
  segmented dome, that the bearded man Apollodorus told to "go and paint his gourds" is
  the emperor) and DETONATED in ch37 (Apollodorus's joke about the goddesses cracking
  their heads → exile → death in exile). Historically faithful (the gourds/melons
  insult, the Temple of Venus and Roma critique).
- Bible "professional respect, rivalry": the ch37 yard scene ("Take it... to remember
  which of us was the better engineer" — the plumb-line gift-as-insult) is peer-rivalry
  to the last. His pride/inflexibility ("A man right for sixty years does not start
  lying to power at seventy") is the bible's planted "his fall comes from pride."
- "Daniel loses a peer and maybe tries and fails to save him" (timeline): rendered with
  unusual honesty — Daniel DOES try (the hedged half-sentence to Theophanes) and fails,
  and the chapter's real subject is Daniel's self-indictment that he let the sentence
  die to protect himself ("I weighed the master of the bridges against my own thin
  walls"). Apollodorus names Daniel's whole survival-by-folding as the price of his
  rise ("you bend... you call that surviving"). This is the bible's Daniel-DON'T ("let
  him win without cost") honored as cost. [OK — exemplary]

### Hadrian — [OK]; the bible's three adjectives are the design of ch35–36
- Bible: "intellectually voracious, prickly, dangerous... receptive to long-term
  projects in some moods, suspicious in others." Every word is dramatized:
  - VORACIOUS: ch35 takes the new figures "by the throat," works the grain-levy sum
    himself, co-opts Daniel's press insight as his own within the quarter-hour; ch36
    seizes the ocean-map "whole inside a quarter-hour."
  - PRICKLY/dangerous: ch35 lays "three traps... and stood over them to watch which I
    stepped in"; the read-light-on-you staging; "I will remember you for it." The
    bible's "dangerous" is rendered as the patron-vs-predator ambiguity Daniel cannot
    resolve ("A patron tries to catch you out so he can trust you. A predator tries to
    catch you out so he can price you").
  - The bible's "receptive to long-term projects in some moods" = the ch36 ocean
    program (a consolidator who counts, not a conqueror): "He gave it the shape of a
    consolidator who counts." Fits Hadrian's historical consolidation + philhellenism.
- Historically grounded and consistent with the timeline divergence ledger (the bounded
  ocean program is logged as the FIRST DIVERGENCE, ch36, with an explicit plausibility
  rationale). The beard "Greek on a Roman face among clean-shaven men" is correct
  period detail and load-bearing for the Apollodorus recognition. [OK — strong]

### Trajan — [OK] (referenced only)
- Used purely as the CONTRAST against Hadrian (ch35 "I had braced for the soldier's
  plainness... and walked instead into a working library"; ch36 "he was no Trajan and
  would not feed an ocean what Trajan fed a desert"). Consistent with his bible profile
  (soldierly, practical, a builder who likes engineers; "the state is lethal"). [OK]
