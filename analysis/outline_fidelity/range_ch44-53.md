- [RENDERED] Gifts serve cruelty. Figures make the army's grain legible; "my drains

- [RENDERED] Darkest chapter. Tone and content (slave-price column, sealed caves)
  deliver this.
- Plant (unresolved moral core): RENDERED. Plant (reckoning with his own legend):
  RENDERED — "The Thulean is the name a slaughtering empire gives the foreigner who
