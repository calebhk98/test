## ch23 — The Things That Don't Work

Brief Beats: (a) honest failure chapter; (b) mechanical clock escapement never
clicks in memory -> beautiful water clock instead; (c) telescope = blurry,
color-fringed toy; (d) rail carts demoed in a mine, NOT adopted because slaves
are cheaper (the bitterest beat); (e) what losing teaches him.
Brief Plants: (1) limits of one man's memory; (2) economics that defeat the
anti-slavery machine.

- Beat (a) honest-failure framing — RENDERED. Opening: "the silence a workshop
  makes when a thing is supposed to move and doesn't... waits for me to explain
  why I lied to them." Whole chapter is three sustained failures.
- Beat (b) clock escapement / water clock — RENDERED, strongly. "I could not
  remember the piece." Long, specific account of the escapement he cannot
  reconstruct ("the tick was the clock"), explicitly leading to the water clock:
  "So I... built the thing I could build... I built a water clock." And the
  water clock honestly does NOT work either: "It kept time the way a drunk keeps
  a promise... a wet decoration." Exceeds the brief (the water clock is itself a
  qualified failure, not a clean substitute).
- Beat (c) telescope blurry/color-fringed — RENDERED, strongly. "a slightly
  closer, dimmer, blurrier, rainbow-edged view of a thing I could already see
  fine with my own eye." Color fringing ("a thin skin of color... blue down one
  side and a sick yellow-red down the other"), striae/bubbles named.
- Beat (d) rail carts demoed, NOT adopted because slaves cheaper — RENDERED,
  strongly, and is the chapter's climax. NOTE the brief says "in a mine"; the
  prose relocates it to a **stone-cutter's yard** hauling blocks to a barge
  landing ("a stone-cutter's yard up past the brickfields"). Minor setting
  deviation, not a contradiction — the economic logic is fully delivered:
  "'You own the men, Maximus.'" and "a back costs less than a wheel." Demo
  works ("Four men and a slope did the work of twenty-six men and a whip"),
  rejected on arithmetic, line torn out.
- Beat (e) what losing teaches him — RENDERED. Closes on the books as the only
  remaining move: "I can write the rungs down... so that when the sum finally
  changes... the men standing where Maximus stood will have the wheel turned
  true."
- Plant (1) limits of one man's memory — RENDERED. "The book was my own head and
  the page was torn out before I arrived." Reinforced re: clock and lens.
- Plant (2) economics defeating the anti-slavery machine — RENDERED. Dacian war
  glut explicitly tied in: "there are always more off the boats from Dacia
  cheaper this year than last."

Additions not in brief: ties the failures to the Minerva contest (a boy's tale
demanding the microscope glass); Marcia's "Everything is a toy until it makes
money" — she sells the bad spyglasses to the rich. Geta's hand/eye referenced
(powder injury, presumably ch21). Goal MET. Special checks all satisfied (mine
vs. stone-yard the only deviation).

   the brief's "Kitos War backdrop" gets atmosphere more than event.
