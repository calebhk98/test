# THE WORLD AT 100 AD — alternate-history snapshot
*(Divergences from our timeline began 260 BC. Simulated in fog-of-war turns; see TIMELINE.md.)*

## The four powers

### EGYPT (Ptolemaic) — independent, wealthy, naval-scientific
- NEVER absorbed by Rome. Stable adult successions skipped the historical decline.
- Holds Egypt + the Levant interior (lost the Levant *coast* + Cyprus to Rome by 100 AD), the
  Kushite/Meroitic **iron** belt, a Red Sea trade **near-monopoly**, and an African dominion (Nubia,
  Red Sea/Horn chokepoints).
- Dominant-but-now-contested navy; native machimoi + Greek phalanx + African war-elephants.
- **Alexandria leads the world in science/engineering.** Invented nothing anachronistic — it's just
  the unbroken Hellenistic scientific frontier, 130+ years longer than in our history.
- Locked in a long bipolar war with Rome. Survives by sea power, wealth, and keeping Rome divided.

### ROME — Mediterranean & European hegemon
- Won both Punic Wars, conquered Gaul, fell into civil war, refounded as a monarchy/empire.
- **Blocked from Egypt and the east, it turned NORTH** — holds a deeper Germania/Danube frontier than
  our history. Also holds Spain, Africa, Greece, Anatolia, inland Syria, and now the Levant coast + Cyprus.
- Invented **PAPER** early (necessity, after Egypt's papyrus embargo).
- Detached Parthia from Egypt by treaty; grinds at Egypt by sea but has never taken the Nile core.
- Only faintly aware of a distant "land of silk."

### CHINA (Han) — permanently militarist, technologically ahead in fire
- Unified (Qin→Han as in our history until ~100 BC), then **never fragmented or moderated** — the Wang
  Mang/Yellow Turban/Three Kingdoms/Xianbei collapses are all CANCELLED.
- Discovered **GUNPOWDER ~95 BC** (~1000 yrs early) and developed it *slowly and realistically*:
  incendiaries → fire-lances → a crude single-ball **proto-gun** + fragmentation **bombs**. No cannon yet,
  but no one else has anything like it.
- Shattered the steppe; its westward-driven nomads set off a **migration cascade** that reshaped the far
  west (it pulled Parthia out of the Egypt–Rome war — China never knew it had done so).
- By 100 AD **knows OF Rome ("Daqin") and Egypt by report** (its westward mission was turned back by
  Parthia). Trades directly with India.

### INDIA (Maurya) — militarist, cavalry-supreme, now reaching by sea
- Ashoka never converted; the empire never fell. A permanently expansionist subcontinental state.
- Beat back the nomad waves and **reversed the historical Kushan conquest** — pushed INTO Bactria.
- Driven by an existential cavalry war, invented **leather then iron loop-stirrups** ahead of our timeline.
- Trades with China along the Silk Road (wary peace); finished Bengal, planted a Ganga-mouth port, and is
  **beginning a maritime push toward Southeast Asia.**

## The connective tissue
- **PARTHIA** is the hinge of Eurasia — it fights the nomad cascade, truced with Rome, takes Egyptian gold,
  and deliberately **blocks direct China–West contact**.
- The **Silk Road** directly links India and China; relayed trade links India and Egypt.
- A single causal web ties the world together (China's steppe wars → nomads → India AND Parthia → the
  Egypt–Rome balance), and **no single power sees the whole chain** — exactly as intended.

## How far "ahead" is each power vs our 100 AD?
- China: centuries ahead in gunpowder/firearms; comparable elsewhere.
- India: ~centuries ahead in cavalry tech (stirrups); a unified, larger, more militarist subcontinent.
- Egypt: an independent scientific superpower that history erased — Alexandria never fell to decline.
- Rome: broadly similar power, redirected north, with early paper and no eastern/Egyptian empire.
