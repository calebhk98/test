### Should fix (revise items)

5. **"There is something..." template opener.** The chapter opens with "There is something that happens in a house in winter..." — this matches the "There is a [sound/silence/thing/step]..." banned template pattern (PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID §2.5).
