### CANON VIOLATION: Glass disc prize and Sextus Pedanius (L133-134)

This is the chapter's most significant problem. At L133, Daniel "had put out a small prize — glass, a disc the width of my palm, clear enough to read through at twice arm's length, no green tinge from the iron in the batch. One month's skilled wages for the first man who could make it." He then pays Sextus Pedanius the wages.

**Problems:**

1. **No peculium:** The canon log establishes that Daniel's peculium is granted by Macer in ch09, which takes place after the public demonstration (ch08). In ch07, Daniel is explicitly "funded on credit for the show bag" with no independent financial resources. He cannot put out prizes or pay wages.

2. **Prize model is Phase III:** The prize/contest model is conceived but explicitly labeled "UNENACTED (he's a slave)" in ch16 (103-104 AD). The formal Macer Prize is enacted in ch19 (106-107 AD). A glass disc prize in mid-99 AD is four to seven years early in both the contest-arc timeline and Daniel's financial development.

3. **Character not in canon log:** "Drusus" (Heras's contact) and "Sextus Pedanius" appear here but are not in the canon log for ch07. The canon log is an append-only record of facts established in prose. Their absence from the log means this content is either new canon that was not logged (coordination failure) or content that does not belong here.

4. **Optics arc continuity:** The telescope/optics work with Demetrios begins in ch23 (109-110 AD). A bone-ash glass clarification technique and a clear reading disc in 99 AD would preempt and contradict the ch23 "honest failure" on optics (where lenses give only a blurry, rainbow-fringed ~2x image). If Sextus Pedanius already solved the iron-color problem in 99 AD, the ch23 optics struggle is undermined.

**Verdict on this section:** The glass disc / Pedanius block (L133-134) is a continuity and canon violation that should not be in ch07. It either belongs in a significantly later chapter (after ch17, when Daniel has real money and the contest model is plausible) or should be cut entirely from this chapter and re-introduced at the appropriate phase.
