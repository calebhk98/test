**Borderline flagged (requires ruling):**

- L37: "...not Spanish, not French from two years of it in school, not anything."
  This is a list of negations used to describe the unrecognizable language sound. It is NOT a correctio in the strict sense: it does not cancel a premise by defining what something is not before defining what it is. It is Daniel ruling out known languages to land on total unfamiliarity. The final beat — "not anything" — is not a positive re-definition; it is the point.
