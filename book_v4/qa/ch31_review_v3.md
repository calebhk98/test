## 7. Verdict

**REVISE**

### Blocking issues (must fix before next use)

1. **Correctio violation — L79** — `"I stood in the ink smell and was, for one breath, less alone than I had been in three years."` — "Less X than Y" as a scene-closing rhetorical pivot is zero-tolerance banned (PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID §1.1, §2.6). The sentence also announces an emotion (the feeling of being less alone) rather than showing it. Fix: cut the comparative entirely and end on the physical sensation. Option: `"She turned back. I stood in the ink smell. The form clicked into its chase. Outside, the press yard was quiet."` The reader can feel the connection through the scene's preceding exchange.

### Revise items (should fix)

2. **Wisdom-button surplus — L31 and L109** — Chapter has two wisdom buttons, one over cap. **L109 is the weaker:** `"...because slow was real and I had nothing better."` This restates what the scene already shows (Daniel accepting the offer because it is the best available). Cut the reasoning clause; end on the action: `"He set the ring down on the chair arm and sat back, and I took what he had offered."` The reader knows why. **L31** (`"Marcia says she loves you by clearing the room of everyone but the problem."`) is better-earned and more character-specific; it can stay as the chapter's one permitted button.

3. **One-sentence paragraph density — 30%** — Exceeds the 15% cap. The excess is primarily dialogue exchanges, but the density is noticeable. The most expendable isolates: `"Not a question."` at L33 (fold into the paragraph before or after — works as `"she said, not a question"`), and one or two short dialogue isolates in the Tyche scene. A trim would bring this to ~20–22%, still above cap but substantially less intrusive.

4. **L43 monologue compression (optional)** — Marcia's colleges/dedication explanation (L43) is one extended unbroken paragraph of speech, approximately 9 lines in dialogue. It is in her voice and structurally necessary, but worth reviewing for a compression pass — either split with a physical beat (Marcia moving, touching something) or trim two sentences that restate a point already made earlier in the briefing.
