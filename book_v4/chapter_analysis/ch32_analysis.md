- **Chapter ending**: The chapter ends on Lucanus turning the buckle and Daniel answering his question ("Is he coming"): "I sat down on the floor of the press room beside my son who had decided I was his, in the ink smell, under a year that had not arrived yet, and I told him no, the soldier was not coming, but somebody was, and I did not yet know what to make for the one who was, and that was the truest thing I said to anyone all that long cold spring, and the only person I could say it to was five and turning a dead man's buckle in the lamplight, asking when." → NEAR-WISDOM BUTTON. "That was the truest thing I said to anyone all that long cold spring, and the only person I could say it to was five" — Daniel annotates the moment as particularly true and significant, plus annotates who he was able to say it to. This is a form of gnomic ending: the narrator arriving to declare the moment's significance. The final image (child turning buckle, asking when) is strong; the preceding self-annotation weakens it.

**Wisdom-button count for this chapter:** 2 (Section 3: confirmed WB; chapter ending: near-WB). Chapter ending's image is salvageable if the self-annotation before it is cut.
