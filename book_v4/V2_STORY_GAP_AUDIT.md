# V2 Story Gap Audit

## How to use this file

**Writing agents:** Before writing your chapter, find your chapter number in Section 2 and check your story requirements. Section 1 gives the full context for each requirement, including the exact text of what V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES.md requires and which chapters need to deliver it.

**Gap notation:**
- ✅ Present in V1 — the beat exists and does not need to be added
- ❌ Missing from V1 — the beat does not appear; writing agent must add it
- ⚠️ Partially present — the beat is gestured at but incomplete; needs expansion or sharpening

### 1.1 History divergence by 110 AD (V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES §1.1, §5)

**Requirement:** By the end of Part III (~110 AD), at least two concrete, on-page, measurable divergences from real history must appear in prose. Candidates per PLOT_NOTES: stirrups in Roman cavalry, epidemic curve bending (dysentery deaths measurably fewer), one siege/engagement with different outcome, one trade route opening earlier via compass. V2_MASTER_CONTEXT §3 (Change 4) specifies: by 105 AD, one documented institutional change; by 110 AD, rag paper in use, Volta pile demonstrated, Eratosthenes proof in encyclopedia.

**Assigned chapters:**
- Stirrups: ch11, ch12 (first army contact — cavalry observation); ch18 (Second Dacian War); ch21 (ch21 vow reversal, ~106 AD). Final confirmation by ch21–23.
- Epidemic bending: ch12 (dysentery in camp), ch18 (siting camps, water discipline — present but not comparative), ch21–23 (follow-up confirmation needed).
- Trade route / compass: ch23–ch26 (prototype compass ~108 AD per timeline).
- Rag paper: ch16 (press begins, ~103–105), ch19, ch23.
- Volta pile / galvanometer: ch23–ch26 (Phase C transition).
- Eratosthenes demonstration: Part I (ch04–ch10).

**Gap status:**
- Stirrups: ❌ Missing. No chapter shows Daniel noticing the absence of stirrups or introducing a leather-loop design. The V2_HISTORICAL_IMPACT.md states "stirrups universal in the cavalry arm from approximately 108 AD" but no chapter dramatizes the introduction. Required scene: Daniel sees a cavalryman mount and registers the absence; sketches a leather loop; army adopts slowly.
- Epidemic bending (comparative dispatch): ⚠️ Partially present. Ch18 shows Daniel's water discipline at work ("the flux thinned where I was listened to") but the specific comparative beat — a dispatch reading "the fourth cohort lost nine men to the flux; the second, who follow the barbarian's water discipline, lost none" — is absent. The comparison exists implicitly but is never placed in a document Daniel reads.
- Rag paper: ⚠️ Partially present. Ch16 shows block-printed sheets on papyrus (or implied). Ch06 explicitly notes "no paper" in 98–99 AD. The V1 timeline bible notes rag paper by Phase B (99–102 AD) was "promised but dropped." No chapter shows rag paper being made or first used at the press.
- Volta pile / galvanometer: ❌ Missing entirely. No chapter mentions zinc-copper cells, electrical shock, or a galvanometer. Required by ~110 AD per V2_MASTER_CONTEXT.
- Eratosthenes two-stick demonstration: ❌ Missing entirely. V2_MASTER_CONTEXT §3 (Change 5) lists this as a required story addition. No chapter shows Daniel doing this measurement. Should appear in Part I (ch04–ch10).
- Compass prototype: ❌ Missing. Ch45 mentions "square to the compass" but this is a surveying compass, not a magnetic compass. No chapter shows Daniel making or demonstrating a magnetized-needle compass. Required by ~108 AD (Phase C, per timeline).

### 1.2 Flight ban reframe — and manned flight return (V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES §1.2)

**Requirement:** After Sabinus's death (ch13–14), the vow must be restated on the page as: "I won't put untrained people into an untested design" — a safety protocol, not an abandonment. Within 5–7 years (~105–107 AD) Daniel returns to unmanned and kite-class glider work, stating the distinction. By 105–107 AD the path back to manned flight becomes clear (trained operators, 50+ hours tethered, written safety protocols, voluntary consent). The Dacian Wars make the case. Daniel chooses to continue manned flight, calculating that military advantage saves more lives than the program risks.

**Assigned chapters:** ch14 (vow statement), ch18 (Second Dacian War, ~105 AD — manned balloons with trained operators), ch21 (the vow's staged erosion — explicitly called the book's most carefully executed special check by ch21 QA).

**Gap status:** ⚠️ Partially present. Ch14's "not yet, maybe not ever" vow is present. Ch21 is flagged by V2_CHAPTER_CHANGE_NOTES as containing "the staged erosion of the ch14 vow" — this means the reversal exists in V1. However, V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES requires the vow be *reframed* more specifically as a safety-protocol rather than a traumatized teen's superstition, and that Daniel's conscious *choice* to return — weighing lives saved vs. program risk — be dramatized. The current ch14 vow wording ("not yet, maybe not ever") does not explicitly frame it as "I won't put untrained people into an untested design." The reframe needs sharper language in ch14, and ch21's erosion needs to show Daniel's deliberate calculation rather than simple drift.

### 1.3 Atlantic crossing in Daniel's lifetime (V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES §1.3)

**Requirement:** The first crossing must happen in Daniel's last years (~145–150 AD departure). Daniel watches the ship leave. The epilogue (ch53) confirms landfall and shows inhabited coast. V2 timeline: compass by ~103–105, expedition planning ~108–110, first crossing ~112–115 AD (Daniel age 31–34). Daniel is ALIVE for departure AND return. Citrus provisions explicitly specified.

**Assigned chapters:** Ch36 (Ocean Gambit setup, ~121 AD), ch44 (divergence signs, hull on the ways), ch46 (Hadrian dies, program endangered), ch49 (securing ladder), ch50 (Daniel's death), ch53 (epilogue: landfall).

**Gap status:** ❌ Missing — major structural gap. V1 as-written defers the actual Atlantic crossing to generations after Daniel's death (ch53 epilogue shows it happening "a generation, then a distant whisper" after him). The departure does NOT happen in Daniel's lifetime in V1. Ch46 explicitly frames Daniel *relinquishing* the hope; ch53 is set "long after" (~190 AD per divergence ledger). V2 requires the departure happen in ch48–ch50 range (~149 AD), with Daniel watching the ship leave from the dock. This is the book's largest structural gap requiring the most significant rewrite.

**Sub-gap — Citrus provisioning scene:** ❌ Missing. No chapter shows Daniel specifying citrus in the ocean voyage provisioning list with an explanation of scurvy symptoms (teeth falling out, bleeding gums, weakness). Required at first serious ocean planning meeting.

**Sub-gap — First Atlantic crossing timing:** V2 targets ~112–115 AD for FIRST crossing. V1 shows only "probe voyages" to ~800–1000 miles in 125, 130, 136 AD. The full crossing in Daniel's lifetime is absent. PLOT_NOTES §1.3 allows some flexibility: the ship may not return before Daniel dies, which is "acceptable dramatically." But the DEPARTURE must happen in Daniel's lifetime.
