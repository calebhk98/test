## Summary

The V2 additions collectively accomplish three things that V1 lacked: a unified briefing infrastructure, a story architecture expansion, and a prose self-correction system.

The V1 book was a complete 53-chapter draft with extensive QA and review files, but those files were scattered across 87 analysis documents that a writing agent would have to read in full to understand. The V2 addition of `V2_MASTER_CONTEXT.md`, `AGENT_WRITER_BRIEF_V2.md`, `V2_CHAPTER_CHANGE_NOTES.md`, and the six `summaries/` files collapses that research into a navigable hierarchy: one mandatory first read, one chapter-specific action list, one prose checklist, and a tiered set of reference documents for specific topics. An agent can now be briefed to write a specific chapter by reading four files instead of eighty-seven.

The story architecture expansion is equally significant. V1 underdelivered on its own premise — an ordinary teen bending history — by keeping macro-history essentially unchanged through 117 AD, permanently banning manned flight after one death, deferring the Atlantic crossing past Daniel's death, leaving all three antagonists to fade out without on-page closure, and omitting obvious modern-teen observations (lead pipes, no soap, no stirrups) that a 2020s teenager would make in week one. The V2 additions (`V2_STORY_PLOT_NOTES.md`, `V2_HISTORICAL_IMPACT.md`, `V2_TECH_DEEP_DIVE.md`, `V2_DANIEL_FINANCES.md`, `V2_DANIEL_COGNITIVE_EDGE.md`, `V2_NEW_WORLD_CONTACT.md`) correct this by specifying required divergence milestones with dates and mechanisms, mandating specific antagonist exit scenes, adding the gambling/games empire as a new revenue and cultural thread, defining the Atlantic crossing timeline (by ~112–115 AD, in Daniel's lifetime), and providing precise feasibility windows for every technology.

The prose self-correction system addresses V1's systematic AI signatures. V1 produced measurable tics at scale: 677 "the way" constructions, 26% one-sentence paragraphs, 25–37 correctio instances, ~55% of chapters ending on packaged wisdom. The V2 additions to `bible/06_style_guide.md`, the new `PROSE_PATTERNS_TO_AVOID.md`, and the `chapter_analysis/` pre-flights build a mechanical checking system into the workflow itself. Rather than hoping agents avoid these patterns, V2 gives them exact counts, specific quoted violating lines from V1, corrected rewrites, and per-chapter tic-frequency tracking. The `CHARACTER_VOICE_GUIDE.md` addresses the related failure of universal eloquence by providing tier-by-tier grammar rules, crude speech guidance, minimum direct-line counts for muted characters, and DO/DON'T dialogue pairs for every major character. Together these documents give an agent everything needed to write a V2 chapter that is not only narratively correct but also technically distinct from V1's machine fingerprint.
