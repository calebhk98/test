## A. Major arcs the manuscript has NOT yet incorporated

These are confirmed by reading the chapter prose, not just the canon log.

### A1. Atlantic crossing completes in Daniel's lifetime — **biggest delta**
- **Outline (target):** First crossing departs ch27, returns ch28 with crops (maize,
  sweet potato, cacao, rubber) and disease report; an indigenous person is brought to
  Rome and met by Daniel in ch33; Hadrian formalizes a second-wave program in ch36;
  crossings continue (ch48 second crossing; ch49 fourth hull).
- **As written:** ch27 is the impiety trial (no departure). ch28 has Daniel only
  *contemplating* "pointing the hunger west," explicitly unsure "if there was anything in
  that western sea to land on," working off "a copy off a dead phone." No expedition has
  returned. ch36 is the bounded survey program. The first actual landfall is the ch53
  epilogue, generations later. (ch46/ch48 *do* reference hulls/crossings departing —
  reconciled in this pass: imperial survey lapses, institutional crossings continue.)
- **To rewrite:** ch25 (foundational "uninhabited" lie), ch26 (expedition organized +
  prospectus + autonomy charter), ch27 (departure off-screen during the trial), ch28
  (return dispatch with crops + court-martial beat + moral-trap note), ch29 (results
  matter; second expedition), ch33 (indigenous-person meeting), ch36 (Hadrian formalizes
  second wave). Then all later chapters must treat the crossing as **done**, not folk
  legend (ch46, ch48, ch49, ch50, ch51, ch52, ch53 framing).
- **ch49 specifically:** has no ocean content and no natural anchor for the legacy
  inventory line (fourth hull under construction). A short woven legacy line should be
  added when ch49 is revised.

### A2. Manned balloon archers operational — **conflicts with a written scene**
- **Outline (target):** manned balloon program resumes ch17; trained crossbow archers
  operational by ch18; two rigs per legion by 108 AD; deployed in the Dacian and Parthian
  wars and on Atlantic ships.
- **As written:** in ch17 Daniel tells Trajan *"I make a thing fly. I don't go up in it,"*
  and reflects only that "the balloon would go back to war" as a tethered observation tool.
  The canon log shows the manned vow held as effectively unconditional.
- **To rewrite:** ch17 (Daniel chooses to resume manned flight under a safety regime, names
  the calculation), ch18 (archers deployed in the siege), and every post-ch18 siege/campaign
  scene must show manned balloon archers as established doctrine. The bible
  (`02_characters.md`, `04_tech_schedule.md`, `05_world_rules.md`) must drop the
  "vow covers all manned flight" framing in favor of "vow covers untrained operators in
  untested designs only."

### A3. Cannon eventually works
- **Outline + bible (now updated):** ch21 burst is deliberate (proves precision is the
  wall); reliable fixed-position bronze siege bombard works ~118–122 AD (ch32–34); later
  ship-mounted; mobile rapid-fire field artillery never achieved; cannon weight motivates
  rails ("slaves cannot push these far — use a train").
- **As written:** ch21 burst is present and correct. The **successful bombard is not
  written** (needs ch32–34), nor the Bar Kokhba siege deployment (~ch39), nor the
  cannon→rails argument, nor ship-mounted bombards (Phase E). ch45 prose referring to
  "no cannon a gunner can trust" must be recontextualized to mean *mobile field artillery*.

### A4. History visibly diverges before ch36
- **Outline (target):** measurable divergences from the First Dacian War (~101 AD) onward;
  a divergence checklist visible by 110 AD (stirrups, soap, rag paper, compass, Volta pile,
  press, water discipline, etc.).
- **As written:** the canon log marks **ch36 as the first divergence-ledger entry**; all
  earlier chapters note the ledger empty. The kite-recon system, stirrups, the ~900-fewer-
  dead First Dacian War outcome, and the 110 AD checklist are outline intentions not yet
  in the prose.
- **To rewrite:** ch12 (kite recon + pontoon-bridge improvement + stirrups), through ch24
  (110 AD divergence checkpoint), and the divergence ledger reconceived to start early.
