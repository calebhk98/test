# Timeline Texture Audit — Author Annotations vs. Outlines

Source: a full read of `Rome_98AD_Intervention_Timeline.md` **including the author's
first-person annotations**, checked against `V2_REVISED_OUTLINE.md` and `master_outline.md`.
Goal: make Daniel read like a real 2020s teenager — fumbling, prompted, sometimes wrong,
sometimes withholding — not a flawless tech dispenser. "Texture" = the fail / prompt /
"that's obvious" / kept-private / encounter-correction flavor, not just the tech's presence.

Status key: **MISSING** = absent from both outlines · **TEXTURE** = tech present but the
annotated reaction-beat is not. Each item gives an anchor chapter and a ready beat.

---

## (a) TRY-AND-FAIL

- **Insurance / actuarial (MISSING)** — Anchor ch19/ch26. Daniel drafts a pooled-risk cargo
  contract; one factor calls it cheating the gods, another a variant of the collegia funeral
  fund; he prices three voyages, pays one claim, then an ex-client charges fraud over a merely
  late ship; a praetor voids it; he does not revisit it.
- **Dynamite / stabilized nitroglycerin (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch34. He says aloud he only has a
  cartoon-level grasp: "Nobel, 1867. I know the name. I do not know the steps." Won't let anyone
  experiment with it. (Pair with the existing nitroglycerin dead-end beat.)
- **Absorption refrigeration (MISSING)** — Anchor ch40. Ammonia from the coke retort + water +
  heat; clay-pot test cools once; "the principle is correct, the application is for a craftsman
  with better iron pipe than I have." Hooked stroke. Doesn't pursue.
- **Photography — surprised by the fixing gap (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch40. He's certain silver
  salts darken; the image appears and he congratulates himself; by morning it's gray; he didn't
  remember a fixing agent was needed. Written in the voice of a man who thought he knew more.
- **Camera obscura — builds it, no idea what for (MISSING as standalone)** — Anchor ch16/ch23.
  Pinhole box, oiled-linen screen, inverted street image; Tyche asks what it does; "I'm not
  entirely sure yet." Sits in the corner for two years before the photography link.

## (b) PROMPTED-BY-OTHERS (wouldn't think of it until someone says something)

- **Overshot watermill (MISSING)** — Anchor ch10/ch15. A mill-hand complains the summer river
  is too slow; Daniel mentions, almost by accident, that water can fall on top of the wheel;
  he's slightly embarrassed it took a grievance to surface it. Output ~doubles.
- **Phonograph — triggered by a recording-moment (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch16. A musician plays
  something that won't be heard again; he wants to keep it and can't; by night's end he has the
  stylus-on-wax idea. He could have built it years earlier; he just hadn't needed to.
- **Cotton gin — triggered by encounter (MISSING)** — Anchor ch10/ch26. He watches women pull
  cotton seed by hand, sketches a rotating-cylinder from hardware-store intuition (not certain
  it's right), posts it as a prize spec; uncertain for a year whether he added anything but the
  idea the problem was worth solving.
- **Semaphore — institutional resistance to the need (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch23/ch35. The army
  already has way-stations and riders; he has to argue the four-day Rome–Rhine gap is a
  structural vulnerability, not a speed inconvenience. Unconvinced for two years.

## (c) "THAT'S OBVIOUS" / ALREADY-EXISTS reactions

- **Double-entry — assumes it exists, is surprised (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch05. A clerk/Tyche
  copies single-column tallies; "where's the other side?"; discovering balanced accounts aren't
  universal feels less like opportunity than finding no one has invented the pencil.
- **Mechanical calculator — Romans cite Antikythera back at him (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch39. He
  demos the marble-adder; a man says "Antikythera" in a "yes, obviously" tone; Daniel has to
  work out why a calculator that *adds* is a different thing — he wasn't expecting to explain that.
- **Three-field rotation — Romans already do two-field (MISSING)** — Anchor ch07/ch12. He pitches
  legumes in the fallow; the manager says fallow is for resting; Daniel can't explain the soil
  science, sells it on the five-year yield he also can't fully explain; one field, to end the talk.
- **Baghdad battery — Romans may already know it (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch15. Hermes reacts with
  mild recognition (heard of similar jars from a Syrian trader); Daniel isn't sure whether to feel
  relieved it's achievable or deflated it may not be his.

## (d) KEPT-PRIVATE-TO-EXPLOIT

- **Probability — withheld for the sportsbook (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch07/ch08. He teaches the
  numerals; he does not teach what they enable once you set odds; he knows it's a choice and is
  making more from the book than anything its size. He'll teach it. Later.
- **Variolation — consciously withheld ~a decade (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch03/ch05. He knows it on
  arrival; no standing, no physician relationship, no way to frame deliberate infection yet;
  cipher: "variolation — not yet."
- **Longitude — face-saving lie about why he can't (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch36. To Hadrian he says
  the celestial math is sailors' work, not his; he does *not* say he knows the principle and can't
  build the chronometer it needs. The true admission stays in private notation. (Preserve the
  existing honesty beat; add that the public version is a deflection.)
- **Newtonian mechanics / thermodynamics — kept private (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch11/ch23. He states
  heliocentrism to Heras but does not follow with F=ma or thermodynamics — no Roman audience for
  pure physics yet; cipher: "for me, not for them. Useless until the machines need explaining."

## (e) ENCOUNTER-DRIVEN CORRECTIONS

- **Horse collar — visceral correction (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch05. First cart-horse he sees is
  choked at the throat; "why is it around the neck?" in English; sketches a shoulder harness from
  films that evening; harness-maker: it'd work but it's not how things are done; "I know."
- **Wound care — sees wine, reacts before he has better (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch12. Watches a
  surgeon pour wine on a wound; asks its "proof"; no one knows the word; begins distillation partly
  because of this moment; surgeon skeptical for years.
- **Scurvy — confused it's a land case (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch12/ch28. A legionary has loose teeth
  and bleeding gums; "scurvy" slips out; then he pauses — he thought it was a sailor's disease;
  orders citrus, can't explain the mechanism, is more surprised than the cook when it works.
- **Soap — Romans don't adopt it, stays niche (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch05/ch07. He gives Heras the
  formula; Heras doesn't change his washing; the household finds the texture strange; for years it's
  his alone; cipher: "soap may take longer than numerals." He doesn't push it.

## (f) UNCERTAIN-IF-A-HS-STUDENT-WOULD-KNOW (affects how confidently he tries)

- **Legume nitrogen fixation — can't explain the mechanism (MISSING)** — Anchor ch07/ch12. "Plant
  beans in the fallow, it feeds the soil"; asked how, he doesn't know — half-remembered biology,
  never says "nitrogen"; works anyway; the manager never asks again.
- **Centrifugal pump — probable knowledge gap (MISSING, flag-as-fail)** — Anchor ch38/ch39. Vague
  hardware-store image of a spinning-center pump; can't draw it well enough to build; posts a prize
  for "a pump that moves water by turning rather than stroking"; no winner for seven years.
- **Coke production — figures it out, doesn't remember it (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch34/ch38. Knows coal
  burns hotter and that coking exists (documentary memory), not the ratios/temperatures; directs a
  smith to heat coal sealed; the third try with a smaller air opening works; "the smith got there."
- **Kerosene refining — improvises the cuts (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch28. Doesn't know the temperature
  cutpoints; separates by smell and burn test; "distil at the lowest heat that condenses; the
  clean-burning fraction comes first; discard what smells like tar." Empirical, not remembered.
- **Trade winds — admits he only knows generics (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch25/ch27. Draws the bands from
  school memory; tells the captain it's approximate, verify as you go; marks uncertainty with striped
  lines ("I believe this, not certain"). The captain treats the chart as a hypothesis — correct.
- **I-beam — unsure Rome even needs it yet (MISSING, conditional)** — Anchor ch34/ch39. Mentions the
  I-section resists bending better per weight; not sure Roman construction needs it (their work is
  genuinely good); posts it as a reference note, not a prize: "for when iron is cheaper than it is now."

## EXTRA techs/games/institutions the author wrote in the timeline, absent from both outlines

- **Board-game roster (MISSING)** — Anchor ch19/ch26. Once the press prints grids: Battleship,
  Chutes-and-Ladders, Connect Four (tavern fixture, copied from one wood set); Scrabble waits on
  literacy. A commercial line, not just chess/cards.
- **Patent law as a company practice (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch34. Can't make it law, so he adds a clause
  to prize contracts: winner gets the money plus a ten-year license fee on commercial use, paid through
  the workshop — patent behavior enforced by being a condition of doing business with him.
- **Monetary theory / inflation as casual argument (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch26/ch28. In a payment dispute
  he argues from the American gold-standard analogy (without saying so); realizes he doesn't actually
  know whether Rome debases its coinage; notes to find out; the answer is worse than he expected.
- **Civil service / merit hiring as a company counter-model (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch09/ch10. Hires for
  demonstrated ability from the first intake; tells no one it's deliberate; ends up with a freedman head
  of glass and a woman running the press — neither planned, both following the only rule he set.
- **Bulkheaded hull + lifeboats (MISSING)** — Anchor ch26/ch27. Specifies watertight compartments ("what
  the Titanic should have had," then doesn't explain what the Titanic is) and a tethered boat for half
  the crew; the shipwright charges extra and builds both.
- **Electroplating — deliberately excluded for inflation risk (MISSING, exclusion beat)** — Anchor ch40.
  A goldsmith asks if metal can be deposited by current; Daniel knows yes but won't pursue it — gilded
  bronze would feed coin-forgers; cipher: "not until the treasury is stable enough to survive it."
- **Electromagnet as a standalone demo (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch23. Day after the Volta pile: wire round an
  iron nail, current, it lifts filings; Heras: "It has become a lodestone." "Yes. And it stops when the
  current stops." The intermediate step between battery and generator.
- **Stirling engine for entertainment before industry (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch35/ch38. Before the Newcomen
  pump he builds a small hot/cold-cylinder engine driving a children's carousel at a fair; walking pace;
  the crowd is delighted; he's noting a multi-cylinder version could drive a paddle. The carousel is proof.
- **Riverine steamboat (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch35/ch39. His first steam argument to Hadrian is rivers, not
  rails: an upstream Tiber boat without tow-path, oars, or wind changes every city on a waterway; rails are
  harder; Romans already understand boats.
- **Kites as a commercial product (MISSING)** — Anchor ch07/ch10. Before the balloon, he sells linen-and-
  bamboo kites to children at market; the proceeds are negligible, the visibility is not — every child
  flying one advertises that the sky is now possible.
- **Morse — possibly misremembered, invented fresh (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch23. Writes a consistent dot-dash
  alphabet from scratch and won't call it Morse because he can't be sure it matches the original; cipher:
  "consistency matters more than authenticity."
- **Stock-market framing of the expedition prospectus (TEXTURE)** — Anchor ch26. After two merchants commit,
  cipher: "I have just run what my economics teacher would have called a stock offering. I did not explain
  this to the merchants." Doesn't formalize it.
